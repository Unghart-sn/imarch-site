This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-3** — veteran post-war
realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry
under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0003-3.6`.

## On the concept

A family at the center — a father, a mother, two daughters; around them —
semi-transparent figures of generations (an old man in a cap, a man in
profile, a man with a folder, a woman with a tablet). Symbols of family,
cycle, globe and analytics form the layer of institutional memory. An ochre
palette and soft light hold the dignity and the silence of the scene.

*Continuity that has become silence.*
