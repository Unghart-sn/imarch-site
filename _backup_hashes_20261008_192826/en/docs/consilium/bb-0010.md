This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-2** — the antique bonus
boutique (researcher's cabinet / elite heritage). Registered in the IMARCH LTD
registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0002-2.4`.

## On the concept

A female figure in Egyptian headdress with scales and a feather — an allegory
of justice in a cabinet setting. A globe, folios, brass instruments and
scrolls form around her an academic context in which law appears as a material
system rooted in tradition. Warm candlelight emphasizes the face and the hands
holding the scales.

*Law that has become a form of sovereign dignity.*
