This is the first digital artifact of series **I-3** — veteran post-war
realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry
under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0003-3.1`.

## On the concept

A family — a father in military uniform, a mother and a child — stands among
ruins. Above them hover symbolic signs: family, network, sprout, globe, shield,
analytics — emblems of hope and restoration. The poster-like clarity of the
composition is joined with the documentary credibility of the setting. The
light is even, the dignity of the figures is emphasized rather than their
suffering.

*Memory that has become strength.*
