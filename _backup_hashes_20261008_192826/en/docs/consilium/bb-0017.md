This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-3** — veteran post-war
realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry
under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0003-3.5`.

## On the concept

A girl with long hair holds a luminous crystal heart; around her — symbols of
family, cycle, globe and growth, while candles burn in the darkness. The
composition is built on the contrast between the warm glow of the heart and
the deep shadow, turning a child's gesture into an act of continuity and hope.

*Light that has become continuity.*
