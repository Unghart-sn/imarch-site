This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-2** — the antique bonus
boutique (researcher's cabinet / elite heritage). Registered in the IMARCH LTD
registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0002-2.5`.

## On the concept

An IMARCH polo at the center of the table, a "SOVEREIGN" casket with banknotes,
scrolls with sealing wax, brass instruments and digital panels with charts form
the layer of an institutional register. Leather-bound books, a stamp seal and
an inkwell complement the cabinet setting. The composition joins material
values and the document as a single act of fixation.

*Value that has become a record.*

## Technical specifications

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** dark wood, brass, blue, green baize, gold
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

## Context of creation

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-2, dedicated
to the continuity of the intellectual elite, where age-old traditions of
research and the modern sovereign contour form a single repository of values.
Series limit — 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate passport as an annex
to the base multi-passport of the series.
