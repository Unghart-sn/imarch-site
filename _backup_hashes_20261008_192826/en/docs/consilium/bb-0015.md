A family at the center of an ochre scene; on either side — witnesses (a man
and a woman with a tablet). Above the group — symbols of family, cycle, globe
and a profile of analytics. The poster-like composition joins personal memory
and institutional testimony in a single act, where loss becomes the
foundation for the future.
