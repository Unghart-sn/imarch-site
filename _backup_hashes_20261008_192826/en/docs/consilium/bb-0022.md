This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-4** — academic romanticism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0004-4.4`.

## On the concept

An antique domed temple with columns and a pediment is placed in the second
plane, while in the first — a scroll with a sealing-wax seal, a bronze
inkwell with a quill, a crystal and the inscription "IMARCH" on a stone
parapet. The composition joins architectural monumentality and institutional
artifacts into a single academic tradition.

*Eternity that has become architecture.*
