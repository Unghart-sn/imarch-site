This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-1** — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier `RN-0044-PS-0001-1.6`.

## On the concept

A paired composition to artifact I-1.4: the same scene of registration — the pharaoh on the throne, Thoth with the tablet, the goddess with the feather of Maat — but in an amethyst-violet palette. Binary sequences `01101010 10001011` in the field, gold on violet, a nocturnal atmosphere instead of a diurnal one.

This is **the same system in a different environment**. One protocol, one hierarchy, one function of recording — but a different material and luminous realisation. The artifact directly illustrates the principle of technological neutrality: the essence is not in the colour nor in the medium, but in the architecture of the action. The pairing with 1.4 is not accidental: it fixes the fact that provenance does not depend on external form.

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** amethyst, gold, silver, graphite

The work was created by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-1, dedicated to the architecture of trust and the preservation of institutional memory. The series limit is 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate passport as an annex to the base multi-passport of the series.
