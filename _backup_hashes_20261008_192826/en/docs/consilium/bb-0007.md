This is the first digital artifact of series **I-2** — the antique bonus
boutique (researcher's cabinet / elite heritage). Registered in the IMARCH LTD
registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0002-2.1`.

## On the concept

A nineteenth-century researcher's cabinet: polished dark wood, brass, leather
bindings and sealing wax. A globe-armillary on a brass stand joins an
astronomical instrument and a digital layer, while the "SOVEREIGN" caskets
and gold coins introduce the institutional register of values. A scroll with
a seal and a quill close the composition on the theme of the document as a
material carrier of trust.

*Knowledge protected by tradition, time and a sovereign contour.*

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-2, dedicated
