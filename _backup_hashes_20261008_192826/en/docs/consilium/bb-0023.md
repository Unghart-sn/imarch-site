This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-4** — academic romanticism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0004-4.5`.

## On the concept

An unrolled parchment scroll with handwritten text and the date
"16 January Anno 1603" occupies the central field. Nearby — a sealing-wax
seal, a stamp seal with a wooden handle, an hourglass and bound volumes. The
composition conveys the idea of the document as a carrier of time: the
horological fixation and the institutional seal are joined in a single
gesture.

*Time that has become testimony.*
