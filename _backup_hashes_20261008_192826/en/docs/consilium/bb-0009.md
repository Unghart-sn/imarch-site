This is the third digital artifact of series **I-2** — the antique bonus
boutique (researcher's cabinet / elite heritage). Registered in the IMARCH LTD
registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0002-2.3`.

## On the concept

A globe with a luminous constellation map and a brass meridian forms the
central object of the composition. Nearby — a brass chronometer and a tube
device with armillaries in a wooden case, closing the technological layer.
The warm light of a lamp and candles, green baize and leather bindings sustain
the cabinet atmosphere of the researcher.

*A world that has become a scheme accessible for reading.*
