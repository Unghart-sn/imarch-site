This is the first digital artifact of series **I-4** — academic romanticism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0004-4.1`.

## On the concept

A classical academic still life: a parchment scroll with a sealing-wax seal,
a laurel branch, a bronze medallion with a leaf and a painting of a tree in a
gilded frame. Brass instruments — an inkwell, scales — sustain the academic
layer, while a map and a leather-bound book complete the image of a
researcher's cabinet. The tree as a symbol of continuity links the natural
and the institutional principles.

*Eternity that has become a document.*
