This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-4** — academic romanticism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0004-4.6`.

## On the concept

A "CONSENSUS MEMORANDUM" scroll with handwritten text and an armorial seal
lies at the center. Nearby — an envelope with a red sealing-wax seal "A", an
inkwell with a quill, golden laurel leaves and antique scissors. The
composition is built as an academic document of accord, where the classical
attributes of writing and sealing form an institutional act.

*Accord that has become form.*
