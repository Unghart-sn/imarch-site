This is the second digital artifact of series **I-3** — veteran post-war
realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry
under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0003-3.2`.

## On the concept

A father in civilian clothing embraces his wife and child among ruins; above
them hover symbolic signs — network, shield, globe, sprout. Sepia and a muted
palette emphasize the memorial character of the scene, while the poster-like
clarity of the composition holds attention on the central group.

*Memory that has become an embrace.*
