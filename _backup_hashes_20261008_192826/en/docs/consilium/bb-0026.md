This is the second digital artifact of series **I-5** — Verdictum style.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0005-5.2`.

## On the concept

A marble triumphal arch with Corinthian columns and golden capitals opens a
luminous portal. At the center — a golden circle with scales and a laurel
wreath; on the pilasters — shields with scales and engraved tablets. The
composition is built on the principle of institutional hierarchy: the symbol
of law inscribed in an architectural form becomes an entrance into the
sovereign contour.

*Law that has become architecture.*
