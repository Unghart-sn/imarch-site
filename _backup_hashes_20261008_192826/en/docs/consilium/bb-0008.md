This is the second digital artifact of series **I-2** — the antique bonus
boutique (researcher's cabinet / elite heritage). Registered in the IMARCH LTD
registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0002-2.2`.

## On the concept

A large brass chronometer with an engraved dial and a double scale is placed
at the center of the composition. An hourglass, keys and coins form around it
a layer of measuring and registration instruments, while a tube device in a
wooden case with armillaries introduces the technological layer of the
ecosystem. Warm candlelight and green baize emphasize the cabinet depth.

*Time that has become institutional testimony.*
