How to Support the Vis Realitatis Project

The Vis Realitatis project is open for participation at different levels — depending on your role, capabilities, and interests.

❤️ Support as a donor
You can contribute to the project's development through the crowdfunding platform.
These funds are directed towards:
- assistance to veterans and their families,
- training for couples and facilitators,
- development of digital infrastructure,
- ensuring transparent reporting.
Every donor gains confidence that their contribution works systematically and transparently.

🤝 Partnership and institutional support
We are open to collaboration with:
- foundations and grant providers,
- NGOs and social initiatives,
- institutional and corporate partners.
For partners, a separate landing page with detailed information about the model, scalability, and sustainability of the project is available.

🎓 Participation as an expert
We invite specialists, facilitators, and researchers ready to:
- participate in practical work,
- develop methodology,
- engage in training and supervision,
- contribute to community development.
The project is built on principles of trust, transparency, and joint action.

Vis Realitatis is not a one-time initiative, but a living system.
You can become part of it right now.
