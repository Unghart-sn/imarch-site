Problem: Deactivation Stress

Veterans and their families face chronic stress, PTSD, loss of role, and lack of accessible recovery tools. The support system is overloaded, specialists are unavailable, and thousands of people are left alone with pain that only intensifies.

Learn more about the problem

 

🤝

Solution

The SunLife-Massage method — an author's protocol of bodily and psycho-emotional support, developed for working with the consequences of stress and trauma.

Experimental work → Couple training → Barter practice → Mutual aid community

We create a sustainable system where each trained couple can provide support to others, forming a "for home, for family" network.

More about the SunLife-Massage method

 

🎯

Project Goals and Objectives

We are building a sustainable model of assistance for veterans and their families.
Our goals are divided into stages so that donors can understand how the project develops:

  • 0–12 months — pilot launch, training of first couples, start of digital infrastructure.
  • 12–24 months — scaling to regions, online school, and expansion of scientific base.
  • 3–5 years — international Vis Realitatis network, SunLife-Massage® franchise, and full sustainability.

Our goal system is built according to the international SMART standard: clear, transparent, and measurable.

More about goals and objectives

Vis Realitatis: Architecture of Trust

The Key to the Project’s Success

The project’s effectiveness depends on a transparent accounting and logistics system. The key solution is implementing modern software to manage all processes.

🔑

Ecosystem and Software — Key to Project Sustainability

Vis Realitatis effectiveness is ensured by its own digital infrastructure.
It integrates accounting, training, practice, and reporting into a single transparent system.

  • Accounting — resource, session, and result control.
  • Training — online school for couples, certification.
  • Practice — schedules, protocols, communication.
  • Reporting — automatic reports for donors.

The software enables scaling assistance, reducing costs, and guaranteeing donors control and trust.
