Operational Model and APC

The journal is founded on the principles of:

  • editorial independence;
  • procedural transparency;

Peer Review Policy🔻

The Chronicle OS Journal employs a transparent double-blind peer review system.

REVIEW REGULATIONS AND STAGES

  1. Registration and Fixation: The manuscript is assigned an ID and a cryptographic SHA-256 hash lock. A Pre-publication Card is published on the website.
  2. Initial Screening: Check by the Editor-in-Chief for compliance with the journal’s scope and COPE ethics.
  3. Expert Evaluation: The anonymized text is sent to 2 independent reviewers. Duration up to 6 weeks.
  4. Verdicts: Accepted (Publication), Revision (Revision required), Rejected (Rejected without APC refund, transferred to closed archive).
  5. Finality: The decision of the Editorial Board is final.

Chronicle OS views a scientific publication not only as a text, but also as an element of research infrastructure.

🚀 More about CCS
Traditional models of scientific publication often limit the author's control over distribution and long-term management of intellectual assets. Chronicle OS offers an alternative model of research coordination in which: publications are recorded in the digital infrastructure of the platform; authorship and priority are documented; materials are integrated into a long-term research archive; elements of digital verification and cryptographic fixation are used to confirm data integrity. The platform is being developed as part of the CCS (Chronicle Communication System) infrastructure under the management of IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom).
