VENUE: City of Kharkiv, 4 Romain-Rolland Street, Cell № 27 of Shevchenko District. All-Ukrainian Organization «Children of War». DATE: 15 January 2024. TIME: 14:00 – 15:30.

PREAMBLE The present protocol formalises the relations and understandings which have evolved over the past eighteen months during the course of sustained dialogue between the Parties. Within this period:

  • Preliminary discussions concerning veterans’ issues were held;
  • The brand and “Vis Realitatis” Centre concept were formulated;
  • The project manifesto was developed;
  • An official request from the Organization describing the problematics was prepared;
  • A proposal including a roadmap, software development and formation of a focus group was drawn up.

This instrument records the attained level of mutual understanding and serves as a basis for the further institutional formalisation of cooperation. The Parties state that the present protocol does not initiate cooperation, but documents the already established practice of interaction, transferring it into an institutional plane.

AGENDA

  1. Discussion of the current situation of veterans and their family members.
  2. Identification of key issues in the sphere of physical and psycho‑emotional rehabilitation.
  3. Consideration of possible pathways for a systemic solution.
  4. Determination of further steps to formalise the cooperation.

1. STATEMENT ON BEHALF OF THE ALL-UKRAINIAN ORGANIZATION «CHILDREN OF WAR» The Chairman of the Cell, V.G. KRAVETS, presented a detailed overview of the situation of veterans and persons affected by hostilities. Principal aspects:

  • Financial‑economic crisis (insufficiency of pensions, lack of access to quality services).
  • Medical‑psychological problems (chronic illnesses, PTSD, anxiety, depression).
  • Social isolation and continuous security threat. Conclusion: The situation is of a systemic nature and requires a comprehensive institutional solution.

2. POSITION OF IMARCH LTD UK («VIS REALITATIS»)

  • The positions heard confirm the necessity of an institutional form of cooperation.
  • The supportive initiative from the Organization demonstrates the relevance of transitioning from a project format to the creation of a specialised institution.
  • The “Vis Realitatis” Manifesto and Memorandum of Values provide the basis for the further institutionalisation of the Centre. Conceptual Proposal:
  • The solution is built around a validated authorial method, integrated into digital and organisational infrastructure.
  • Training of mutual‑aid peer‑to‑peer pairs becomes key for creating a sustainable support network.
  • Effectiveness is ensured by scientific validation, digital record‑keeping and transparency (GitChronicle platform).
  • The model is designed as reproducible and scalable.

3. RESOLUTIVE PART The Parties have agreed as follows: 3.1. To recognise the described situation as a systemic problem requiring an institutional solution. 3.2. To prepare, by 31 December 2025, an official written request from Cell № 27 of the All-Ukrainian Organization «Children of War» to IMARCH LTD UK, outlining the problematics and expressing preliminary consent to participate in the rehabilitation programme. 3.3. IMARCH LTD UK undertakes to prepare and submit by 31 December 2025:

  • A concept of the pilot project with a roadmap and Key Performance Indicators (KPI).
  • A preliminary resource estimate.
  • A draft tripartite Memorandum of Cooperation (Organization – IMARCH LTD UK – Experts). 3.4. To schedule the next coordination call for 25 January 2026 to discuss the draft documents.
