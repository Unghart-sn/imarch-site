How it works: architectural logistics

🌐 Local Circuit (Front-end)
The association receives a dedicated subdomain (for example, `assoc.imarch.ltd`) with full branding. The official symbolism, member registers and full texts of publications are hosted here. You retain complete control over the internal agenda.

The Cryptographic Core of the System: The Architecture of Trust

Any distributed system or publishing platform looks attractive on paper precisely up to the moment when the question arises of security, the human factor and protection against forgery. For VERDICTUM to function as an autonomous, incorruptible Swiss mechanism rather than a vulnerable database, its foundation incorporates an autonomous cryptographic core.

This is not merely a set of scripts, but a full-fledged software and hardware circuit that addresses three fundamental tasks:

  1. Mathematical verification instead of a ‘gentleman’s word’ (Ed25519 & JCS) Every action of a participant — from registration in the system to the final verdict on a document — is signed with a personal cryptographic key. Thanks to strict data canonisation, any discrepancies or attempts to contest authorship are excluded: if a decision is signed, it cannot be revoked or forged, technically speaking.

  2. An immutable digital archive (Append-only Journal) All key events of the system are fixed in a protected journal on the principle of a continuous hash chain. Each new block is rigidly bound to the preceding one. This excludes the possibility of imperceptibly deleting a record, rewriting history retroactively or rigging peer review results.

  3. An autonomous circuit of control and protection (Watchers & Anti-Replay) The system does not require constant manual oversight. Built-in protection mechanisms cut off repeated network attacks (replay attacks) by timestamps and unique markers, while background watchdog processes automatically manage deadlines and task statuses, excluding any downtime due to the human factor.

Why this is necessary: Without this technological foundation, the entire system degrades into an ordinary chat or a vulnerable website where data can be amended and decisions contested. The cryptographic core elevates the project from the category of an ‘idea on paper’ to the status of a sovereign, protected and self-sufficient digital infrastructure that can be trusted at the level of pure mathematics.
