What is Proof-of-Work and how is it applied?

For different types of development, such confirmation may be a working prototype, software code, test results, a computational model, experimental data or a reproducible methodology.

📋 REQUIREMENTS FOR SUBMITTED MATERIALS

All incoming materials undergo automatic primary validation, SHA-256 cryptographic fixation and independent anonymous peer review (Double-Blind Peer Review).

1. File package (Dual-circuit security system)

To ensure blind peer review and the protection of authors' rights, the author uploads two files (in .docx, .doc or .pdf format):

  • Master File (Master Copy): The complete version of the work. It contains the authors' metadata (full name, academic degree, organisation, ORCID, e-mail), the abstract, the main body of the text and the reference list.
  • Blind Vector (Blind Copy): An identical text of the work from which the authors' full names, affiliations, contacts and mentions of grants/acknowledgements that could reveal the researcher's identity have been fully removed. This file is transmitted to the experts of the Editorial Board.
2. Length and structure

Recommended length: from 10,000 to 35,000 characters with spaces (from 5 to 15 A4 pages).

Mandatory elements:

  1. Title of the work: in the original language and in English.
  2. Abstract: 150–250 words. It must briefly reflect the problem, method, results and practical value.
  3. Keywords: 5–8 terms.
  4. Main body of the text:
    • Introduction: statement of the problem, relevance and aim of the research.
    • Methodology / Methods: description of the approaches and sources used.
    • Results and Discussion: principal conclusions, formulas, charts, tables.
    • Conclusion: final conclusion and prospects for application.
  5. References: formatted in accordance with the APA 7th or GOST standard. The DOIs of the cited sources must be indicated (where available).
3. Language standards and international status

The official working language of the journal VERDICTUM International Journal is English.

Options for submitting materials:

  • Fully in English (Recommended): The main body of the text, the abstract, the metadata and the bibliography are submitted in English for maximum international citability.
  • Bilingual mode: Publication of the main body of the text in the language of the authors' jurisdiction is permitted provided that complete English metadata are supplied (title, extended abstract of 200–250 words, keywords, affiliation and the reference list).

Responsibility for translation quality: Authors bear personal responsibility for the linguistic and stylistic accuracy of the materials submitted. The editorial office does not provide free translation or proofreading services.
