🏛️ Institutional Manifesto: Peer Review as Capital

The world of experts has been confronted with a crisis of peer review: experts are for years required to provide ‘free labour’ in favour of giant publishing corporations, spending weeks proofreading incorrectly formatted materials. VERDICTUM breaks this paradigm.

In accordance with the Institutional Protocol RN-0031, we regard expert peer review as a measurable professional contribution requiring direct monetisation, institutional recognition and legal protection. We guarantee that every hour of an expert’s work is transformed into their personal capital — professional, financial or status-based.

How is the economy of an expert’s time structured?

  1. Primary screening on the side of the platform: Works undergo automatic anti-plagiarism control, structure verification and computation of the SHA-256 cryptographic hash.
  2. Cleaned-up ‘Blind Vector’: The reviewer receives a structured text without authors’ identifying details (Double-Blind Peer Review).
  3. The 15-minute regulation: Thanks to the complete technical pre-preparation of the work, the expert’s net time for completing the standardised form is up to 15 minutes.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Who may become a VERDICTUM reviewer?

Our expert network invites scholars, doctors and candidates of sciences, as well as independent experts with a demonstrated professional background (the presence of a profile on ORCID, Google Scholar, ResearchGate or Scopus). Priority is given to specialists in the fields of systems analysis, institutional governance, risk management and AI architecture.

How are reviewer remuneration payments justified and made?

All payments are made officially by the international company IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom) on the basis of a Consultancy Services Agreement. Payments are accumulated and transferred monthly to your details (cards, IBAN, PayPal, Wise or USDT). At your election, fiat may be substituted by publication points or tokens of Series 8888.

What are the regulation and timeframes of blind peer review?

Peer review proceeds on the principle of Double-Blind Peer Review (the names of authors and reviewers are mutually concealed). The expert takes a work from the closed pool. The baseline regulation is up to 14 days. For hot express sprints, a rapid assessment regime of up to 2 hours is provided, with a coefficient of x3 XP.

What is Skin-in-the-Game and how does it work?

Skin-in-the-Game is a remuneration model in which the reviewer receives not only a fixed payment for the audit, but also a long-term percentage of the project's success (grant, commercialisation). Your verdict is a financially and reputationally secured mark of quality. Protection against cartels rests upon stochastic distribution and reputational staking.
