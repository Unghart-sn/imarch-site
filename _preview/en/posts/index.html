The Vis Realitatis project is built as a sustainable support system for veterans and their families.
This requires resources directed at specific, verifiable tasks.

We fundamentally do not raise funds for “abstract help.”

Funds are allocated for:

Frequently Asked Questions about Vis Realitatis

What exactly will the raised funds support?

Funds from the current fundraiser are directed exclusively to Stage 1 of the project:

  • methodology development and validation,
  • MVP organizational software creation,
  • payment for initial expert consultations,
  • prototypes (suit and mobile cabin),
  • pilot testing.

Why is fundraising divided into stages?

We consciously rejected the “raise everything at once” model.
Staged approach allows:

  • risk reduction,
  • giving donors a clear progress picture,
  • decision-making based on real results.

Why Vis Realitatis Can Be Trusted

Trust — Not a Slogan, but Architecture

We don’t consider trust a matter of faith or “word-of-mouth” reputation.
For us, trust is the result of a correctly built system.

That’s why Vis Realitatis is initially designed as a transparent and verifiable structure.

Principle 1. Staging Instead of Promises

We don’t raise funds “for everything at once.”

Each project stage:

  • has a clear goal,
  • limited timeline,
  • understandable budget,
  • specific KPIs,
  • mandatory completion report.

A new stage isn’t launched until the previous one is completed and documented.
