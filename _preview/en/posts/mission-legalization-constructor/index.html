Solution 1: Library of Institutional 'Templates' (CIC, LTD, Mandates)

📅 16 November 2023
🔍

Imagine that to build a house you don’t need to study architecture, material science, and building codes. There are ready-made designs: you choose “family home with garden,” add your preferences — and receive a complete package of blueprints, estimates, and permits.

That’s how our Library of Institutional Templates works.

Constructor Instead of Chaos

Before: A year with lawyers, thousands of pounds, risk of errors, constant stress.

Now: Choose a template → Customize for your mission → Receive a ready package.

What’s in the Library?

1. CIC (Community Interest Company) + LTD

The perfect combination for social enterprise:

  • CIC — for missionary activities, grant applications, volunteering
  • LTD — for commercial activities, investments, scaling

Advantages:

  • Legal handling of donations
  • Ability to attract investments
  • Transparent reporting
  • Protection of founders’ personal liability

2. Mandate Structures

Ready-made authority distribution schemes:

  • Operational mandate (day-to-day decisions)
  • Strategic mandate (development direction)
  • Supervisory mandate (control and audit)

No disputes about “who’s in charge” — everything is documented.

3. Succession Protocols

Automatic mechanisms for handover:

  • Criteria for successors
  • Phased transfer of authority
  • Protection of mission from “takeover”

The project outlives its founders.

How Does It Work?

Step 1: Diagnosis of your mission
Step 2: Selection of optimal template
Step 3: Customization for your needs
Step 4: Document package + step-by-step instructions
Step 5: Support during registration

Time: Days instead of months.
Cost: Minimal instead of astronomical.
Quality: Tested by dozens of projects.

Example: Educational Center

Problem: Need to legally teach children, accept donations, pay teachers, scale.

Our solution:

  1. CIC — for educational programs and grants
  2. LTD — for paid courses and investments
  3. Mandate system — clear role distribution
  4. Succession protocol — mission continuation guarantee

Result: Fully legal structure in 2 weeks.

Why Does This Change the Game?

  1. Accessibility: No more expensive lawyers needed
  2. Speed: Launch in days, not months
  3. Reliability: Tested, working schemes
  4. Focus: You work on mission, not bureaucracy

This Is Already Reality

Dozens of projects already operate using our templates:

  • Environmental initiatives
  • Educational platforms

  • Cultural projects
