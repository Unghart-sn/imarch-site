    Publishing Doctrine of IMARCH Chronicle OS

    Principles of Formation, Expert Evaluation and Publication of Knowledge

    📖
    VR-0003 | Publishing Doctrine of IMARCH Chronicle OS
    Official Publishing Doctrine of IMARCH Chronicle OS, establishing the principles of formation, expert evaluation and publication of knowledge as the foundation for academic and expert activity. The document defines the mission of the publishing system — support for the full life cycle of knowledge, including creation, expert peer review, publication and long-term institutional preservation. It enshrines the foundational principles: academic integrity, professional dialogue, institutional memory, reproducibility, respect for authorship and the independence of editorial and expert activity. It introduces a two-circuit architecture of the publishing ecosystem: an academic circuit for scholarly articles, monographs and research, and an expert circuit (including the Verdictum platform) for analytical and applied publications. It establishes the role of expert peer review as an autonomous professional activity aimed at enhancing the quality and methodological rigour of works. It defines the principle of institutional memory through the preservation of all editions of documents, from draft to archival version. It is an open normative instrument, evolving together with the ecosystem, and all its editions are preserved as part of institutional memory.

  • VR-0002

    Founding Institutional Declaration of VERDICTUM

    International Institutional Registry of Documented Facts, Decisions, Obligations and Verified Events

    📑
    VR-0002 | Founding Institutional Declaration of VERDICTUM
    Official founding instrument of VERDICTUM — an independent international institutional registry operating within the Public Knowledge Infrastructure (PKI). The Declaration establishes the institutional, methodological and organisational foundations of the Registry and defines its mission as the formation of a trusted institutional environment for the creation, verification, preservation and use of documented public knowledge. It enshrines four foundational principles: institutional integrity, verifiability, accountability and institutional continuity. It introduces an architecture of institutional interaction grounded in documented actions as the primary unit of processing, together with the full life cycle of the Verdictum Record (VR) — from creation and verification to development, institutional use and preservation. It defines the complementary relationship with Chronicle OS, forming a continuous institutional cycle that links the creation of knowledge to its practical implementation. It constitutes the primary constitutional instrument defining the institutional identity of VERDICTUM as an accumulating public asset rather than a temporary archive.
