I-9

  • RN-0044-PS-0009

    VISUAL STYLE PASSPORT No. 9

    Secret Cosmo-Laboratory

    Neon blue/magenta, holograms, a hybrid of antiquity and cyberpunk. Science fiction, discovery, danger, mystery. A visual language in which sacred antiquity and a high-tech secret laboratory exist as a single system of sovereign knowledge.

    🛡️

  • Artifact I-9.1: Holographic Operator

    This is the first digital artifact of series I-9 — the secret cosmo laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0009-9.1.

    On the concept

    A young operator in a cap works at a desk with a holographic screen on which the profile of a pharaoh emerges in blue-magenta glow. Interface icons at the bottom of the screen point to the functional character of the image: the ancient sacred object appears as a working unit of a secret system. The composition is built on the contrast of the dark background and the neon light.

  • Artifact I-9.2: Cybernetic Pharaoh

    This is the second digital artifact of series I-9 — the secret cosmo laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0009-9.2.

    On the concept

    A cybernetic pharaoh in a golden helmet-mask with luminous blue eyes is placed against a starry field pierced by blue schematic lines. The ancient sacred image and the digital network are joined into a single form: the mask becomes a terminal, the gaze — a communication channel. The composition is frontal and symmetrical, giving the object the character of an icon of the system.

  • Artifact I-9.3: Sacred Capsule

    This is the third digital artifact of series I-9 — the secret cosmo laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0009-9.3.

    On the concept

    In a laboratory, two researchers in white coats examine a pharaoh enclosed in a glass capsule with a cold blue glow. On the left — a digital body scheme, on the right — hieroglyphs on a holographic panel. The composition is built on the principle of laboratory hierarchy: the object at the center, the operators on the sides, the interfaces as witnesses.

  • Artifact I-9.4: Hieroglyphic Contact

    This is the fourth digital artifact of series I-9 — the secret cosmo laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0009-9.4.

    On the concept

    An operator in a cap and tie is turned toward a hologram with the profile of a pharaoh; a cybernetic hand touches the image. The uraeus-cobra, the blue eyes and the golden collar are joined with the hieroglyphs and numerals of the background. The composition is built on the gesture of touch: the contact between the human and the system appears as an act of access.

  • Artifact I-9.5: Two-Sphere Portal

    This is the fifth digital artifact of series I-9 — the secret cosmo laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0009-9.5.

    On the concept

    A round portal reveals two spheres: on the left — a blue starry abyss, on the right — a red-fiery one. At the center — a pharaoh with a staff, whose figure joins both mediums. Before the portal stands an old man with a cane. The composition is built on the principle of axial symmetry: mystery and control, abyss and fire, antiquity and system — in a single circle.

  • Artifact I-9.6: Sovereign Observer

    This is the sixth digital artifact of series I-9 — the secret cosmo laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0009-9.6.

    On the concept

    An operator in a cap and uniform with crossed arms stands before an enormous hologram of a pharaoh inscribed in a blue-magenta neon interface. Data panels, digital schemes and icons form the setting of a secret laboratory. The composition is built on the contrast between the living observer and the digital object: control and observation as functions of the system.
