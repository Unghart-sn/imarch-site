    IMARCH Institutional Academic Wing Protocol

    A Model for Integrating Universities into the International Research Space on the Basis of Chronicle OS

    🎓
    VR-0006-RN-0034 | IMARCH Institutional Academic Wing Protocol
    Official protocol by IMARCH LTD, offering universities, research institutes and scientific centres a model for integration into the international research space through the Institutional Academic Wing based on the fault-tolerant Chronicle OS platform. The document describes a two-tier architecture: a local front-end circuit with full branding, and an international distribution hub publishing structured metadata. It provides a complete infrastructure package: subdomain circuit, official email, metric widgets, Telegram gateway, a hybrid application processing circuit with sovereign document workflow without paid commercial APIs (Know-How Act RN-0032), and cryptographic SHA-256 fixation with registration in VERDICTUM. Includes an international reviewing circuit with double-blind peer review through the Expert Reviewer Network and priority protection under the UK Trade Secrets Regulations 2018. Financial model: annual subscription £1,500–5,000, APC £150. Integration stages: memorandum, circuit creation, publication programme, training, international integration. The document is based on Know-How Acts RN-0019, RN-0028, RN-0032.
