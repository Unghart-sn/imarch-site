    VISUAL STYLE PASSPORT No. 11

    Imperial Neoclassicism

    Monumental portraits in the technique of oil painting. Classical symmetry, deep shadows, noble shades of blue and gold. A visual language in which imperial grandeur and institutional dignity form a single system of sovereign power.

    🛡️

  • Artifact I-11.1: Master at Work

    This is the first digital artifact of series I-11 — imperial neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0011-11.1.

    On the concept

    A man in a blue suit works at a laptop in a dark study. A brass lamp, an inkwell with a quill and leather-bound volumes form a material setting in which the modern tool and the imperial tradition exist as one institutional form. The painterly texture of the background and the deep blue tones sustain the monumental character of the image.

  • Artifact I-11.2: Focused Master

    This is the second digital artifact of series I-11 — imperial neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0011-11.2.

    On the concept

    A woman in a blue suit and a white shirt with a dark tie works at a laptop. A brass lamp, an inkwell and volumes complete the composition, while the painterly wall with a broad brushstroke gives it depth. The image is built on the canon of the imperial portrait: central axis, restraint, professional dignity.

  • Artifact I-11.3: Honoured Academic

    This is the third digital artifact of series I-11 — imperial neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0011-11.3.

    On the concept

    A woman in an academic gown and a cap with a tassel holds a tablet with a digital scheme. A quill in an inkwell, volumes and bookcases form a setting in which the academic tradition and the modern tool are joined in a single gesture. The warm light from the window brings out the figure, emphasizing the official character of the portrait.

  • Artifact I-11.4: Creative Inspiration

    This is the fourth digital artifact of series I-11 — imperial neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0011-11.4.

    On the concept

    A woman with red hair works at a laptop in a bright impressionist setting. The dense color brushstroke and the golden frame turn the scene into a painterly canvas, where creation acts as a form of sovereign dignity. The composition is built on the contrast between the calm figure and the active painterly background.

  • Artifact I-11.5: Rational Constructor

    This is the fifth digital artifact of series I-11 — imperial neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0011-11.5.

    On the concept

    A man in a grey suit and spectacles works at a laptop. Behind him — a geometric Art Deco relief with cubes, pyramids and golden elements, emphasizing the rational basis of the image. The composition is built on the canon of the imperial portrait: figure, symbol and setting are joined in a single system.

  • Artifact I-11.6: Judging Justice

    This is the sixth digital artifact of series I-11 — imperial neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0011-11.6.

    On the concept

    An elderly woman in a judge’s robe sits at a table with an open book. A green lamp, a portrait of a predecessor on the wall, bookcases, a glass of water and spectacles create a setting of institutional authority. The composition is built on the canon of the official portrait: central figure, restraint, material setting.
