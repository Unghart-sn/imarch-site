I-5

  • RN-0044-PS-0005

    VISUAL STYLE PASSPORT No. 5

    Verdictum Style

    Isometric corporate graphics, gloss, symbols of law. Trust, law, system, order. A visual language in which legal precision and institutional aesthetics form a single system of sovereign law.

    🛡️

  • Artifact I-5.1: Contractual Handshake

    This is the first digital artifact of series I-5 — Verdictum style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0005-5.1.

    On the concept

    An isometric composition is built around a marble handshake — blue and white stone with golden veins — symbolizing a contract as a material act. Above it — a golden globe, around it — orbital golden lines connecting an academic cap with a diploma, marble “INS” plaques, a “CERTIFICATION” certificate, a “CCN” medal and a model of an antique temple. Law is presented as a system possessing form, weight and cryptographic credibility.

  • Artifact I-5.2: Legal Portal

    This is the second digital artifact of series I-5 — Verdictum style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0005-5.2.

    On the concept

    A marble triumphal arch with Corinthian columns and golden capitals opens a luminous portal. At the center — a golden circle with scales and a laurel wreath; on the pilasters — shields with scales and engraved tablets. The composition is built on the principle of institutional hierarchy: the symbol of law inscribed in an architectural form becomes an entrance into the sovereign contour.

  • Artifact I-5.3: Judicial Column

    This is the third digital artifact of series I-5 — Verdictum style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0005-5.3.

    On the concept

    A marble Corinthian column with a golden capital is crowned with a globe; on its body — a golden seal with the scales of justice. On the left — a scroll with a golden seal, on the right — a marble shield with scales and a laurel wreath. Orbital golden lines connect the elements into a single system in which law appears as an axial institutional principle.

  • Artifact I-5.4: Sovereign Academy

    This is the fourth digital artifact of series I-5 — Verdictum style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0005-5.4.

    On the concept

    A white-marble composition is built around a pedestal with an academic cap and an open book; above them — a golden globe in orbit. On either side — marble Ionic columns, on the right — a scroll with a seal. The image joins the academic tradition and the institutional order into a single canon of sovereign knowledge.

  • Artifact I-5.5: Lawful Tree

    This is the fifth digital artifact of series I-5 — Verdictum style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0005-5.5.

    On the concept

    A golden tree with a crystal-gold crown is crowned with a globe; at its base — a marble shield with scales. On either side — a scroll with a seal and a marble triumphal arch. Orbital lines connect the layers of the composition. The tree acts as a metaphor of institutional continuity rooted in law.

  • Artifact I-5.6: Eternal Codex

    This is the sixth digital artifact of series I-5 — Verdictum style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0005-5.6.

    On the concept

    At the center — an open book with golden engraving and a hovering quill; above the book — a golden “document” emblem. On the left — a marble inkwell-seal, on the right — a scroll on a stand. Along the edges — marble columns, around — orbital golden lines. The composition presents law as an eternal text fixed in material and form.
