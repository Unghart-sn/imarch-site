  • Artifact I-4.1: Treeborne Scroll

    This is the first digital artifact of series I-4 — academic romanticism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0004-4.1.

    On the concept

    A classical academic still life: a parchment scroll with a sealing-wax seal, a laurel branch, a bronze medallion with a leaf and a painting of a tree in a gilded frame. Brass instruments — an inkwell, scales — sustain the academic layer, while a map and a leather-bound book complete the image of a researcher’s cabinet. The tree as a symbol of continuity links the natural and the institutional principles.

    This is the third digital artifact of series I-4 — academic romanticism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0004-4.3.
