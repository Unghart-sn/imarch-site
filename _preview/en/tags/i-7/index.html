  • RN-0044-PS-0007

    VISUAL STYLE PASSPORT No. 7

    Magazine Beauty Glamour

    Art Deco, liquid gold, satin, perfect female profiles. Luxury, aesthetics, care, serenity. A visual language in which premium cosmetic aesthetics and the sovereign institutional circuit form a single system of magazine glamour.

    🛡️

  • Artifact I-7.1: Aurora Golden

    This is the first digital artifact of series I-7 — magazine beauty glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0007-7.1.

    On the concept

    A woman’s profile is executed in dense golden filigree: the lines of the coiffure, the face and the neck intertwine into a single ornamental stream. Closed eyes and a dark background emphasize the serenity of the image, while the inscriptions “AURORA” and “DARK LUXE” introduce it into a premium magazine context. The composition is built on the contrast between a living golden glow and deep shadow.

  • Artifact I-7.2: Eternal Flask

    This is the second digital artifact of series I-7 — magazine beauty glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0007-7.2.

    On the concept

    A golden filigree hand holds a faceted flask “L’OR ÉTERNEL” with golden liquid. The dark background, particles of golden dust and a diagonal composition give the scene the character of a premium gesture. The material of the hand and the flask form a single material system in which care is presented as an institutional form of dignity.

  • Artifact I-7.3: Pearl Profile

    This is the third digital artifact of series I-7 — magazine beauty glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0007-7.3.

    On the concept

    A woman’s profile in a pearl-pink range with flowers in the coiffure and closed eyes is inscribed in a soft glow. Light curls, luminous bubbles and semi-transparent details form the impression of serenity. The composition continues the tradition of the magazine portrait translated into the language of Art Deco.

  • Artifact I-7.4: Crystal Lotus

    This is the fourth digital artifact of series I-7 — magazine beauty glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0007-7.4.

    On the concept

    Two golden hands with diamond incrustation carefully hold a crystal lotus. Satin draperies and Art Deco ray panels form the background setting, while warm light emphasizes the premium materiality. The composition is built on symmetry and the gesture of careful holding, turning care into an institutional act.

  • Artifact I-7.5: Celestial Scent

    This is the fifth digital artifact of series I-7 — magazine beauty glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0007-7.5.

    On the concept

    A woman’s profile in golden filigree hovers above a flask “L’OR ÉTERNEL”; around — pearl spheres, roses and silk draperies. The composition joins the portrait tradition of Art Deco and a premium object, creating the image of a scent as a celestial substance. Warm radiance and soft light transitions sustain the magazine aesthetic.

  • Artifact I-7.6: Ray Relief

    This is the sixth digital artifact of series I-7 — magazine beauty glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0007-7.6.

    On the concept

    A woman’s profile is executed in the technique of repoussé gold against the background of Art Deco ray panels. Laurel branches, crystal pendants and stylized flowers frame the figure, while the terracotta-gold palette enhances the relief materiality. The composition is built according to the canon of Art Deco: central axis, symmetry, light flux.
