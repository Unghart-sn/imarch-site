I-3

  • RN-0044-PS-0003

    VISUAL STYLE PASSPORT No. 3

    Veterans’ Post-War Realism (Memory / Hope / Continuity)

    An evocation of oil painting, 1940s poster graphics, sepia, Socialist Realism. Tragedy, hope, respect, patriotism. A visual language in which the memory of destruction and the will to rebuild exist as a single act of witness.

    🛡️

  • Artifact I-3.1: Family Preserving

    This is the first digital artifact of series I-3 — veteran post-war realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0003-3.1.

    On the concept

    A family — a father in military uniform, a mother and a child — stands among ruins. Above them hover symbolic signs: family, network, sprout, globe, shield, analytics — emblems of hope and restoration. The poster-like clarity of the composition is joined with the documentary credibility of the setting. The light is even, the dignity of the figures is emphasized rather than their suffering.

  • Artifact I-3.2: Faithful Embrace

    This is the second digital artifact of series I-3 — veteran post-war realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0003-3.2.

    On the concept

    A father in civilian clothing embraces his wife and child among ruins; above them hover symbolic signs — network, shield, globe, sprout. Sepia and a muted palette emphasize the memorial character of the scene, while the poster-like clarity of the composition holds attention on the central group.

  • Artifact I-3.3: Warm Testimony

    This is the third digital artifact of series I-3 — veteran post-war realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0003-3.3.

    On the concept

    A family at the center of an ochre scene; on either side — witnesses (a man and a woman with a tablet). Above the group — symbols of family, cycle, globe and a profile of analytics. The poster-like composition joins personal memory and institutional testimony in a single act, where loss becomes the foundation for the future.

  • Artifact I-3.4: Memory Carried

    This is the fourth digital artifact of series I-3 — veteran post-war realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0003-3.4.

    On the concept

    A veteran in a helmet (a silhouette-shadow) and a veteran in civilian clothes holding his head form a double portrait. A wife and a daughter with a teddy bear complete the family group. Symbols of home and infinity remind of continuity, while warm light brings out the faces, preserving the dignity of the figures.

  • Artifact I-3.5: Luminous Heart

    This is the fifth digital artifact of series I-3 — veteran post-war realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0003-3.5.

    On the concept

    A girl with long hair holds a luminous crystal heart; around her — symbols of family, cycle, globe and growth, while candles burn in the darkness. The composition is built on the contrast between the warm glow of the heart and the deep shadow, turning a child’s gesture into an act of continuity and hope.

  • Artifact I-3.6: Quiet Continuity

    This is the sixth digital artifact of series I-3 — veteran post-war realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0003-3.6.

    On the concept

    A family at the center — a father, a mother, two daughters; around them — semi-transparent figures of generations (an old man in a cap, a man in profile, a man with a folder, a woman with a tablet). Symbols of family, cycle, globe and analytics form the layer of institutional memory. An ochre palette and soft light hold the dignity and the silence of the scene.
