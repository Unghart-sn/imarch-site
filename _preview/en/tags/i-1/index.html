I-1

  • Artifact I-1.1: Isis the Lapis

    This is the first digital artifact of series I-1 — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier rn-0044-ps-pch-0001-1.1.

    On the concept

    The artwork is built on the contrast between the sacred architecture of Ancient Egypt and the cybernetic layer. Lapis lazuli, gold, biomechanics — three materials of one civilisational system in which the past has become the architectural foundation of the future.

  • Artifact I-1.2: Horus the Blueflame

    This is the second digital artifact of series I-1 — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier rn-0044-ps-pch-0001-1.2.

    On the concept

    A profile portrait of a cybernetic pharaoh — a bearer of code, not of a crown. Gold and biomechanics converge in a single figure: facial plate, hinged joints, integrated optical nodes. The staff with a lapis core is not a regalia but an interface. The background hieroglyphs are written as glowing code — knowledge that has become a protocol.

  • Artifact I-1.3: The Aten Council

    This is the third digital artifact of series I-1 — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier rn-0044-ps-pch-0001-1.3.

    On the concept

    A symmetrical scene of a collegium: the central pharaoh with the halo-disk of Aten, and four cybernetic satellites on either side. One of them passes fire — a gesture that reads simultaneously as ritual and as technology transfer.

    Here I-1 works not through a single figure but through the architecture of the scene: hierarchy, modularity, repetition. The composition is built as a structural scheme, not as a portrait. This is why the artifact is the key to understanding the whole series: civilisation as a system of modules, each holding its own function.

  • Artifact I-1.4: Thoth the Scribe

    This is the fourth digital artifact of series I-1 — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier rn-0044-ps-pch-0001-1.4.

    On the concept

    A scene of registration: the pharaoh on the throne, Thoth with the ibis head keeping a record on a tablet, the goddess with the feather of Maat standing nearby as a witness. This is a direct visual parallel to the theme of provenance — the act of fixation, the act of recording, the act of attestation.

  • Artifact I-1.5: Osiris the Winged

    This is the fifth digital artifact of series I-1 — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier rn-0044-ps-pch-0001-1.5.

    On the concept

    A frontal portrait of a winged pharaoh, hands folded on the chest — a gesture of preservation, not of power. The wings are symmetrical, executed as an engineering construction: the feathers read as blades, the metal as a frame. Around the head are orbital circles and service marks. On the sides are gears and technical nodes inscribed in the architectural field.

  • Artifact I-1.6: Thoth the Amethyst

    This is the sixth digital artifact of series I-1 — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier rn-0044-ps-pch-0001-1.6.

    On the concept

    A paired composition to artifact I-1.4: the same scene of registration — the pharaoh on the throne, Thoth with the tablet, the goddess with the feather of Maat — but in an amethyst-violet palette. Binary sequences 01101010 10001011 in the field, gold on violet, a nocturnal atmosphere instead of a diurnal one.
