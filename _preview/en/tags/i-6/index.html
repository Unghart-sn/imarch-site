I-6

  • RN-0044-PS-0006

    VISUAL STYLE PASSPORT No. 6

    Formal Ceremonial Memorial Style

    Engraving, bronze, pencil drawing, laurel wreaths. Significance, respect, memory, honour. A visual language in which institutional memory and sovereign dignity exist as a single memorial tradition.

    🛡️

  • Artifact I-6.1: Engraved Portrait

    This is the first digital artifact of series I-6 — the official ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0006-6.1.

    On the concept

    A fine hatched engraving presents a male portrait in a three-piece suit, inscribed in a round medallion. A dense floral ornament executed in banknote technique frames the figure and gives the image the character of an official testimony. The composition is built on strict symmetry and frontal placement — the canon of the ceremonial portrait translated into the language of engraving.

  • Artifact I-6.2: Memorial Bust

    This is the second digital artifact of series I-6 — the official ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0006-6.2.

    On the concept

    A bronze relief bust of a moustached man is placed on a patinated plaque; above it — the inscription “IN MEMORIAM”, below it — a plaque with the name and dates “ARTHUR V. HEMLOCK · 1878–1952”. Warm directed light brings out the face, while the deep shadow of the background gives the composition a memorial restraint.

  • Artifact I-6.3: Commemorative Relief

    This is the third digital artifact of series I-6 — the official ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0006-6.3.

    On the concept

    A bronze relief portrait of a man occupies the left part of the composition; on the right — engraved inscriptions “IN MEMORIAM” and “MAGNUS DOLOREM ET DESIDERIUM”. In the corners — laurel branches in an ornamental frame. The composition is built according to the canon of a memorial plaque: the name, the image and the formula of memory are joined in a single institutional act.

  • Artifact I-6.4: Bronze Portrait

    This is the fourth digital artifact of series I-6 — the official ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0006-6.4.

    On the concept

    A bronze portrait of a bearded man in spectacles is inscribed in a strict rectangular frame with floral corners. A plaque “JOHN SMITH” in the bottom right corner designates the name. Warm bronze light and a soft shadow create the effect of the physical presence of the material rather than of the image.

  • Artifact I-6.5: Feminine Medallion

    This is the fifth digital artifact of series I-6 — the official ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0006-6.5.

    On the concept

    An engraved female portrait in a high collar is inscribed in a round medallion. Around it — a complex banknote mesh and floral rosettes executed in the technique of fine hatched engraving. The composition continues the tradition of the official portrait on banknotes and charters, giving the image dignity and timeless clarity.

  • Artifact I-6.6: Imperial Mosaic

    This is the sixth digital artifact of series I-6 — the official ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier RN-0044-PS-0006-6.6.

    On the concept

    A portrait of a man in imperial attire with a fibula is placed against the background of a Byzantine mosaic with a laurel halo. Craquelure, losses and traces of time emphasize the material antiquity of the image. The composition is built according to the canon of the memorial portrait: the face, the symbol and the setting are joined in a single institutional tradition.
