CONSOLIDATED SYSTEM PASSPORT

Series I

1. System identification

Code: RN-0044-PS-0000 Series: I Name: The IMARCH system of visual standards Type: institutional system document Base structure: the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix

Composition of the system:

12 visual styles of Series I (I-1 … I-12), each set out in a separate multi-passport of the format RN-0044-PS-00NN.

The open facet of the system:

The numerical index of the classical Chinese meridian system (‘Zi Wu Liu Zhu’, 子午流注) — the circadian cycle of 12 meridians.

The closed facets of the system:

The remaining five facets of the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix are treated in the concept of ‘Universal Formology’ and constitute know-how accessible under NDA.

2. Conceptual core

The core principle of the system:

The 12 visual styles of Series I form not a list of directions, but a closed structural system, in which each style occupies a fixed position within the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix.

The system is built on three levels:

The first level — the numerical index. The 12 styles are mapped to the 12 classical Chinese meridians in the circadian sequence ‘Zi Wu Liu Zhu’. This gives the system a clear sequence, rather than an arbitrary set.

The second level — the structural models. Three equally weighted models (the colour wheel, the Five Phases, the clock face) describe one and the same cycle from different angles. They do not contradict one another; they complement one another.

The third level — the cubic matrix. The Chinese matrix of meridians is one facet of the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix. The remaining five facets are treated in the concept of ‘Universal Formology’ (NDA). The cube as a whole is not a square, but a six-faceted structure.

The principal criterion of truth for the system: Not the antiquity of the form, but the result of the practical application of the proposition within the methodical onboarding of the practical application of knowledge.

3. The three reference models

3.1. The colour wheel

The 12 styles are arranged in a circle:

  • Adjacent styles — complementary in aesthetic, with a smooth transition.
  • Styles six apart — opposite, contrasting in aesthetic vector.
  • Styles three apart — neighbouring sectors, moderate contrast.
  • Styles four apart — a third-order axis, functional opposition.

The colour wheel describes the aesthetic compatibility of the styles: which styles may be combined in a single project without visual conflict.

3.2. The numerical index of the meridians

The 12 styles are mapped to the 12 meridians in the circadian sequence ‘Zi Wu Liu Zhu’:

  • The sequence I-1 → I-12 is the circadian flow, a smooth passage through the ‘hours’.
  • Styles six apart are opposite (寅 ↔ 申, 卯 ↔ 酉, 辰 ↔ 戌, 巳 ↔ 亥, 午 ↔ 子, 未 ↔ 丑).
  • Solid (zang, 脏): I-1, I-4, I-5, I-8, I-9, I-12.
  • Hollow (fu, 腑): I-2, I-3, I-6, I-7, I-10, I-11.
  • The four fires (a structural feature of the Chinese facet): I-5 (心), I-6 (小肠), I-9 (心包), I-10 (三焦).

A methodological note on the four fires:

Authentic tradition is a matter of the specificity of the methodology as applied to the specificity of psycho-cultural endemicity. The system does not deny tradition, but asserts: authenticity and endemicity belong rather to the domain of anthropology than to that of mathematics and medicine, which are the system’s priorities. The system is not a local-history museum but a full-cycle production process with its own list of practical tasks. The principal criterion of truth is the result of the practical application of the proposition within the methodical onboarding of the practical application of knowledge.

3.3. The clock face

The 12 styles as the 12 hours:

  • Adjacent — smooth transition.
  • Six apart — opposition.
  • Three apart — a neighbouring sector.
  • Four apart — a third-order axis.

The clock face duplicates the logic of the colour wheel and of the numerical index, providing a third system of navigation among the styles.

4. The IRSHOM Cubic Matrix

The IRSHOM Cubic Matrix is a conceptual structure in which the 12 styles of Series I are considered as one of six facets.

The open facet — the Chinese matrix of 12 meridians (the circadian cycle ‘Zi Wu Liu Zhu’). It is disclosed in the present document.

The five closed facets — treated in the concept of ‘Universal Formology’, they constitute know-how accessible under NDA.

The cube as a whole — the structural basis of the system, irreducible to any one of its facets.

A practical consequence: the system does not claim the absolute completeness of a single facet. The Chinese matrix is beautiful and self-sufficient, but structurally it is a flat matrix of the magic square, whereas the system operates at the level of a cubic structure. The remaining five facets complement the open one, allowing the system not to be confined within a truncated Chinese variant, but to preserve its completeness.

6. Structural regularities

6.1. The four fires

The Chinese facet yields four fires rather than one:

  • I-5 (心, Heart) — the ‘inner’ fire;
  • I-6 (小肠, Small Intestine) — the ‘inner’ fire, hollow;
  • I-9 (心包, Pericardium) — the ‘outer’ fire, the envelope;
  • I-10 (三焦, Triple Burner) — the ‘outer’ fire, the skin.

This is a structural feature, not an error. It confirms that a flat matrix is not equivalent to the cube.

6.2. Paired styles (six apart)

Six pairs of opposite styles:

Pair Styles Opposition
1 I-1 ↔ I-7 Ancient Egyptian cyberpunk ↔ magazine glamour
2 I-2 ↔ I-8 antiquarian study ↔ Classical Renaissance
3 I-3 ↔ I-9 veterans' realism ↔ cosmo-laboratory
4 I-4 ↔ I-10 Academic Romanticism ↔ Corporate Portrait
5 I-5 ↔ I-11 Verdictum Style ↔ Imperial Neoclassicism
6 I-6 ↔ I-12 memorial style ↔ Digital Minimalism

6.3. Triads (three apart)

Four triads of neighbouring sectors:

  • I-1, I-4, I-7, I-10 — the ‘material/structural’ axis;
  • I-2, I-5, I-8, I-11 — the ‘institutional/sovereign’ axis;
  • I-3, I-6, I-9, I-12 — the ‘memory/technology’ axis.

6.4. Axes (four apart)

Three third-order axes:

  • I-1 — I-5 — I-9 — the ‘ancient → institutional → technological’ axis;
  • I-2 — I-6 — I-10 — the ’elite → memorial → corporate’ axis;
  • I-3 — I-7 — I-11 — the ‘memory → aesthetics → grandeur’ axis;
  • I-4 — I-8 — I-12 — the ‘academic → sacred → digital’ axis.

6.5. Solid and hollow (6 + 6)

  • Solid (zang): I-1, I-4, I-5, I-8, I-9, I-12.
  • Hollow (fu): I-2, I-3, I-6, I-7, I-10, I-11.

The solid styles are ‘repositories’ (documents, memory, sovereignty, eternity). The hollow styles are ’transit routes’ (dynamics, exchange, aesthetics, communication).

8. Rules for selecting a style

The choice of a style for a task is determined by three criteria:

8.1. The functional criterion

The task determines the functional class:

Class of task Priority styles
Institutional document I-2, I-5, I-6, I-10, I-11
Publishing system I-4, I-8, I-11
Visual identity I-2, I-5, I-7, I-10, I-11
Art environment I-1, I-3, I-4, I-8, I-9
Therapeutic work selected according to an individual profile

8.2. The structural criterion

The style must not contradict the structural position of the task within the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix:

  • Solid styles (I-1, I-4, I-5, I-8, I-9, I-12) — for tasks requiring stability, preservation, sovereignty.
  • Hollow styles (I-2, I-3, I-6, I-7, I-10, I-11) — for tasks requiring dynamics, exchange, communication.

8.3. The aesthetic criterion

The style must correspond to the aesthetic vector of the task:

  • if the task requires monumentality — I-1, I-6, I-11;
  • if intimacy — I-2, I-4, I-10;
  • if technological quality — I-5, I-9, I-12;
  • if memory — I-3, I-6, I-8;
  • if premium quality — I-2, I-7, I-11.

8.4. The rule of combination

Within a single project, the combination of adjacent styles is permitted (by the colour wheel, the numerical index or the clock face). The combination of opposite styles (six apart) is permitted only as a conscious contrast with an explicit compositional justification.

9. Rules for extending the system

The system of 12 styles is not closed forever. The rules for extension are:

9.1. Extension within the open facet

If an additional level is identified within the Chinese matrix of 12 meridians (for example, 12 meridians × 2 = 24), the system may be extended to 24 styles without disrupting its structure. Each new style will occupy a fixed position within the extended facet.

9.2. Extension through new facets

The disclosure of a new facet of the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix (through Universal Formology) yields a new set of styles — not replacing, but complementing the existing one. Such a set is presented as a separate series (for example, Series II, Series III) with its own consolidated passport.

9.3. The condition of extension

Any extension of the system is permitted only on the following conditions:

  • the new style has a fixed position within the cubic matrix;
  • the new style does not contradict the existing structural regularities;
  • the new style corresponds to the criteria of section 8;
  • the new style is entered into the VERDICTUM registry with a full multi-passport.

9.4. Prohibition of arbitrary extension

The arbitrary addition of styles without structural justification is prohibited. The system is not a collection of aesthetics, but a closed structural matrix. Extension is permitted only under rules 9.1–9.3.

10. The methodological position on tradition

The system of 12 visual styles is built with an awareness of the base and in accordance with the regularity of the circadian cycle of the classical Chinese meridians.

The position of the system:

  1. The system is not a formal logic derived from an arbitrary number. It is created with an awareness of the base.
  2. The system is not a literal transposition of the Chinese tradition. Authenticity and endemicity belong rather to the domain of anthropology than to that of mathematics and medicine, which are the system’s priorities.
  3. The system is not a local-history museum but a full-cycle production process with its own list of practical tasks.
  4. The principal criterion of truth is not the antiquity of the form, but the result of the practical application of the proposition within the methodical onboarding of the practical application of knowledge.
  5. Secondary analysis (colour coding, structural-elemental decomposition, a finite number of elements, the fixed specificity of their systemic organisation) may be carried out as a separate analytical work — but this is already a matter of operational nuance for the technical tuning of attention parameters during conscious visual scanning of an image.

A practical consequence: the system operates not at the level of a single facet, but at the level of the cubic matrix as a whole, which allows it to preserve its completeness and not lose itself in a truncated variant, however beautiful the Chinese model may be.

11. The therapeutic direction

Work with imagery within the system of 12 visual styles is a separate methodological direction of therapeutic correction of the general state and is applied in the methodology for training specialists in the field of visual art.

11.1. Resonant visual activation

The contemplation of images in a particular style resonates with a particular state of the person. The system uses this as a methodological principle:

  • each style corresponds to a particular mode of attention;
  • the sequence of styles (by the circadian cycle) corresponds to the natural change of modes over the course of the day;
  • the combination of styles allows the fine psychic tuning of attention to be regulated.

11.2. Regulation of the fine psychic tuning of attention

The system is applied to the regulation of the fine psychic tuning of attention in a therapeutic key:

  • conscious visual scanning of an image;
  • sequential work with several styles in a particular order;
  • individual selection of styles to fit the state profile.

11.3. Connection with the School of Nine Arts

The therapeutic direction is developed within the methodological concept of the School of Nine Arts.

The School of Nine Arts is a separate work. The direction is protected by know-how act RN-0046. Access is by NDA memorandum.

In the present document, the content of the School of Nine Arts is not disclosed.

11.4. Status of the therapeutic direction

The therapeutic direction is recorded as:

  • a methodological hypothesis within the concept of the School of Nine Arts;
  • an applied principle, already used in the methodology for training specialists in the field of visual art;
  • both statuses simultaneously — first hypothesis, then applied principle.

12. Connection with the VERDICTUM registry

The transfer of rights for all artefacts of the Series I system of visual standards takes place within the umbrella category of Collector & Commercial Rights, as recorded in the archive manifesto RN-0044-GD-0001 (section 8, subsection 1). This category is the common legal field of the series: it describes the full scope of rights that may be transferred at all, while each specific model defines the scope of rights the purchaser receives. The specific model — Collector License, Commercial & Publication License or Exclusive Transfer — is selected by the purchaser and recorded in the lot passport and the public registry.

The system of visual standards is integrated into the common institutional circuit of IMARCH / Chronicle OS / Verdictum:

  1. Each style is set out in a separate multi-passport of the format RN-0044-PS-00NN.
  2. Each artefact is recorded in the sovereign registry of IMARCH LTD with a marker and a SHA-256.
  3. Authentication is carried out via the VERDICTUM Hash Calculator.
  4. The legal regime — UK Trade Secrets Regulations 2018 (SI 2018/597).

The consolidated system passport is a top-level navigational document: it binds the 12 multi-passports into a single system and defines the rules for working with it.
