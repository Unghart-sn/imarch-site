GLOSSARY OF THE RN-0044 CORPUS

1. Umbrella rights category

Collector & Commercial Rights is the umbrella rights category within which any transfer of ownership rights over the artefacts of the series takes place. The category is recorded in the archive manifesto RN-0044-GD-0001 (section 8.1) and constitutes the common legal field for all lots of the Genesis Vault complex.

The category is not a separate licence. It describes the full scope of rights that may be transferred at all within the series. The specific scope of rights obtained by the buyer is determined by one of the three licensing models described below.

2. Three licensing models

2.1. Collector License

In Russian: Лицензия персонального коллекционирования. Purpose: private collections, personal digital galleries, personal display panels. Scope of rights: private exhibition on personal devices; perpetual ownership of the provenance record in the public registry; resale or transfer of the copy with mandatory re-registration of the owner. Restrictions: commercial reproduction, use in advertising campaigns and resale of digital copies to third parties are prohibited.

2.2. Commercial & Publication License

In Russian: Коммерческо-публикационная лицензия. Purpose: media projects, corporate collections, design of architectural spaces, publications, branding. Scope of rights: commercial exhibition; integration of the artefact into composite media products; public reproduction in print and digital publications with mandatory attribution of the Node ID. Restrictions: proprietary authorial rights (IP Core) remain with the Chronicle OS ecosystem; assignment of sublicensing rights requires separate approval.

2.3. Exclusive Transfer

In Russian: Полная передача исключительных прав. Purpose: outright purchase of the artefact with the transfer of exclusive intellectual rights. Scope of rights: full transfer of commercial and proprietary rights over the object to the buyer; closure of the edition buyout (Edition Closed / Exclusive Sold Out) in the public registry; transfer of the encrypted archive of source files and all accompanying working materials from the Vault. Restrictions: determined by a separate act of transfer.

3. Adjacent legal and marketing terms

Term Definition
Full Series Buyout A marketing option for the outright purchase of a series (all lots of a style). Recorded in the manifesto RN-0044-GD-0001, section 8.3. It is not a separate licensing model; it is implemented through Exclusive Transfer for each lot of the series.
IMARCH-LTD-METADATA-RESTRICTED A service licence for the metadata of passports and registry records. It does not belong to the Collector & Commercial Rights category and does not concern rights over the artefact.
Edition Index The sequential number of a copy within the limited edition (1–10 for Series I).
Node ID The unique identifier of a digital artefact in the IMARCH LTD registry. Format: RN-0044-PS-000N-M.K.
Marker A service record marker. Format: (8888) • HTTPS • IMARCH.LTD • MKT-YYMMDD-HHMMSS.
Vault The closed depository of master files. Access is granted after verification of rights.

5. Procedure of application

  1. Any transfer of rights within the series belongs to the category Collector & Commercial Rights.
  2. The specific licensing model is selected by the buyer when submitting the request.
  3. The selected model is recorded in the header of the passport of the corresponding lot and in the public VERDICTUM registry.
  4. A change of model after registration of the transfer of rights requires a separate re-registration procedure.
