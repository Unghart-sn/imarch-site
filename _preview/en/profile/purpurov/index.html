GENERAL INFORMATION

Oleksandr Purpurov is the founder and Managing Director of IMARCH LTD UK, a research and education company that turns bureaucracy and infrastructure into a living chronicle.

About the company🔻

IMARCH LTD is a registered company in the United Kingdom, focused on trust architecture, legal clarity, and institutional packaging of donor driven solutions. We design infrastructure that puts everything in its place, from documents to visual systems, from contracts to collective rituals.

Company number: 16791765
Legal form: Private Limited Company (LTD)
Mission: to turn technical and legal complexity into clear, reproducible solutions for communities and donors.

The official methodology and legal framework of the IMARCH | Chronicle OS project fix the architecture of the functional-cognitive contour of preventive renovation.

Ecosystem deployment and data verification are carried out by the international platform Chronicle OS Journal based on the concept of multidimensional institutional sustainability (DSA) and open peer review.

📄 Open methodology and White Paper (PDF / Markdown) 🤝 Support the humanitarian research track on WhyDonate

Practical approbation of the model takes place within the non-profit humanitarian track Vis Realitatis (comprehensive drug-free rehabilitation of veterans) with recording of results in the cryptographic Archivum archive.

Reports, decision archive and hashed preprints are published in Archivum as the project develops.
