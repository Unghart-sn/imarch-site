PILOT MODULE

The Vis Realitatis V.01™ Pilot is the first practical implementation stage of the renovation and social adaptation system Vis Realitatis, based on the provisions of the newly officially recognised discipline Universal Formology.

The Vis Realitatis V.01™ Pilot is the launch of the renovation system in an applied format. Here methodology, digital recording and scientometrics are combined into a single working environment. Every action is recorded in the Chronicle and becomes part of the emerging institutional structure.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

The pilot is based on three key components: CHRONICAL OS 8888™, SUNLIFE-MASSAGE® and UNIVERSAL FORMOLOGY™.

They are integrated into the VIS REALITATIS™ system and implemented under the corporate flag of IMARCH LTD UK.

The pilot is not a testing phase, but the initial deployment stage of the system. Participation, access and recording are combined into a sustainable institutional model.

GENERAL INFORMATION 🔻

Chronicle OS 8888

The Vis Realitatis V.01™ Pilot is built on three practices:

  • institutional design
  • educational and humanitarian projects
  • digital environment for recording and coordinating participant actions

The pilot forms the first layer of methodological modules and launches a focus group for scientometric research into the effectiveness of the approach.
This is a transition from fragmented experience to a reproducible system.
The pilot is a starting environment for entering the system.

Participation formats vary from observation to active contribution.
Every action is recorded in the Chronicle and becomes part of the emerging institutional structure.

🔹 The Essence of the Pilot

The pilot is an entry space into the system where a participant gains access to the emerging modules, can engage in the process, and record their participation in Chronicle OS 8888™.
It combines participation, access and recording into a single working logic.

🔹 Uniqueness

The pilot is neither a product nor a course.
It is a stage in the formation of the Vis Realitatis™ system, where the participant gains access not to a showcase but to the process.

Key features:

  • Recording of actions — every action is preserved in Chronicle OS 8888™ and becomes part of institutional memory
  • Module integration — Sunlife-Massage®, Universal Formology™ and Chronicle OS 8888™ are combined into a single environment
  • Unified logic — participation, access and recording work as a single process
  • Institutional layer — the pilot lays the foundation for the Renovation and Adaptation Centre, including work with veterans

The pilot acts as a practical laboratory of a new institutional form.

🔹 Target Audience

  • participants seeking a structured entry into systems and projects
  • practitioners and specialists for whom recording results is important
  • teams at the formative stage
  • participants ready to engage in the development of the system

🔹 Practical Tasks

  • formation of the basic methodological modules of the Vis Realitatis™ system
  • conducting scientometric research into the effectiveness of the modules
  • recording participant actions for analysis and process transparency
  • structuring participation forms and roles
  • forming the project chronicle as a basis for development

🔹 Potential

  • creation of methodological products
  • accumulation and systematisation of research results
  • formation of a core of participants and partners
  • development of a reproducible entry model
  • preparation for scaling
  • integration with educational and social initiatives

🔹 Legal Framework

The pilot is implemented within the infrastructure of IMARCH LTD UK as part of the Chronicle OS 8888™ system.
All processes are treated as elements of an evolving institutional environment.
