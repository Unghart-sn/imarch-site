❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🐙
Why GitHub?
Because we don’t need a pretty “trust dashboard” – we need an environment that leaves traces. GitHub records publication history, file changes, dates, comments, and archive structure. It is not a “proof of goodwill”, but a technical environment for verifying the sequence of actions.
recording environment
#️⃣
Does a hash guarantee truth?
A hash by itself does not replace conscience or audit. It solves a different problem: it fixes a specific record in an immutable form. If the record is altered, the hash no longer matches. We do not ask you to trust words – we give a technical marker of data integrity.
integrity control
🏛️
How is this different from an ordinary NGO?
Ordinary NGOs usually raise funds under a general cause and publish reports after the fact. Here, your contribution is immediately embedded into the project architecture: recorded as an event, linked to a stage, artefacts, and outcomes. You are not supporting a foundation as a form, but a protocol as a working system.
model difference
🧾
Where exactly does the money go?
Into the first launch circle: physical centre, Sunlife-Massage® pilot, expert and methodological assembly, recording and reporting system. There is no “raise first, figure out later”. Every budget item exists within a concrete stage.
stage budget
🧪
What if it is just beautiful packaging?
Then the project will collapse at the very first verification cycle. That is exactly why we publish not only theses, but also the traces of work: stages, documents, roles, case studies, reports, and external verification points. Packaging cannot withstand a long‑term chronicle; a working system can.
stress test
🌍
What will be the outcome beyond “help here and now”?
The outcome is not only people supported, but also a formalised reproduction protocol – so that the centre, the method, and the reporting system can be deployed in another country, city, or community without relying on the accidental heroism of individuals.
scalable outcome
