📋 REQUIREMENTS FOR SUBMITTED MATERIALS (INSTRUCTION FOR AUTHORS)

All submitted materials undergo automated initial validation, SHA-256 cryptographic fixation, and independent anonymous peer review (Double-Blind Peer Review).

1. File Package (Dual-Circuit Security System)

To ensure blind review and copyright protection, the author uploads two files (in .docx, .doc, or .pdf format):

  • Master Copy: The full version of the article. Contains author metadata (full name, academic degree, university/organisation, ORCID, e-mail), abstract, main text, and references.
  • Blind Copy: An identical version of the article with all author names, affiliations, contacts, and grant/acknowledgment references that could reveal the researcher's identity completely removed. This file is forwarded to the Editorial Board's expert reviewers.
2. Length and Academic Structure

Recommended length: 10,000 to 35,000 characters including spaces (5 to 15 A4 pages).

Required manuscript elements:

  1. Title: in the original language and in English.
  2. Abstract: 150–250 words. Should briefly outline the problem, method, results, and scientific novelty.
  3. Keywords: 5–8 terms.
  4. Main text (IMRAD):
    • Introduction: problem statement, relevance, and research aim.
    • Methods: description of approaches and sources used.
    • Results & Discussion: key findings, formulas, figures, tables.
    • Conclusion: final conclusion and application prospects.
  5. References: formatted according to APA 7th or GOST standard. DOI of cited sources must be provided where available.
3. Language Standards and International Status

The official working language of the IMARCH Chronicle OS International Journal is English.

Submission options:

  • Fully in English (Recommended): Main text, abstract, metadata, and bibliography are submitted in English for maximum international citation impact.
  • Bilingual Mode (for national and university nodes): Publication of the main text in the authors' jurisdictional language (Ukrainian or other European languages) is permitted provided that complete English metadata (title, extended abstract of 200–250 words, keywords, affiliation, and references) are included.

Translation quality responsibility: Authors are personally responsible for the linguistic and stylistic accuracy of the submitted materials (both in English and in the jurisdictional language). The editorial office does not provide free translation or proofreading services.
