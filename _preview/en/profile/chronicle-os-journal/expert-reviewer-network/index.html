🏛️ Institutional Manifesto: Peer Review as Capital

The academic world faces a peer review crisis: experts are expected to provide “free labour” for years to benefit giant publishing corporations, spending weeks proofreading incorrectly formatted materials. IMARCH Chronicle OS breaks this paradigm.

According to Institutional Protocol RN-0031, we view expert peer review as a measurable professional contribution requiring direct monetisation, institutional recognition, and legal protection. We guarantee that every hour of an expert’s work is transformed into personal capital — academic, financial, or status-based.

How does the expert’s time economy work?

  1. Primary screening on the platform side: Manuscripts undergo automatic plagiarism control, structure verification, and SHA-256 cryptographic hash calculation.
  2. Purified “Blind Vector”: The reviewer receives a structured text without author details (Double-Blind Peer Review).
  3. 15-Minute Standard: Thanks to complete technical pre-processing of the manuscript, the expert’s net time for completing the standardised form is up to 15 minutes.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Who can become a reviewer for IMARCH Chronicle OS?

Our expert network invites scientists, doctors and candidates of sciences, professors, and independent researchers with a confirmed scientific background (presence of a profile in ORCID, Google Scholar, ResearchGate, or Scopus). Priority is given to specialists in the fields of preventive medicine, rehabilitation, gerontology, systems analysis, and biomedical technologies within the VIS REALITATIS™ framework.

How are review remuneration justified and paid?

All payments are made officially by the international company IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom) on the basis of a Consulting Services Agreement. Payments are accumulated and transferred monthly to your details (cards, IBAN, PayPal, Wise, or USDT). At your choice, fiat may be replaced with publication credits or Series 8888 tokens.

What are the procedures and timelines for "blind" peer review?

Peer review follows the Double-Blind Peer Review principle (the names of authors and reviewers are mutually concealed). The expert takes a manuscript from the closed pool. The standard timeframe is up to 14 days. For hot express sprints, an accelerated assessment mode of up to 2 hours is available with a ×3 XP multiplier.
