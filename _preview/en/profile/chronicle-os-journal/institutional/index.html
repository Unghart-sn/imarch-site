How it works: architectural logistics

🌐 Local Circuit (Frontend)
Your university receives a dedicated subdomain (e.g. `university.imarch.ltd`) with full branding. The official symbols, departmental registries and full texts of publications are hosted here. You retain full control over your internal agenda.
📡 International Distribution Hub
Digital teasers are published on the central IMARCH platform (London, United Kingdom): abstracts, keywords, conclusions, verified ORCID profiles. This is the entry point for foreign scholars and search engines.
🔗 Global Traffic
Foreign researchers find the material on the IMARCH hub and transition seamlessly to your subdomain to read the full version. You receive a direct channel of targeted international traffic.

Cryptographic Core of the System: The Architecture of Trust

Any distributed system or publishing platform looks attractive on paper exactly until the question of security, human factor and protection against forgery arises. For our platform to operate as an autonomous, incorruptible Swiss mechanism rather than as a vulnerable database, an autonomous cryptographic core is embedded at its foundation.

This is not merely a set of scripts, but a full-fledged software and hardware circuit solving three fundamental tasks:

  1. Mathematical verification instead of a “word of honour” (Ed25519 & JCS) Every action of a participant — from registration in the system to the final verdict on a document — is signed with a personal cryptographic key. Thanks to strict data canonicalisation, any discrepancies or attempts to dispute authorship are excluded: if a decision is signed, it cannot be revoked or forged technically.
  2. Immutable digital archive (Append-only Journal) All key events of the system are recorded in a protected journal on the principle of a continuous hash chain. Each new block is rigidly linked to the previous one. This excludes the possibility of silently deleting a record, rewriting history retroactively or manipulating review results.
  3. Autonomous control and protection circuit (Watchers & Anti-Replay) The system does not require constant manual oversight. Built-in protection mechanisms cut off repeated network attacks (replay attacks) using timestamps and unique markers, while background watchdog processes automatically manage deadlines and task statuses, excluding any downtime due to human factor.

Why this matters: Without this technological foundation, the entire system turns into an ordinary chat or a vulnerable website where data can be edited and decisions can be disputed. The cryptographic core moves the project from the category of “an idea on paper” into the status of a sovereign, protected and self-sufficient digital infrastructure that can be trusted at the level of pure mathematics.
