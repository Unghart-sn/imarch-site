Subject: Memorandum of Intent for Cooperation within the Vis Realitatis Programme

Dear Nikolai Gavrilovich!

Thank you for the preliminary discussion and confirmation of your interest in cooperation within the Vis Realitatis programme.

I am sending you materials to formalise the first step of our interaction.

1. Basic Memorandum of Intent This document formalises the general intention of the parties to cooperate within the Vis Realitatis programme without specifying the forms and scope of participation.

Memorandum publication: https://imarch.ltd/la/post/0022-a4f2b7c6v8e1g4l3i0j5k9l2w7e4o8p3q1l6s/

2. Overview of the Programme Architecture and Annex Logic To understand the programme structure, areas of responsibility, and principles of forming the Annex to the memorandum, an informative article is attached:

https://imarch.ltd/la/post/0020-d9f2b7c4m8e1g6h3i0j5k9l2w7n4o8p3q1r6s/

3. Next Step After formalising the memorandum, we will proceed to prepare the Annex to the Memorandum, which will specify:

  • the participation sector,
  • area of responsibility,
  • forms of interaction,
  • prospects for institutional expansion.

To formalise the signing of the memorandum at this stage, a reply email confirming your consent is sufficient. The confirmation text is provided below and can be used unchanged.

Confirmation Text (for reply):

I have familiarised myself with the content of the Memorandum of Intent for Cooperation within the Vis Realitatis programme. I confirm my intention to participate in the Vis Realitatis programme. I am ready to proceed to the next stage: forming the Annex to the Memorandum.

If you have any questions regarding the programme structure or the logic of the next steps, I will be glad to discuss them in a working manner.

1. Базовий меморандум про наміри Документ фіксує спільний намір сторін до співпраці в рамках програми Vis Realitatis без конкретизації форм та обсягу участі.

Публікація меморандуму: https://imarch.ltd/la/post/0022-a4f2b7c6v8e1g4l3i0j5k9l2w7e4o8p3q1l6s/
