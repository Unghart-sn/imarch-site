Salutem,

Per praesentem epistulam, IMARCH LTD UK (hinc Societas) ad FOP PURPUROV O.V. (hinc FOP) verba facit de sequenti.

I. CONTEXTUS Partes iam Protocollum Praeviorum Colloquiorum (Document ID: PROTOCOL-IMARCH-FOP-2026-01-26-ML) confecerunt, quod fundamentum operationale statuit. Ut hoc fundamentum in planum strategiae et longioris cooperationis evolvatur, Societas Memoranda Intentionum (hinc Memoranda) elaboravit et publicavit: → Memoranda Intentionum: https://imarch.ltd/la/post/0024-c1l2c3d4e5f1g7h8i9j0k1l2m4n4op/

II. PROPOSITUM OFFERTAE Hoc epistula est offerta officialis Societatis. Societas per hanc FOP proponit suum plenum et irrevocabile consensum cum omnibus praeceptis et principiis in supra dicto Memoranda Intentionum contentis exprimere.

III. MODUS ACCEPTATIONIS Consensus FOP cum Memoranda per hunc modum exprimi et validari debet:

  1. FOP epistulam responsivam ex suo email officiali (admin@fop-purpurov-ov.imarch.ltd) ad Societatem (legal@imarch.ltd) mittat.
  2. In epistula responsiva, FOP clare confirmet se:

    • Paratum esse ad cooperationem secundum normas in Memoranda definitas.

IV. EFFECTUS IURIDICUS Momentum quo epistula responsiva FOP (acceptus) ad Societatem perveniet, erit momentum quo Memoranda Intentionum ut documentum consensus mutui et voluntatis partium plene validatum et in vigorem ingressum erit.

To: FOP PURPUROV O.V. From: IMARCH LTD UK (Oleksandr Purpurov, Director) Subject: Proposal to express agreement with the Memorandum of Understanding between IMARCH LTD UK and FOP PURPUROV O.V.

Greetings,

Through this letter, IMARCH LTD UK (hereinafter the Company) addresses FOP PURPUROV O.V. (hereinafter the FOP) regarding the following.

I. CONTEXT The Parties have already concluded a Protocol of Preliminary Negotiations (Document ID: PROTOCOL-IMARCH-FOP-2026-01-26-ML), which established the operational foundation. To evolve this foundation into a strategic and longer-term cooperation plane, the Company has elaborated and published a Memorandum of Understanding (hereinafter the MoU): → Memorandum of Understanding: https://imarch.ltd/la/post/0024-c1l2c3d4e5f1g7h8i9j0k1l2m4n4op/

II. PURPOSE OF THE OFFER This letter is the Company’s official offer. Through it, the Company proposes that the FOP express its full and irrevocable agreement with all the provisions and principles contained in the aforementioned Memorandum of Understanding.

III. METHOD OF ACCEPTANCE The FOP’s agreement with the MoU must be expressed and validated in the following manner:

  1. The FOP shall send a response letter from its official email (admin@fop-purpurov-ov.imarch.ltd) to the Company (legal@imarch.ltd).
  2. In the response letter, the FOP must clearly confirm that it:
    • Has accepted this letter (the offer);
    • Fully acknowledges and agrees to all provisions of the Memorandum of Understanding;
    • Is ready to cooperate according to the norms defined in the MoU.

IV. LEGAL EFFECT The moment the FOP’s response letter (acceptance) reaches the Company will be the moment when the Memorandum of Understanding becomes fully validated and enters into force as a document of mutual consensus and will of the parties.

Date: January 28, 2026

For IMARCH LTD UK, Director, Oleksandr Purpurov
