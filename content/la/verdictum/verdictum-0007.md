### COMMENTARIUS PUBLICUS DECLARATIONUM IMARCH

**ARGUMENTUM:** Memorandum internum de constitutione substructurae organizationalis intra fines IMARCH LTD UK.

**DESTINATARIVS:** Membra Coetus Inceptorum «Vis Realitatis» ad usum officialem.

**SYNOPSIS:** Documentum formativum pro creatione Organizationis Autonomae Non-Civilis «Centrum Renovationis et Adaptationis «Vis Realitatis»» ut substructurae specialis intra mandatum IMARCH LTD UK, cum analysi juridica, oeconomica et strategica.

**STATVS:** Confectum et sigillatum. Ad usum internum Coetus Inceptorum.

**DIES EDITIONIS:** XIII Decembris MMXXV (A.D. 2025 mense Decembri die 13).

&nbsp;

### COMMENTARY ON IMARCH DECLARATIONS

**SUBJECT:** Internal memorandum on the establishment of an organizational substructure within the scope of IMARCH LTD UK.

**ADDRESSEE:** Members of the Initiative Group «Vis Realitatis» for official use.

**SYNOPSIS:** Formative document for the creation of the Autonomous Non-Commercial Organization «Center for Renewal and Adaptation «Vis Realitatis»» as a specialized substructure within the mandate of IMARCH LTD UK, with legal, economic and strategic analysis.

**STATUS:** Completed and sealed. For internal use of the Initiative Group.

**DATE OF PUBLICATION:** 13 December 2025.
