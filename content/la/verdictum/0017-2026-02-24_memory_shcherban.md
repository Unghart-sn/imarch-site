# d.m.n. N.G. Scherban (in memoriam)

**Latine:**

Praeclarus Praeses Consilii Expertorum Vis Realitatis (in memoriam).  

Projectus «Vis Realitatis» cum magna tristitia nuntiat decessum nostri honorandi collegae et praesidis consilii expertorum, d.m.n. Nicolai Gavrilovici Scherban.  

Sinceras condolentias exprimimus propinquis et amicis, dolorem communem participamus et memoriam luculentam de eius insigni opere et servitio conservamus.  

In vita sua consensum officialem dedit ad memorandum cooperationis subscribendum et Projectum sustinuit in praeviis tractatibus atque epistolis personalibus, confirmans valorem et momentum eius directionis.  

### Munus Symbolicum

– Praeses Consilii Expertorum (in memoriam)  
– Intentio participationis agnita et documentis confirmata  
– Ethicus conatus ad fundamentum communitatis  

Nomen N.G. Scherban in chronica Vis Realitatis in aeternum inscriptum est ut pars fundamenti eius.  

Memoria in institutione exprimitur non solum conservatione nominis, sed etiam continuatione operis. Quod vita sua sustinuit, fit pars fundamenti et manet in linea eius ut signum fidei et initii.  

---

**English (Validation):**

Distinguished Chairman of the Expert Council of Vis Realitatis (in memoriam).  

The project «Vis Realitatis» with deep sorrow announces the passing of our esteemed colleague and chairman of the expert council, Doctor of Medical Sciences Nikolai Gavrilovich Scherban.  

We express our sincere condolences to his family and friends, sharing the grief of loss and preserving the bright memory of his outstanding contribution and service.  

During his lifetime he gave official consent to sign the memorandum of cooperation and supported the Project in preliminary negotiations and personal correspondence, confirming the value and significance of its direction.  

### Symbolic Role

– Chairman of the Expert Council (in memoriam)  
– Recognized intention of participation, documented and confirmed  
– Ethical contribution to the foundation of the community  

The name of N.G. Scherban is forever inscribed in the chronicle of Vis Realitatis as part of its foundation.  

Memory in the institution is expressed not only by preserving the name but also by continuing the work. What was supported during his lifetime becomes part of the foundation and remains in its line as a sign of trust and beginning.
