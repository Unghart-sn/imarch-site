# VERSIO LATINA

*(Memorandum de Intellectu, MoU v1.0.0)*

inter
**Projectum Vis Realitatis / IMARCH**
et
**Peritum** ___________________________

---

## 1. Praenotiones Generales

Hoc Memorandum de Intellectu (quod in sequentibus "Memorandum" appellatur) principia et modos cooperationis inter Projectum **Vis Realitatis / IMARCH** (quod in sequentibus "Projectum" appellatur) et Peritum (quod in sequentibus "Peritus" appellatur) in ambitu inceptorum humanitariorum, investigatoriorum et educationalium Projecti definit.

Memorandum non est contractus operarum praestandarum nec obligationes oeconomicas automaticas partium secumfert, exceptis casibus qui separatis conventionibus directe designantur.

---

## 2. Propositum Cooperationis

Propositum huius Memorandi est:

* participatio Periti in evolutione, verificatione et probatione resolutionum methodologicarum, investigatoriarum et organicarum Projecti;
* adiutorium in formatione systematis stabilis sustentationis corporalis et affectuosae veteranorum, familiarum et communitatum;
* contributio in fundamentum scientificum-methodologicum, analyticum et peritorum Projecti.

---

## 3. Status et Munus Periti

Peritus in Projecto participat in statu:

* specialistae independentis intra ambitum suae competentiae professionalis;
* consultoris et/vel participis in processibus investigatoriis et methodologicis;
* membrum circuitus peritorum vel consultorii Projecti (sine potestatibus administrativis).

Peritus **non est** operarius, conductor vel repraesentator Projecti, nisi aliud separato documento designetur.

---

## 4. Formae Participationis Periti

Formae participationis includere possunt (sed non circumscribuntur):

* consultationes peritorum;
* participatio in sessionibus operariis, probationibus, coetibus focalibus;
* recognitio materiarum, methodorum, protocollorum;
* participatio in evolutione commendationum, relationum et conclusionum analyticarum;
* sustentationem peritorum in stadio probationis et amplificandarum resolutionum.

Concretus amplitudo et forma participationis inter partes separatim concordantur.

---

## 5. Conditiones Oeconomicae

* Participatio fundamentalis Periti peragi potest **gratis** tamquam contributio in projectum humanitarium et investigatorium.
* Si fundatio existit (donationes, pecuniae donatorum), **compensatio singularis vel per stadia** possibilis est, quae separata conventione vel appendice designatur.
* Factum compensationis non afficit statum independentem Periti eiusque conclusiones peritorum.

---

## 6. Confidentialitas et Ethica

Peritus se obligat ad:

* observandam confidentialitatem datorum sensibilium participantium projecti;
* agendum intra ambitum ethicae professionalis;
* non utendum informatione accepta ad damnum Projecti vel eius participantium.

Projectum se obligat ad:

* utendum contribuitione Periti recte et cum indicatione auctoritatis (si applicabile);
* non detorquendum conclusiones peritorum in materiis publicis.

---

## 7. Proprietas Intellectualis

* Iura auctorum in materiis individualibus Periti penes Peritum manent.
* Projectum ius accipit utendi fructibus cooperationis intra ambitum suae missionis cum indicatione fontis.
* Aliae conditiones per separatas conventiones, si necesse, reguntur.

---

## 8. Duratio Valoris

Hoc Memorandum in vim intrat a momento subscriptiones et valet usque ad eius resolutionem ab ulla parte per notificationem scriptam.

---

## 9. Positiones Finales

Hoc Memorandum intentiones bonae fidei partium reflectit et in principiis nititur:

* perspicuitatis,
* mutui honoris,
* responsalitatis humanitariae,
* bonae fidei scientificae et professionalis.

## 1. General Provisions

This Memorandum of Understanding (hereinafter referred to as "Memorandum") defines the principles and frameworks of cooperation between the Project **Vis Realitatis / IMARCH** (hereinafter referred to as "Project") and the Expert (hereinafter referred to as "Expert") within the scope of the Project's humanitarian, research, and educational initiatives.

The Memorandum is not a service contract and does not entail automatic financial obligations of the parties, except for cases directly specified in separate agreements.

---

## 2. Purpose of Cooperation

The purpose of this Memorandum is:

* participation of the Expert in the development, verification, and testing of methodological, research, and organizational solutions of the Project;
* assistance in forming a sustainable system of bodily-emotional support for veterans, families, and communities;
* contribution to the scientific-methodological, analytical, and expert foundation of the Project.

---

## 3. Status and Role of the Expert

The Expert participates in the Project in the status of:

* an independent specialist within their professional competence;
* a consultant and/or participant in research and methodological processes;
* a member of the Project's expert or advisory circuit (without administrative powers).

The Expert **is not** an employee, contractor, or representative of the Project, unless otherwise specified in a separate document.

---

## 4. Forms of Expert Participation

Forms of participation may include (but are not limited to):

* expert consultations;
* participation in working sessions, pilot tests, focus groups;
* review of materials, methodologies, protocols;
* participation in developing recommendations, reports, and analytical conclusions;
* expert support at the stage of testing and scaling solutions.

The specific scope and format of participation are agreed upon separately by the parties.

---

## 5. Financial Conditions

* Basic participation of the Expert may be carried out **on a gratuitous basis** as a contribution to a humanitarian and research project.
* If funding exists (grants, donor funds), **one-time or stage-based compensation** is possible, formalized by a separate agreement or appendix.
* The fact of compensation does not affect the independent status of the Expert and their expert conclusions.

---

## 6. Confidentiality and Ethics

The Expert undertakes to:

* maintain confidentiality of sensitive data of project participants;
* act within the framework of professional ethics;
* not use the obtained information to the detriment of the Project or its participants.

The Project undertakes to:

* use the Expert's contribution correctly and with attribution (if applicable);
* not distort expert conclusions in public materials.

---

## 7. Intellectual Property

* Copyrights to the Expert's individual materials remain with the Expert.
* The Project receives the right to use the results of cooperation within the scope of its mission with indication of the source.
* Other conditions are regulated by separate agreements if necessary.

---

## 8. Term of Validity

This Memorandum enters into force from the moment of signing and remains valid until its termination by either party through written notification.

---

## 9. Final Provisions

This Memorandum reflects the good faith intentions of the parties and is based on the principles of:

* transparency,
* mutual respect,
* humanitarian responsibility,
* scientific and professional good faith.

---
