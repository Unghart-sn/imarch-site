####  📖 Chronicle OS: scientometric journal and publication platform { .fade-in-up }

**Chronicle OS** is an international peer‑reviewed interdisciplinary open‑access journal integrated into the infrastructure of IMARCH LTD.

The journal offers:

- **Double‑Blind Peer Review**
- **Cryptographic pre‑registration** of articles and hypotheses (SHA‑256 hashing)
- **DOI assignment** and indexing in international databases (DOAJ, Scopus, WoS — in perspective)
- **Open funding model** (reasonable APC with full waiver possibilities)

By publishing in Chronicle OS, you embed your scientific results into an ecosystem where they are protected, verified and can be capitalised.

####  Context { .fade-in-up }

IMARCH emerged as a response to the absence of a space where deep practices can exist without being simplified for visibility.

The structure develops at the intersection of humanitarian initiatives, research processes and applied disciplines.

Various practice directions are already forming within the environment. Some participants work at the border of science and therapy. Others develop craft and body‑oriented approaches. Some explore new forms of interaction and social architecture.

####  Who we are looking for { .fade-in-up }

We focus not so much on status as on the depth of practice.

We are interested in researchers, therapists, engineers, masters of applied disciplines, mentors, authors of methods and bearers of unique experience.

**We especially welcome those who want to:**

- publish in an open peer‑reviewed journal and obtain DOI for their work;
- participate in the development of the scientometric platform;
- influence the formation of a new research ecosystem.

If your work does not fit into standard categories, this may be a sign of alignment.

#### Priority areas and participant profile { .fade-in-up }

The Editorial Board focuses on the academic and practical depth of research, giving priority to authors with verified applied experience.

We invite researchers, practising therapists, engineers, authors of proprietary methods, mentors and interdisciplinary specialists with unique empirical data to long‑term cooperation.

The interaction programme is aimed at authors interested in:

**Deposition and verification of results**: Publication in an open peer‑reviewed journal with mandatory assignment of an international DOI.

**Infrastructure development**: Expert participation in the methodological and technical formation of the scientometric platform.

**Institutional integration**: Joining the international research ecosystem and contour projects.

The publisher’s special attention is given to non‑standard, transdisciplinary works that lie at the intersection of several scientific fields and require individual peer review.

#### Mirror of practice { .fade-in-up }

Some practices are difficult to represent through conventional forms of description. Perhaps you have encountered situations where long‑term work is reduced to short formulations, complex processes require simplification for clarity, and the depth of experience remains beyond the visible.

If these states are familiar to you, your practice is likely already moving in a direction that IMARCH strives to make observable.

Different lines do not compete with each other. They intertwine and are preserved through the chronicle of actions.

Some aspects of the future structure are not yet defined and will be shaped by participants through practice.

#### Structure of participation { .fade-in-up }

A participant receives a personal institutional cell:

- personal office site on a subdomain  
- space for recording projects and consultations  
- integration with Chronicle OS (publications, preprints, chronicle)

This is not a profile or portfolio, but a working presence within the structure.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up">
  <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #4b2e2e, transparent);" />

&nbsp;

#### Economic model { .fade-in-up }

IMARCH Githronical uses the principle of mutual reinforcement. If a participant's activity attracts new resources or people, the contribution is recorded and part of the flows is redistributed through a transparent mechanism.

The economy serves the sustainability of the structure, not marketing growth.

#### Possible roles { .fade-in-up }

- **Journal author / contributor** — publication of articles, obtaining DOI and hashes
- **Reviewer** — participation in double‑blind peer review
- **Custos of practice** — recording and support of applied methods
- **Chronicle researcher** — data analysis and systematisation
- **Master of applied discipline**
- **Direction mentor**

#### Entry process { .fade-in-up }

1. **Publication**: start by submitting an article to Chronicle OS or registering a preprint
2. **Application** for participation in the ecosystem (personal cell)
3. **Initial acquaintance**
4. **Alignment of intentions**
5. **Signing of memorandum**
6. **Deployment of personal cell**

We view participation as a process of mutual alignment.
