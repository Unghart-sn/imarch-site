<a id="section-1-purpose"></a>
### 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the composition of the operational documentation required for the deployment and commissioning of the University Chronicle Node as an institutional publishing node of the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

This document forms an integral part of the Functional Deployment Package and shall be applied following the adoption of the Documentation System Master Edition.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="section-2-scope"></a>
### 2. SCOPE

This Package applies to:

- universities;
- academic institutes;
- research centres;
- learned societies;
- state scientific organisations;
- partner institutional publishing structures.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="section-3-objective"></a>
### 3. OBJECTIVE

The objective of this Package is to enable the full-cycle launch of an institutional publishing node without altering the fundamental documentation of Chronicle OS.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="section-4-functional-composition"></a>
### 4. FUNCTIONAL COMPOSITION

The University Chronicle Node Deployment Kit comprises the following groups of documents.

**Group A — Institution Entry Documents**  
Documents establishing institutional interaction.

**Group B — Institution Governance Documents**  
Documents for the formation of governing bodies.

**Group C — Editorial Operations**  
Documents organising the editorial process.

**Group D — Scientific Review Operations**  
Documents organising expert peer review.

**Group E — Publishing Operations**  
Documents for the publication cycle.

**Group F — Metadata Operations**  
Documents for scientific metadata management.

**Group G — Infrastructure Operations**  
Documents for technical deployment.

**Group H — Quality Assurance**  
Documents for quality control.

**Group I — Acceptance Documentation**  
Documents for acceptance.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="section-5-deployment-principle"></a>
### 5. DEPLOYMENT PRINCIPLE

The deployment of the University Chronicle Node is carried out sequentially.

1. Establishment of institutional interaction.
2. Formation of governing bodies.
3. Appointment of editorial staff.
4. Deployment of technical infrastructure.
5. Configuration of the publication process.
6. Conduct of a test publishing cycle.
7. Signing of the Readiness Certificate.
8. Publication of the first issue.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="section-6-relation-to-documentation-system"></a>
### 6. RELATION TO THE DOCUMENTATION SYSTEM

The Functional Deployment Package does not amend the provisions of the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

The documents of this Package constitute the operational implementation of the provisions of the Documentation System.

In the event of any conflict, the Documentation System shall prevail.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="section-7-result"></a>
### 7. RESULT

Upon completion of the procedures prescribed by this Package, the University Chronicle Node shall be deemed commissioned and shall be entitled to operate within Chronicle OS as an independent institutional publishing node.

### FDP-001.1 — OFFICIAL LETTER OF INVITATION TO INSTITUTIONAL COOPERATION

**Document ID:** FDP-001.1

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### OFFICIAL INVITATION

**To the Rector (President, Director)**

Dear Colleague,

The international institutional operator IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom) invites your university to consider the possibility of joining the international publishing ecosystem Chronicle OS through the establishment of its own institutional publishing node — the University Chronicle Node.

Chronicle OS constitutes an institutional system for the organisation of scholarly communication, designed to support universities, research institutes, learned societies, and academic organisations in creating their own autonomous publishing units integrated into a unified international scholarly space.

The proposed model of cooperation is based on the following principles:

- preservation of the university's institutional sovereignty;
- preservation of editorial independence;
- observance of academic integrity;
- transparency of editorial procedures;
- uniform international standards for scientific metadata;
- long-term preservation of scholarly publications;
- enhancement of the international visibility of scholarly output.

The establishment of a University Chronicle Node does not entail the transfer by the university of rights to its own scholarly publications, any change in institutional identity, or the transfer of editorial authority to external organisations.

Chronicle OS provides the university with:

- normative documentation;
- methodology for the deployment of the publishing node;
- an organisational governance model;
- technical recommendations for the creation of publishing infrastructure;
- implementation support.

#### Procedure of Interaction

The proposed cooperation procedure comprises the following stages:

1. Conduct of a preliminary consultation.
2. Exchange of basic information on the university's scholarly and publishing activities.
3. Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU).
4. Formation of the institutional editorial structure.
5. Deployment of the University Chronicle Node.
6. Conduct of a pilot publishing cycle.
7. Signing of the Readiness Certificate.
8. Publication of the first official issue of the scholarly journal.

#### Result of Cooperation

Upon completion of the deployment procedure, the university shall have a functioning institutional publishing node operating within the international Chronicle OS ecosystem, with full retention of its own academic autonomy and organisational independence.

#### Accompanying Documents

Upon request, the university shall be provided with:

- Chronicle OS Documentation System (Master Edition);
- University Chronicle Node Deployment Kit;
- Memorandum of Understanding (MoU);
- Draft Cooperation Agreement;
- University Chronicle Node Implementation Roadmap.

Yours sincerely,

**IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)**  
International Institutional Operator  
Chronicle OS Documentation System

### FDP-001.2 — PROTOCOL OF INTENT

**Document ID:** FDP-001.2

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### PROTOCOL OF INTENT

This Protocol of Intent records the mutual interest of the parties in considering the possibility of establishing an institutional publishing node — the University Chronicle Node — within the international publishing ecosystem Chronicle OS.

The signing of this Protocol does not create financial obligations and does not constitute an agreement to commence economic activity.

This document confirms solely the intention of the parties to proceed to the next stage of preparatory cooperation.

#### Purpose of the Document

This Protocol is intended for:

- confirmation of the parties' interest;
- commencement of official consultations;
- exchange of necessary organisational information;
- preparation of the draft Memorandum of Understanding (MoU).

#### Subject of Consultations

During the preparatory phase, the parties shall consider:

- the feasibility of establishing a University Chronicle Node;
- the organisational model of the editorial structure;
- the composition of the editorial board;
- the proposed scholarly specialisations;
- the technical deployment model;
- the procedure for integration into Chronicle OS.

#### Principles of Interaction

During the consultation phase, the parties shall be guided by the following principles:

- voluntary participation;
- academic independence;
- institutional equality;
- confidentiality of information received;
- absence of obligations pending the signing of the MoU.

#### Actions of the Parties

**IMARCH LTD undertakes to:**

- provide the necessary documentation;
- conduct consultations;
- present the University Chronicle Node deployment model;
- respond to the university's questions.

**The University undertakes to:**

- designate a contact person;
- provide general information on its scholarly infrastructure;
- consider the materials presented;
- decide on proceeding to the MoU preparation stage.

#### Term of Validity

This Protocol shall remain in effect until:

- the signing of the Memorandum of Understanding;

**OR**

- written notification by either party of the termination of negotiations.

#### Legal Status

This document does not constitute a contract, does not create property obligations, and is used solely for the purpose of preparing possible institutional cooperation.

#### Result

Upon completion of the stage provided for by this Protocol, the parties shall take one of the following decisions:

- proceed to the signing of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU);

**OR**

- terminate negotiations without incurring mutual obligations.

### FDP-001.3-B — STRATEGIC INSTITUTIONAL PARTNERSHIP MEMORANDUM

**Document ID:** FDP-001.3-B

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

#### Preamble

Recognising the growing importance of open scholarly communication, international academic cooperation, and the institutional independence of universities,

guided by the principles of academic freedom, scholarly integrity, long-term development of scholarly infrastructure, and mutual respect for institutional sovereignty,

the parties enter into this Strategic Partnership Memorandum.

This document defines the general principles for the formation of a long-term institutional alliance between the university and the international Chronicle OS system.

#### 1. Subject of Strategic Partnership

The parties confirm their intention to jointly participate in the development of the international scholarly communication infrastructure Chronicle OS through:

- the formation of the University Chronicle Node;
- the joint development of academic publishing practices;
- participation in the development of international standards for scholarly communication;
- the development of expert communities;
- the creation of a sustainable institutional scholarly ecosystem.

#### 2. Strategic Objectives

The Partnership is directed towards the achievement of the following long-term objectives:

- development of international academic integration;
- enhancement of the institutional agency of universities;
- development of independent scholarly publishing platforms;
- formation of a sustainable expert infrastructure;
- development of an open system of scholarly knowledge;
- ensuring long-term preservation and institutional continuity of scholarly output.

#### 3. Status of the Parties

This Memorandum does not establish a relationship of subordination.

Each party retains:

- legal autonomy;
- organisational autonomy;
- financial autonomy;
- editorial autonomy;
- academic autonomy.

Cooperation shall be conducted exclusively on the basis of institutional partnership.

#### 4. Role of IMARCH LTD

IMARCH LTD acts as the international institutional operator of Chronicle OS and provides:

- development of the normative framework;
- support for institutional implementation;
- development of the international Chronicle OS network;
- coordination of joint initiatives;
- support for international interaction among network participants.

#### 5. Role of the University

The University independently determines:

- its own scholarly policy;
- editorial policy;
- staffing;
- research directions;
- publication strategy;
- the internal structure of the University Chronicle Node.

#### 6. Joint Activities

Within the framework of this Partnership, the parties may jointly undertake:

- scholarly research;
- publishing projects;
- international conferences;
- educational programmes;
- expert programmes;
- development of sectoral recommendations;
- development of international scholarly networks.

#### 7. Joint Development of Chronicle OS

The parties recognise the possibility of joint participation in the further development of Chronicle OS.

The University has the right to:

- submit proposals for the development of the Documentation System;
- participate in expert discussions;
- take part in working groups;
- initiate the development of new academic modules;
- participate in the development of international Chronicle OS standards.

All amendments to the Documentation System shall be adopted exclusively in accordance with the internal procedures of Chronicle OS.

- the International Expert Council of Chronicle OS;
- thematic editorial boards;
- working expert groups;
- international scholarly commissions.

Such participation shall be conducted exclusively on a voluntary basis and shall not alter the institutional autonomy of the University.

#### 9. Intellectual Property

Each party retains exclusive rights to its own intellectual property.

Joint results shall be governed by separate agreements.

This Memorandum does not provide for the transfer of proprietary rights.

#### 10. Confidentiality

Information obtained by the parties during the preparation of joint projects shall be used solely for the purpose of implementing this Partnership.

#### 11. Term of Partnership

This Memorandum shall enter into force upon signature.

The Partnership is considered by the parties to be long-term and shall remain in force until terminated by either party.

#### 12. Final Provisions

This Memorandum constitutes a declaration of strategic institutional partnership.

All projects arising under this document shall be implemented through separate agreements, regulations, and operational documentation of the Functional Deployment Package.

This Framework Agreement defines the legal and organisational principles of long-term cooperation between IMARCH LTD and the University in the establishment and operation of the University Chronicle Node.

#### 2. Subject of the Agreement

The Parties jointly undertake activities for:

- the establishment of the University Chronicle Node;
- the development of scholarly publishing infrastructure;
- the organisation of editorial activities;
- the development of international scholarly communication;
- the implementation of Chronicle OS standards.

**IMARCH LTD shall provide:**

- support for the Documentation System;
- development of methodology;
- support for the international infrastructure;
- development of the normative framework;
- coordination of international interaction.

**The University shall independently exercise:**

- scholarly activities;
- editorial activities;
- management of the University Chronicle Node;
- staffing appointments;
- publication of scholarly materials;
- development of its own scholarly policy.

#### 4. Financial Independence

Each party shall bear its own costs associated with the conduct of its own activities.

Any joint financial projects shall be governed by separate agreements.

IMARCH LTD shall not interfere with:

- the scholarly policy of the University;
- the decisions of the Editorial Board;
- the review procedures;
- the decision-making on publication.

The University shall independently take all editorial decisions.

#### 6. Joint Use of Brands

The use of:

- Chronicle OS;
- University Chronicle Node;
- IMARCH;

shall be conducted exclusively in accordance with separate regulations.

The parties shall seek to resolve all issues through negotiation.

#### 9. Termination of Cooperation

Termination of this Agreement shall not affect:

- previously published materials;
- the rights of authors;
- the University's rights to its own scholarly output;
- archival data of the University Chronicle Node.

#### 10. Final Provisions

This Agreement defines the institutional model for interaction between the parties.

All specialised projects shall be governed by separate appendices or independent contracts.

#### Appendices

This Agreement may include:

- Technical Appendix;
- Financial Appendix;
- Implementation Plan;
- Support Plan;
- List of Responsible Persons;
- Project Roadmap.

### FDP-003.1 — PUBLICATION WORKFLOW

**Regulation of the Full Lifecycle of a Scholarly Publication**

**Document ID:** FDP-003.1

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

This Regulation defines the full lifecycle of a scholarly publication within the University Chronicle Node, from the moment of receipt of the manuscript by the editorial office to its publication, archiving, and subsequent support.

This document shall be applied by all participants in the editorial process, irrespective of their official position.

#### 2. General Principles

The editorial process is founded on the following principles:

- scholarly integrity;
- independence of expert assessment;
- transparency of procedures;
- traceability of all editorial actions;
- preservation of editorial history;
- immutability of the published version.

#### 3. Participants in the Publication Process

The editorial process involves:

- Author;
- Managing Editor;
- Editor-in-Chief;
- Issue Editor;
- Reviewers;
- Technical Editor;
- Scientific Metadata Specialist;
- Publishing Platform Administrator.

#### 4. Core Stages of the Publication Lifecycle

A publication passes through the following stages:

**Stage 1** — Receipt of Manuscript.

**Stage 2** — Initial Technical Check.

**Stage 3** — Preliminary Editorial Assessment.

**Stage 4** — Appointment of Issue Editor.

**Stage 5** — Expert Peer Review.

**Stage 6** — Author Revision.

**Stage 7** — Editorial Approval.

**Stage 8** — Technical Preparation.

**Stage 9** — Formation of Scientific Metadata.

**Stage 10** — Assignment of Publication Identifiers.

**Stage 11** — Official Publication.

**Stage 12** — Archiving.

**Stage 13** — Post-Publication Support.

#### 5. Publication State Management

The current state of each publication shall be recorded.

Each change of state shall be accompanied by:

- time of change;
- responsible participant;
- basis for the change;
- entry in the editorial event log.

#### 6. Traceability

No editorial action shall be performed without registration.

All changes to the editorial state shall be preserved in the publication process log.

#### 7. Completion of the Lifecycle

Following official publication, the article shall be deemed to have completed the editorial cycle and shall enter the archival support phase.

Subsequent amendments shall be permitted exclusively in accordance with the separate regulations of Chronicle OS.

#### 8. Final Provisions

This Regulation defines solely the sequence of stages of the publication process.

Detailed procedures for the execution of each stage shall be established by the specialised operational protocols of the Functional Deployment Package.

### FDP-003.2 — MANUSCRIPT INTAKE PROTOCOL

**(Manuscript Intake Protocol)**

**Document ID:** FDP-003.2

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### 1. Purpose

This Protocol defines the procedure for the receipt of scholarly manuscripts by the editorial office of the University Chronicle Node.

The Protocol shall apply to all materials, irrespective of their scholarly field, type of publication, or method of submission.

#### 2. Objective

To ensure uniform registration of each received manuscript, to prevent loss of materials, and to guarantee the traceability of the subsequent editorial process.

#### 3. Responsible Person

Execution of this Protocol is the responsibility of the Managing Editor.

#### 4. Basis for Commencement

The procedure commences upon receipt of the manuscript through one of the editorial office's official channels.

Permissible channels:

- editorial information system;
- official website;
- official email address;
- other approved means of receiving materials.

#### 5. Registration

Upon receipt of the material, the Managing Editor shall:

- register the date of receipt;
- assign an internal registration number;
- create an editorial publication card;
- open a publication dossier.

#### 6. Completeness Check

The following shall be verified:

- text of the manuscript;
- author details;
- contact information;
- required accompanying documents;
- declarations required by the Publication Policy.

#### 7. Results of the Check

Based on the results of the check, one of the following decisions shall be taken:

**7.1 Material accepted for consideration**  
The manuscript shall receive the status *Received* and be transferred to the preliminary editorial assessment stage.

**7.2 Material requires supplementation**  
The author shall be notified of the missing documents. Until the deficiencies are rectified, the publication shall receive the status *Incomplete Submission*.

**7.3 Material does not meet formal requirements**  
The manuscript shall be returned to the author without initiating the editorial process. Status — *Returned Without Review*.

#### 8. Documentation

Each action shall be recorded:

- date;
- time;
- executor;
- basis;
- decision taken.

All records shall be preserved in the publication log.

#### 9. Transfer to Next Stage

Upon completion of registration, the publication shall be transferred to the Editor-in-Chief for preliminary assessment.

The publication status shall be changed to *Editorial Screening*.

#### 10. Completion of Procedure

This Protocol shall be deemed executed upon transfer of the publication to the next stage of the editorial process.

#### Result

The output of this Protocol is an officially registered manuscript with a complete publication dossier, ready for preliminary editorial assessment.

### FDP-003.3 — EDITORIAL SCREENING PROTOCOL

**(Editorial Screening Protocol)**

**Document ID:** FDP-003.3

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### 1. Purpose

This Protocol defines the procedure for conducting preliminary editorial assessment of a manuscript prior to its transfer to independent expert peer review.

#### 2. Objective

Preliminary editorial assessment is intended to determine whether the submitted manuscript meets the basic requirements of the scholarly journal.

This stage does not constitute scholarly review and does not involve assessment of the scientific novelty of the research.

#### 3. Responsible Person

Preliminary editorial assessment shall be conducted by the Editor-in-Chief or an Issue Editor duly authorised by the Editor-in-Chief.

#### 4. Subject of Assessment

The assessment shall verify exclusively compliance with the following criteria:

- thematic relevance to the journal;
- compliance with editorial policy;
- compliance with the scholarly profile of the issue;
- compliance with the Publication Policy;
- compliance with Publication Ethics;
- absence of obvious violations of academic integrity;
- completeness of submitted materials.

No scholarly assessment of the research results shall be performed at this stage.

#### 5. Possible Decisions

Based on the results of the preliminary assessment, one of the following decisions shall be taken.

**Decision A — Transfer to Expert Peer Review**  
Publication status: *Peer Review Assigned*

**Decision B — Return to Author for Preliminary Revision**  
Status: *Revision Before Review*  
The author shall be provided with a list of comments to be addressed prior to the commencement of the review procedure.

**Decision C — Reject Without Transfer to Review**  
Status: *Desk Rejected*  
Grounds may include:
- thematic mismatch;
- gross violation of editorial policy;
- lack of scholarly character of the material;
- obvious violations of Publication Ethics;
- incompleteness of submitted materials after repeated request.

#### 6. Prohibitions

During preliminary assessment, it is prohibited to:

- assess the scientific novelty of the research;
- assess the validity of scientific results;
- substitute for the independent peer review procedure;
- take the author's identity into account in decision-making.

#### 7. Documentation

Each decision shall be accompanied by:

- date;
- time;
- responsible editor;
- basis for the decision;
- change of publication status.

#### 8. Transfer of Publication

In the event of a positive decision, the publication shall be transferred to the Managing Editor for the assignment of independent reviewers.

#### 9. Completion of Procedure

This Protocol shall be deemed executed upon the change of publication status and transfer of materials to the next stage of the publication cycle.

#### Result

The output of this Protocol is one of three decisions:

- referral to peer review;
- return for preliminary revision;
- rejection without conducting expert peer review.

The decision shall be recorded in the Chronicle OS publication log and shall form part of the editorial history of the manuscript.

### FDP-003.5 — PEER REVIEW MODELS REGULATION

**(Peer Review Models Regulation)**

**Document ID:** FDP-003.5

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### 1. Purpose

This Regulation defines the models of expert peer review supported by Chronicle OS, as well as the principles for their selection and application within the University Chronicle Node.

#### 2. General Principles

Irrespective of the selected model, peer review shall be conducted on the following principles:

- independence of the expert;
- objectivity;
- scholarly integrity;
- absence of conflict of interest;
- confidentiality;
- professional competence.

#### 3. Selection of Model

The specific model shall be determined by the University or by the editorial policy of the individual scholarly journal.

Chronicle OS does not impose a mandatory model of expert assessment.

#### 4. Supported Models

**4.1 Single Blind Peer Review**  
The reviewer knows the author. The author does not know the reviewer. Primarily used in applied disciplines.

**4.2 Double Blind Peer Review**  
Neither the author nor the reviewer knows the identity of the other. This is the recommended model for most academic journals.

**4.3 Open Peer Review**  
The identities of both author and reviewer are disclosed. The review may be published alongside the article. Used where the relevant editorial policy exists.

**4.4 Collaborative Review**  
Multiple experts jointly discuss the manuscript and formulate a single report. Used for interdisciplinary research and large collaborative projects.

**4.5 Registered Review**  
The research programme is subjected to expert assessment prior to the acquisition of research results. Following the execution of the approved plan, publication is considered irrespective of the nature of the results obtained.

**4.6 Post Publication Review**  
Scholarly discussion continues after publication of the article. The publication is accompanied by open expert discussion.

#### 5. Change of Model

The editorial office may change the review model for a specific manuscript only:

- in agreement with the Editor-in-Chief;
- where objective grounds exist;
- with mandatory registration of the change.

#### 6. Conflict of Interest

In the event of the identification of a conflict of interest:

- the expert shall immediately notify the editorial office;
- the participation of that expert shall be terminated;
- a new reviewer shall be appointed.

#### 7. Documentation

For each publication, Chronicle OS records:

- the selected model;
- the number of experts;
- deadlines;
- the stages of the assessment process;
- the final decision.

#### 8. Final Provisions

This Regulation defines the models of expert assessment supported by Chronicle OS.

Specific editorial procedures shall be established by the Publication Policy of the relevant scholarly journal.

**(Peer Review Execution Protocol)**

**Document ID:** FDP-003.6

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### 1. Purpose

This Protocol defines the procedure for conducting independent scholarly peer review of a manuscript following the formation of the expert group.

#### 2. Objective

To ensure objective scholarly assessment of the research through an independent expert procedure.

#### 3. Basis for Commencement

The procedure shall commence upon the change of the publication status to *Under Peer Review*.

#### 4. Receipt of Materials

Each appointed reviewer shall receive:

- the text of the manuscript;
- editorial instructions;
- assessment criteria;
- deadlines for the preparation of the report;
- the expert report form.

#### 5. Conduct of Expert Assessment

The expert shall independently analyse:

- scientific novelty;
- methodological correctness;
- logical coherence of the research;
- reproducibility of results;
- quality of argumentation;
- compliance with scholarly ethics;
- correctness of references;
- completeness of the scholarly apparatus.

#### 6. Prohibitions

The reviewer shall not:

- contact the author directly;
- transfer materials to third parties;
- use the research results prior to publication;
- disclose the content of the manuscript;
- use official information for personal purposes.

#### 7. Final Report

Upon completion of the assessment, the reviewer shall select one of the following decisions:

**A** — Accept without changes.  
**B** — Accept after minor revision.  
**C** — Refer for substantial revision.  
**D** — Reject.

Each decision shall be accompanied by a detailed expert report.

#### 8. Independence of Experts

Each reviewer shall formulate their report independently.

Until the completion of the procedure, communication between reviewers shall not be permitted, unless otherwise provided for by the selected review model.

#### 9. Conflicting Reports

Where fundamentally contradictory expert conclusions are present, the Editor-in-Chief may:

- appoint an additional reviewer;
- initiate a collective review;
- refer the materials to the Expert Council.

#### 10. Documentation

Chronicle OS records:

- the date of receipt of the report;
- compliance with deadlines;
- the final decision;
- the editorial response;
- subsequent actions.

#### 11. Transfer of Results

Following receipt of all expert reports, the publication shall be transferred to the Editor-in-Chief.

The status shall be changed to *Editorial Decision Pending*.

#### 12. Completion of Procedure

This Protocol shall be deemed executed upon registration of all expert reports and transfer of materials to the Editor-in-Chief.

#### Result

The output of this Protocol is a complete set of independent expert reports, which shall serve as the basis for the editorial decision.

The output of this Protocol is an officially published scholarly issue, constituting a complete unit of the publishing activity of the University Chronicle Node.

### FDP-005.3 — ANNUAL EDITORIAL AUDIT PROTOCOL

**(Annual Editorial Audit Protocol)**

**Document ID:** FDP-005.3

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### 1. Purpose

This Protocol defines the procedure for the annual internal assessment of the activities of the University Chronicle Node.

#### 2. Objective

To ensure continuous improvement of editorial processes, maintenance of high standards of publication culture, and timely updating of the normative framework.

#### 3. Frequency

The editorial audit shall be conducted no less than once per calendar year.

- compliance with Publication Ethics;
- execution of editorial regulations;
- quality of expert peer review;

- operability of Chronicle OS;

- identified strengths;

The output of this Protocol is an official annual report on the state of the University Chronicle Node and an approved programme for its further development.

### FDP-005.4 — NORMATIVE DOCUMENTATION GOVERNANCE PROTOCOL

**(Normative Documentation Governance Protocol)**

**Document ID:** FDP-005.4

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### 1. Purpose

This Protocol defines the uniform procedure for the creation, amendment, approval, registration, and repeal of normative documentation of Chronicle OS.

#### 2. Objective

To ensure the integrity of the normative framework, its internal consistency, the traceability of its development, and institutional continuity.

#### 3. Scope

This Protocol extends to:

- regulations;
- editorial policies;
- procedures;
- instructions;
- standards;
- architectural documents;
- methodological documents;
- internal normative acts of the University Chronicle Node.

#### 4. Principles

Normative documentation of Chronicle OS is founded on the principles of:

- unity;
- consistency;
- traceability of changes;
- documented continuity;
- preservation of archival versions.

#### 5. Creation of a Document

The proponent of development shall submit:

- the purpose of the document;
- the scope of application;
- the draft version;
- justification of the need.

Following review, the document shall receive the status of working draft.

#### 6. Review

The draft shall be reviewed by:

- the Editor-in-Chief;
- where necessary, the Editorial Collegium;
- the Expert Council;
- the administration of the publishing node.

#### 7. Approval

Following decision, the document shall receive:

- a registration number;
- a date of approval;
- a version number;
- official status.

#### 8. Amendment of a Document

Each new version shall be recorded as an independent edition.

The previous version shall be preserved in the Chronicle OS archive.

Deletion of archival versions shall not be permitted.

#### 9. Repeal of a Document

A document may be declared invalid exclusively by official decision of the competent authority.

The following shall be preserved:

- the archival version;
- the registration record;
- the history of the document's operation.

#### 10. Normative Documentation Registry

Chronicle OS shall maintain a unified Registry of Normative Documents, containing:

- registration number;
- title;
- date of approval;
- current version;
- history of amendments;
- status of the document;
- links to other normative acts.

#### 11. Principle of Continuity

No amendment to the normative framework shall disrupt the integrity of previously adopted procedures without a documented basis.

#### 12. Completion of Procedure

This Protocol shall be deemed executed upon registration of the relevant change in the Normative Documentation Registry of Chronicle OS.

#### Result

The output of this Protocol is a unified, governed, and historically traceable normative system of Chronicle OS, ensuring the stability of the functioning of the University Chronicle Node irrespective of organisational and personnel changes.

### FDP-005.5 — UNIVERSITY CHRONICLE NODE OPERATIONAL CHARTER

**(University Chronicle Node Operational Charter)**

**Document ID:** FDP-005.5

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)

#### Preamble

The University Chronicle Node is an autonomous university publishing node operating within Chronicle OS and implementing the full lifecycle of a scholarly publication.

This Charter defines the principles of existence, objectives, functions, authority, and procedure for the operation of the University Chronicle Node as an independent scholarly publishing institution.

#### 1. Mission

The primary mission of the University Chronicle Node is to ensure a sustainable, reproducible, ethically sound, and scientifically rigorous process of creation, expert assessment, publication, support, and long-term preservation of the results of scholarly activity.

#### 2. Core Principles

The University Chronicle Node shall conduct its activities on the basis of the following principles:

- scholarly independence;
- editorial autonomy;
- academic integrity;
- transparency of procedures;
- traceability of decisions;
- institutional continuity;
- openness to scholarly development;
- preservation of the historical memory of science.

#### 3. Core Functions

The University Chronicle Node shall ensure:

- receipt of scholarly manuscripts;
- editorial assessment;
- organisation of expert peer review;
- taking of editorial decisions;
- preparation of publications;
- release of scholarly materials;
- support of publications;
- archiving;
- registration of scientific interconnections;
- support of the lifecycle of scholarly knowledge.

#### 4. Structure

The University Chronicle Node shall comprise:

- the Editor-in-Chief;
- the Editorial Collegium;
- the Managing Editor;
- the Technical Editorial Office;
- the Expert Network;
- the Chronicle OS Archive;
- the Normative Documentation Registry;
- the Publication Repository.

The structure may be developed without contravening this Charter.

#### 5. Normative Framework

The activities of the University Chronicle Node shall be conducted on the basis of:

- the Publication Policy;
- Publication Ethics;
- Chronicle OS regulations;
- internal normative documents;
- decisions of editorial bodies.

All procedures shall be in accordance with the approved normative system of Chronicle OS.

#### 6. Publication Lifecycle

Each scholarly publication shall pass through the full lifecycle:

1. Submission
2. Editorial Assessment
3. Peer Review
4. Editorial Decision
5. Production
6. Publication
7. Post Publication Lifecycle
8. Long Term Preservation
9. Scientific Knowledge Evolution

#### 7. Governance

The governance of the University Chronicle Node shall be conducted through documented procedures.

No function shall depend exclusively on a specific office holder.

All processes shall be reproducible.

#### 8. Institutional Memory

The University Chronicle Node shall ensure the preservation of:

- publications;
- editorial history;
- expert decisions;
- normative documentation;
- scientific interconnections;
- the history of the development of scholarly knowledge.

#### 9. Development

The University Chronicle Node is considered as a self-developing scholarly system.

Improvement of procedures shall be conducted through the official amendment of the normative documentation of Chronicle OS.

#### 10. Continuity

Even in the event of changes to:

- the editorial staff;
- the leadership;
- the University;
- the digital platform;
- the technological infrastructure,

the University Chronicle Node shall retain:

- its identity;
- its archive;
- its normative system;
- its scholarly memory;
- its publication repository.

#### 11. Final Provision

This Charter constitutes the foundational organisational document of the University Chronicle Node.

All subsequent normative acts, procedures, and regulations shall apply exclusively to the extent that they do not conflict with this Charter.

#### Result

This document defines the University Chronicle Node as an independent scholarly publishing institution operating within Chronicle OS and ensuring the full lifecycle of scholarly knowledge, from the emergence of a manuscript to its indefinite existence in the scholarly memory of humanity.
