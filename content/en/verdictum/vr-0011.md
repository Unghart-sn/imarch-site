### PREAMBLE

This Volume V constitutes an integral annex to the Chronicle OS Documentation System (Volumes I–IV).

Volume V consolidates provisions which:

- are present on the journal's front page;
- are used in operational activity;
- are not fixed in the core documentation;
- require normative consolidation in order to ensure the completeness of the system.

**Volume V does not amend** Volumes I–IV.

**Volume V supplements** them, ensuring the completeness of the description of the institutional model.

This Volume constitutes an official normative annex and shall be applied in conjunction with Volumes I–IV of the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

### Section 1. FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL POLICY (APC)

#### 1.1. General Provisions

Chronicle OS operates under an Open Access model following the completion of peer review.

The journal has no third-party funding and does not carry commercial advertising.

In order to maintain the platform's digital infrastructure, a fixed editorial processing charge (APC) has been established.

#### 1.2. Amount of the Editorial Processing Charge

The APC amounts to **£150 (one hundred and fifty pounds sterling)** per submitted manuscript.

The charge is levied in pounds sterling (GBP) at the exchange rate applicable at the time of payment.

#### 1.3. Purpose of the Charge

The charge is levied by the platform operator **IMARCH LTD (UK Registration No: 16791765)** exclusively to cover the costs of:

- cryptographic sealing of the manuscript (SHA-256);
- generation and registration of the DOI;
- remuneration of independent reviewers;
- maintenance of depositary and archival storage servers;
- plagiarism checking;
- editorial support of the publication process;
- ensuring the long-term availability of published materials.

#### 1.4. Non-Refundability of the Charge

**Payment of the APC does not constitute the purchase of a publication.**

The charge is made at the stage of preliminary editorial assessment in order to initiate the independent review mechanism.

If, as a result of double-blind peer review, the article is rejected (verdict *Rejected*), **the APC amount is not refunded**, as the infrastructural work of analysing, checking and reviewing the material has been performed in full.

The material in this case is packaged into a closed archive without the right of public display.

#### 1.5. Waiver Policy

Free publications are possible only in special cases:

- where grant coverage provides for the payment of the publication;
- by invitation of the editorial office (invited articles);
- by decision of the Editorial Board in exceptional cases;
- for authors from low-income countries (by agreement with the editorial office).

The waiver policy does not affect the review procedure and does not constitute a ground for the acceptance or rejection of a manuscript.

#### 1.6. Compliance with International Practice

The Chronicle OS APC policy corresponds to the generally accepted international practice of open-access scholarly journals and does not create discriminatory conditions for authors.

The amount of the charge is set at a level that ensures the coverage of operational costs without imposing an excessive commercial burden on researchers.

### Section 2. CRYPTOGRAPHIC FIXATION (SHA-256)

#### 2.1. Purpose

Chronicle OS ensures the cryptographic fixation of all materials passing through the editorial process.

The purpose of the fixation is:

- confirmation of authorial priority;
- proof of the time of registration of materials;
- ensuring the immutability of manuscripts at all stages of consideration;
- protection of the author's intellectual property;
- creation of legally significant evidence of submission.

#### 2.2. Fixation Procedure

Upon receipt of a manuscript by the editorial office:

1. The material is assigned a unique system identifier.
2. The source file undergoes cryptographic sealing with the generation of a **SHA-256 hash**.
3. A **Pre-publication Card** is published on the journal's official website, containing:
   - the title of the manuscript;
   - the abstract;
   - the keywords;
   - the SHA-256 hash lock;
   - the date of registration.
4. The full text of the article is preserved in the IMARCH LTD institutional repository in order to protect the author's intellectual property until the completion of peer review.

#### 2.3. Public Register

The SHA-256 hashes of registered manuscripts are published in the public domain.

Any interested party may verify the integrity of a publication by comparing the SHA-256 hash against the public register.

#### 2.4. Immutability

Once the SHA-256 hash has been generated, modification of the fixed file is impossible without altering the hash.

Any modification is recorded as a new version with a new hash. The version history is preserved in full.

#### 2.5. Compliance with International Standards

The SHA-256 mechanism corresponds to international practice in the cryptographic protection of scholarly publications and to the recommendations of COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) regarding the integrity and traceability of scholarly materials.

### Section 3. METHODOLOGICAL FOUNDATION

#### 3.1. Interdisciplinary Approach

The *Chronicle OS* research model is oriented towards the interdisciplinary analysis of complex systems and processes.

The journal examines scientific, social, technological and institutional phenomena as interconnected elements of a unified operational environment.

#### 3.2. Research Directions

Chronicle OS accepts for consideration works in the following directions:

- biomedical and environmental risks;
- digital infrastructure and information security;
- behavioural and institutional models;
- matters of resilience and adaptation of complex systems;
- methodology of interdisciplinary research;
- social and organisational resilience;
- knowledge management and digital archiving.

#### 3.3. Methodological Framework

The following approaches are used as the methodological foundation:

- systems analysis;
- resilience studies;
- institutional governance;
- complex modelling;
- interdisciplinary coordination.

#### 3.4. One Health Systems Engineering

The journal's approach is consistent with the principles of interdisciplinary coordination applied within the framework of the **One Health Systems Engineering** concept, which regards human, animal and ecosystem health as a unified whole.

#### 3.5. Scientific Heritage

The methodological foundation draws upon the scientific heritage of **Prof. M. G. Shcherban, MD, PhD (In Memoriam)** in the field of environmental and biomedical safety.

The heritage of Professor M. G. Shcherban constitutes the foundational basis of the biomedical and environmental safety section of the Chronicle OS journal.

### Section 4. ECOSYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (PLATAN+)

#### 4.1. Place of Chronicle OS within the Ecosystem

Chronicle OS constitutes the technological core of the full-cycle scientific-production enterprise **IMARCH Githronical**, which serves as the infrastructural foundation of the international institutional consortium **PLATAN+**.

#### 4.2. Purpose of the Ecosystem

PLATAN+ represents a new architecture for knowledge management, scholarly cooperation and the capitalisation of intellectual assets.

The ecosystem unites the functions of various scholarly services within a single institutional circuit based on Chronicle OS, ensuring the transparency of processes, digital sovereignty and the long-term resilience of data.

#### 4.3. Three Levels of the Ecosystem

**I. Integrated Scientometric Circuit**

The level of verification and management of scholarly capital:

- SHA-256 Hash Locking;
- compatibility with international standards (Crossref, ORCID, Zenodo, ROR);
- registration, support and accounting of publication activity;
- fixation of authorial priority and change history.

**II. Social and Applied Testing Ground**

The level of personnel training and practical socialisation:

- social integration programmes;
- training of specialists of the new technological order;
- involvement of participants in real knowledge management processes;
- mastering of contemporary compliance and data management instruments.

**III. Intellectual Property as a Service**

The level of capitalisation and protection of intangible assets:

- fixation and protection of intellectual rights;
- documentation and audit of the commercial value of developments;
- support of know-how and research results;
- creation of commercialisation mechanisms at the international level.

#### 4.4. Legal Status

The PLATAN+ architecture, including the logic of interaction among registries, verification mechanisms, models of institutional integration and individual elements of technological orchestration, is protected by a system of registered know-how acts and the internal registry of IMARCH LTD in accordance with the UK Trade Secrets Regulations 2018.

#### 4.5. Readiness for International Certification

The availability of a complete documentation system (Volumes I–IV) and of applied instruments (Volume V) ensures the readiness of University Chronicle Nodes for international institutional certification and inclusion in international scholarly journal indexing databases.

### Section 5. INSTITUTIONAL SUSTAINABILITY

#### 5.1. Sustainability as a Principle

Chronicle OS regards institutional sustainability as one of the foundational principles of its activity.

Sustainability is ensured through:

- a documented normative framework;
- the reproducibility of editorial procedures;
- long-term archival preservation;
- institutional memory;
- the transparency of decision-making;
- continuity of operation irrespective of personnel changes.

#### 5.2. Resilience Studies

In methodological terms, the journal supports research in the field of the resilience of complex systems (Resilience Studies).

The editorial office considers works devoted to:

- the resilience of social and organisational systems;
- adaptation to changes in the external environment;
- the long-term viability of institutions;
- the prevention of systemic risks;
- the restoration of functions following crisis events.

#### 5.3. Institutional Continuity

Chronicle OS ensures the continuity of scholarly activity irrespective of:

- changes in editorial composition;
- changes of digital platforms;
- organisational transformations;
- personnel changes in partner universities.

**The institution endures longer than its administration. Scholarly knowledge endures longer than the institution.**

#### 5.4. International Standards

Chronicle OS ensures the compliance of its activity with international academic standards, including:

- COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics);
- DOAJ recommendations;
- open science principles;
- scholarly metadata standards (Crossref, ORCID, ROR).
