<a id="introduction"></a>
### 1. Introduction

IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom, registration number 16791765) offers universities, research institutes and scientific centres a fundamentally different model for integration into the international research space — the **Institutional Academic Wing (Office-Site)** based on the fault-tolerant Chronicle OS platform.

In contrast to standard solutions, which require either full immersion in third-party platforms or the maintenance of expensive IT infrastructure, our protocol ensures **complete brand autonomy** and **simultaneous integration into global scientific traffic**.

This model offers sovereign document workflow without external paid services (Know-How Act RN-0032), cryptographic priority fixation (SHA-256) and legal protection under the UK Trade Secrets Regulations 2018 (SI 2018/597).

<a id="dilemma"></a>
### 2. The Modern University's Dilemma

The contemporary academic environment presents university leadership with a complex dilemma:

On the one hand, it is necessary to develop the unique identity of the local scientific school, preserving the autonomy and independent brand of the university.

On the other hand, isolated departmental websites and bulletins inevitably degenerate into 'digital archives' with zero visibility to the global community, incapable of providing staff with the necessary growth in Hirsch indices and citation rates in international databases (Scopus, Web of Science).

Traditional solutions — publication in third-party journals, purchase of platform licences, maintenance of in-house IT departments — do not solve the problem, but merely create new dependencies: on external platforms, on commercial form-collection services, on expensive infrastructure.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

<a id="architecture"></a>
### 3. Architectural Solution: A Two-Tier Model

The model is built on two levels that operate in a unified linkage.

<a id="local-contour"></a>
#### 🏛️ 3.1. Local Front-End Circuit

The university receives a dedicated digital space — a **subdomain circuit** (for example, `university.imarch.sbs`), fully branded under its own symbolism.

**Functions:**

- display of official branding, leadership registers and departmental listings;
- publication of full texts of scholarly works;
- full control over the internal agenda;
- display of current citation metrics through integrated widgets.

<a id="global-hub"></a>
#### 🌐 3.2. International Distribution Hub

On the central IMARCH platform (London, United Kingdom), what is published is not voluminous data arrays, but high-technology **digital teasers** — structured abstracts, keywords, conclusions, verified ORCID profiles.

**Functions:**

- publication of structured metadata;
- entry point for foreign scholars, search engine crawlers and international aggregators;
- seamless transition to the university's subdomain for access to the full version.

### 5. International Reviewing Circuit

Having an attractive website does not guarantee Scopus indexing. Metrics grow only when articles undergo rigorous, independent international audit.

**What the reviewing circuit provides:**

- Double-blind peer review through the **Expert Reviewer Network**
- Support by an international editorial board (London)
- Immaculate metadata purity for Scopus and Web of Science
- Correct indexing of DOIs and metadata
- Priority protection under the **UK Trade Secrets Regulations 2018**

<a id="document-flow"></a>
### 6. Sovereign Document Workflow

The key differentiator of our model is the **complete renunciation of external paid services** for data collection, processing and storage.

Instead of Formspree, Typeform or Jotform, the university uses a **hybrid processing circuit** (RN-0032):

- Operates on open protocols (Telegram, Google Sheets, GitHub)
- Integrates with VERDICTUM for immutable fixation of each application
- Requires no paid subscriptions or licences
- Ensures full control over data

**This is not merely economy — it is institutional independence.**

### 10. Legal Protection

All materials passing through the international IMARCH hub are protected under the **UK Trade Secrets Regulations 2018 (SI 2018/597)**.

This provides your researchers with an additional level of intellectual priority protection unavailable in standard repositories and open platforms.

**Any copying of the architecture, method or functional matrix without the written consent of IMARCH LTD constitutes a violation of the trade secret regime and is actionable under the law.**
