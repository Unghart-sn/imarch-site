### Introduction

IMARCH Chronicle OS is an international publishing ecosystem designed to support the creation, expert evaluation, publication and long-term preservation of scientific and professional knowledge.

The International Expert Reviewer Network is one of the key institutional components of this ecosystem. Its purpose is to unite qualified independent experts who contribute to maintaining the highest standards of academic quality, research integrity and editorial excellence.

This Protocol establishes the principles of participation in the Reviewer Network, defines the institutional obligations of all parties, and regulates the procedures of cooperation between IMARCH LTD and external reviewers.

Participation begins with professional registration, verification of academic qualifications and the signing of a Digital Declaration of No Conflict of Interest.

A fundamental principle of the IMARCH platform is the reduction of the administrative burden on reviewers. Initial technical review, verification of document completeness and preliminary preparation of manuscripts are performed automatically by the platform before expert evaluation commences.

Reviewers therefore receive structured materials that are fully prepared for professional assessment.

For manuscripts conforming to the platform's standardised format, the average net expert review time is expected to be up to fifteen minutes, enabling reviewers to focus exclusively on scientific quality rather than on administrative processing.

### Chapter 1. Four Frameworks of Expert Participation

The International Expert Reviewer Network operates in accordance with five institutional principles.

#### Independence

All reviewers provide independent professional assessments based exclusively on academic merit, scientific methodology and generally recognised principles of academic integrity.

#### Confidentiality

All manuscripts, editorial communications and review materials remain confidential unless their disclosure has been expressly authorised by the Editorial Board.

#### Objectivity

Expert assessments must be impartial, substantiated and free from personal, institutional, commercial or political bias.

#### Professional Responsibility

Reviewers undertake to complete accepted assignments within the agreed timeframes, observing the highest standards of analytical quality and professional ethics.

#### Institutional Trust

Each completed review contributes not only to the assessment of an individual manuscript, but also to the development of a trusted international knowledge infrastructure founded upon transparency, accountability and institutional continuity.

### Chapter 2. The Professional Rating System and the IMARCH Top-10 Charter

The International Expert Reviewer Network recognises that high-quality peer review constitutes a measurable professional contribution deserving of transparent institutional recognition.

To that end, Chronicle OS maintains a professional rating system that is continuously updated, records every completed review, evaluates the quality of expert work, and supports the long-term development of each reviewer's institutional profile.

The system has not been designed as a competitive game. Rather, it serves as a transparent mechanism for recognising sustained professional excellence, editorial reliability and scholarly responsibility.

#### Professional Experience Points (XP)

Each successfully completed review generates Professional Experience Points (XP), which contribute to the reviewer's institutional standing on the platform.

The standard allocation is as follows:

- 100 XP for each review officially accepted by the Editorial Board;
- up to 20 additional XP for early completion;
- up to 30 additional XP for exceptional analytical quality, as assessed by the Editorial Board.

If a review fails to meet editorial quality standards or is submitted after the agreed deadline without prior arrangement, XP may be reduced or annulled at the Editorial Board's discretion.

This assessment process ensures that the rating reflects not only productivity, but also professional consistency and academic integrity.

#### Monthly Institutional Rankings

Reviewer rankings are updated continuously throughout the month.

At the end of each calendar month, Chronicle OS automatically generates the IMARCH Top-10 Reviewers Charter, recognising those experts who have demonstrated exceptional dedication and professional effectiveness.

The ranking is formed exclusively on the basis of recorded institutional activity and verified editorial data.

Editorial discretion may be applied only in exceptional circumstances requiring procedural correction.

#### League of Distinguished Reviewers

The top three reviewers receive additional institutional recognition during the following assessment period.

Such recognition may include:

- an enhanced professional remuneration coefficient;
- priority access to selected publishing opportunities;
- invitations to strategic editorial consultations;
- participation in pilot institutional initiatives;
- entitlement to additional long-term cooperation programmes within the IMARCH ecosystem.

Where appropriate, distinguished reviewers may also be invited to participate in consultative discussions concerning significant institutional research initiatives.

#### League of Professional Excellence

Reviewers occupying positions four to ten are recognised for their contribution to editorial quality and institutional reliability.

Depending on current programmes, recognition may include:

- additional publication opportunities;
- institutional publication vouchers;
- support for affiliated research groups and academic departments;
- commemorative certificates and limited-edition institutional materials.

The precise composition of recognition programmes may change as the platform expands internationally.

#### Rapid Review Initiative

In exceptional circumstances, the Editorial Board may activate the Rapid Review Initiative for manuscripts requiring expedited editorial processing.

Under this procedure:

- review assignments remain available for acceptance for a limited period;
- the target completion time is within two hours of acceptance;
- the first reviewer to complete a high-quality assessment in accordance with editorial standards receives triple XP credit (×3).

Activation of the Rapid Review Initiative remains entirely at the Editorial Board's discretion and is reserved for exceptional editorial circumstances.

#### Institutional Reputation

The IMARCH rating system is designed to support long-term professional development, not short-term competition.

A reviewer's profile constitutes a record of scholarly contribution, editorial reliability and institutional trust that evolves continuously.

As verified experience accumulates within the Network, this institutional record may become a significant component of future editorial appointments, collaborative research initiatives and international academic partnerships.

Chronicle OS therefore regards each completed review not merely as an editorial assignment, but as a long-term contribution to the integrity and sustainability of the scholarly communication process.

### Chapter 3. Qualification, Verification and Professional Standards

Trust in any scholarly publishing ecosystem depends fundamentally on the quality, integrity and independence of its reviewers.

Admission to the International Expert Reviewer Network therefore proceeds through a structured verification process designed to ensure that each reviewer possesses the professional qualifications, ethical standards and institutional responsibility required for independent scholarly assessment.

Membership in the Network is regarded not as an automatic entitlement, but as a professional appointment on the basis of verified academic competence.

#### Academic Qualifications

Candidates must possess an internationally recognised academic qualification appropriate to their field of expertise.

Typically, candidates hold one of the following degrees:

- Doctor of Philosophy (PhD);
- Doctorate;
- Candidate of Sciences or an equivalent research qualification recognised within the candidate's national academic system.

Equivalent professional qualifications may also be considered where significant scholarly or institutional experience is demonstrated.

Particular interest is extended to specialists working in the following fields:

- Preventive Medicine and Lifestyle Medicine;
- Rehabilitation Sciences;
- Gerontology and Healthy Ageing;
- Biomedical Technologies;
- Systems Analysis;
- Cybernetics;
- Institutional Governance;
- Risk Management;
- Artificial Intelligence;
- Other interdisciplinary research fields corresponding to the IMARCH editorial profile.

#### Professional Reputation

Academic qualifications alone do not determine professional excellence.

The International Expert Reviewer Network therefore assesses each candidate from a broader institutional perspective, recognising that significant scholarly contribution extends beyond degrees and publication metrics.

Candidates are encouraged to demonstrate sustained engagement with the international scholarly community through recognised academic identifiers and evidence of professional activity.

Such evidence may include, where available:

- ORCID iD;
- Scopus Author ID;
- Web of Science Researcher Profile;
- Google Scholar profile;
- Publons records or equivalent review records;
- membership of editorial boards;
- participation in funded research projects;
- institutional appointments;
- evidence of prior review experience for reputable scholarly journals.

The absence of a particular identifier does not constitute an automatic ground for rejection. The Editorial Board assesses the overall academic profile rather than any single indicator.

#### Institutional Verification

Following application, each candidate undergoes a structured institutional verification process.

The purpose of this procedure is not to create administrative complexity, but to ensure the trust, transparency and long-term reliability of the Reviewer Network.

Verification typically consists of three stages.

#### Stage One: Identification and Verification of Qualifications

The Editorial Board confirms the candidate's identity and the authenticity of the declared academic qualifications and institutional affiliations. Additional supporting documents may be requested where necessary.

#### Stage Two: Professional Assessment

The Editorial Board evaluates the candidate's research background, areas of expertise, publication history and prior editorial experience. This assessment serves to determine the most appropriate disciplinary profile for future assignments, rather than to rank academic prestige.

#### Stage Three: Institutional Compliance

Successful candidates are invited to sign the Digital Reviewer Agreement together with the Declaration of No Conflict of Interest. Completion of these documents establishes the formal institutional relationship between the reviewer and IMARCH LTD. Only upon completion of this stage does the candidate become an active member of the International Expert Reviewer Network.

#### Conflict of Interest

The preservation of editorial independence is a fundamental principle of the IMARCH publishing ecosystem.

Reviewers are therefore obliged to disclose any actual, potential or apparent conflict of interest that could reasonably affect the impartiality of their assessment.

Examples include, but are not limited to:

- personal relationships with authors;
- institutional affiliation with the submitting organisation;
- direct financial interests;
- joint research conducted in the recent past;
- any circumstances that could reasonably cast doubt upon the independence of judgement.

Where a conflict of interest exists, reviewers are obliged to decline the assignment without delay, so that another reviewer may be appointed without undue postponement.

Transparency is regarded not as a weakness, but as an integral component of professional integrity.

#### Confidentiality

Every manuscript submitted for review remains confidential throughout the entire editorial process.

Reviewers undertake not to disclose, reproduce, distribute or use unpublished materials for personal, institutional or commercial purposes.

Confidentiality obligations continue to apply after the completion of the review process, unless the Editorial Board has granted written permission.

This obligation protects authors, reviewers and the integrity of scholarly communication.

#### Ethical Standards

Members of the International Expert Reviewer Network are obliged to observe the highest standards of academic ethics.

Review must always be conducted with professionalism, intellectual honesty and respect for scholarly dialogue.

Constructive criticism is encouraged.

Personal criticism is not.

Editorial recommendations must be based exclusively on scholarly merit, methodological soundness, clarity of exposition and correspondence with the journal's profile.

Reviewers need not agree with every scholarly position expressed by the author.

They must assess whether the work meets accepted standards of scholarly quality and whether it makes a substantive contribution to scholarly discussion.

#### Equal Opportunities

The Editorial Board assesses manuscripts without discrimination on the grounds of nationality, ethnicity, gender, religion, political convictions, institutional prestige or geographical location.

Similarly, reviewer appointments are made exclusively on the basis of professional competence, disciplinary expertise and demonstrated commitment to scholarly integrity.

IMARCH supports an international academic community founded upon openness, mutual respect and intellectual diversity.

### Chapter 4. Editorial Workflow and the Peer Review Life Cycle

The IMARCH publishing ecosystem is designed to ensure a transparent, consistent and accountable editorial workflow — from manuscript submission to long-term institutional preservation.

Every manuscript passes through a structured sequence of editorial stages. Each stage has its own purpose and contributes to the overall integrity of the publication process.

The workflow is designed to minimise administrative burden while ensuring that every editorial decision is fully documented.

#### Stage 1. Manuscript Submission

Authors submit manuscripts through the official IMARCH platform in the prescribed format. Upon submission, each manuscript receives an internal institutional identifier and enters the editorial processing queue.

#### Stage 2. Technical Review

Before being passed to an editor or reviewer, the manuscript undergoes an initial technical assessment. This review confirms: completeness of submission; conformity with the instructions for authors; integrity of documents; required declarations; consistency of metadata; accompanying documentation. Where appropriate, automated tools may assist editorial staff in identifying formatting deficiencies or missing documents.

#### Stage 3. Editorial Assessment

Following technical review, the Editorial Board assesses whether the manuscript corresponds to the journal's profile and satisfies minimum standards for external peer review. Editors evaluate: subject-matter relevance; scholarly maturity; methodological suitability; conformity with editorial policy. Manuscripts that fail to meet these requirements may be returned to the authors for revision before peer review.

#### Stage 4. Reviewer Appointment

Suitable reviewers are selected in accordance with their disciplinary expertise, research profile, publication history and declared competences. The Editorial Board avoids conflicts of interest while maintaining a balance of scholarly perspectives. Reviewer selection is conducted independently and remains confidential.

#### Stage 5. Independent Peer Review

Reviewers conduct an independent scholarly assessment of the manuscript. The purpose of peer review is not to endorse or reject ideas on the basis of personal opinion, but to assess conformity with accepted standards of scholarly quality, methodological rigour, intellectual originality and scholarly relevance. Reviewers are encouraged to provide constructive recommendations that assist authors in improving their work.

#### Stage 6. Author Revision

Where revisions are required, the Editorial Board transmits the reviewers' recommendations to the authors. Authors must respond to each substantive comment and submit a revised manuscript with an explanation of the changes made. Where necessary, revised manuscripts may be returned to the original reviewers for further assessment.

#### Stage 7. Editorial Decision

Following the completion of peer review and any required revisions, the Editorial Board reaches one of the following decisions: Accepted; Minor Revisions; Major Revisions; Resubmission Required; Rejected. Final decisions on publication remain with the Editorial Board. Reviewer recommendations are an important component of editorial decision-making, but are advisory rather than determinative.

#### Stage 8. Production and Publication

Accepted manuscripts proceed to production: copy-editing; language checking (where necessary); typesetting; DOI registration; preparation of publication metadata; final author approval. Upon completion of the production stages, the article is officially published within the IMARCH ecosystem.

#### Stage 9. Institutional Preservation

Publication is the beginning, not the conclusion, of a manuscript's institutional life cycle. Following publication, the article becomes part of the permanent institutional registry of IMARCH Chronicle OS. The publication record includes: DOI registration; cryptographic integrity verification; institutional archival record; editorial version history; associated peer review metadata in accordance with confidentiality requirements.

#### Principles of the Editorial Workflow

IMARCH adheres to five operational principles at every stage of the publication process:

- **Transparency:** Editorial decisions are documented, traceable and institutionally accountable.
- **Consistency:** Comparable manuscripts are processed according to identical editorial standards.
- **Efficiency:** Administrative procedures are optimised to maximise the time devoted to scholarly assessment.
- **Integrity:** Every editorial action is guided by recognised principles of research ethics and academic publishing.
- **Institutional Continuity:** Editorial records are preserved as part of IMARCH's long-term institutional memory.

### Chapter 5. Rights and Obligations

Participation in the International Expert Reviewer Network is governed by a balanced system of rights and obligations applicable to reviewers, the Editorial Board and authors.

This system ensures professional independence, editorial quality and institutional accountability of the publication process.

#### Rights of Reviewers

Reviewers within the Network have the right to:

- decline review invitations where a conflict of interest exists or where the subject lies beyond their expertise;
- receive appropriate recognition for completed reviews in accordance with the applicable participation framework;
- expect honest editorial communication and transparent procedural standards;
- maintain the confidentiality of their personal information in accordance with legislative requirements and institutional policy.

Reviewers may also offer recommendations for the improvement of editorial procedures, review methodology and publication quality.

Constructive professional feedback is regarded as a significant contribution to the further development of the IMARCH publishing ecosystem.

#### Obligations of Reviewers

Members of the Network undertake to:

- conduct reviews objectively, impartially and independently;
- assess manuscripts exclusively on the basis of scholarly merit;
- maintain strict confidentiality throughout the process;
- declare without delay any actual or potential conflict of interest;
- complete accepted assignments within the agreed time frame where possible;
- provide constructive, respectful and substantiated recommendations.

Reviewers must clearly distinguish scholarly criticism from personal opinion.

Professional disagreement is recognised as an integral component of scholarly discourse.

Personal criticism has no place in the review process.

#### Obligations of the Editorial Board

The Editorial Board undertakes to:

- select reviewers honestly and objectively;
- provide reviewers with complete editorial documentation;
- protect the independence of reviewers throughout the assessment process;
- respect the confidentiality of reviewers;
- communicate editorial decisions promptly and professionally;
- recognise reviewers' contributions through the institutional systems described in this Protocol.

Where editorial circumstances require changes to assignments or deadlines, reviewers will be notified at the earliest opportunity.

#### Obligations of Authors

Authors submitting manuscripts to IMARCH undertake to:

- submit original scholarly work;
- disclose relevant sources of funding and potential conflicts of interest;
- respond professionally to reviewers' comments;
- revise manuscripts in good faith where such revisions are requested;
- respect the independence of the review process.

Authors should regard peer review as a collaborative process aimed at enhancing the quality and clarity of scholarly communication.

#### Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions remain exclusively within the competence of the Editorial Board.

Neither reviewers, nor authors, nor institutional partners, nor commercial stakeholders may exert undue influence upon editorial judgement.

The Editorial Board is obliged to preserve the independence, impartiality and scholarly reliability of every publication decision.

Editorial independence is regarded as one of the fundamental principles of the IMARCH publishing ecosystem.

#### Mutual Respect

Effective scholarly communication depends upon professional respect among all participants.

IMARCH expects authors, reviewers, editors and institutional partners to conduct all interactions with courtesy, integrity and respect for differing scholarly perspectives.

Scholarly disagreements should promote dialogue rather than conflict.

The purpose of peer review is not to determine who is right, but to strengthen the quality of scholarly knowledge through rigorous and constructive assessment.

#### Continuous Improvement

The IMARCH publishing ecosystem is designed as an environment that constantly evolves.

Editorial procedures, reviewer guidance, technological infrastructure and publication standards may be refined over time in response to changes in scholarly publishing, international best practice and constructive feedback from the academic community.

Such improvements do not affect the fundamental principles established by this Institutional Protocol.

### Chapter 6. Professional Recognition and Long-Term Cooperation

The International Expert Reviewer Network is established not merely as a repository of independent reviewers, but as a sustainable professional community supporting the long-term development of scholarly publishing.

Participation in the Network is a continuing institutional relationship founded upon academic excellence, editorial integrity and continuous professional contribution.

Reviewers are recognised not only for individual assessments, but also for their contribution to the development of the IMARCH publishing ecosystem.

#### Institutional Recognition

Each completed review contributes to the formation of the reviewer's institutional profile.

Over time, this profile reflects not only scholarly expertise, but also professional reliability, editorial judgement and commitment to the principles of responsible academic publishing.

Institutional recognition is based upon long-term consistency rather than isolated achievements.

Reviewers who demonstrate sustained excellence may be invited to extended responsibilities within the Editorial Network.

#### Editorial Development Pathway

The IMARCH ecosystem supports a progressive model of professional development.

Depending on editorial requirements and verified experience, reviewers may be invited to additional institutional roles, in particular:

- Senior Reviewer;
- Special Issue Guest Reviewer;
- Thematic Area Coordinator;
- Guest Editor;
- Member of the Editorial Board;
- Academic Adviser to institutional programmes;
- Participant in international research initiatives coordinated by IMARCH.

Appointments are made exclusively on the basis of professional merit, editorial experience and institutional trust.

#### Institutional Partnerships

The Editorial Board recognises that reviewers frequently represent universities, research institutes, professional societies and interdisciplinary groups.

Cooperation with IMARCH may support wider institutional partnerships.

Such cooperation may include:

- thematic special issues;
- joint publication initiatives;
- reviewer training programmes;
- methodological seminars;
- international academic networking;
- institutional publication agreements.

These activities aim to strengthen scholarly communication while respecting the independence of all participants.

#### Continuous Professional Development

The landscape of scholarly publishing continues to change rapidly.

To support reviewers in maintaining the highest professional standards, IMARCH plans to develop educational resources on topics such as:

- review methodology development;
- publication ethics;
- research integrity;
- emerging editorial technologies;
- responsible application of artificial intelligence in scholarly publishing;
- international publication standards.

Participation in these activities is voluntary and intended to support professional growth rather than formal certification.

#### Institutional Contribution

Peer review is one of the least visible yet most essential forms of academic activity.

Each carefully prepared review strengthens not only an individual manuscript, but also the broader integrity of scholarly communication.

The IMARCH publishing ecosystem regards each reviewer as an active participant in the preservation, verification and transmission of scholarly knowledge.

Institutional memory is built gradually through thousands of individual acts of professional responsibility.

Each review becomes part of this continuous process.

#### Future Opportunities

As the IMARCH ecosystem expands, additional opportunities for cooperation may arise across a range of editorial, educational and research initiatives.

These opportunities may include participation in interdisciplinary projects, advisory groups, editorial innovation programmes and international institutional partnerships.

Such initiatives will be announced through official IMARCH channels.

Participation in future programmes remains entirely voluntary and based upon mutual professional interest.

#### Community of Scholars

The International Expert Reviewer Network aspires to become more than an operational review service.

Its long-term aim is to foster an international community of scholars united by shared standards of academic integrity, professional independence and constructive scholarly dialogue.

The strength of such a community is measured not by its size, but by the quality of its members and the trust they place in one another.

IMARCH seeks to contribute to this tradition through openness, responsibility and a sustained commitment to academic excellence.

### Chapter 7. Registration and Onboarding Procedure

Joining the International Expert Reviewer Network is designed to be straightforward, transparent and respectful of each candidate's professional time.

The registration procedure is designed to minimise administrative complexity while ensuring proper academic verification and institutional compliance.

#### Step 1. Application Submission

Candidates complete the official online form on the IMARCH website. Candidates are invited to provide: academic qualifications; institutional affiliation; areas of expertise; publication profiles; contact information; desired participation framework. Submission of an application does not constitute automatic admission to the Network.

#### Step 2. Editorial Verification

Following submission, the Editorial Board conducts an institutional assessment of the application. This process may include verification of: academic qualifications; publication history; editorial experience; professional identifiers; declared areas of expertise. Where necessary, the Editorial Board may contact the candidate for clarification.

#### Step 3. Institutional Compliance

Successful candidates receive institutional documentation for formal participation: the Digital Reviewer Agreement; the Declaration of No Conflict of Interest; methodological materials on editorial procedures; instructions for the reviewer portal. Participation takes effect upon completion of the required documents.

#### Step 4. Reviewer Profile Activation

Following approval, the reviewer gains access to their institutional profile within the IMARCH ecosystem. The profile serves as the primary communication interface between the Editorial Board and the reviewer throughout participation in the Network.

#### Step 5. First Review Assignment

Review invitations are issued in accordance with: subject-matter expertise; reviewer availability; absence of conflicts of interest; editorial priorities. Reviewers may accept or decline invitations in accordance with their professional availability.

#### Communication

Official communication between IMARCH and reviewers is conducted through the contact details provided at registration.

Reviewers are encouraged to keep their professional details up to date for effective editorial coordination.

The Editorial Board is always available to assist with procedural questions, technical issues and editorial guidance.

Professional communication is an integral component of an effective international reviewer community.

### Chapter 8. Legal Status and Institutional Governance

The International Expert Reviewer Network operates within the institutional system of IMARCH LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom.

The Network is an independent professional infrastructure supporting scholarly publishing through internationally recognised editorial standards, transparent governance and documented institutional procedures.

This Institutional Protocol establishes the general principles of participation in the Reviewer Network.

Specific contractual relationships are formalised through individual digital agreements between IMARCH LTD and reviewers.

#### Applicable Policies

Participation in the Network is governed by the following institutional documents (where applicable):

- the IMARCH Publishing Doctrine;
- the Publication Ethics Policy;
- the Conflict of Interest Policy;
- the Data Protection Policy;
- the Confidentiality Agreement;
- the Reviewer Agreement;
- applicable United Kingdom legislation.

In the event of discrepancies between institutional documents, the individual Reviewer Agreement prevails in contractual matters.

#### Intellectual Property

Editorial procedures, documentation, review methodology, institutional architecture and associated organisational materials constitute intellectual assets of IMARCH LTD.

No provision of this Protocol restricts reviewers in the application of their own academic expertise or professional knowledge.

However, reproduction or commercial use of proprietary institutional materials without written permission may infringe the relevant intellectual property rights.

#### Amendments

This Institutional Protocol may be updated periodically in response to:

- developments in scholarly publishing;
- changes in applicable legislation;
- advances in editorial technologies;
- institutional experience;
- recommendations from the academic community.

New revisions take effect upon official publication by IMARCH LTD.

#### Guiding Principle

This Protocol is intended to provide a stable institutional foundation while preserving the flexibility required for continuous improvement.

Its purpose is not to regulate academic thought, but to support an editorial environment in which scholarly quality, professional independence and institutional trust may flourish.

### Closing Address

Scientific progress depends not only upon discoveries.

It depends upon the willingness of scholars to examine one another's work with honesty, intellectual integrity and professional responsibility.

Peer review remains one of the most valuable forms of academic activity precisely because it is founded upon trust.

The International Expert Reviewer Network has been established to preserve and strengthen this tradition within the contemporary digital publishing environment.

In joining the Network, reviewers contribute not only to the assessment of individual manuscripts, but also to the development of a durable institutional memory capable of supporting future generations of researchers.

We welcome colleagues who share these principles and wish to participate in an international scholarly community founded upon integrity, independence and excellence.
