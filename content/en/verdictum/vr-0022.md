### What Has Been Released

A **foundational institutional monograph of IMARCH** has been published, uniting under a single cover:

- **The Manifesto of the Chronicle OS Ecosystem** — the philosophical and architectural foundation.
- **The Regulations of Academic Publishing Houses** — standards of layout, infographics, tables and banners.
- **The Standards of the Dual-Circuit Architecture** — Open Science and Secure Vault.
- **The Specifications of Trustless Digital Environments** — the Zero-Trust Evidence Layer.

The document accumulates the outcome of **six months of work** on the creation of a sovereign technological foundation.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="section-2-why-created"></a>
### Why This Has Been Created

Contemporary digital and academic infrastructure is critically vulnerable. The existing standards of publication and information storage **do not meet the requirements of Zero-Trust**.

**The purpose of the monograph's release** is to provide the market, the academic community and technological partners with a **ready-made, mathematically verified and technologically independent corpus of rules**.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="section-3-why-important"></a>
### Why This Is Critically Important

#### 1. The Threat of External Dependency

Governance infrastructure must not depend upon the stability of external cloud providers.

#### 2. The Absence of Transparent Trust

Cryptographic fixation (SHA-256) in the Verdictum registry guarantees the immutability and verifiability of every artefact.

#### 3. A Unified Zero-Trust Circuit

Academic repositories and closed circuits are united into a single Zero-Trust Evidence Layer.
