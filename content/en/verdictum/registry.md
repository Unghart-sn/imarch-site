#### Strategic Concept of the IMARCH Githronical Ecosystem

The IMARCH Githronical project constitutes a **closed-cycle scientific-production enterprise**, in which philosophy, technology, verification and production and service operate as a single system.

Six blocks:

- **Vis Realitatis™ (VR)**, a centre for renovation and social adaptation
- **Chronicle Communication System™ (CCS)**, the complete digital chronicle protocol
- **Chronicle OS™**, the international peer-reviewed scholarly journal
- **VERDICTUM™**, the scholarly-publicistic publication of the international institute for risk deconstruction and the recording of institutional actions (Risk Deconstruction &amp; Institutional Action Recording | **RDIAR**)
- **CONSILIUM™**, the production and service sector
- **COLLEGIUM™**, the B2B sector

form a closed, structurally organised circuit.

Each element reinforces the others, and the journals act not as a dead archive, but as a **conveyor of knowledge**, without which the cycle can be neither begun nor completed.
