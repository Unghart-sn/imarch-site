### PREAMBLE

The Institutional Relations Package defines the legal, organisational and institutional foundations of cooperation between Chronicle OS and universities, research organisations, research institutes and other educational institutions.

The Package comprises ten documents (INS-001 – INS-010). Each document governs a distinct aspect of institutional interaction within the international Chronicle OS ecosystem.

All documents in the Package form an integral part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and are applied in conjunction with Volume I (Institutional Codex) and Volume III (Operational Governance).

### INS-001 — UNIVERSITY PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT FRAMEWORK

**Document ID:** INS-001

**Document Type:** University Partnership Agreement Framework

**Hierarchy:** Institutional Relations Package

**Status:** MASTER TEMPLATE

**Edition:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

The Framework Agreement defines the legal, organisational and institutional foundations of cooperation between Chronicle OS and universities, research organisations, research institutes and other educational institutions.

The document establishes the general model of interaction between the parties and serves as the basis for the conclusion of subsequent specialised agreements.

#### 2. Subject of the Agreement

The subject of the Agreement is the establishment of long-term institutional cooperation in the fields of:

- scholarly publications;
- the development of academic communication;
- the operation of the University Chronicle Node (the term is used in the meaning established by the Institutional Codex, Volume I);
- the development of the international expert network;
- the deployment of Chronicle OS;
- joint research projects;
- the digital transformation of publishing activity.

#### 3. Status of the Parties

The Parties recognise one another as autonomous and independent institutions.

The Agreement does not restrict the legal autonomy of the university and does not entail any change to its organisational or legal status.

#### 4. Principle of Academic Autonomy

The university retains full academic, organisational and scholarly autonomy.

Chronicle OS provides normative, organisational and technological infrastructure without interference in the academic policy of the university.

#### 5. Subject of Cooperation

The Parties undertake joint activity in the following areas:

- the organisation of scholarly journals;
- the conduct of international peer review;
- the formation of editorial boards;
- the conduct of scholarly events;
- the preparation of international publications;
- the development of expert networks;
- the development of digital publishing infrastructure;
- the enhancement of the university's international scholarly visibility.

#### 6. Obligations of Chronicle OS

Chronicle OS provides:

- the provision of the normative framework;
- the provision of the Chronicle OS architecture;
- support during deployment;
- methodological assistance;
- the development of the international expert network;
- support for the University Chronicle Node;
- the development of international scholarly integration.

#### 7. Obligations of the University

The university provides:

- observance of the Chronicle OS normative framework;
- the organisation of a local editorial office;
- the appointment of responsible representatives;
- observance of Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004);
- support for the operation of the University Chronicle Node;
- participation in international scholarly cooperation.

#### 8. Intellectual Property

Each Party retains the rights to the intellectual property objects belonging to it.

The Agreement does not provide for the transfer of exclusive proprietary rights unless otherwise stipulated by separate agreements.

#### 9. Use of Chronicle OS

The university uses Chronicle OS within the limits of this Agreement and the applicable normative framework.

Use of the Chronicle OS architecture is carried out in compliance with the requirements of the Institutional Codex (see Volume I).

#### 10. Confidentiality

The Parties observe a regime of confidentiality in respect of information received in the course of cooperation.

The procedure for processing confidential information is determined by separate agreements and applicable legislation.

#### 11. International Cooperation

The Agreement provides for the possibility of the university's participation in international Chronicle OS projects, including joint publications, scholarly research, educational initiatives and the activity of the International Expert Network.

#### 12. Term of the Agreement

The Agreement enters into force upon signature by the authorised representatives of the Parties and remains in force until its termination in the prescribed manner.

#### 13. Termination of Cooperation

Either Party may terminate the Agreement by written notice to the other Party.

Termination of cooperation does not affect:

- materials already published;
- registered objects;
- the preservation of institutional memory (the principle is defined in Volume I);
- the validity of previously adopted editorial decisions.

#### 14. Settlement of Disagreements

The Parties resolve arising disagreements through negotiation and mutual consultation.

Where agreement cannot be reached, the procedure for dispute resolution is determined by a separate legal agreement and applicable law.

#### 15. Final Provisions

The Framework Agreement is the foundational document of the Institutional Relations Package and is applied in conjunction with:

- the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- the Deployment Framework (see Volume I);
- the Operational Standards (see Volume I);
- specialised Chronicle OS agreements.

This document forms an integral part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and is applied in conjunction with the other normative documents of the system.

### INS-002 — UNIVERSITY CHRONICLE NODE AGREEMENT

**Document ID:** INS-002

**Document Type:** University Chronicle Node Agreement

**Hierarchy:** Institutional Relations Package

**Status:** MASTER TEMPLATE

**Edition:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

The Agreement defines the procedure for the establishment, deployment and operation of the University Chronicle Node as a local institutional circuit of Chronicle OS.

The document is a specialised agreement. The Agreement is concluded following the signing of the University Partnership Agreement Framework (INS-001).

#### 2. Subject of the Agreement

The subject of the Agreement is the establishment within the university of an autonomous University Chronicle Node. The Node operates within the international Chronicle OS ecosystem.

#### 3. Status of the University Chronicle Node

The University Chronicle Node constitutes the local publishing infrastructure of the university. The Node operates in accordance with:

- the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- the Deployment Framework (see Volume I);
- the Operational Standards (see Volume I);
- the local normative documents of the university.

The Node is not an autonomous legal entity and operates within the structure of the university.

#### 4. Purpose of the University Chronicle Node

The University Chronicle Node provides:

- the organisation of scholarly journals;
- the receipt and handling of publications;
- international peer review;
- interaction with authors;
- editorial activity;
- integration with the Chronicle Registry;
- participation in the Chronicle OS International Expert Network.

#### 5. Powers of the University

The university independently determines:

- the scholarly directions of the journals;
- the composition of the editorial board;
- the internal organisation of editorial activity;
- the staffing of the University Chronicle Node;
- editorial policy within the limits of the Chronicle OS normative framework.

#### 6. Powers of Chronicle OS

Chronicle OS provides:

- the provision of the normative architecture;
- methodological support;
- the development of digital infrastructure;
- support for the international expert network;
- support for the Chronicle Registry;
- the development of international integration.

#### 7. Organisational Structure

The minimum structure of the University Chronicle Node comprises:

- the Editor-in-Chief;
- the Managing Editor;
- the Expert Network Coordinator;
- the Technical Editor;
- the Digital Infrastructure Administrator.

The university is entitled to expand the structure in accordance with its own requirements.

#### 8. International Interaction

The University Chronicle Node participates in:

- joint editorial projects;
- international thematic issues;
- international expert programmes;
- Chronicle OS projects;
- the activity of the International Expert Network.

#### 9. Use of Digital Infrastructure

The university gains access to the digital components of Chronicle OS, including:

- the Chronicle Registry;
- the document registration system;
- international metadata;
- Chronicle OS digital services;
- other infrastructure elements in accordance with applicable regulations.

#### 10. Institutional Identity

The University Chronicle Node retains the university's own institutional identity.

Use of Chronicle OS does not alter the official name, status or symbolism of the university.

#### 11. Use of Brands

Use of Chronicle OS designations is carried out in accordance with the Brand Usage Policy (INS-008).

Use of the official symbolism of the university is carried out in accordance with the internal normative documents of the university.

#### 12. Institutional Memory

All materials created by the University Chronicle Node are incorporated into the Chronicle OS institutional memory system through the Chronicle Registry.

At the same time, the university retains its own archives and internal storage systems.

#### 13. Suspension of Node Activity

Temporary suspension of the activity of the University Chronicle Node is permitted:

- at the initiative of the university;
- by mutual agreement of the Parties;
- in cases provided for by the Chronicle OS normative framework.

Suspension of activity does not affect the preservation of published materials and registration records.

#### 14. Termination of the Agreement

Termination of the Agreement does not entail:

- the deletion of publications;
- the deletion of registration records;
- the destruction of institutional memory;
- the cessation of the validity of previously published scholarly results.

#### 15. Final Provisions

The Agreement is applied in conjunction with:

- the University Partnership Agreement Framework (INS-001);
- the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- the Deployment Framework (see Volume I);
- the Operational Standards Chronicle OS (see Volume I).

This document forms an integral part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and is applied in conjunction with the other normative documents of the system.

### INS-003 — EDITORIAL BOARD CHARTER

**Document ID:** INS-003

**Document Type:** Editorial Board Charter

**Hierarchy:** Institutional Relations Package

**Status:** MASTER TEMPLATE

**Edition:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

The Charter defines the status, structure, powers, formation procedure and principles of activity of the editorial boards of Chronicle OS and University Chronicle Nodes.

The document establishes unified international requirements for the organisation of editorial governance.

#### 2. Fundamental Principle

The Editorial Board is an independent academic body. The Board exercises scholarly leadership over editorial activity.

The Editorial Board adopts decisions on the basis of scholarly criteria, Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004) and the Chronicle OS normative framework.

#### 3. Types of Editorial Boards

The following operate within Chronicle OS:

- the Chronicle OS International Editorial Board;
- the editorial boards of University Chronicle Nodes;
- special editorial boards of thematic projects;
- temporary editorial committees.

#### 4. Powers of the Editorial Board

The Editorial Board:

- determines the scholarly policy of the journal;
- approves editorial standards;
- considers strategic development issues;
- participates in the formation of the expert network;
- considers complex editorial cases;
- ensures the academic quality of publishing activity.

#### 5. Competence

The Editorial Board does not interfere in the administrative management of the university.

The Editorial Board does not exercise financial management.

The Editorial Board does not replace the activity of the editorial office.

#### 6. Composition

- Editor-in-Chief;
- scholarly editors;
- international editors;
- subject-matter editors;
- invited specialists;
- representatives of the international expert community.

#### 7. Requirements for Members of the Editorial Board

A member of the Editorial Board:

- possesses recognised scholarly qualifications;
- observes Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004);
- observes the Research Integrity Policy (see Volume I, DOC-006);
- maintains professional reputation;
- observes the requirements of Chronicle OS;
- acts independently.

#### 8. Principle of Independence

The Editorial Board exercises its activity independently of:

- administrative pressure;
- political influence;
- commercial interests;
- departmental affiliation;
- other factors capable of affecting scholarly objectivity.

#### 9. Meetings

The Editorial Board holds meetings:

- in person;
- remotely;
- in a hybrid format.

Decisions are recorded in the appropriate minutes.

#### 10. Decision-Making

- by consensus;
- by majority vote;
- by other means established by the internal regulations of the Editorial Board.

The priority is the achievement of scholarly substantiated agreement.

#### 11. Conflict of Interest

A member of the Editorial Board discloses the existence of a conflict of interest.

In the event of a conflict of interest, the member concerned does not participate in the consideration of the matter.

#### 12. International Cooperation

The editorial boards of University Chronicle Nodes interact with the Chronicle OS International Editorial Board on matters of:

- joint publications;
- international issues;
- the development of the normative framework;
- expert activity;
- the exchange of professional experience.

#### 13. Termination of Powers

The powers of a member of the Editorial Board terminate:

- upon the member's own application;
- upon the expiry of the term of office;
- by decision of the appointing body;
- in the event of violation of the Chronicle OS normative framework;
- in other cases provided for by internal documents.

#### 14. Institutional Succession

Changes in the composition of the Editorial Board do not affect the continuity of the activity of the University Chronicle Node.

All changes are recorded in the Chronicle Registry.

#### 15. Final Provisions

The Charter is applied in conjunction with:

- the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004);
- International Expert Network Operations (INS-004);
- the University Chronicle Node Agreement (INS-002);
- the Operational Standards Chronicle OS (see Volume I).

This document forms an integral part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and is applied in conjunction with the other normative documents of the system.

### INS-004 — INTERNATIONAL EXPERT NETWORK CHARTER

**Document ID:** INS-004

**Document Type:** International Expert Network Charter

**Hierarchy:** Institutional Relations Package

**Status:** MASTER TEMPLATE

**Edition:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

The Charter defines the legal status, structure, principles of operation and procedure of activity of the Chronicle OS International Network of Scholarly Experts.

#### 2. Fundamental Principle

The International Network of Scholarly Experts is a permanent professional system of academic cooperation. The Network ensures independent expert assessment of scholarly materials and the development of international scholarly communication.

#### 3. Purpose of the Network

The International Network is established for:

- the organisation of independent peer review;
- the formation of an international expert community;
- ensuring the high quality of scholarly expertise;
- the exchange of professional experience;
- the development of international scholarly cooperation;
- support for university editorial offices.

#### 4. Principle of Independence

An expert exercises their activity independently of:

- the editorial office;
- the university administration;
- the authors;
- funding organisations;
- political, commercial and other external factors.

In preparing an assessment, the expert is guided by scholarly criteria, Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004) and the Chronicle OS normative framework.

#### 5. Status of an Expert

An expert is not a staff member of Chronicle OS unless otherwise provided by a separate agreement.

Participation in the International Network is of a professional and partner nature.

#### 6. Categories of Experts

The International Network may include:

- scholarly experts;
- subject-matter experts;
- methodological experts;
- research ethics experts;
- technical experts;
- invited international specialists.

#### 7. Criteria for Inclusion

A candidate for the International Network:

- possesses verified professional qualifications;
- has a scholarly or expert reputation;
- observes Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004);
- observes the requirements of Chronicle OS;
- confirms readiness for independent expert activity.

#### 8. Rights of an Expert

- to participate in international peer review;
- to receive methodological support;
- to participate in professional development;
- to decline consideration of materials where a conflict of interest exists;
- to submit proposals for the development of the expert system.

#### 9. Obligations of an Expert

An expert is obliged:

- to observe confidentiality;
- to conduct peer review conscientiously and in a timely manner;
- to maintain independence;
- to report conflicts of interest;
- to observe the Chronicle OS normative framework.

#### 10. Expert Network Coordinator

The activity of the International Network is organised by the Chronicle OS Expert Network Coordinator.

The Coordinator:

- distributes expert assignments;
- maintains communication;
- organises the development of the expert community;
- monitors compliance with procedures.

#### 11. Development of the Expert Network

Chronicle OS provides:

- the training of new experts;
- international exchange of experience;
- the development of professional competences;
- the improvement of expert procedures.

#### 12. Termination of Participation

An expert's participation may be terminated:

- at the expert's own request;
- by mutual agreement;
- in the event of violation of Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004);
- in the event of systematic non-performance of obligations;
- in other cases provided for by the Chronicle OS normative framework.

#### 13. Institutional Memory

Information on the activity of experts is recorded in the Chronicle Registry system to the extent provided for by the privacy policy and applicable legislation.

#### 14. Final Provisions

The Charter is applied in conjunction with:

- Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004);
- the Editorial Board Charter (INS-003);
- the University Chronicle Node Agreement (INS-002);
- the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- the Operational Standards (see Volume I).

This document forms an integral part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and is applied in conjunction with the other normative documents of the system.

### INS-005 — INSTITUTIONAL MEMBERSHIP RULES

**Document ID:** INS-005

**Document Type:** Institutional Membership Rules

**Hierarchy:** Institutional Relations Package

**Status:** MASTER TEMPLATE

**Edition:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

The Rules define the categories of participants in Chronicle OS, the procedure for acquiring institutional status, and the rights, obligations and principles of participation in the development of the international Chronicle OS ecosystem.

#### 2. Fundamental Principle

Chronicle OS constitutes an open international professional community of scholarly institutions and specialists. The community is united by shared normative principles rather than by a system of administrative subordination.

Participation in Chronicle OS is founded upon cooperation, professional trust and observance of a unified normative framework.

#### 3. Categories of Participants

The following may participate in the Chronicle OS system:

- universities;
- research institutes;
- academies;
- educational organisations;
- scholarly journals;
- research laboratories;
- editorial teams;
- scholarly societies;
- individual experts;
- authors;
- editors;
- reviewers;
- technical specialists.

#### 4. Institutional Participants

Institutional participants are organisations that have officially joined Chronicle OS through the conclusion of the relevant agreements.

Institutional participants enjoy the right to establish and develop their own University Chronicle Nodes.

#### 5. Individual Participants

Individual participants are natural persons. They take part in the activity of Chronicle OS as:

- authors;
- experts;
- reviewers;
- editors;
- technical specialists.

#### 6. Acquisition of Participant Status

Participant status is acquired following:

- the submission of an application;
- completion of the prescribed review procedure;
- the adoption of the relevant decision;
- acceptance of the Chronicle OS normative framework.

#### 7. Principal Rights of Participants

Participants have the right:

- to participate in the activity of Chronicle OS;
- to use the prescribed infrastructure;
- to take part in international projects;
- to propose initiatives for the development of the system;
- to receive methodological support;
- to participate in professional development.

#### 8. Principal Obligations of Participants

Participants are obliged:

- to observe the Chronicle OS normative framework;
- to observe Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004);
- to maintain a high level of professional culture;
- to respect the academic independence of other participants;
- to promote the development of international scholarly cooperation.

#### 9. Principle of Equality

All participants in Chronicle OS enjoy equal professional opportunities irrespective of:

- state;
- language;
- organisational affiliation;
- scholarly school;
- nationality;
- other characteristics.

#### 10. Suspension of Participation

Assessment of activity is carried out according to professional criteria.

Participation may be temporarily suspended:

- at the initiative of the participant;
- by mutual agreement;
- where it is necessary to conduct a review of compliance with the normative framework;
- in other cases provided for by the internal documents of Chronicle OS.

#### 11. Termination of Participation

Participation terminates:

- upon the participant's own application;
- by agreement of the parties;
- in the event of systematic violation of the normative framework;
- in other cases provided for by applicable regulations.

Termination of participation does not affect the validity of previously published scholarly materials.

#### 12. Development of the Community

Chronicle OS regards the development of the professional community as one of its key institutional tasks.

The following are supported:

- international exchange of experience;
- joint research;
- educational initiatives;
- the development of editors;
- the development of the expert community;
- the development of early-career researchers.

#### 13. Final Provisions

The Rules are applied in conjunction with:

- the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- the University Partnership Agreement Framework (INS-001);
- the University Chronicle Node Agreement (INS-002);
- the International Expert Network Charter (INS-004);
- the Operational Standards Chronicle OS (see Volume I).

This document forms an integral part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and is applied in conjunction with the other normative documents of the system.

### INS-006 — UNIVERSITY ONBOARDING PROTOCOL

**Document ID:** INS-006

**Document Type:** University Onboarding Protocol

**Hierarchy:** Institutional Relations Package

**Status:** MASTER TEMPLATE

**Edition:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

The Protocol establishes a unified procedure for connecting a university to the Chronicle OS ecosystem and launching the University Chronicle Node.

The document is intended for practical use by universities, research institutes and the Chronicle OS administration.

#### 2. Fundamental Principle

The connection of a university is carried out according to a standardised procedure. The procedure ensures the reproducibility of deployment irrespective of country, scale of organisation or scholarly specialisation.

#### 3. Participants in the Process

The following participate in the connection procedure:

- the authorised representative of the university;
- the deployment project manager;
- the Chronicle OS coordinator;
- the future Editor-in-Chief of the University Chronicle Node;
- the digital infrastructure administrator.

#### 4. Stage 1. Preliminary Enquiry

The university submits an application of intent to join Chronicle OS.

The application specifies:

- the official name of the organisation;
- the contact person;
- scholarly directions;
- planned journals;
- preliminary objectives of cooperation.

#### 5. Stage 2. Institutional Diagnostic

An analysis of the university's readiness is conducted in the following areas:

- editorial capacity;
- expert community;
- digital infrastructure;
- international activity;
- readiness to observe the Chronicle OS normative framework.

#### 6. Stage 3. Signing of Agreements

- INS-001 University Partnership Agreement Framework;
- INS-002 University Chronicle Node Agreement;
- other necessary documents.

#### 7. Stage 4. Appointment of the Local Team

The university appoints:

- the Editor-in-Chief;
- the Managing Editor;
- the Expert Network Coordinator;
- the Technical Editor;

#### 8. Stage 5. Deployment of the Digital Circuit

- the official website of the University Chronicle Node;

- access to the Chronicle Registry;
- the metadata exchange system;
- the official communication channels.

#### 9. Stage 6. Formation of the Editorial Board

The composition of the Editorial Board is approved in accordance with INS-003 Editorial Board Charter.

#### 10. Stage 7. Formation of the Expert Network

An initial pool of reviewers and experts is created in accordance with INS-004 International Expert Network Charter.

#### 11. Stage 8. Trial Editorial Cycle

- editorial procedures;
- the peer review system;
- metadata;

### INS-007 — INSTITUTIONAL CERTIFICATION STANDARD

**Document ID:** INS-007

**Document Type:** Institutional Certification Standard

**Hierarchy:** Institutional Relations Package

**Status:** MASTER TEMPLATE

**Edition:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

The Standard establishes unified principles of institutional certification for organisations joining the Chronicle OS ecosystem.

Certification confirms the university's conformity with the requirements of the normative, organisational and technological architecture of Chronicle OS.

#### 2. Purpose of Certification

Institutional certification is directed towards:

- confirming the readiness of the University Chronicle Node for full-scale operation;
- ensuring unified international standards;
- strengthening trust among participants;
- supporting the quality of scholarly communication.

#### 3. Objects of Certification

The following may be subject to certification:

- the University Chronicle Node;
- university journals;
- editorial boards;
- digital infrastructure;
- local expert networks;
- internal editorial processes.

#### 4. Principles of Certification

Certification is founded upon the principles of:

- independence;
- transparency;
- reproducibility;
- documentation;
- professional objectivity;
- institutional trust.

#### 5. Assessment Criteria

During certification, the following are analysed:

- observance of the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- fulfilment of the Operational Standards (see Volume I);
- observance of Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004);
- the operation of the University Chronicle Node;
- the work of the Editorial Board;
- the operation of the expert network;
- the quality of digital infrastructure;
- the maintenance of the Chronicle Registry.

#### 6. Levels of Conformity

On the basis of the assessment, one of the following statuses may be established:

**Candidate Node** — the university is at the deployment stage.

**Certified University Chronicle Node** — full conformity with the requirements of Chronicle OS.

**Advanced Institutional Partner** — a high level of maturity, participation in the development of the international Chronicle OS architecture.

**Strategic Institutional Partner** — the organisation participates in the development of the normative framework, international programmes and new components of Chronicle OS.

#### 7. Certification Procedure

The procedure includes:

- the submission of an application;
- analysis of documentation;
- institutional assessment;
- preparation of the assessment report;
- the adoption of a decision;
- registration of the result in the Chronicle Registry.

#### 8. Term of Validity

Certification status remains valid indefinitely provided that the Chronicle OS normative framework is observed.

Periodic monitoring is carried out within the Institutional Compliance system.

#### 9. Suspension of Status

Status may be temporarily suspended:

- at the initiative of the university;
- in the event of a substantial change in organisational structure;
- upon the identification of serious violations of the normative framework.

#### 10. Revocation of Status

Revocation of status is permitted following an institutional review procedure.

The decision is recorded in a separate protocol.

#### 11. International Recognition

The status of Certified University Chronicle Node confirms the university's conformity with the international normative architecture of Chronicle OS.

This status may be used by the university in presenting its publishing infrastructure to international partners.

#### 12. Final Provisions

The Standard is applied in conjunction with:

- the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- the University Onboarding Protocol (INS-006);
- the Operational Compliance Manual (see Volume I);
- the Chronicle Registry Standard (see Volume I).

This document forms an integral part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and is applied in conjunction with the other normative documents of the system.

### INS-008 — BRAND AND INSTITUTIONAL IDENTITY POLICY

**Document ID:** INS-008

**Document Type:** Brand and Institutional Identity Policy

**Hierarchy:** Institutional Relations Package

**Status:** MASTER TEMPLATE

**Edition:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

The Policy defines unified principles for the use of the official name, symbolism, visual identity and institutional status of Chronicle OS by participants in the international ecosystem.

#### 2. Fundamental Principle

The Chronicle OS brand is a bearer of institutional trust.

Use of the brand signifies confirmation of conformity with the established normative requirements.

#### 3. Objects of Institutional Identity

The objects of institutional identity include:

- the name Chronicle OS;
- official symbolism;
- official logos;
- corporate style;
- digital identifiers;
- official designations of the University Chronicle Node;
- other registered elements of identity.

#### 4. Right of Use

The right to use the official identity of Chronicle OS is granted to:

- the International Editorial Office;
- certified University Chronicle Nodes;
- organisations that have concluded the relevant agreements;
- other participants within the limits of the powers granted.

#### 5. Use of University Chronicle Node

A certified university is entitled to use the designation University Chronicle Node together with its own official name.

*Example: University Chronicle Node / University Medical School*

#### 6. Restrictions

Use of the Chronicle OS brand is not permitted:

- in the absence of a valid agreement;
- in violation of the normative framework;
- in a manner that is misleading;
- in commercial advertising unrelated to the activity of Chronicle OS;
- for political purposes;
- for purposes contrary to Publication Ethics (see Volume I, DOC-004).

#### 7. Co-Branding

Joint use of the symbolism of Chronicle OS and of the university is permitted.

In doing so, both Parties retain their own institutional identity.

Co-branding does not signify the emergence of a relationship of organisational subordination.

#### 8. International Representation

Use of the Chronicle OS brand at international events is permitted within the framework of the participant's current institutional status.

#### 9. Protection of Reputation

Every participant in Chronicle OS is obliged to refrain from actions capable of causing harm to:

- the international reputation of Chronicle OS;
- academic trust;
- the reputation of partner organisations;
- the reputation of the scholarly community.

#### 10. Monitoring of Use

Chronicle OS monitors observance of this Policy.

Where violations are identified, a notice is sent to the participant proposing that the identified non-conformities be remedied.

#### 11. Suspension of the Right of Use

The right to use the official identity may be temporarily restricted:

- upon the loss of institutional status;
- in the event of substantial violations of the normative framework;
- by mutual agreement of the Parties.

#### 12. Final Provisions

The Policy is applied in conjunction with:

- the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- the Institutional Certification Standard (INS-007);
- the University Partnership Agreement Framework (INS-001);
- the Operational Compliance Manual (see Volume I).

<a id="ins-010-institutional-continuity-and-exit-protocol"></a>
### INS-010 — INSTITUTIONAL CONTINUITY AND EXIT PROTOCOL

**Document ID:** INS-010

**Document Type:** Institutional Continuity and Exit Protocol

**Hierarchy:** Institutional Relations Package

**Status:** MASTER TEMPLATE

**Edition:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose of the Document

The Protocol establishes the procedure for ensuring the institutional continuity of the activity of Chronicle OS participants in the event of a change of organisational structure, change of leadership, reorganisation, temporary suspension of activity or exit from the international ecosystem.

#### 2. Fundamental Principle

Chronicle OS proceeds from the principle that scholarly communication possesses institutional resilience. Such resilience does not depend upon individual office-holders, project leaders or the current organisational situation.

The institution endures longer than its administration.

Scholarly knowledge endures longer than the institution.

#### 3. Principle of Institutional Continuity

Any changes in the structure of the university, editorial office or University Chronicle Node do not disrupt:

- the continuity of scholarly activity;
- the accessibility of published materials;
- the functioning of editorial processes;
- the operation of the expert network;
- the preservation of institutional memory (the principle is defined in Volume I).

#### 4. Grounds for Application of the Protocol

The Protocol applies in the event of:

- a change of university leadership;
- a change of Editor-in-Chief;
- a change in the composition of the Editorial Board;
- the reorganisation of the university;
- the merger of organisations;
- the liquidation of structural units;
- voluntary exit from Chronicle OS;
- termination of cooperation.

#### 5. Principle of Preserving the Scholarly Heritage

Organisational change does not constitute a ground for:

- the deletion of published scholarly materials;
- the destruction of registration records;
- the removal of information from the Chronicle Registry;
- the violation of the citation integrity of scholarly publications.

#### 6. Change of Editorial Leadership

In the event of a change of Editor-in-Chief, a procedure of institutional transfer of powers is carried out.

The following are transferred:

- editorial documentation;
- current projects;
- the editorial archive;
- peer review records;
- access rights to the digital infrastructure;
- current international agreements.

#### 7. Reorganisation of the University Chronicle Node

In the event of a change in the structure of the university, the following are permitted:

- a change in the organisational model of the Node;
- a change in the internal structure;
- a change in the composition of the editorial bodies.

At the same time, the continuity of the institutional status is preserved.

#### 8. Voluntary Exit from Chronicle OS

The university is entitled to terminate its participation in Chronicle OS. To this end, an official notice is submitted.

The exit is carried out in a manner that precludes violation of the rights of authors, editors and other participants in scholarly communication.

#### 9. Consequences of Termination of Participation

Following the termination of participation:

- use of the status of University Chronicle Node ceases;
- the right to official use of the institutional symbolism of Chronicle OS ceases;
- previously published scholarly materials are preserved;
- registration records in the Chronicle Registry are preserved;
- the history of scholarly activity is preserved.

#### 10. Archival Status

Following the termination of participation, the University Chronicle Node may receive the status of **Archived Institutional Node**.

This status confirms that the organisation was formerly part of Chronicle OS. Records of its activity are preserved within the institutional memory of the system.

#### 11. Restoration of Participation

An organisation is entitled to undergo the connection procedure anew in accordance with the University Onboarding Protocol (INS-006).

In doing so, the previously accumulated institutional history may be restored.

#### 12. Institutional Memory

The Chronicle Registry ensures the long-term preservation of:

- registration data;
- the history of cooperation;
- editorial decisions;
- records of publishing activity;
- institutional chronology.

#### 13. Final Provisions

The Protocol is applied in conjunction with:

- the Chronicle OS Institutional Codex (see Volume I);
- the University Onboarding Protocol (INS-006);
- the Institutional Certification Standard (INS-007);
- the Chronicle Registry Standard (see Volume I);
- the Operational Standards Chronicle OS (see Volume I).

This document forms an integral part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and is applied in conjunction with the other normative documents of the system.
