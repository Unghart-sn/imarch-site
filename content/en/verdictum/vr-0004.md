### Why IMARCH?

The IMARCH ecosystem (comprising Chronicle OS, Verdictum and specialised academic registries) implements a non-custodial high-trust model for scientific publications and peer review. We are creating an independent, decentralised infrastructure for verification, indexing and long-term preservation of monographs, fundamental research and research protocols.

In order to ensure the highest standards of academic integrity, we invite leading scholars, systems analysts, industry experts and researchers to join the First Circle of Founding Reviewers of the expert community.

<a id="distinctive"></a>
### What Distinguishes This Initiative?

- **Decentralised trust:** Your expertise directly shapes the immutable registry of global knowledge.
- **Operational flexibility:** Review workflows are optimised through a dedicated Telegram bot (@IMARCH_bot), eliminating excessive bureaucratic overhead.
- **Institutional recognition:** Founding Reviewers receive verified digital certificates and priority indexing of their own scholarly works within the ecosystem.

<a id="faq"></a>
### Frequently Asked Questions

#### 1. Is an academic degree mandatory?

For the Academic Circuit, yes. For the Expert Circuit (Verdictum), demonstrated practical experience, case studies and recognised industry expertise are of key importance.

#### 2. How does the Telegram bot work?

@IMARCH_bot is your primary secure interface. It receives applications, sends notifications, tracks statuses and ensures secure data exchange.

#### 3. Is participation in every review mandatory?

No. Participation is entirely voluntary. You decide when and how many reviews you are willing to undertake.

#### 4. Is remuneration provided?

Yes. The system provides priority publication rights, expanded access to analytics and institutional credits within Verdictum and Chronicle OS projects.

#### 5. May I submit my own research for publication?

Yes. Network members enjoy priority submission rights for monographs, analytical reports and articles to the ecosystem's editorial boards.
