### 1. General Provisions

This Policy establishes the procedure for licensing, allocation of rights and terms of use of materials published in the journal **VERDICTUM: Journal of Cultural-Engineering Reconstruction and Renovation** (hereinafter — the Journal).

The Journal is published by the operator **IMARCH LTD** (Company No. 16791765), England and Wales, United Kingdom.

The legal basis is the **Founding Institutional Declaration of VERDICTUM, Act RN-0028 (VR-DECL-2026-001 of 06 July 2026)** and the **Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (CDPA 1988)** of the United Kingdom.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

### 2. Copyright

**2.1.** The Author retains **moral rights** over the work in full, including the right of authorship and the right to the integrity of the work.

**2.2.** The economic (exclusive) rights to the work **remain with the Author**. The Author grants the Journal a **non-exclusive licence** for publication, reproduction, distribution and institutional fixation of the work.

**2.3.** A non-exclusive licence means that the Author may independently use the work in any other manner not prohibited by this Policy, including placement in personal and institutional repositories, use in teaching and further processing.

**2.4.** The Author confirms that the work is **original**, does not infringe the rights of third parties, is not under consideration in other publications and has not been previously published in a volume violating the terms of this Policy.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

### 3. Licence of Publication

**3.1.** All materials published in the Journal are distributed under the **Creative Commons Attribution — NonCommercial — NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)** licence.

**3.2.** The said licence means that third parties are entitled to:

- **copy** and **distribute** the material in any medium and format;
- **cite** the material with mandatory attribution of authorship and source.

**3.3.** The said licence **prohibits**:

- **commercial use** of the material without the written consent of IMARCH LTD;
- **creation of derivative works** — processing, modification, translation, adaptation and any other alteration of content.

**3.4.** The Author retains the right to conclude separate agreements on commercial use of their work through IMARCH LTD.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

### 4. Rights of Readers and Third Parties

**4.1.** Any person is entitled to **freely** read, download and distribute published materials subject to the terms of the CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 licence.

**4.2.** When citing, the following must be indicated:

- name of the author (authors);
- title of the work;
- name of the Journal;
- number of the Verdictum Record (VR);
- licence CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

**4.3.** To obtain permission for **commercial use** or **creation of derivative works**, one must contact **info@imarch.ltd**.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

### 5. Institutional Fixation

**5.1.** Each published material is fixed in the format of a **Verdictum Record (VR)** with a cryptographic checksum SHA-256.

**5.2.** Fixation within the VR contour confirms the immutability of the fixed version of the work and the priority of the Author at the moment of publication.

**5.3.** VR fixation is not a substitute for copyright and operates in addition to it.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

### 6. Retraction

**6.1.** Retraction of a published material is possible in the following cases:

- discovery of substantial errors casting doubt on the reliability of the result;
- detection of infringement of copyright of third parties;
- detection of unfair borrowing (plagiarism);
- other circumstances recognised by the Editorial Board as substantial.

**6.2.** Upon retraction, the publication is not deleted. It is marked as **"RETRACTED"** with an indication of the date and reason for retraction.

**6.3.** The VR record of a retracted publication is preserved in the registry as a **historical fact**.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

### 7. Final Provisions

**7.1.** This Policy enters into force on **17 September 2026**.

**7.2.** All materials of the Journal previously published fall under the terms of this Policy.

**7.3.** Amendments and additions to this Policy are published in the VERDICTUM Registry and enter into force upon publication.

**7.4.** This Policy is an open normative document and is to be applied in conjunction with the Protocol for Authors (VR-0016) and the Protocol of the Expert Reviewer Network (VR-0005).
