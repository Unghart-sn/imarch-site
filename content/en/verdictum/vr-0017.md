### Preface. Chronicle OS Documentation System

The **Chronicle OS Documentation System** constitutes an extended integration monograph and a corpus of normative documents designed for the deployment of a fully-fledged university publishing node of the international institution **VERDICTUM**.

In contrast to traditional sets of local instructions, this system regards a scholarly publishing house as an autonomous institution with its own normative and legal framework, governance mechanism, expert circuit and long-term scholarly memory.

The **principal objective** of the system is the creation of a reproducible architecture enabling a university or research structure to deploy a modern scholarly publishing infrastructure without the need for years of independent design work.

**The Chronicle OS Documentation System is not merely a compilation of instructions, but the operating system of a university scholarly publishing house.**

### Volume I. Institutional Codex

**The constitutional level of the system**

The Institutional Codex constitutes the supreme normative corpus of the international scholarly communication ecosystem operating under the aegis of the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

The Codex establishes a unified institutional governance system through three integrated normative levels:

- **Level I — Constitutional Level:** 15 constitutional instruments (DOC-001 — DOC-015), defining the philosophy, legal foundation, architecture, editorial model, publication ethics and foundational principles of the Chronicle OS ecosystem.
- **Level II — Deployment Level:** Deployment frameworks, university publishing nodes, digital infrastructure, the international expert network and the scaling model.
- **Level III — Operational Level:** Operational standards (OPS-001 — OPS-010), regulating the day-to-day activity of editorial offices, nodes, the expert network, the registry and digital trust services.

#### Constitutional Instruments (Volume I)

- **DOC-001** — Institutional Publishing Doctrine
- **DOC-002** — Editorial Policy
- **DOC-003** — Editorial Board Charter
- **DOC-004** — Publication Ethics Code
- **DOC-005** — Peer Review Policy
- **DOC-006** — Research Integrity Policy
- **DOC-007** — Research Data Policy
- **DOC-008** — Digital Preservation Policy
- **DOC-009** — Metadata and Persistent Identification Policy
- **DOC-010** — Transparency Policy
- **DOC-011** — Appeals and Complaints Policy
- **DOC-012** — Quality Assurance Policy
- **DOC-013** — International Cooperation Policy
- **DOC-014** — Continuous Development Policy
- **DOC-015** — Institutional Governance Policy

All instruments included in the Institutional Codex are normative documents and are binding upon every institutional participant in the Chronicle OS ecosystem.

### Volume IV. Functional Deployment

**The practical executive circuit**

The University Chronicle Node Deployment Kit constitutes the complete institutional documentation for launching and commissioning a university publishing node within the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

<a id="applied-protocols"></a>
### Applied Protocols

In addition to the five principal volumes, the system includes applied institutional protocols regulating interaction with specific groups of participants:

#### Protocol for Reviewers (RN-0031)

- Founded upon five institutional principles (independence, confidentiality, objectivity, professional responsibility, institutional trust)
- Professional rating system and IMARCH Top 10
- Registration and onboarding procedure
- Rights and obligations of reviewers

#### Protocol for Authors (RN-0039 / VD-0017)

- Full publication lifecycle (submission → fixation → peer review → publication)
- Cryptographic priority fixation through SHA-256
- Double-blind peer review (up to 7 days)
- Targeted funding structure (£50 + £100)
- Institutional recognition (Series 8888 tokens, media support, profile)
- Departmental bundles and referral network
- Equity distribution model (10:30:30:30)
