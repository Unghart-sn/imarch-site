#### 🔹 The Essence of VERDICTUM

The platform operates with atomic objects — **Verdictum Record (VR)**. Each recorded case becomes an element of a unified knowledge base, suitable for subsequent audit and improvement of administrative processes. It is not the user that is routed — it is the user's institutional action that is routed.

#### 🔹 Key Distinction

* **Analysis of causes instead of reaction to consequences:** VERDICTUM concentrates on identifying, formalising and documenting the root causes of risk emergence.  
* **Institutional consistency:** each case becomes an element of a unified knowledge base, suitable for subsequent analysis, audit and improvement of administrative processes.  
* **Methodological continuity:** the platform ensures data comparability and uniformity of standards regardless of jurisdiction and industry.

#### 🔹 Institutional Architecture

Each case is registered within the sovereign infrastructure **CHRONICLE OS 8888™**, accompanied by digital identifiers, versioning and normative attribution. This approach ensures data integrity, transparency of change history and the possibility of their further use as elements of institutional memory.

#### 🔹 Cross-Jurisdictional Applicability

Unified methods of analysis and documentation are applicable regardless of territorial affiliation, administrative structure or industry specificity, preserving the comparability of results and the stability of standards under the legal guarantees of **IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom)**.

VERDICTUM is not intended to fight institutions. It is intended to improve the quality of institutional decisions through the formalisation of risks, the accumulation of verifiable precedents and the development of reproducible organisational memory.

## From Publication to Verification

**Knowledge acquires value when it can be verified, applied and developed.**

A scholarly publication records the result of research. But for many developments this is not enough.

A research hypothesis, an engineering solution, software code, a methodology, an architecture or an author's know-how may remain for years within a publication, expert or departmental circuit, without passing to the next stage: **verification, application and capitalisation of the result**.

**VERDICTUM creates a separate professional circuit for this.**

Here the author presents not only text, but also the very object of development: evidence, a prototype, calculations, code, test results, a methodology or another form of substantiation appropriate to the nature of the work.

### Three Participants in a Single Process

**The author** gains the opportunity to present a development in a structured form and to undergo the procedure of professional verification.

**The expert** gains a tool for the independent evaluation of a work and records the result of their expertise within the professional circuit.

**The investor, grant operator or institutional partner** receives not merely a project description, but structured material with an identified evidentiary base, expert assessment and level of readiness.

### The VERDICTUM Principle

**Proof-of-Work.**

It is not the status of the author that determines the value of a development, but what the author is able to present for verification.

A working prototype.
A reproducible experiment.
Compilable code.
A computational model.
A technical architecture.
A confirmed methodology.

The form of substantiation depends on the nature of the development.

### What Is Recorded

The results of procedures may be formalised as a **Verdictum Record (VR)** with cryptographic fixation of the version of the material.

Where necessary, the development passes through the sequence:

**technical review → expert assessment → institutional fixation.**

VERDICTUM does not replace the existing academic or professional system.

It adds to it one more layer: **a circuit in which the path from an idea to a verifiable result becomes visible and documentable.**

### Who May Participate

Authors of research and developments.
Engineers and developers.
Experts and reviewers.
Research teams.
Institutions.
Partners interested in the application and development of verified solutions.

**Entry is open. Status is confirmed by work.**

VERDICTUM operates as a professional association and verification infrastructure within the **IMARCH** ecosystem.

If you have a development that you are ready to present not only for publication, but also for professional verification, **submit an application to VERDICTUM**.

**From imitation to verification.**

  <summary>Review Rules🔻</summary>

The journal *VERDICTUM* applies a transparent system of **double-blind peer review**.

#### REGULATION AND STAGES OF EXPERT ASSESSMENT

1. **Registration and fixation:** The manuscript is assigned an ID and a cryptographic **SHA-256 hash-lock**. A Pre-publication Card is published on the website.
2. **Initial screening:** Review by the Editor-in-Chief for compliance with the profile and COPE ethics.
3. **Expert assessment:** Transfer of the anonymised text to 2 independent reviewers. Term of up to 6 weeks.
4. **Verdicts:** *Accepted* (Publication), *Revision* (Revisions required), *Rejected* (Rejected without refund of APC, transfer to a closed archive).
5. **Finality:** The decision of the Editorial Council is final.
