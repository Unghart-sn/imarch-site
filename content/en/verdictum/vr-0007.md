## PREAMBLE

The **Institutional Codex** constitutes the supreme normative corpus of the international scientific communication ecosystem operating under the **Chronicle OS Documentation System**.

The Codex establishes a unified institutional governance framework through three integrated regulatory layers.

---

## LEVEL I — CONSTITUTIONAL LAYER

**Master Regulatory Package (15 Constitutional Instruments)**

Defines the constitutional philosophy, legal foundation, institutional architecture, editorial governance model, publication ethics, research integrity framework, and the fundamental principles governing the Chronicle OS ecosystem.

---

## LEVEL II — DEPLOYMENT LAYER

**Deployment Framework (12 Institutional Modules)**

Defines the institutional deployment architecture, university publishing nodes, digital infrastructure, international expert network, interoperability framework, governance mechanisms, implementation procedures, and institutional scalability model.

---

## LEVEL III — OPERATIONAL LAYER

**Operational Standards (OPS-001 – OPS-010)**

Defines the operational procedures governing editorial offices, University Chronicle Nodes, the International Expert Network, the Chronicle Registry, digital trust services, artificial intelligence governance, metadata exchange, institutional interoperability, regulatory compliance, and the day-to-day operation of the Chronicle OS publishing ecosystem.

---

All instruments incorporated into the Institutional Codex shall be regarded as normative documents and shall be binding upon every institutional participant operating within the Chronicle OS ecosystem.

The Institutional Codex serves as the single authoritative regulatory source governing the establishment, operation, governance, maintenance, and continuous development of the international publishing infrastructure of Chronicle OS.

Local regulations, institutional statutes, and operational procedures adopted by individual **University Chronicle Nodes** shall remain fully compatible with, and shall not conflict with, the constitutional provisions established by this Codex.

# DOC-001

## INSTITUTIONAL PUBLISHING DOCTRINE

### Foundational Constitutional Instrument

**Document Identifier:** DOC-001

**Document Classification:** Foundational Constitutional Instrument

**Document Layer:** Constitutional Layer

**Edition Status:** Master Edition

**Language Version:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. General Provisions

The **Institutional Publishing Doctrine** establishes the constitutional principles governing the organisation, operation, governance, and long-term development of the international scientific communication ecosystem operating under the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

The Doctrine constitutes the supreme constitutional instrument of the Chronicle OS regulatory framework. It defines the unified principles of institutional governance, editorial activity, scientific communication, publication policy, and sustainable institutional development.

All internal regulatory instruments, policies, procedures, operational standards, and technical specifications adopted within Chronicle OS shall derive their authority from this Doctrine and shall remain fully consistent with its provisions.

---

## 2. Institutional Status of Chronicle OS

Chronicle OS is an international institutional scientific communication ecosystem designed to support the complete life cycle of scholarly publication through an integrated framework of constitutional governance, editorial regulation, organisational procedures, and digital infrastructure.

The scientific journal constitutes the first operational implementation of Chronicle OS and functions in full compliance with the principles established by this Doctrine.

Chronicle OS shall not be regarded merely as a scientific journal or publishing platform. It represents a comprehensive institutional framework intended to establish a sustainable international infrastructure for scholarly communication.

---

## 3. Mission

The mission of Chronicle OS is to establish an open, sustainable, resilient, and institutionally reliable environment for international scientific communication.

The ecosystem is founded upon the principles of research integrity, procedural transparency, editorial independence, institutional responsibility, and respect for intellectual contribution.

Chronicle OS exists to facilitate the continuous advancement of scientific knowledge independently of political, governmental, commercial, ideological, or other external influences.

---

## 4. Scope of Institutional Activity

Chronicle OS shall undertake activities including, but not limited to:

- organisation and administration of scientific peer review;
- editorial preparation and publication management;
- facilitation of international scientific communication;
- establishment and development of expert communities;
- long-term preservation of scientific heritage;
- development of the institutional regulatory framework governing scholarly publishing;
- implementation of digital technologies supporting scientific publication and research communication.

---

## 5. Fundamental Principles

The activities of Chronicle OS shall be governed by the following constitutional principles:

- legality;
- academic freedom;
- research integrity;
- editorial independence;
- institutional accountability;
- procedural transparency;
- impartial expert evaluation;
- equality of authors;
- protection of intellectual property;
- long-term preservation of scientific knowledge;
- international cooperation;
- continuous institutional development.

---

## 6. Principle of Institutional Continuity

Every regulatory instrument adopted within Chronicle OS shall derive from, implement, and further specify the constitutional provisions established by this Doctrine.

In the event of inconsistency between regulatory instruments, the provisions of the Institutional Publishing Doctrine shall prevail.

---

## 7. Principle of Transparency

Chronicle OS shall maintain the highest practicable level of transparency throughout its editorial, organisational, and governance procedures.

Confidentiality shall apply only where required by publication ethics, applicable legislation, contractual obligations, or the integrity of the peer-review process.

---

## 8. Institutional Memory

Institutional Memory comprises the complete body of constitutional instruments, regulatory documents, editorial decisions, scholarly publications, metadata, archival records, and all associated institutional knowledge generated within Chronicle OS.

Institutional Memory shall ensure the continuity, integrity, and sustainability of the ecosystem irrespective of changes in personnel, governance structures, technological platforms, or organisational arrangements.

---

## 9. Structure of the Regulatory Framework

The Chronicle OS regulatory framework shall operate according to a hierarchical constitutional model.

The Institutional Publishing Doctrine constitutes the highest regulatory authority.

Pursuant to this Doctrine, Chronicle OS shall adopt:

- Editorial Policies;
- Codes of Publication Ethics;
- Procedural Regulations;
- Institutional Protocols;
- Operational Standards;
- Technical Specifications;
- Administrative Forms.

Each successive regulatory level shall elaborate and implement the constitutional provisions established at the preceding level.

---

## 10. International Cooperation

Chronicle OS shall actively promote cooperation with universities, research institutions, scientific organisations, professional associations, learned societies, and independent researchers worldwide.

Such cooperation shall be conducted exclusively in accordance with the constitutional principles established by this Doctrine.

---

## 11. Institutional Development

Chronicle OS shall evolve as a long-term international institutional ecosystem.

Technological solutions, digital services, software platforms, artificial intelligence systems, and user interfaces constitute implementation mechanisms of this Doctrine and may evolve without altering its constitutional foundations.

---

## 12. Final Provisions

This Doctrine shall enter into force upon formal adoption in accordance with the constitutional procedures established within Chronicle OS and shall remain in force until superseded by a duly adopted revised edition.

Amendments to this Doctrine shall be adopted exclusively in accordance with the constitutional procedures established by the Chronicle OS regulatory framework.

This Doctrine forms an integral component of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and shall be interpreted and applied together with all other constitutional and regulatory instruments comprising the system.

# DOC-002

## EDITORIAL POLICY

### Constitutional Editorial Governance Instrument

**Document Identifier:** DOC-002

**Document Classification:** Editorial Policy

**Document Layer:** Constitutional Layer

**Edition Status:** Master Edition

**Language Version:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This Editorial Policy establishes the constitutional principles governing the editorial activities of the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

The Policy defines the framework regulating the interaction between the Editorial Office, Authors, Reviewers, Members of the Editorial Board, and all other participants engaged in the scholarly publishing process.

This Editorial Policy is adopted pursuant to the Institutional Publishing Doctrine of Chronicle OS and shall be binding upon every participant involved in the editorial process.

---

## 2. Editorial Mission

The Editorial Office shall ensure the highest standards of scholarly quality, impartial scientific assessment, research integrity, and the sustainable development of international scientific communication.

Each publication shall be regarded as a substantive contribution to the advancement of global scientific knowledge.

---

## 3. Fundamental Editorial Principles

Editorial activities within Chronicle OS shall be governed by the following constitutional principles:

- scientific independence;
- editorial autonomy;
- research integrity;
- impartial manuscript evaluation;
- non-discrimination;
- equality of authors;
- procedural transparency;
- compliance with publication ethics;
- respect for intellectual property;
- institutional accountability.

---

## 4. Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions shall be based exclusively upon the scientific merit, originality, methodological quality, and scholarly significance of submitted manuscripts.

The Editorial Office shall remain free from commercial, political, governmental, religious, national, corporate, ideological, or any other external influence unrelated to the scientific content of the publication.

---

## 5. Editorial Authority

The Editorial Office shall have the authority to:

- receive and register manuscript submissions;
- assign manuscripts for scientific peer review;
- return manuscripts to authors for revision;
- reject submissions that fail to satisfy the editorial or scientific requirements of the Journal;
- retract publications in accordance with the Code of Publication Ethics;
- publish corrections, amendments, editorial notices, and official statements;
- establish and manage special issues and thematic editions.

---

## 6. Editorial Responsibilities

The Editorial Office shall:

- ensure the impartiality of scientific evaluation;
- preserve the confidentiality of the editorial process;
- guarantee equal treatment of all authors;
- prevent and manage conflicts of interest;
- comply fully with the Code of Publication Ethics;
- safeguard all publication materials and associated records;
- ensure the long-term accessibility and preservation of published research.

---

## 7. Relations with Authors

The Editorial Office shall ensure:

- professional, respectful, and impartial communication with authors;
- timely notification regarding the progress of manuscript evaluation;
- the opportunity for authors to revise manuscripts in response to reviewer recommendations;
- the right of authors to submit reasoned responses to editorial observations;
- consideration of complaints and appeals in accordance with the established institutional procedures.

---

## 8. Relations with Reviewers

The Editorial Office shall organise scientific peer review in accordance with the approved Peer Review Policy.

The Editorial Office shall guarantee both the independence of reviewers and the confidentiality of the peer-review process.

---

## 9. International Cooperation

The Editorial Office shall actively promote the development of an international scholarly community by fostering cooperation among universities, research institutions, scientific organisations, professional associations, and independent researchers.

---

## 10. Conflict of Interest

Every participant engaged in the editorial process shall disclose any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest.

Where a conflict of interest exists, the Editorial Office shall implement appropriate measures to preserve the impartiality, integrity, and credibility of the editorial decision-making process.

---

## 11. Presumption of Good Faith

The Editorial Office shall operate on the presumption that Authors, Reviewers, Editors, and Members of the Editorial Board act in good faith and in accordance with accepted standards of scholarly integrity.

Allegations of research misconduct or breaches of publication ethics shall be investigated exclusively on the basis of objective evidence and in accordance with formally established institutional procedures.

---

## 12. Development of Editorial Policy

This Editorial Policy shall be subject to periodic review and continuous improvement.

Its development shall take into account:

- the evolution of international scholarly publishing practices;
- recommendations issued by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE);
- internationally recognised academic standards;
- accumulated institutional experience within Chronicle OS.

---

## 13. Final Provisions

This Editorial Policy shall be interpreted and applied together with the Institutional Publishing Doctrine, the Code of Publication Ethics, the Peer Review Policy, and all other constitutional and regulatory instruments forming part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

This document constitutes an integral component of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and shall be applied in conjunction with the complete body of constitutional, regulatory, operational, and procedural instruments governing the Chronicle OS ecosystem.

# DOC-003

## EDITORIAL BOARD CHARTER

### Constitutional Editorial Governance Instrument

**Document Identifier:** DOC-003

**Document Classification:** Editorial Board Charter

**Document Layer:** Constitutional Layer

**Edition Status:** Master Edition

**Language Version:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This Charter establishes the legal status, institutional composition, authority, appointment procedures, and operational principles governing the Editorial Board of the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

The Charter is adopted pursuant to the Institutional Publishing Doctrine of Chronicle OS and shall be binding upon every Member of the Editorial Board.

---

## 2. Institutional Status of the Editorial Board

The Editorial Board shall constitute the permanent collegiate governing body responsible for the editorial governance of Chronicle OS.

The Editorial Board shall ensure the implementation of the Institutional Publishing Doctrine, the Editorial Policy, the strategic development of the Journal, and compliance with internationally recognised standards of scholarly publishing.

---

## 3. Functions and Objectives

The Editorial Board shall be responsible for:

- establishing editorial policy;
- defining the scientific development strategy of Chronicle OS;
- safeguarding the quality of published research;
- fostering the international scholarly community;
- developing the international network of reviewers and subject-matter experts;
- considering matters of fundamental editorial importance;
- approving internal constitutional and regulatory instruments.

---

## 4. Principles of Operation

The Editorial Board shall conduct its activities in accordance with the following principles:

- academic independence;
- scientific objectivity;
- collective governance;
- transparency of decision-making;
- institutional accountability;
- respect for academic freedom;
- compliance with publication ethics.

---

## 5. Composition of the Editorial Board

The Editorial Board may include:

- the Editor-in-Chief;
- Deputy Editors-in-Chief;
- Managing Editors;
- Scientific Editors;
- International Editors;
- invited experts;
- representatives of universities and research institutions;
- other specialists appointed by decision of the Editorial Board.

---

## 6. Editor-in-Chief

The Editor-in-Chief shall:

- provide overall strategic leadership of Chronicle OS;
- represent Chronicle OS before external organisations and institutions;
- approve final editorial decisions;
- convene and chair meetings of the Editorial Board;
- execute official institutional documents;
- ensure compliance with the Institutional Publishing Doctrine.

---

## 7. Authority of the Editorial Board

The Editorial Board shall have the authority to:

- determine the strategic development of Chronicle OS;
- approve editorial regulations and institutional policies;
- establish special issues and thematic editions;
- constitute international advisory and expert panels;
- appoint new members of the Editorial Board;
- resolve significant editorial disputes;
- initiate amendments to the Chronicle OS regulatory framework.

---

## 8. Rights of Members

Each Member of the Editorial Board shall have the right to:

- participate in deliberations concerning editorial governance;
- submit proposals relating to the development of Chronicle OS;
- receive all information necessary for the proper performance of editorial duties;
- participate in collective decision-making;
- record a reasoned dissenting opinion;
- resign voluntarily from membership of the Editorial Board.

---

## 9. Responsibilities of Members

Members of the Editorial Board shall:

- comply with the Institutional Publishing Doctrine;
- comply with the Code of Publication Ethics;
- maintain confidentiality where required;
- disclose and prevent conflicts of interest;
- uphold the highest standards of scientific quality within Chronicle OS;
- act exclusively in the best interests of the institutional development of the system.

---

## 10. Appointment and Termination of Membership

Members of the Editorial Board shall be appointed by decision of the Editor-in-Chief or, where applicable, by resolution of the Editorial Board in accordance with the internal constitutional procedures of Chronicle OS.

Membership may be terminated on the following grounds:

- voluntary resignation;
- expiration of the appointed term;
- inability to perform editorial duties;
- violation of the Code of Publication Ethics;
- conduct materially damaging to the reputation or institutional integrity of Chronicle OS.

---

## 11. Meetings

The Editorial Board shall meet whenever required for the effective governance of Chronicle OS.

Meetings may be conducted in the following formats:

- in-person meetings;
- remote meetings;
- hybrid meetings;
- electronic voting procedures.

---

## 12. Decision-Making Procedures

The Editorial Board shall seek to adopt decisions through professional consensus.

Where consensus cannot reasonably be achieved, decisions shall be adopted by a simple majority of Members participating in the meeting unless otherwise provided by the constitutional instruments of Chronicle OS.

---

## 13. International Character of the Editorial Board

The Editorial Board shall be constituted in accordance with the principles of international scholarly cooperation.

Appointments shall be based upon:

- academic qualifications;
- professional reputation;
- editorial experience;
- recognised international scholarly expertise.

Nationality, citizenship, political affiliation, or institutional origin shall not constitute criteria for appointment.

---

## 14. Institutional Independence

Members of the Editorial Board shall perform their responsibilities independently of political, governmental, commercial, administrative, ideological, or any other external influence.

Editorial decisions shall be based exclusively upon scientific and academic considerations.

---

## 15. Final Provisions

This Charter shall be interpreted and applied together with the Institutional Publishing Doctrine, the Editorial Policy, the Code of Publication Ethics, the Peer Review Policy, and all other constitutional and regulatory instruments forming part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

This document constitutes an integral component of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and shall be applied in conjunction with the complete body of constitutional, regulatory, operational, and procedural instruments governing the Chronicle OS ecosystem.

# DOC-004

## CODE OF PUBLICATION ETHICS

### Constitutional Ethical Governance Instrument

**Document Identifier:** DOC-004

**Document Classification:** Code of Publication Ethics

**Document Layer:** Constitutional Layer

**Edition Status:** Master Edition

**Language Version:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This Code establishes the mandatory standards of publication ethics governing the Chronicle OS Documentation System and defines the rights, responsibilities, and ethical obligations of every participant engaged in the scholarly publishing process.

The Code is adopted pursuant to the Institutional Publishing Doctrine of Chronicle OS and is developed in accordance with internationally recognised principles of academic publishing, including the recommendations of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).

---

## 2. Mission

The purpose of this Code is to preserve trust in scholarly publication through uniform ethical standards, transparent procedures, impartial editorial governance, and fair scientific assessment.

Chronicle OS recognises publication ethics as one of the constitutional foundations of institutional trust.

---

## 3. Fundamental Ethical Principles

Publishing activities within Chronicle OS shall be governed by the following principles:

- research integrity;
- scientific objectivity;
- editorial independence;
- respect for intellectual property;
- openness of scientific discourse;
- procedural transparency;
- institutional accountability;
- equality and non-discrimination;
- compliance with internationally recognised academic standards.

---

## 4. Responsibilities of Authors

Authors shall:

- ensure the accuracy and reliability of all submitted data;
- provide complete and accurate references to all sources used;
- disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest;
- promptly notify the Editorial Office of any material errors identified after submission or publication;
- comply fully with the provisions of this Code.

Authors shall not:

- submit knowingly false or fabricated research results;
- engage in plagiarism in any form;
- submit duplicate or redundant publications without prior disclosure;
- manipulate authorship or artificially expand the list of authors;
- conceal material information affecting the scientific integrity of the research.

---

## 5. Responsibilities of the Editorial Office

The Editorial Office shall:

- ensure impartial evaluation of all submissions;
- preserve the confidentiality of the editorial process;
- prevent and appropriately manage conflicts of interest;
- base editorial decisions exclusively upon scientific merit;
- investigate all reports concerning potential breaches of publication ethics;
- publish corrections, amendments, expressions of concern, or retractions where appropriate.

The Editorial Office shall not discriminate on the basis of nationality, citizenship, language, gender, age, ethnicity, religion, political opinion, institutional affiliation, or any other status unrelated to scientific merit.

---

## 6. Responsibilities of Reviewers

Reviewers shall:

- preserve the confidentiality of all materials received for review;
- conduct independent, objective, and evidence-based scientific evaluations;
- disclose and avoid conflicts of interest;
- provide constructive, reasoned, and professionally respectful assessments;
- treat authors with fairness and professional courtesy;
- immediately notify the Editorial Office of any indication of research misconduct or publication malpractice.

---

## 7. Conflict of Interest

Every participant in the publication process shall disclose any personal, professional, financial, institutional, or other interest capable of influencing impartial judgement.

Where a conflict of interest is identified, the Editorial Office shall implement appropriate measures to preserve the integrity and impartiality of the editorial process.

---

## 8. Research Integrity

Chronicle OS maintains a policy of zero tolerance toward:

- plagiarism;
- fabrication of research data;
- falsification of scientific results;
- manipulation of images or research materials;
- fraudulent authorship;
- citation manipulation;
- any other form of academic misconduct.

Every allegation shall be examined individually in accordance with the principles of procedural fairness, impartiality, proportionality, and due process.

---

## 9. Presumption of Good Faith

Chronicle OS operates on the constitutional presumption that Authors, Reviewers, Editors, and Members of the Editorial Board act in good faith and in accordance with accepted standards of scholarly integrity.

An allegation of publication misconduct shall not, in itself, constitute evidence of wrongdoing.

Every allegation shall be subject to objective verification before any institutional decision is adopted.

---

## 10. Corrections and Retractions

Where significant errors or violations of publication ethics are identified, the Editorial Office may:

- publish corrections;
- publish explanatory notices;
- issue official editorial statements;
- retract publications;
- publish formal notices of retraction.

Every decision shall be adopted only after careful consideration of all relevant facts and circumstances.

---

## 11. Investigation of Ethical Misconduct

Every report alleging a breach of publication ethics shall be investigated in accordance with formally established institutional procedures.

The investigation shall guarantee:

- impartiality;
- equality of all parties;
- the right to provide explanations and supporting evidence;
- complete procedural documentation;
- confidentiality until a final institutional decision has been reached.

---

## 12. Institutional Responsibility

Chronicle OS recognises compliance with publication ethics as a collective institutional responsibility.

Responsibility for the implementation of this Code shall be shared by:

- the Editorial Office;
- the Editorial Board;
- Authors;
- Reviewers;
- invited experts;
- every other participant involved in the scholarly publishing process.

---

## 13. International Standards

Chronicle OS shall ensure that its editorial practices remain consistent with:

- the recommendations of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE);
- the principles of Open Science;
- internationally recognised standards of scholarly publishing;
- evolving international best practices in research integrity.

Where international standards evolve, this Code may be revised without altering the constitutional principles established by the Institutional Publishing Doctrine.

---

## 14. Final Provisions

This Code shall be binding upon every participant engaged in the Chronicle OS scholarly publishing process.

# DOC-005

## PEER REVIEW POLICY

### Constitutional Scientific Review Instrument

**Document Identifier:** DOC-005

**Document Classification:** Peer Review Policy

**Document Layer:** Constitutional Layer

**Edition Status:** Master Edition

**Language Version:** Master English Edition

**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This Policy establishes the objectives, principles, procedures, and institutional framework governing the scientific peer review of manuscripts submitted for publication within the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

This Policy shall be binding upon the Editorial Office, the Editorial Board, Reviewers, Authors, and all other participants involved in the scholarly publishing process.

---

## 2. Purpose of Peer Review

Peer review safeguards the scientific quality of published research through independent expert assessment of:

- scientific validity;
- methodological soundness;
- reliability of results;
- originality of contribution;
- compliance with internationally recognised standards of scholarly publishing.

Peer review constitutes the principal institutional mechanism through which confidence in scientific publication is established and maintained.

---

## 3. Fundamental Principles

Scientific peer review within Chronicle OS shall be conducted in accordance with the following principles:

- reviewer independence;
- scientific objectivity;
- professional competence;
- confidentiality;
- absence of conflicts of interest;
- professional respect toward authors;
- evidence-based and reasoned evaluation.

---

## 4. Review Model

Chronicle OS adopts **Double-Blind Peer Review** as its standard review model, whereby neither Authors nor Reviewers are informed of each other's identity throughout the evaluation process.

Where justified by the nature of a submission, disciplinary practice, or internationally recognised editorial standards, the Editorial Board may approve an alternative model of scholarly evaluation.

---

## 5. Appointment of Reviewers

Reviewers shall be appointed by the Editorial Office on the basis of:

- academic qualifications;
- demonstrated research experience;
- professional reputation;
- absence of conflicts of interest;
- recognised expertise directly relevant to the submitted manuscript.

The Editorial Office may appoint one or more independent Reviewers for any submission.

---

## 6. Rights of Reviewers

Reviewers shall have the right to:

- decline a review assignment;
- request additional materials where necessary;
- disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest;
- submit an independent or dissenting scientific opinion;
- recommend the appointment of an additional expert reviewer.

---

## 7. Responsibilities of Reviewers

Reviewers shall:

- preserve the confidentiality of all submitted materials;
- conduct an independent and objective scientific evaluation;
- complete the review within the agreed timeframe;
- provide clear, reasoned, and evidence-based comments;
- refrain from personal criticism of Authors;
- immediately inform the Editorial Office of any indication of plagiarism, fabricated data, falsified results, duplicate publication, or any other form of publication misconduct.

---

## 8. Evaluation Criteria

During peer review, Reviewers shall assess, where applicable:

- originality and scientific novelty;
- relevance of the research;
- methodological validity;
- reliability of findings;
- logical consistency of presentation;
- quality of scientific reasoning;
- accuracy of citations and references;
- relevance to the editorial scope of Chronicle OS.

---

## 9. Review Recommendations

Upon completion of the review, the Reviewer may recommend that the manuscript be:

- accepted without revision;
- accepted subject to minor revisions;
- returned for major revisions;
- subjected to additional peer review;
- rejected.

The final editorial decision shall remain the exclusive responsibility of the Editorial Office.

---

## 10. Secondary Review

Where substantial revisions have been made, the manuscript may be returned to:

- the original Reviewer;
- a newly appointed independent Reviewer;
- multiple Reviewers simultaneously.

The necessity for secondary review shall be determined by the Editorial Office.

---

## 11. Confidentiality

All materials received during peer review shall remain strictly confidential.

Reviewers shall not disclose, reproduce, distribute, cite, or otherwise use unpublished material for personal, academic, institutional, or commercial purposes prior to its official publication.

---

## 12. Conflict of Interest

Any Reviewer whose impartiality may reasonably be compromised by personal, professional, institutional, financial, or other interests shall immediately notify the Editorial Office and decline participation in the review process.

The Editorial Office shall appoint a replacement Reviewer without prejudice to the integrity or continuity of the editorial process.

---

## 13. Presumption of Good Faith

Chronicle OS operates on the constitutional presumption that both Authors and Reviewers participate honestly and in good faith.

Scientific evaluation shall be based exclusively upon the academic content of the submitted work and shall never be influenced by assumptions concerning the identity, affiliation, nationality, reputation, or personal characteristics of the Author.

---

## 14. Independence of Scientific Assessment

The Editorial Office shall guarantee the complete independence of peer review.

Administrative, commercial, political, institutional, ideological, or other external influences shall not affect the substance or outcome of any expert assessment.

---

## 15. Appeals

Authors shall have the right to submit a reasoned appeal against the outcome of peer review.

Appeals shall be considered in accordance with the constitutional procedures established by Chronicle OS.

Where appropriate, the Editorial Office may appoint one or more additional independent Reviewers.

---

## 16. International Standards

Chronicle OS conducts peer review in accordance with internationally recognised principles of scholarly publishing, including the recommendations of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and other accepted standards of international academic practice.

The Editorial Office shall continuously review and improve peer review procedures to reflect developments in global scholarly communication.

---

## 17. Final Provisions

This Policy shall be interpreted and applied together with the Institutional Publishing Doctrine, the Editorial Policy, the Code of Publication Ethics, and all other constitutional and regulatory instruments forming part of the Chronicle OS Documentation System.

Every participant in the scholarly publishing process shall comply fully with the provisions of this Policy.

This document constitutes an integral component of the Chronicle OS Documentation System and shall be applied together with the complete constitutional, regulatory, operational, and procedural framework governing the Chronicle OS ecosystem.
