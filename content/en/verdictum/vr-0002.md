<a id="preamble"></a>
### I. PREAMBLE

Founding Institutional Declaration of the International Institutional Registry VERDICTUM.

This Declaration establishes the institutional, methodological and organisational foundations for the functioning of the International Institutional Registry VERDICTUM.

VERDICTUM is established as a foundational institution of the Public Knowledge Infrastructure (PKI), providing a structured environment for the registration, verification, preservation and institutional use of documented public knowledge.

This Declaration sets forth the purpose, institutional principles, governance model and architectural foundations in accordance with which VERDICTUM operates and develops.

The provisions of this Declaration constitute the highest level of the Registry's internal normative system and serve as the foundation for the development of all subsequent architectural, methodological, editorial and technological specifications.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="institutional-status"></a>
### II. INSTITUTIONAL STATUS OF VERDICTUM

VERDICTUM is established as an independent institutional registry operating within the Public Knowledge Infrastructure (PKI).

The Registry is not a mass media outlet, a scientific periodical, a social media platform, a crowdfunding service, or a commercial information system.

Its institutional purpose is fundamentally distinct from the functions traditionally performed by such entities.

VERDICTUM provides a permanent institutional environment for the registration, verification, preservation and structured use of documented events, evidence, decisions and institutional records.

The Registry fulfils the function of institutional memory by maintaining a continuously evolving system of documented objects, the integrity, traceability and evidentiary value of which are preserved throughout their entire life cycle.

Thus, VERDICTUM is to be understood as an institutional infrastructure, not as a publication platform.

Its primary function is not the dissemination of information, but the formation of verified institutional knowledge capable of serving as a basis for responsible decision-making in the public, scientific, governmental and commercial spheres.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="mission"></a>
### III. MISSION AND PUBLIC PURPOSE

The mission of VERDICTUM is to foster a trusted institutional environment in which documented public knowledge is created, preserved, verified and used as a reliable foundation for responsible decision-making.

VERDICTUM proceeds from the premise that the long-term sustainability of public institutions depends not only on the accessibility of information, but above all on the existence of documented evidence whose provenance, integrity and institutional context can be verified independently of time.

Accordingly, the Registry is established to reduce uncertainty in public, scientific, commercial and civic activity by introducing an institutional mechanism through which documented events acquire continuity, traceability and enduring evidentiary value.

VERDICTUM does not aim to replace existing information systems, scientific publications, governmental registries or commercial databases.

Its purpose is to complement such systems by creating a common evidentiary layer on which independently documented facts, institutional obligations and validated outcomes are joined into a unified system of institutional memory that evolves continuously.

Thus, the public value of VERDICTUM lies not in creating an additional information flow, but in enhancing the reliability, accountability and long-term fitness of documented knowledge within the Public Knowledge Infrastructure.

<a id="principles"></a>
### IV. FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES

The institutional functioning of VERDICTUM is based on an integrated system of foundational principles that govern the creation, verification, preservation and institutional use of documented knowledge within the Public Knowledge Infrastructure.

These principles constitute the normative basis in accordance with which all organisational, methodological and technological components of the Registry operate.

No architectural decision, software implementation or administrative procedure may contravene the principles established by this Declaration.

<a id="principle-integrity"></a>
#### 4.1. Principle of Institutional Integrity

Each documented object retains its institutional identity throughout its entire life cycle.

Its provenance, evidentiary basis, institutional context and documented interconnections must remain traceable irrespective of subsequent technological, organisational or procedural changes.

Institutional integrity is a mandatory condition for the recognition of any object within VERDICTUM.

<a id="principle-verifiability"></a>
#### 4.2. Principle of Verifiability

Every institutional assertion, documented event or registered decision must possess a verifiable evidentiary basis.

Verification is carried out on the basis of documented sources, transparent provenance and reproducible institutional procedures.

Assertions that cannot be independently verified do not acquire institutional status within the Registry.

<a id="principle-accountability"></a>
#### 4.3. Principle of Accountability

Every documented action is accompanied by institutional responsibility.

The Registry preserves not only documented outcomes, but also the institutional accountability associated with their creation, verification and subsequent use.

Thus, responsibility is an integral component of any documented institutional activity.

<a id="principle-continuity"></a>
#### 4.4. Principle of Institutional Continuity

Documented knowledge is understood as a continuous institutional process, not as a collection of isolated records.

Each new documented object may develop, refine or contextualise already established institutional knowledge, while preserving the continuity of its evidentiary history.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

<a id="interaction-architecture"></a>
### V. ARCHITECTURE OF INSTITUTIONAL INTERACTION

VERDICTUM operates on the basis of a structured model of institutional interaction, in which the primary objects of institutional processing are not individual participants, but documented actions.

Participants initiate institutional processes by performing documented actions in accordance with their institutional roles.

Such actions generate documented objects, which subsequently become constituent elements of the continuously formed institutional memory of the Registry.

Consequently, institutional interaction within VERDICTUM is understood not as communication between users, but as coordinated processing of documented institutional actions within a unified evidentiary environment.

<a id="institutional-action"></a>
#### 5.1. Institutional Action as the Primary Unit of Processing

Within VERDICTUM, institutional action is the primary operational unit.

The Registry does not evaluate the personality of the participant as an independent object, nor does it undertake institutional processing of the user as such.

The basis for institutional processing is exclusively the documented action that gives rise to legally or organisationally significant consequences.

Each documented action creates or modifies institutional objects in accordance with the principles established by this Declaration.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

<a id="lifecycle"></a>
### VI. LIFE CYCLE OF A VERDICTUM RECORD (VR)

Each Verdictum Record (VR) is understood as an institutional object possessing a defined life cycle.

The institutional value of a VR derives not only from the information it contains, but from the documented sequence of actions through which the object is created, verified, maintained and subsequently used within the Public Knowledge Infrastructure.

Accordingly, a Verdictum Record is not to be understood as a static publication.

It constitutes a continuously evolving institutional object, the evidentiary significance of which may develop over time while preserving the integrity of its documented history.

<a id="lifecycle-creation"></a>
#### 6.1. Creation

The life cycle of a Verdictum Record commences with the emergence of a documented institutional action.

It is such an action that creates the legal and organisational grounds for the formation of a new institutional object.

At the moment of creation, the Registry records the primary identity of the VR, its documented provenance, institutional context and initial evidentiary basis.

<a id="lifecycle-verification"></a>
#### 6.2. Verification

Following creation, each Verdictum Record undergoes a procedure of institutional verification.

During the verification process, the authenticity of documented sources is confirmed, the evidentiary basis of the recorded action is assessed, and the institutional status of the object is determined.

The level of verification is an integral component of the VR's institutional identity throughout its subsequent life cycle.

<a id="lifecycle-development"></a>
#### 6.3. Development

Institutional objects may be supplemented with new documented evidence, analytical conclusions, legal references and verified connections to other Verdictum Records.

Such development expands the institutional significance of the object without altering its documented provenance and without violating the continuity of its evidentiary history.

Consequently, institutional development is cumulative, not substitutive, in nature.

<a id="lifecycle-use"></a>
#### 6.4. Institutional Use

Following the completion of verification, Verdictum Records may be used as an evidentiary basis for institutional decision-making, scientific research, governance, investment assessment, legal documentation and other activities requiring documented reliability.

Institutional use does not alter the identity of the VR.

Rather, it expands the practical value of documented knowledge across various institutional contexts.

<a id="lifecycle-preservation"></a>
#### 6.5. Preservation

The conclusion of individual institutional processes does not terminate the existence of a Verdictum Record.

Each VR remains a constituent element of the institutional memory of the Public Knowledge Infrastructure, preserving its documented interconnections, evidentiary history and institutional significance for future use.

Thus, institutional memory is understood as an accumulated public asset, not as a temporary archive.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

<a id="vr-as-object"></a>
### VII. VERDICTUM RECORD AS AN INSTITUTIONAL OBJECT

Within VERDICTUM, the Verdictum Record (VR) is the primary institutional object through which documented knowledge is created, organised, verified and preserved.

A Verdictum Record is not to be regarded as an ordinary document, publication or database entry.

It is an autonomous institutional entity possessing permanent identity, documented provenance, evidentiary status, a system of institutional interconnections and a continuously evolving operational history.

Thus, the institutional value of a VR extends beyond the informational content of an individual record.

Its significance is determined by the structured system of documented interconnections formed throughout the entire institutional life cycle.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

<a id="pki"></a>
### VIII. PLACE OF VERDICTUM WITHIN THE PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE INFRASTRUCTURE

VERDICTUM is one of the foundational institutional components of the Public Knowledge Infrastructure (PKI).

Its function is to ensure institutional memory through the registration, verification and long-term stewardship of documented institutional objects.

Within the structure of the Public Knowledge Infrastructure, VERDICTUM performs functions that complement, but do not duplicate, the systems of scientific research, software development, governance, education and institutional administration.

Thus, the Registry does not replace existing institutional mechanisms, nor does it compete with them.

Instead, it provides evidentiary continuity, through which independent institutional systems may exchange documented knowledge within a shared space of trust.

<a id="chronicle-os"></a>
#### 8.1. Relationship with Chronicle OS

Within the IMARCH ecosystem, Chronicle OS and VERDICTUM form complementary institutional environments.

Chronicle OS is responsible for the creation, systematisation and dissemination of reproducible public knowledge.

VERDICTUM ensures the documentation of institutional reality through the formation of verified records, evidentiary chains and documented outcomes.

Scientific knowledge formed within Chronicle OS may acquire institutional embodiment through the mechanisms of VERDICTUM.

At the same time, documented institutional experience accumulated within VERDICTUM is capable of initiating new scientific research, methodological developments and technological solutions within Chronicle OS.

Together, both systems form a continuous institutional cycle connecting the creation of knowledge with its practical realisation.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

<a id="governance"></a>
### IX. INSTITUTIONAL GOVERNANCE AND DEVELOPMENT

The Public Knowledge Infrastructure is designed as an institutional system capable of continuous development.

Accordingly, VERDICTUM evolves through documented institutional processes while preserving the continuity of its foundational principles.

Institutional development is understood as controlled expansion of organisational, methodological and technological capabilities without violation of institutional integrity, evidentiary continuity or public accountability.

Any changes concerning the Registry are themselves documented institutional actions and become constituent elements of VERDICTUM's institutional memory.

Thus, institutional governance is inseparable from institutional documentation.

The very development of the Registry is regarded as a documented institutional process.

<a id="self-documentation"></a>
#### 9.1. Principle of Self-Documentation

Any institutional change concerning the architecture, governance or methodology of VERDICTUM is subject to documentation within the institutional environment defined by this Declaration.

Thus, the Registry ensures the preservation not only of documented external events, but also of its own institutional history of development.

The principle of transparency extends also to the governance system of the Registry itself.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

<a id="founding-provision"></a>
### X. FOUNDING PROVISION

This Declaration establishes the institutional foundations of VERDICTUM as an integral component of the Public Knowledge Infrastructure.

The principles, definitions and institutional interconnections set forth herein constitute the conceptual basis for the further organisational, methodological, technological and legal development of the Registry.

All future regulations, specifications, governance documents, software architectures and operational procedures shall be based upon and remain consistent with the institutional principles established by this Declaration.

Thus, this Declaration is the primary founding instrument, defining the institutional identity, public purpose and continuity of development of VERDICTUM.

Its purpose is not merely to describe the institution, but to establish the normative architecture within which that institution is capable of continuous development while preserving its own identity.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="final-provision"></a>
### XI. FINAL PROVISION

The institutional principles established by this Declaration are intended to form a sustainable conceptual foundation for the further development of VERDICTUM and the entire Public Knowledge Infrastructure.

This Declaration serves as the common point of reference for institutional governance, methodological development, software architecture, legal documentation and organisational practice of the Registry.

Further development of VERDICTUM shall ensure the continuity of these principles, permitting expansion of the institutional system exclusively through documented, verified and publicly accountable processes.

Accordingly, the institutional identity of VERDICTUM is determined not by particular technologies, software implementations or organisational forms, but by the principles set forth in this Declaration.

Technological solutions may change.

Administrative structures may change.

Legal mechanisms may evolve.

Operational procedures may be improved.

Institutional identity remains continuous.

It is for this reason that this Declaration is the foundational instrument of VERDICTUM until amended through the institutional procedures established by the Registry itself.
