## 🏛️ Institutional Manifesto: Peer Review as Capital { .fade-in-up }

The world of experts has been confronted with a crisis of peer review: experts are for years required to provide 'free labour' in favour of giant publishing corporations, spending weeks proofreading incorrectly formatted materials. **VERDICTUM breaks this paradigm.**

In accordance with the **Institutional Protocol RN-0031**, we regard expert peer review as a measurable professional contribution requiring direct monetisation, institutional recognition and legal protection. We guarantee that every hour of an expert's work is transformed into their personal capital — professional, financial or status-based.

### How is the economy of an expert's time structured?
1. **Primary screening on the side of the platform:** Works undergo automatic anti-plagiarism control, structure verification and computation of the SHA-256 cryptographic hash.
2. **Cleaned-up 'Blind Vector':** The reviewer receives a structured text without authors' identifying details (Double-Blind Peer Review).
3. **The 15-minute regulation:** Thanks to the complete technical pre-preparation of the work, the expert's net time for completing the standardised form is **up to 15 minutes**.

## ⚙️ Detailed working circuits and the interaction regulation { .fade-in-up }

### 1. The Barter Circuit (Professional Offset)
For actively publishing specialists and heads of scholarly schools. For every **6 completed reviews**, the platform automatically generates a personal digital **Publication Voucher**. The voucher covers 100% of the APC for your own work, or the work of your doctoral candidate. The publication includes the assignment of a DOI, registration in the British registry and cryptographic protection of priority.

### 2. The Tokenised Circuit (Investment Capital)
For specialists oriented towards long-term capitalisation. The accrual of **25 tokens of Series 8888** for each review is fixed in the VERDICTUM registry. The IMARCH LTD ecosystem redistributes **30% of net commercial profit** among token holders. Your tokens are protected from inflation and yield cascading coefficients when voting on grants.

### 3. The Commercial Circuit (Fiat payments + Skin-in-the-Game)
Direct financial compensation under a consultancy services agreement with IMARCH LTD (UK).
* **Fixed rate:** **£25** for one fully completed expert form.
* **Skin-in-the-Game:** a long-term percentage of the project's success (grant, commercialisation).
* **Speed and comfort:** All works are submitted in a standardised format. You do not spend time searching for typos — you assess the essence and applied value.
* **Payments:** Effected monthly via PayPal, Wise, Payoneer, international IBAN or cryptocurrency equivalent (USDT).
