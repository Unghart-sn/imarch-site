### Preface

The global knowledge ecosystem is undergoing a period of profound transformation.

The rapid development of digital technologies, an unprecedented growth in the volume of scientific publications, and the proliferation of generative artificial intelligence systems are fundamentally altering the processes of knowledge creation, evaluation, dissemination and preservation.

Despite the constant increase in the quantity of information, its volume alone no longer guarantees scientific quality, reproducibility of results, or long-term institutional value.

Under these conditions, publishing activity ceases to be merely a means of making materials publicly available. It becomes an institutional mechanism for ensuring academic integrity, expert evaluation and the formation of collective memory within research communities.

The IMARCH Chronicle OS ecosystem has been created as a response to these challenges.

Its purpose is to foster an environment in which knowledge is understood not as a collection of individual publications, but as interconnected elements of a unified institutional memory, passing through a sequential path from the emergence of an idea to its professional recognition.

This Publishing Doctrine defines the fundamental principles governing the operation of the IMARCH publishing system and serves as the foundational normative document for all academic and expert publishing platforms within the ecosystem.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="chapter-1"></a>
### Chapter 1. Mission of the Publishing System

The mission of the IMARCH publishing system is not merely to publish documents, but to ensure the full knowledge life cycle: from creation and expert evaluation to long-term institutional preservation.

Each publication is regarded as the commencement of an open professional dialogue, not as the conclusion of a research endeavour.

We strive to create an environment in which new ideas develop through constructive professional discussion, independent expert evaluation and the continuity of institutional memory.

Our aim is the creation not of a document archive, but of a sustainable ecosystem for knowledge development.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="chapter-2"></a>
### Chapter 2. Foundational Principles

IMARCH publishing activity is founded upon the following principles.

#### Academic Integrity

Each publication must accurately reflect the intellectual contribution of the author and contain appropriate references to all sources utilised.

#### Professional Dialogue

Constructive criticism is an integral component of the development of science and professional knowledge.

#### Institutional Memory

Each revision of a document possesses independent value and is preserved as part of the history of development of the corresponding research.

#### Reproducibility

Methodology, research results and argumentation must be presented in a manner that enables independent verification, critical analysis and further development.

#### Respect for Authorship

The author retains intellectual responsibility for the content of their work at all stages of the publishing process.

#### Independence of Editorial and Expert Activity

Editorial decisions and expert evaluations are made professionally, objectively and independently.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="chapter-3"></a>
### Chapter 3. Architecture of the Publishing Ecosystem

The IMARCH publishing ecosystem consists of two complementary publishing circuits.

#### Academic Publishing Circuit

Designed for the publication of scientific articles, monographs, methodological materials, research findings and other academic works.

Its activity is based on the principles of scientific peer review, academic ethics and reproducibility of results.

#### Expert Publishing Circuit

The expert circuit, including the Verdictum platform, is designed for the publication of analytical materials, strategic research, institutional case studies, legal opinions and interdisciplinary professional publications.

Its primary purpose is the development of applied knowledge and professional expert dialogue.

Notwithstanding the differences in subject matter and publication formats, both circuits operate on the basis of a common institutional architecture, unified editorial standards and consistent principles of expert evaluation.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="chapter-4"></a>
### Chapter 4. Expert Peer Review

Expert peer review is regarded as an independent form of scientific and professional activity.

The task of the reviewer is not merely to identify deficiencies in the manuscript, but above all to contribute to the enhancement of its scientific quality, methodological soundness and clarity.

Authors, editors and reviewers are regarded as equal participants in a unified process of knowledge formation.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="chapter-5"></a>
### Chapter 5. Institutional Memory

Knowledge develops through a sequence of revisions, not only through individual publications.

Each document may pass through the following stages of development:

- Draft
- Working revision
- Peer review revision
- Published revision
- Archival revision
- Revision history

The preservation of all revisions enables the reconstruction not only of the final outcome, but also of the logic of the research development.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="chapter-6"></a>
### Chapter 6. Chronicle OS

Chronicle OS provides the digital infrastructure for the implementation of this Doctrine.

The platform integrates publishing processes, editorial support, expert evaluation, institutional archiving, professional communication and long-term knowledge management within a unified digital environment.

Its primary purpose is to support the sustainable development of the international research community.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="chapter-7"></a>
### Chapter 7. Continuous Development

This Publishing Doctrine constitutes an open normative document that evolves alongside the IMARCH ecosystem.

As the platform develops, its provisions may be refined, expanded and supplemented.

All revisions of the Doctrine form an integral part of IMARCH's institutional memory and are preserved as the official history of the document's development.

<div style="width: 100%; text-align: center; margin: 3rem 0;" class="fade-in-up"> <hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, #8b7355, transparent);" /> </div>

&nbsp;

<a id="conclusion"></a>
### Conclusion

The advancement of science is impossible without trust, academic integrity and responsible professional collaboration.

The IMARCH publishing system is created not merely as a platform for the publication of materials, but as an institutional infrastructure designed to ensure the creation, expert evaluation, preservation and transmission of knowledge between academic and professional communities.

By uniting publishing activity, expert peer review and institutional memory within a unified architecture, IMARCH seeks to contribute to the formation of an open, transparent, sustainable and responsible international knowledge space.
