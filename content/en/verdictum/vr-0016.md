### Chapter 1. General Provisions

#### 1.1. Preamble

IMARCH Chronicle OS constitutes an international publishing ecosystem established to support the creation, expert evaluation, publication, and long-term preservation of scientific and professional knowledge.

The contemporary academic system is subject to a profound structural crisis: classical scientometrics remains exclusively oriented towards citation indices — metrics that frequently reflect the level of media visibility surrounding a publication rather than the practical value of the underlying research.

The IMARCH CitHronical Platform introduces a fundamentally distinct discipline — Scientometrics 2.0. The objective is not the accumulation of citations, but the transformation of scientific knowledge into real, protected, and operational capital.

This Protocol proceeds from a rigorous pragmatic principle: the value of a technology is determined by its applicability and effectiveness, not by its representation in institutional reports. Publication in this Journal constitutes not the conclusion of research, but the necessary legal prelude to the scaling and commercialisation of the Author's intellectual contribution.

#### 1.2. Scope and Application

This Protocol governs the relationship between IMARCH LTD and Authors submitting manuscripts for consideration to the IMARCH Chronicle OS International Journal. It establishes the procedural framework for submission, cryptographic fixation, peer review, publication, and post-publication institutional recognition.

#### 1.3. Jurisdiction

This Protocol and all matters arising therefrom shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The courts of London, United Kingdom, shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Protocol, subject always to the provisions of Clause 15 (Dispute Resolution).

### Chapter 2. Interpretation and Definitions

#### 2.1. Definitions

**"Chronicle OS"** means the institutional documentation and registry system operated by IMARCH LTD.

**"DOI"** means Digital Object Identifier, a persistent identifier assigned to published works.

**"Double-Blind Peer Review"** means the evaluation process in which neither the Author nor the Reviewer are aware of each other's identity.

**"Editorial Board"** means the editorial body appointed by IMARCH LTD to oversee the peer review and publication process.

**"Institutional Protocol"** means this document, RN-0039, as amended from time to time.

**"IMARCH LTD"** means the company registered in England and Wales under registration number 16791765.

**"Master File"** means the complete version of the manuscript containing all author identification information.

**"Registration Contribution"** means the initial payment of £50 due upon submission of the manuscript.

**"Reviewer"** means an independent expert engaged by the Editorial Board to evaluate submitted manuscripts.

**"SHA-256"** means the cryptographic hash algorithm used for priority fixation and integrity verification.

**"Third Party"** means any person or entity other than the Author, IMARCH LTD, or the Editorial Board.

### Chapter 3. Publication Process Overview

#### 3.1. Stage 1: Submission — Security Package

The Author shall submit two files through the official Platform:

(a) **Master File:** Complete version containing the Author's name, institutional affiliation, contact details, abstract, and full text. This file is immediately transmitted to the secure encrypted repository.

(b) **Blind Vector:** An identical text of the manuscript from which all identifying information (Author name, institutional affiliation, and contact details) has been removed.

#### 3.2. Stage 2: Cryptographic Fixation — Instant Priority

At the moment of file upload, the system shall compute unique digital fingerprints — SHA-256 hashes — for each file.

The Platform shall publish a pre-publication card on the international pre-print showcase at `imarch.sbs`. The Author's identity shall remain concealed, but the English-language abstract and file hashes shall be publicly accessible.

This mechanism provides legally cognisable proof of priority. Search engines index the page immediately. The Author's intellectual contribution is protected against misappropriation during the peer review process through London-based temporal evidence of priority.

#### 3.3. Stage 3: Double-Blind Peer Review — Up to 7 Days

The Blind Vector (anonymised file) shall be transmitted to an independent international expert appointed by the Editorial Board.

The Reviewer shall evaluate the manuscript exclusively on the basis of scholarly quality, methodological rigour, and scientific contribution. The Reviewer shall not have access to the Author's institutional affiliation, geographic origin, or personal identity.

The Registration Contribution of £50 ensures expeditious processing and covers the technical costs of conducting the peer review within the prescribed 7-day period.

**Notwithstanding** the payment of the Registration Contribution, the Editorial Board retains sole discretion to extend the review period where exceptional circumstances (including but not limited to Reviewer unavailability, force majeure, or technical failure) so require. The Author shall be notified of any such extension without undue delay.

#### 3.4. Stage 4: Editorial Decision and Publication

Upon receipt of the review, the Editorial Board shall issue one of the following decisions: Accepted; Minor Revisions; Major Revisions; Resubmit; Rejected.

Where a favourable decision is issued, the Author shall pay the final Publication Contribution of £100.

The system shall automatically link the Author's identity from the secure vault with the previously published Master File hash, assign an international DOI to the article, and update the publication status to "Officially Published".

**Without prejudice** to the rights of IMARCH LTD under this Protocol, the Editorial Board's decision on publication shall be final and binding.

### Chapter 4. Cryptographic Priority Fixation

#### 4.1. SHA-256 Technology

The Platform employs the SHA-256 cryptographic algorithm to generate unique digital fingerprints for each uploaded file. The hash value constitutes an irreversible mathematical transformation that:

(a) uniquely identifies the file contents;

(b) enables verification of document integrity at any subsequent time;

(c) serves as legally cognisable evidence of the document's existence at a specific point in time.

#### 4.2. Hash Publication

Following computation, the hashes shall be published on the pre-publication page. This creates:

(a) a verifiable timestamp of the work's existence;

(b) legally cognisable evidence of the Author's priority;

(c) protection against misappropriation during the review process.

#### 4.3. DOI Association

At final publication, the Master File hash shall be associated with the assigned DOI. This establishes a continuous chain of evidence from initial fixation to official publication.

#### 4.4. Integrity of Fixation

**Without prejudice** to any other rights or remedies available to the Author, the cryptographic fixation provided by the Platform constitutes evidence of the existence and content of the manuscript at the time of submission. The Author acknowledges that the Platform does not guarantee the continued availability of any third-party services or external indexing systems.

### Chapter 5. Double-Blind Peer Review System

#### 5.1. Review Principles

Peer review in the IMARCH Chronicle OS Journal shall be conducted in accordance with five institutional principles:

(a) **Independence:** Reviewers shall provide independent professional opinions based exclusively on scholarly quality, scientific methodology, and generally recognised principles of academic integrity.

(b) **Confidentiality:** All manuscripts, editorial communications, and review materials shall remain confidential unless disclosure is expressly authorised by the Editorial Board.

(c) **Objectivity:** Expert assessments shall be impartial, reasoned, and free from personal, institutional, commercial, or political bias.

(d) **Professional Responsibility:** Reviewers shall complete accepted assignments within agreed timeframes, adhering to high standards of analytical quality and professional ethics.

(e) **Institutional Trust:** Each completed review contributes not only to the evaluation of an individual manuscript but to the development of a trusted international knowledge infrastructure.

#### 5.2. Double-Blind Anonymity

The Author's identity shall be concealed from the Reviewer. The Reviewer's identity shall be concealed from the Author. This excludes:

(a) bias based on institutional prestige;

(b) influence of national origin;

(c) personal sympathies or antipathies;

(d) academic protectionism.

Reviewers shall evaluate exclusively the quality of the research, methodology, and scholarly significance of the work.

#### 5.3. Reviewer Independence

Reviewers are independent experts engaged by the Editorial Board. IMARCH LTD shall not be responsible for, and disclaims all liability in respect of, the content, quality, or conclusions of any review provided by a Reviewer.

### Chapter 6. Targeted Funding Structure

#### 6.1. General Principles

The Platform operates as a non-commercial open science infrastructure. The Platform does not trade in "journal placements". Targeted contributions from Authors are applied to the costs of high-technology independent evaluation, cryptographic fixation, and international registration.

#### 6.2. Registration Contribution — £50

Due upon initial submission. Covers:

(a) initial AI-based anti-plagiarism audit;

(b) SHA-256 fixation;

(c) RN-0030 registry server operations;

(d) preparation of materials for independent expert evaluation.

#### 6.3. Publication Contribution — £100

Due **only after** the manuscript has successfully completed blind peer review and been approved for publication. Covers:

(a) generation of British DOI;

(b) permanent server indexing;

(c) official release.

#### 6.4. Legal Status of Contributions

The transfer of funds constitutes a voluntary targeted grant/contribution to the development of the ecosystem and support of the IMARCH LTD peer review protocol (UK Trade Secrets Regulations / VERDICTUM). This payment is not a commercial purchase of goods or services.

Contributions cover the costs of organising the review process and do not guarantee a favourable decision from reviewers.

**Notwithstanding** the payment of any contribution, the Editorial Board retains the absolute and unfettered discretion to reject any manuscript on scholarly grounds. In such event, the Registration Contribution shall not be refundable, as it covers services already rendered (including technical review, hash fixation, and administrative processing).

### Chapter 7. Author Rights and Obligations

#### 7.1. Author Rights

Authors shall have the right:

(a) to cryptographic priority fixation at the time of submission;

(b) to receive an official deposition protocol;

(c) to professional, independent, and impartial peer review;

(d) to receive a DOI for published articles;

(e) to receive institutional recognition within the IMARCH ecosystem.

#### 7.2. Author Obligations

Authors shall undertake:

(a) to submit original scholarly works;

(b) to disclose relevant funding sources and potential conflicts of interest;

(c) to respond professionally to reviewer comments;

(d) to revise manuscripts in good faith where revisions are requested;

(e) to respect the independence of the review process.

#### 7.3. Author Revisions

Where revisions are required, the Editorial Board shall communicate reviewer recommendations to the Author. The Author shall respond to each substantive comment and submit a revised manuscript with an explanation of amendments made.

#### 7.4. Representations and Warranties

The Author represents and warrants that:

(a) the submitted manuscript is the Author's original work;

(b) it has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere;

(c) all necessary permissions for the use of third-party material have been obtained;

(d) the manuscript does not infringe any intellectual property rights of any Third Party.

#### 7.5. Withdrawal

The Author may withdraw the manuscript at any time prior to formal acceptance by providing written notice to the Editorial Board. The Registration Contribution shall not be refundable in the event of withdrawal.

### Chapter 8. Confidentiality

#### 8.1. Author's Obligation of Confidentiality

The Author shall treat as confidential and shall not disclose to any Third Party:

(a) the content of any correspondence with the Editorial Board;

(b) the identity of any Reviewer assigned to the manuscript;

(c) the content of any review received, except as necessary to respond to reviewer comments in the revision process;

(d) any information obtained during the review process that is not in the public domain.

#### 8.2. Exceptions

The obligation of confidentiality shall not apply to:

(a) information already in the public domain at the time of disclosure;

(b) information required to be disclosed by applicable law or by order of a competent court or regulatory authority.

#### 8.3. Duration

The obligations set out in this Clause 8 shall survive the termination of this Protocol and the completion or discontinuation of the publication process.

### Chapter 9. Registration and Deposition Procedure

#### 9.1. Submission Procedure

Submission shall be made through the official form on the Platform. The Author shall provide:

(a) full name (in English and native language);

(b) professional email address;

(c) institutional affiliation;

(d) ORCID iD (where available);

(e) article title;

(f) abstract.

#### 9.2. File Upload

Two files shall be uploaded:

(a) Master File (complete version);

(b) Blind Vector (anonymised version).

#### 9.3. Hash Generation

The system shall automatically compute SHA-256 hashes for both files and display them in the form. Hashes shall be preserved in session storage for subsequent use.

#### 9.4. Deposition Protocol

Following submission, an official Cryptographic Deposition Protocol (RN-0030) shall be generated, containing:

(a) author details and affiliation;

(b) publication metadata;

(c) cryptographic digital fingerprints (SHA-256);

(d) compliance status and session token.

#### 9.5. Protocol Submission

The Author shall copy the protocol and forward it together with the files to the official email gateway `info@imarch.sbs`.

### Chapter 12. Ecosystem Integration Advantages

#### 12.1. Equity Distribution Model (10:30:30:30)

For projects demonstrating substantial commercial and engineering potential, IMARCH LTD may open an investment circuit. Revenues derived from commercialisation shall be distributed according to the following formula:

(a) **10% — Social and Charitable Circuit:** funding for veteran support programmes, orphan support, and grants for emerging talent.

(b) **30% — Author Sovereign Income:** net licensing and commercial income of the intellectual property creator.

(c) **30% — Re-investment in Development and Marketing:** budget for project promotion, pitch deck creation, and international market entry.

(d) **30% — IMARCH LTD Infrastructure Share:** compensation to the company for legal framework, Chronicle OS operational system, and know-how protection.

#### 12.2. Institutional Protection

The project shall become part of the IMARCH LTD asset portfolio. Legal and patent disputes shall be resolved through the legal circuit.

#### 12.3. Research Focus

The Platform relieves the burden of financing, negotiation, and legal procedures. The Author remains the Architect of the idea; IMARCH becomes its commercial instrument.

(a) the Platform and the Journal;

(c) the accuracy, completeness, or suitability of any information provided through the Platform;

(d) the timing or outcome of the review process.

The Platform and Journal are provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis.

#### 13.2. Limitation of Liability

IMARCH LTD shall not be liable to the Author or any Third Party for any direct, indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or exemplary damages, including but not limited to:

(a) loss of profits, revenue, or business opportunities;

(b) loss of data or goodwill;

(c) any claim arising from the content or quality of reviews received;

(d) any delay or failure to publish;

(e) any actions taken by Reviewers or Third Parties.

#### 13.3. Aggregate Liability Cap

Notwithstanding any other provision of this Protocol, the aggregate liability of IMARCH LTD to the Author under or in connection with this Protocol, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, shall not exceed the total amount of contributions paid by the Author to IMARCH LTD in connection with the relevant manuscript.

#### 13.4. Independent Reviewers

Reviewers are independent experts engaged by the Editorial Board. IMARCH LTD acts solely as an administrative intermediary and shall not be liable for the content, quality, or consequences of any review.

#### 13.5. Third-Party Services

The Platform may utilise third-party services (including payment processing and indexing systems). IMARCH LTD shall not be liable for any failure, error, or omission of such third-party services.

#### 13.6. Force Majeure

IMARCH LTD shall not be liable for any delay or failure to perform its obligations under this Protocol where such delay or failure arises from circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to:

(a) acts of God, war, terrorism, or civil unrest;

(b) technical failures, cyber-attacks, or system outages;

(c) government action or regulatory changes;

(d) unavailability of third-party services.

### Chapter 14. Timeframes and Consequences of Breach

#### 14.1. Standard Review Period

The standard review period shall be seven (7) calendar days from the date of assignment to a Reviewer. This period may be extended by the Editorial Board in exceptional circumstances.

#### 14.2. Author's Response Period

The Author shall respond to requests for revisions within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt of the review. Failure to respond within this period may result in the manuscript being considered withdrawn.

#### 14.3. Consequences of Breach by Author

Without prejudice to any other rights or remedies available to IMARCH LTD, the following consequences shall apply in the event of breach by the Author:

(a) **Failure to pay:** If the Author fails to pay any contribution when due, the submission shall be suspended and may be rejected.

(b) **Misrepresentation:** If the Author is found to have made a false representation or warranty (including plagiarism, duplicate submission, or failure to disclose conflicts of interest), the manuscript shall be rejected and the Author may be excluded from future submissions to the Journal.

(c) **Breach of confidentiality:** Any breach of confidentiality by the Author shall constitute a material breach of this Protocol and may result in the rejection of the manuscript and the exclusion of the Author from future submissions.

#### 14.4. Consequences of Breach by IMARCH LTD

In the event of breach by IMARCH LTD, the Author's sole remedy shall be limited to a refund of the contributions paid in respect of the relevant manuscript, subject always to the limitations set out in Clause 13 (Limitation of Liability).

### Chapter 15. Dispute Resolution

#### 15.1. Negotiation

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Protocol shall first be referred to the Editorial Board for informal resolution. The Author shall submit a written statement of the dispute to `info@imarch.sbs`. The Editorial Board shall respond within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt.

#### 15.2. Mediation

If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation within thirty (30) calendar days of the initial referral, the parties shall submit the dispute to mediation administered by the Centre for Effective Dispute Resolution (CEDR) or another recognised mediation institution agreed by the parties.

#### 15.3. Mediation Procedure

The mediation shall be conducted in accordance with the CEDR Model Mediation Procedure (or the equivalent procedure of the agreed institution). The mediator shall be appointed by the parties acting jointly, or failing agreement, by the mediation institution.

#### 15.4. Costs of Mediation
