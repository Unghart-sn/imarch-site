**Dear Colleagues,**

We are launching **VERDICTUM** with a simple practical purpose: to create an environment in which an author's development can travel from idea and research to a verifiable result, professional evaluation and further application.

Scholarly publication remains an important part of this process. But publication alone does not always answer the principal question: **what exactly has been created, how well is it substantiated, can the claimed result be verified, and what is to be done with it next?**

It is precisely for this that VERDICTUM has been conceived.

We regard knowledge not merely as text, but as a **result of work**, which may take various forms: a scholarly hypothesis, an engineering solution, software code, a methodology, a system architecture, a technological protocol, an organisational model or other authors' know-how.

VERDICTUM unites such developments within a single contour, where they can be described, verified, expertly evaluated and recorded in such a way that further work with them is clear to the author, the expert, the partner, the investor or a grant-making organisation.

## For Whom VERDICTUM Works

### For Authors

If you have developed a method, a technology, a software solution or an architecture and wish to move from description to a verifiable result, VERDICTUM provides the appropriate contour for this.

Our principle may be stated simply:

**Proof-of-Work: a result must be substantiated by work.**

For different types of development, such substantiation may take the form of a working prototype, software code, test results, a computational model, experimental data, a reproducible methodology or another form of proof appropriate to the nature of the project.

Accordingly, the value of a work is determined not only by the status of the author or the form of publication, but above all by **what exactly has been created and what can be verified**.

### For Reviewers and Experts

High-quality expert assessment requires time, professional responsibility and clear evaluation criteria.

VERDICTUM is building a model in which expert labour is regarded as an autonomous professional activity. The expert receives remuneration for the work performed, while the system of reputation and distribution of expertise is designed to reduce the dependence of assessment on personal connections and closed professional circles.

The task here is not to replace traditional peer review, but to **add to it a more measurable contour of accountability for the outcome of expert assessment**.

### For Investors, Grant Operators and Institutional Partners

For those who take decisions on the financing or implementation of developments, the problem is often not a lack of information, but its quality and structure.

VERDICTUM seeks to form a structured flow of projects in which the development itself is accompanied by its evidentiary base, the results of expert assessment, its limitations, its level of readiness and its further roadmap.

This makes it possible to consider a project not as a body of text, but as a **structured object for decision-making**.

## How the Verification Contour Is Structured

Every material that has passed the procedure is recorded in the format of a **Verdictum Record (VR)** with cryptographic anchoring of the SHA-256 checksum.

At the same time, cryptographic anchoring does not substitute for expert assessment. It performs a different function: it makes it possible to record a specific version of the material and to confirm the immutability of the recorded object.

The VERDICTUM procedure provides for successive passage through several levels:

**technical review → expert assessment → institutional recording.**

The specific set of procedures may vary depending on the type of development.

For scholarly and research materials, the corresponding forms of peer review may be applied. For engineering and software solutions, reproducibility, testing and demonstration of operability play an essential role. For architectural and organisational models, their own verification criteria are required.

Our task, therefore, is not to apply a single template to all types of knowledge, but to **match the verification procedure to the nature of the object itself**.

## The VERDICTUM Association

VERDICTUM is being created as an open professional association with a defined verification contour.

The principle of participation is simple:

**entry is open, but professional status must be confirmed by work.**

We do not propose a closed community based on belonging to a particular school, organisation or academic environment.

What interests us above all is the ability of an author, researcher, engineer or expert to present a work in a form amenable to professional verification.

Platform operator: **IMARCH LTD**, England and Wales, registration number **16791765**.

The legal basis of the project is the **Founding Institutional Declaration of VERDICTUM, Act RN-0028 (VR-DECL-2026-001 of 06 July 2026)**.

The infrastructure contour of the project includes **CHRONICLE OS 8888™**. Certain elements of the infrastructure and internal procedures may constitute a trade secret under applicable law.

## What We Offer

VERDICTUM does not set out to abolish existing academic, scholarly or investment institutions.

We offer an **additional working layer** that connects research, proof, expert assessment and further application.

For the author, this is an opportunity to turn a development into a structured and verifiable object.

For the expert, this is an opportunity to formalise professional assessment as an autonomous result.

For the investor or grant operator, this is an opportunity to receive projects together with their evidentiary and expert base.

For an institution, this is an opportunity to see not only a publication, but also the **history of a development's passage through verification procedures**.

Ultimately, what is at issue is a fairly simple transition:

**from knowledge that is merely asserted to knowledge that can be verified;
from publication as an endpoint to publication as a stage;
from expert opinion to a recorded professional verdict.**

If you have a development, a research project or know-how that you are ready to submit for professional verification, **VERDICTUM is open to your application**.

**From Imitation to Verification.**
