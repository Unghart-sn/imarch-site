<h4 id="1-назначение-и-область-применения">1. Purpose and Scope of Application</h4>

This Annex consolidates the hierarchical system of visual standards of the IMARCH ecosystem. The purpose of the document is to ensure unified, verifiable and adaptive visual execution of all output formats defined by the IPP Protocol: from slide presentations to complete printed monographs.

The document performs two functions:

1. **Official registration of the standard:** Fixation of colour codes, typography, compositional rules and illustration styles as part of the intellectual domain of IMARCH LTD UK.
2. **Consultation RAG tool:** It serves as a knowledge base for the preliminary consultation of clients on the selection of a visual style depending on their objectives and target audience.

**The structure of the Annex corresponds to the five official Annexes of the Manifesto (RN-0043):**
- **Annex A** — Layout Standard (Section 2)
- **Annex B** — Slide Infographics Standard (Section 3)
- **Annex C** — Tables Standard (Section 4)
- **Annex D** — Illustrative Banners Standard (Section 5)
- **Annex E** — RAG Format Standard (Section 6)

<h4 id="2-уровень-1-стандарт-вёрстки-annex-a">2. Level 1: Layout Standard (Annex A)</h4>

**Standard ID:** `IMARCH-STD-VERSTKA-001`

This level defines *how* content is packaged. Six layout styles (styles A-F) are containers that are applied to four types of monograph output:

1. **Presentation format:** Compression of the monograph into slides.
2. **RAG format:** Loading into the knowledge base, with a focus on typography.
3. **Hybrid format:** A file for loading into RAG agents, a structured summary.
4. **Full printed format:** A PDF book with complete layout.

<h4 id="6-уровень-5-стандарт-rag-формата-annex-e">6. Level 5: RAG Format Standard (Annex E)</h4>

<p><strong>Standard ID:</strong> <code>IMARCH-STD-RAG-001</code></p>

<p>This level defines the protocol for structuring text for loading into AI agents (RAG systems). This is a 'clean' format, devoid of visual noise, intended exclusively for machine knowledge extraction.</p>

<h5>6.1. Principles of the RAG Format</h5>

<div class="verdictum-table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Principle</th>
      <th>Description</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Principle"><strong>Semantic purity</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Description">The text is cleared of visual elements (colours, fonts, layouts); only the semantic structure is preserved.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Principle"><strong>Hierarchical structure</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Description">Standard Markdown elements are used (<code>#</code>, <code>##</code>, <code>-</code>, <code>1.</code>).</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Principle"><strong>Meta-tagging</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Description">Each data block contains meta-information (document ID, hash, date).</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Principle"><strong>Verification linkage</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Description">Each response of the AI agent may be traced to the source block via its hash.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h5>6.2. Structure of a RAG Document</h5>

<pre style="background: #f0f5f8; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid #b8c8d8; font-family: monospace; font-size: 0.85rem; overflow-x: auto; white-space: pre-wrap; word-break: break-word; max-width: 100%; color: #1f3a5f;">
# DOCUMENT TITLE

**ID:** IMARCH-STD-XXX-001
**Hash:** SHA-256: [hash]
**Date of publication:** 2026-XX-XX

## 1. Introduction

[Text of the introduction]

### 1.1. Subsection

[Text of the subsection]

## 2. Main Body

[Text of the main body]

### 2.1. First Subsection

[Text of the subsection]

#### 2.1.1. Detail

[Text of the detail]

## 3. Conclusion

[Text of the conclusion]
</pre>

<h5>6.3. Formatting Rules</h5>

<div class="verdictum-table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Element</th>
      <th>Rule</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Element"><strong>Headings</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Rule">Only <code>#</code>, <code>##</code>, <code>###</code>, <code>####</code> are used. No additional formatting.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Element"><strong>Lists</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Rule">Only bulleted (<code>-</code>) and numbered (<code>1.</code>). No nesting deeper than 3 levels.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Element"><strong>Tables</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Rule">Only simple tables are permitted (without cell merging).</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Element"><strong>Quotations</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Rule"><code>&gt;</code> is used. A quotation block must contain a reference to the source.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Element"><strong>Links</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Rule">Full URLs only. No short links.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Element"><strong>Emphasis</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Rule">Only <code>**bold**</code> and <code>*italic*</code>. No colour emphasis.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td data-label="Element"><strong>Code</strong></td>
      <td data-label="Rule">Blocks with language indication are used (<code>```python</code>).</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h5>6.4. The Noise-Free Principle</h5>

<p>The text in the RAG format must be fully 'noise-free' — that is, it must not contain elements which may distort semantics during machine processing:</p>

<ul style="list-style: none; padding-left: 0;">
  <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">❌ <strong>Prohibited:</strong> Colours, tables with complex structure, nested lists deeper than 3 levels, images.</li>
  <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">✅ <strong>Permitted:</strong> Text only, Markdown markup, meta-tags.</li>
</ul>

<h5>6.5. Verification Protocol</h5>

<p>Each RAG document contains in its header:</p>

<ol style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
  <li><strong>Document ID</strong> — a unique identifier.</li>
  <li><strong>SHA-256 hash</strong> — fixation of the version in the Verdictum registry.</li>
  <li><strong>Date of publication</strong> — a timestamp.</li>
</ol>

<p>This ensures the possibility of verifying the responses of the AI agent against the primary source.</p>
