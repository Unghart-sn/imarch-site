The Vis Realitatis financial model is built as a **sustainability mechanism**, not a one-time fundraising scheme.  
Every invested dollar works multiple times, creating long-term social impact.

---

## 📌 Base Rates and Formats

### 🔬 Experimental Work — **$50 / hour**
- discounted sessions for veterans;
- collection of research data;
- methodology adaptation;
- direct help to the target group.

### 👫 Couple Training — **$70 / hour**
- individual preparation of couples in the SunLife-Massage® method;
- formation of sustainable home practices;
- creation of local mutual aid nodes.

### ♻️ Barter Practice — **no monetary transactions**
- trained couples help others;
- dozens of free sessions without additional funding;
- growth of social sustainability without increasing the budget.

---

## 🔄 Principle of Fund Transformation

Each donor contribution goes through three stages:

1. **Payment for professional work**  
   → help to veterans and training.

2. **Skill transfer**  
   → couples become autonomous sources of support.

3. **Self-reproduction of effect**  
   → dozens of free sessions without new costs.

> The donor funds **not a service**, but a **system** that continues to work independently.

---

## 📊 Why This Model is Sustainable

- costs decrease as the network grows;
- training replaces constant subsidizing;
- barter reduces budget load;
- digital tracking eliminates misuse of funds.

---

## 🔍 Transparency and Control

Financial and operational processes are recorded through a digital tracking system:

- session and training tracking;
- stage reporting;
- transparent resource distribution;
- donor report formation.

This allows donors to **see the real impact of their investments**, not just final declarations.

---

## 🌱 Long-term Sustainability

As the project develops, additional support sources are launched:

- online courses and certification;
- service aggregator;
- personal studio franchise;
- platform use by other NGOs.

This reduces dependence on grants and forms a self-sustaining ecosystem.

---

## ✅ Outcome

The Vis Realitatis financial model:
- transparent;
- scalable;
- focused on long-term impact;
- minimizes repeated costs;
- turns help into sustainable care infrastructure.

Donor funds here **are not spent**, they **work**.
