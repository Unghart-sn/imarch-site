# 🚀 Project Development Stages
## Roadmap from Pilot to Scalable Humanitarian System

Our project develops not chaotically, but along a clear evolutionary line: from the first pilot sessions to an international support network. Each stage builds on the previous and strengthens the next.

---

# 🔬 1. Experimental Work

The starting stage is a **research phase**, where we test and adapt the method to real-life conditions of veterans and their families.

### What the pilot includes:
- Sessions with veterans of different categories  
- Collection of bodily and emotional responses  
- Adjusting methodology to real needs: PTSD, anxiety, sleep disorders, family overload  

### Extended directions:
- **Research metrics:** effectiveness metrics, data collection, scientific notes, article preparation  
- **Marketing analytics:** target audience research (veterans, families, facilitators, volunteers)  
- **Game practices and animation:** soft forms of attracting attention and reducing barriers  
- **Participant selection:** identifying people who can become facilitators and community leaders  
- **All-weather suit:** allows conducting practices:  
  - year-round  
  - in field conditions  
  - as part of equipment for mobile specialists  

### Outcome:
We obtain an evidence base validated by practice and data.

---

# 👫 2. Couple Training

The second stage creates **micro-cells of family sustainability** — couples who can support each other at home.

### Stage content:
- Training in basic SunLife-Massage techniques  
- Creating sustainable "for home, for family" nodes  
- Minimizing the need for regular external services  

### Outcome:
The family receives not one-time help, but a constant recovery mechanism.

---

# 🎓 3. Educational Product and Online Course

After the pilot and first trained couples, we form a full-fledged mass training system.

### Course structure:
- **Level 1 — "For Self"**  
  Basic stabilization, working with anxiety and bodily tension  

- **Level 2 — "For Family"**  
  Working in pairs, supporting spouses, strengthening relationships  

- **Level 3 — "Profi"**  
  Deep training of method specialists  

### Certification:
Built into the gamified IMARCH system:
- levels  
- badges  
- exams  
- reputation  

### Outcome:
The method becomes accessible to a wide range of people with quality assurance.

---

# 🌐 4. Community and Gamification

The project structure forms a **living community** where every person can both receive and give support.

### Components:
- internal social network  
- ratings and levels  
- referral circles  
- automated practice tracking  

Every session conducted is:
- help to another person  
- skill growth  
- reputation contribution  

### Outcome:
A self-sustaining social system emerges.

---

# 📱 5. Massage Aggregator

The aggregator is the technological core that unites the humanitarian block into a sustainable economic model.

### Functions:
- search and booking of specialists  
- commercial infrastructure  
- field testing of the business model  

### Supporting elements:
- **specialist training only through NGOs / licensed studios**  
- **mobile massage cabin** — turns any room into a therapeutic space  

### Outcome:
The project gains its own financial sustainability, reducing dependence on donors.

---

# ✅ Final Conclusion

The project development stages form a holistic, sequential system:

- **Experimental work** → evidence base  
- **Couple training** → local sustainability  
- **Educational product** → mass reach  
- **Community** → social fabric  
- **Aggregator** → economic model  

This is not just a plan.  
This is **evolution from helping one person to an international support system**, working anywhere in the world.
