> Programmers have Git — a unified standard for collaborative code work. They don't argue about "whose code is primary" or "how to merge changes." There's a protocol — and everyone follows it.

**Social projects lacked such a language. Until now.**

## Introducing Formology

**Formology** is a unified language for describing:
*   Missions
*   Roles
*   Authorities
*   Interactions

**Like musical notes.** You can play any melody with them, and any musician will understand what to play.

## Three Language Levels

### 1. **Mission Level**
*   **Purpose:** Why do we exist?
*   **Principles:** By what rules do we operate?
*   **Metrics:** How do we measure success?

**Example:** "Teaching digital skills to teenagers from remote areas. Principle: practice over theory. Metric: 1000 trained per year."

### 2. **Role Level**
*   **Mandate:** What can this role decide?
*   **Responsibility:** What is it accountable for?
*   **Interaction:** Who does it interact with?

**Example:** "Role: Methodologist. Mandate: choose educational materials. Responsibility: quality of education. Interaction: with teachers and students."

### 3. **Protocol Level**
*   **How are decisions made?**
*   **How are conflicts resolved?**
*   **How are rules changed?**

**Example:** "Budget decision: 75% founder vote. Conflict: mediation through standard protocol."

## What Does This Provide?

### For Founders:
*   **Clarity:** All rules are documented
*   **Control:** Processes are predictable
*   **Scalability:** New people quickly get up to speed

### For the Team:
*   **Understanding:** I know my boundaries and capabilities
*   **Security:** Protected from arbitrariness
*   **Development:** Clear career path

### For the Project:
*   **Resilience:** Not dependent on specific individuals
*   **Efficiency:** Less time spent on relationship clarification
*   **Attractiveness:** Transparency attracts the best talent

## What Does It Look Like in Practice?

**Before:** "Vasya, handle marketing. Masha, you're in charge of finances."

**After:**

ROLE: Marketer
MANDATE:

- Develop content plan

- Launch advertising up to £1000/month

- Analyze reach metrics
RESPONSIBILITY:

- Attracting 100 leads/month

- Website conversion 3%+
INTERACTION:

- Budget approval with Financial Officer

- Receiving content from Methodologist


**The difference is like between a verbal agreement and a legal contract.**

## Why Is This a Breakthrough?

1.  **Universality:** One language for any projects
2.  **Transparency:** Everyone sees all rules
3.  **Automation:** Can build software based on it
4.  **Succession:** New leaders understand the system in an hour

## Who's Already Using It?

*   Educational projects — for describing teacher roles
*   Environmental initiatives — for coordinating volunteers
*   Social startups — for attracting investors (they love structure!)

**They stopped "agreeing verbally." They operate by protocol.**
