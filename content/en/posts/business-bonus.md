## 📊 How funds are used

We believe in absolute transparency. Here is how the funds are distributed:

### 🛠️ 70% — Platform development
- **New feature development:** gamification, referral system, integrations
- **Technical infrastructure:** hosting, domains, CDN, security
- **User support:** documentation, training materials
- **Internationalization:** translations into 7 languages, localization

### 🎯 20% — Marketing and growth
- **Attracting new communities:** NGOs, startups, local initiatives
- **Content marketing:** articles, case studies, research on institutionalization
- **Partnerships:** collaboration with other platforms and services
- **Events:** online meetings, webinars, hackathons

### 💼 10% — Administrative expenses
- **Legal support:** UK LTD maintenance, compliance
- **Accounting:** reports, taxes, audits
- **Operational expenses:** team tools, communication

---

## 📈 Our principles of financial transparency

1. **Quarterly reports:** We publish detailed expense reports in the blog
2. **Development priority:** Minimal administrative costs, maximum on product
3. **Community decides:** Major expenses (over €1,000) are discussed with active participants
4. **Reserve fund:** 10% of all proceeds go to a stability fund for 6 months
5. Verification of the intended purpose of expenses in the public space is carried out by the Board of Trustees of the Public Association "Children of War"

## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Who do I actually send money to?

> Our company works exclusively with the WhyDonate platform as a payment processor. No other options are provided at this stage of our project development.

### 📊 Where can I see reports?

> Financial reports are prepared quarterly and are available in our blog for project participants.  
The report archive is stored in a closed section, access is granted to donors via GitHub.

### 💰 What if the project fails?

> We are already ensuring sustainability through the OECXAB service (website creation). These funds go toward platform development. Even if growth is slow, the platform will continue to operate.

### 🌍 What if I am from another country?

> Support works from any country. WhyDonate accepts most currencies and cards.

### ⚖️ Are there legal guarantees?

> As a UK LTD, we provide all necessary documents for institutional partners. For micro‑support via WhyDonate, your contributions are treated as donations with possible symbolic thank‑you perks, not as investment contracts.
