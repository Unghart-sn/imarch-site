## Trust — Not a Slogan, but Architecture

We don't consider trust a matter of faith or "word-of-mouth" reputation.  
For us, trust is the result of a correctly built system.

That's why Vis Realitatis is initially designed as a **transparent and verifiable structure**.

---

## Principle 1. Staging Instead of Promises

We don't raise funds "for everything at once."

Each project stage:
- has a clear goal,
- limited timeline,
- understandable budget,
- specific KPIs,
- mandatory completion report.

A new stage isn't launched until the previous one is completed and documented.

---

## Principle 2. Transparent Financial Logic

We show in advance:
- what exactly funds will support,
- what expense items are planned,
- what payments are fixed (including experts),
- what results should be obtained.

For us, donor money is responsibility, not a resource "on trust."

---

## Principle 3. Documenting Every Step

All key project actions:
- methodological decisions,
- pilot events,
- developments,
- reports,

are recorded and formatted as verifiable artifacts.

This eliminates "result blurring" and allows returning to each stage.

---

## Principle 4. Ethics of Working with Beneficiaries

We consciously:
- avoid story dramatization,
- don't use pain as marketing,
- work only with voluntary participation and consents.

First — real help and pilot.  
Public stories — only after and with participant permission.

---

## Principle 5. Scaling Limitation

The project **doesn't grow faster than quality can be maintained**.

If the system cannot handle the load:
- we don't expand coverage,
- don't increase fundraising,
- don't connect new regions.

Sustainability is more important to us than speed.

---

## What This Means for the Donor

- You understand where and why your contribution is directed.
- You can see stage results, not promises.
- You don't fund an "idea," you support a verifiable process.

---

## Our Basic Rule

**First — structure.  
Then — trust.  
And only after that — scaling.**

This is how we build Vis Realitatis.
