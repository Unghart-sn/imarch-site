## 🌟 Project Mission

The **Vis Realitatis** project creates a sustainable system of psycho-emotional and bodily support for veterans and their families based on the proprietary **SunLife-Massage®** methodology and digital open-reporting infrastructure.

We combine modern body practices, social technologies, digital transparency, and training to restore people's sense of control, reduce stress levels, and form a living mutual aid community.

{{< morebutton target="https://imarch.sbs/en/sunlife-massage/" text="ABOUT THE METHOD" >}}

---

## ⚠️ Scale of the Problem

- Veterans and their families face chronic stress, PTSD, isolation, and lack of accessible support tools.  
- Traditional assistance methods are often unavailable, too expensive, or do not solve the problem in everyday life.  
- Veterans' families bear the burden not only emotionally but also economically — and are often left alone with the stress.

The problem requires **scalable, accessible, and evidence-based solutions**, based on training, community, and transparency.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

We create a **three-level support system**:

1. **Experimental Work** — assistance to veterans, data collection, protocol adaptation.  
2. **Couple Training** — preparation of family and colleague pairs for "for home, for family" practice.  
3. **Community + Gamification** — a mutual aid network that operates without constant financial investments.

The core connecting element of the project is **software** that provides:

- unified participant profile;  
- referral system;  
- session and activity tracking;  
- gamification;  
- Git reporting;  
- transparent data for donors.

---

## 🧩 Why Our Model Works

- Every dollar creates a **double effect**: immediate assistance + long-term autonomous network.  
- A trained couple conducts **dozens of free sessions**, creating a multiplier effect.  
- Software ensures full transparency so the donor sees **where every minute and every dollar goes**.

This is not a service project.  
This is an **infrastructure of care** project.

---

## 🎯 First Stage Goals (6–12 months)

- Launch of the renovation center.  
- Conducting pilot sessions for veterans.  
- Training **50 family couples**.  
- Preparing the first group of facilitators.  
- Launch of the MVP version of the software core.  
- Formation of a mutual aid network and Git reporting.

---

## 📈 Potential Scale

The system develops horizontally:  
each new couple creates a local "node of sustainability" — a family, home, mini-community.

In perspective, the model can be adapted:

- for different regions;  
- for NGOs;  
- for schools and work collectives;  
- for international rehabilitation projects;  
- for franchise of personal studios and mobile offices.

---

## 🤝 Why the Project Deserves Support

- We offer an **evidence-based humanitarian tool**.  
- We create **scalable infrastructure**, not a one-time service.  
- We ensure **full transparency and digital reporting**.  
- We form a **long-term mutual aid network**.

Your contribution becomes **sustainable social capital** that continues to work even after funding ends.
