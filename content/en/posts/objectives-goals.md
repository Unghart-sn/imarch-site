The Vis Realitatis project creates a sustainable humanitarian infrastructure around the **SunLife-Massage®** method, combining rehabilitation, training, family support, and digital transparency.

Goals are distributed across three levels: short-term, medium-term, and long-term — in accordance with international project management protocols.

---

# 🟦 Short-term Goals (0–12 months)

### 1. Launch of the Vis Realitatis Pilot Center
- conducting 150 experimental sessions for veterans;  
- collecting scientific data, forming primary research metrics;  
- adapting protocols based on pilot results.

### 2. Training the First Family Couples
- preparing **50 massage couples** at the "For Family" level;  
- forming the first barter mutual aid nodes;  
- creating methodological materials and safety guidelines.

### 3. Training "Profi"-Level Facilitators
- training **10 facilitators** capable of leading groups and couples;  
- involving facilitators in research work.

### 4. Launch of MVP Digital Infrastructure
- profile (mask), referral system, GitChronicle;  
- session tracking, first badges, trust system;  
- integration with landing page and donor reporting.

---

# 🟩 Medium-term Goals (12–24 months)

### 1. Scaling to 3 Regions
- launching regional Vis Realitatis nodes;  
- training **150 new couples**;  
- expanding the facilitator network to 30 people.

### 2. SunLife-Massage® Online School
- video course + exams + supervision;  
- certification at all levels: "For Self", "For Family", "Profi";  
- automating the learning process through a platform.

### 3. Expanding the Research Block
- publications in specialized journals;  
- international testing of protocols;  
- preparing documentation for EU/US certification.

### 4. Community and Barter Economy
- launching a global community;  
- developing a reputation system;  
- 10,000 recorded barter sessions.

---

# 🟥 Long-term Goals (3–5 years)

### 1. International Vis Realitatis Network
- centers in Europe and the US;  
- unified specialist training standard;  
- international partnerships and scientific consortia.

### 2. SunLife-Massage® Personal Studio Franchise
- ready franchise package;  
- mobile cabins;  
- regional studios operating under a unified protocol and tracking system.

### 3. Economic Sustainability
- self-financing through training, aggregator, and franchise;  
- minimal dependence on grants;  
- using the software as a SaaS platform for other NGOs.

### 4. Global Social Impact
- evidence-based reduction of stress levels in families;  
- strengthening family bonds;  
- forming a new culture of care "for home and for family."

---

# ✅ Outcome

The goal system ensures:

- predictable growth,  
- high transparency,  
- sustainable veteran support model,  
- and the ability to scale internationally.

Goals are formed according to the SMART standard and allow donors to see a clear project development trajectory.
