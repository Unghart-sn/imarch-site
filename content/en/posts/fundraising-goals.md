## 🎯 Why We Raise Funds

The **Vis Realitatis** project is built as a sustainable support system for veterans and their families.  
This requires resources directed at specific, verifiable tasks.

We fundamentally do not raise funds for "abstract help."  
Every contribution works within a clear framework and produces measurable outcomes.

---

## 🧩 Primary Funding Areas

### 1. 🏛️ Launch and Operation of the Renovation Center

Funds are allocated for:
- Organizing space for practices;
- Conducting pilot programs with veterans;
- Providing basic infrastructure for training and meetings.

The Center is the focal point of the entire system.

---

### 2. 💻 Development of the Project's Digital Core

A digital platform is essential for:
- Tracking participants and practices;
- Transparent reporting to donors;
- Surveys and data collection;
- Supporting gamification and referral logic.

Without software, the project cannot be scaled or monitored effectively.

---

### 3. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Training of Couples and Specialists

Funding enables:
- Training spouse and partner pairs;
- Preparing facilitators and method instructors;
- Launching barter-based mutual aid practices.

One trained pair creates dozens of free sessions for others.

---

### 4. 🔬 Research Metrics and Effectiveness Analysis

All project participants are involved in an ongoing research process:
- Collection of anonymized data;
- Analysis of condition dynamics;
- Publication of aggregated results.

This improves the methodology's quality and builds trust in the project.

---

## 📊 How We Measure Effectiveness

For us, results are not merely a report on "funds spent," but rather:
- Number of trained pairs;
- Count of sessions conducted;
- Sustainability of the community without additional funding;
- Growth in trust and participant engagement.

---

## 🤝 What This Means for Donors

By supporting the project, donors:
- Understand **exactly where their funds go**;
- See the logic behind resource allocation;
- Participate in building a long-term system, not just providing one‑time aid.

Every contribution is an investment in a care infrastructure that continues to work long into the future.

---

## ✅ Summary

The fundraising goals of the **Vis Realitatis** project are:
- Transparent;
- Structured;
- Focused on sustainable impact.

We are building a system, not merely addressing a temporary need.
