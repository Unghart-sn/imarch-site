> Imagine: every member of your consortium has a professional website. All websites are connected into a single network. Referral chains are preserved forever. Value-based content is available only to participants.

**This is not a dream. This is our platform.**

## The Problem of Fragmentation

**Currently:**
*   Everyone has their own website on different platforms
*   No unified branding
*   Referral connections are lost
*   Content is duplicated or contradictory
*   No synergy

**Result:** 1+1=1.5 instead of 1+1=3.

## Our Solution: A Unified Ecosystem

### 1. **Ready-to-Use Template Websites**
*   Professional templates
*   Automatic content population
*   Social media integration
*   Responsive design for all devices

**In 15 minutes — a ready member website.**

### 2. **Referral Matrix**
*   Lifetime preservation of connections
*   Automatic contribution tracking
*   Transparent payouts
*   Protection against "tail cutting"

**Your network grows — you receive a share from every level.**

### 3. **Content Hub**
*   Centralized knowledge base
*   Access based on participation level
*   Automatic updates
*   Copy protection

**Valuable content — only for your community.**

## How Does It Work Technically?

### For Participants:
1.  Register in the consortium
2.  Choose a website template
3.  Customize it (logo, colors, text)
4.  Get a ready website on your own domain
5.  Your website automatically integrates into the network

### For Consortium Leaders:
1.  Create a "head" website
2.  Invite participants
3.  Configure referral rules
4.  Upload exclusive content
5.  Manage the entire network from a single panel

## Business Model

### For Participants:
*   **Free:** Basic website + access to open content
*   **Paid:** Advanced features + exclusive content

### For Consortium:
*   **Percentage** from network transactions

**The network grows — everyone's income grows.**
