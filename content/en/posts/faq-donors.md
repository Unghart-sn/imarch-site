## What exactly will the raised funds support?

Funds from the current fundraiser are directed **exclusively to Stage 1** of the project:
- methodology development and validation,
- MVP organizational software creation,
- payment for initial expert consultations,
- prototypes (suit and mobile cabin),
- pilot testing.

---

## Why is fundraising divided into stages?

We consciously rejected the "raise everything at once" model.  
Staged approach allows:
- risk reduction,
- giving donors a clear progress picture,
- decision-making based on real results.

---

## How will I know the project is really being implemented?

Upon stage completion, we publish:
- financial summary,
- description of completed work,
- pilot photos and videos,
- brief analytical report.

Reporting is publicly available.

---

## What happens after Stage 1 completion?

After Stage 1 completion, the project moves to the next phase:
- practice scaling,
- training expansion,
- digital platform development,
- international partnership preparation.

The decision about the next stage is made **based on achieved KPIs**.

---

## Can I support the project other than with money?

Yes.  
The project is open to:
- expert participation,
- partnerships,
- informational support,
- organizational assistance.

---

## How is this project different from one-time charity?

Vis Realitatis creates a **sustainable system**, not one-time help.  
One investment forms a mechanism that continues working and helping further — for families, communities, and specialists.

---

## Where to address additional questions?

You can contact the project team through the feedback form or via contacts listed on the website.

We are open to dialogue.
