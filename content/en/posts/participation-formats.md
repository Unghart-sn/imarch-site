The **Vis Realitatis** project is open to participation from people with different possibilities, resources, and motivations.  
We have consciously built the system so that **every contribution — financial or non-financial — has meaning and is recorded**.

Below are the main participation formats.

---

## ❤️ Micro-donation

**For whom:**  
For those who want to support the project with any comfortable amount.

**How it works:**  
- you make a donation through a crowdfunding platform;
- funds are directed to a specific project stage (e.g., Stage 1);
- a report is published upon stage completion.

**Why it matters:**  
Even small contributions collected en masse enable launching real processes.

---

## 🎯 Targeted Donation

**For whom:**  
For donors who care about understanding **exactly what their funds support**.

**How it works:**  
- support for a specific expense item (methodology, software, prototypes);
- transparent recording in reporting;
- linking contribution to stage results.

**Result:**  
The donor sees a direct connection between their contribution and the achieved effect.

---

## 🤝 Partnership

**For whom:**  
For organizations and initiatives ready to support the project with resources, infrastructure, or expertise.

**Partnership formats:**  
- providing venues and premises;
- logistical and organizational support;
- technological partnership;
- informational support.

**Important:**  
Partnership is recorded as part of the ecosystem and reflected in the project's public materials.

---

## 🎓 Expert Participation

**For whom:**  
For specialists ready to contribute professionally to methodology development, research, and standards.

**Participation forms:**  
- consultations and review;
- participation in research and methodological blocks;
- supervision and results evaluation.

**Principle:**  
Expert participation is not one-time help, but a contribution to project sustainability and quality.

---

## 🌱 Volunteer Participation

**For whom:**  
For people ready to help with time, skills, and involvement.

**Examples:**  
- assistance in event organization;
- communication and participant support;
- participation in pilot activities.

**What matters:**  
Volunteer contribution is recognized and accounted for as part of the overall work.

---

## 🔎 Participation Transparency

Regardless of chosen format:
- every contribution is recorded;
- stage results are published;
- the logic of fund and resource distribution is open.

We build a project where **participation is not an anonymous gesture, but a conscious contribution to a common system**.

---

## ✅ Outcome

You can participate:
- as a donor,
- as a partner,
- as an expert,
- as a volunteer.

Every format matters.  
Every contribution has significance.  
Every participation becomes part of the shared architecture of trust.
