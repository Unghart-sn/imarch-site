## Why We Show "What's Next" in Advance

For us, it's important that the donor understands:  
their contribution is not the end, but the beginning of a process.

The Vis Realitatis project develops through a staged model, where each next step builds on the results of the previous one.

---

## Step 1. Completion of Stage 1 (12 weeks)

By the end of the current stage, the following will be ready:
- validated methodology,
- MVP organizational software,
- prototypes (suit and mobile cabin),
- pilot event,
- public donor report.

This is the project verification point.

---

## Step 2. Analysis and Result Documentation

After stage completion, the team:
- analyzes achieved KPIs,
- collects participant feedback,
- adjusts methodology and tools,
- publishes the final report.

Only after this is the decision about the next stage made.

---

## Step 3. Transition to Scaling

If Stage 1 goals are achieved, the project moves to:
- expanding training programs,
- connecting new specialists,
- developing the digital platform,
- launching additional regional pilots.

Each new stage has its own budget and goals.

---

## Step 4. Long-term Perspective

In the long-term logic, Vis Realitatis:
- forms a sustainable mutual aid network,
- creates a standard for practice replication,
- develops a digital trust ecosystem,
- opens possibilities for international partnerships.

---

## What This Means for the Donor

- Your contribution doesn't "dissolve" — it's recorded in results.
- Each stage has a beginning, completion, and report.
- The project doesn't expand until the previous step's viability is proven.

---

## The Principle We Stand On

We don't promise everything at once.  
We promise **to move honestly, step by step, and transparently**.

And this is how a sustainable project is built.
