### 1. **CIC (Community Interest Company) + LTD**
The perfect combination for social enterprise:
*   **CIC** — for missionary activities, grant applications, volunteering
*   **LTD** — for commercial activities, investments, scaling

**Advantages:**
*   Legal handling of donations
*   Ability to attract investments
*   Transparent reporting
*   Protection of founders' personal liability

Ready-made authority distribution schemes:
*   Operational mandate (day-to-day decisions)
*   Strategic mandate (development direction)
