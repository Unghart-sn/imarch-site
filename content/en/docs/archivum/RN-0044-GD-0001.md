#### 1. MANIFESTO

#### 1.1. Why the archive exists

Genesis Vault is the sovereign archive of the IMARCH visual standard, in which
every digital artefact exists as a registered institutional object rather than
as an image. The archive was founded to record the transition from the retail
digital market to the institutional transaction.

#### 1.2. The formula of sovereignty

> **Hic stilus est standardum visualis sui iuris IMARCH LTD UK.**

This standard is the sovereign visual standard of IMARCH LTD UK and is
subject to no external platform, no external blockchain and no external
registry. Every element of the standard — style, artefact, licence, hash —
exists within a single institutional system.

{{< steel_button link="/ru/verdictum/vr-0020/" text="Verdictum Records" >}}

&nbsp;

#### 1.3. A change of paradigm

The traditional retail market for digital art follows the logic of the print
run: mass issuance, a low threshold, speculative turnover. Genesis Vault
follows the opposite logic: strictly limited issuance, a high threshold of
entry, institutional recording of ownership.

There are no tokens here. There is no speculative turnover. There is a
document, a seal, a hash, a licence and a passport — that is, precisely what
the institutional buyer already knows how to read.

#### 1.4. What the archive records

- Diploma of Style (Diploma Stili) — the visual certificate of each style.
- Lot passport — the legal and technical document of each artefact.
- Cryptographic fingerprint — the SHA-256 of each master file.
- Licence of title — the type of rights transferred. All transfers of rights within the series fall under the umbrella category **Collector & Commercial Rights**, which covers three licence models: *Collector License* (personal collecting), *Commercial & Publication License* (commercial use and publication) and *Exclusive Transfer* (full assignment of exclusive rights). A detailed description of the models is given in the style multi-passport, section 3, and in the Glossary of the RN-0044 corpus.
- Authorial provenance — the source of the hand and of the discipline of form.

#### 1.5. What the archive does not do

Genesis Vault is not a financial instrument. It does not provide for secondary
token trading. It is not connected to external blockchain networks. It is not
an investment product. The archive is an institutional attestation of
authorship, form and authenticity.

by a visual certificate (Diploma Stili). The certificate format is 9:16 within
an overall 3:2 banner. The vertical records the artefact as object; the
horizontal, as environment. The ratio expresses an institutional stance: the
artefact is inscribed within space, not cut out of it.

#### 4. MONOGRAPHS · MONOGRAPHIAE

#### 4.1. Format and concept

Each monograph in the series is a **sovereign digital artefact** of institutional
standing. We have dispensed with loss-making physical logistics, printing costs
and paper bindings in favour of a clean, protected digital standard.

* **Digital standard:** The monograph is issued as a structured document in **PDF (A4, single-sided)**, optimised both for on-screen study and, where required, for immediate local printing on the user's own printer.
* **Institutional status:** The document combines the visual sequence, technical verification and legal force, recording the owner's rights in a closed registry.

---

#### 4.2. Structure of the digital monograph

Each issue of the monograph follows a single standard and comprises the following mandatory elements:

1. **Title page:** the unique registration number of the lot, the node identifier (`node_id`) and the owner's marker.
2. **Lot passport:** a detailed legal and technical document describing the parameters of the artefact.
3. **Master reproduction:** the digital object at high resolution (4K / 9:16 format).
4. **Cryptographic trace:** the official **SHA-256** hash, establishing priority in the VERDICTUM deposit registry.
5. **Protective QR seal:** an interactive cryptographic element. Scanning the QR code redirects to a protected verification node, confirming the authenticity of the document and providing a direct interface for the management of rights or for a purchase request.
6. **Licence block:** the official agreement on the transfer of rights (*Collector & Commercial Rights*) under the legal regime of *UK Trade Secrets Regulations 2018*.

The monograph serves not as a decorative booklet, but as a **comprehensive
digital attestation**, guaranteeing the immutability, transparency and legal
protection of every lot in the series.

<a id="section-5"></a>
#### 5. CRYPTOGRAPHIC REGISTRY · REGISTRUM

#### 5.1. Principle

Genesis Vault employs cryptographic recording without blockchain technology,
tokens or external decentralised networks. The principle is expressed in the
formula:

> **'Blockchain without crypto'** — the notarial function of cryptography
> without the speculative function of the market.

Each master file of Series I is recorded with a SHA-256 fingerprint at the
moment of issue. The fingerprint is immutable: a change of a single bit in the
file's contents destroys the hash and makes any further verification impossible.

#### 5.2. What the registry provides

- Provenance — the public history of the issue of each lot.
- Authenticity — a guarantee that the master file matches the original bit for bit.
- Immutability — the impossibility of substituting a file without destroying its hash.
- Verifiability — the ability to verify a file in a browser without third-party services.

#### 5.3. What the registry does not do

- It is not a financial instrument.
- It does not provide for secondary token trading.
- It is not connected to external blockchain networks.
- It is not an investment product.
- It yields no speculative return.

#### 6. AUTHORIAL PROVENANCE · PROVENANTIA AUCTORIS

#### 6.1. Source and authorship · Genesis

The author of the visual standard and founder of IMARCH LTD (UK) is
**Oleksandr Purpurov**. His professional and theoretical method was formed at
the intersection of strict material discipline, historical reconstruction and
a foundational philosophy of form:

1. **Material canon and the jeweller's school:** Experience as lead designer at the international company *Skifskaya Etnika* (2010–2011), specialising in the plastic interpretation of historical Scythian prototypes in silver CAD and semi-precious stones. The decisive stage was the development of a signature collection based on the Tsarist Scythian pectoral, prepared for presentation at the Paris Biennale as the principal cultural event of Ukraine (the project was halted at the implementation stage owing to a casting defect for which the manufacturers were responsible). This experience laid the foundation of an uncompromising approach to construction: material and form tolerate no approximation, being subject to a strict canon.
2. **Artistic space and exhibitions:** Participant in the landmark exhibitions *Revelation of the Universe* (2004) at the Maestro Gallery (KhATOB, Kharkiv) and at the Cosmos Centre for Culture and Business (Simferopol).
3. **Theoretical foundation:** Author of the foundational monograph *Introduction to Formology: The Transfiguration of the Micro- and Macrocosm* (Kharkiv, Lego Press, 2007, 334 pp.), which laid the foundations of the discipline of universal formology and today commands collector value on the secondary market, with copies valued at up to $2,000.

**Foundational principles of the authorial method:**

* **Discipline of form:** Every line, proportion and weight is subordinate to the internal architecture of the object, not to momentary mood or stylisation.
* **Symbolism as load:** Sign and ornament never serve a purely decorative role — their function is to hold meaning, to fix memory and to structure space.

#### 6.2. Transition to the digital standard

The transfer of the jeweller's discipline into the digital environment occurred
not as a rejection of material but as its extension. The digital artefact in
the IMARCH standard is treated according to the same laws as a piece of
jewellery:

- mass and weight;
- patina and the trace of time;
- constructive logic;
- symbolic function;
- institutional attribution.

#### 6.3. The union of the engineering and the symbolic

IMARCH Studio works at the meeting point of two traditions:

- engineering architecture — systems, registries, verification, protocols;
- the school of enduring materials — metal, stone, leather, parchment, light.

Genesis Vault is the direct consequence of this union: an archive in which
technical rigour and symbolic load do not contradict one another but form a
single institutional shape.

Every page of the catalogue bearing a visual lot is equipped with an
individual **QR code**, serving as a protective cryptographic seal.

* **Function of the seal:** The QR code is not a mere link. It is a direct dynamic verifier, tied to the passport of the lot.
* **Scenario of interaction:** Scanning the QR code from a printed or digital A4 sheet redirects the user to a protected target page for the specific lot on the official web portal. On that page the system performs an instantaneous comparison of the local fingerprint with the reference registry and provides a direct interface for submitting a request for acquisition or for closing rights over the artefact in question, in accordance with the open number of the artefact in the limited series.

progressive scale, with a price increment of **50 units** for each successive
