## 1. Foundational Linkage

IMARCH LTD UK establishes Veritatis Silentis CIC as a mission-driven form operating under UK law. The CIC is created to formalize the humanitarian architecture **PLATAN+** and the public formula of action developed by IMARCH.

---

## 2. Role of Veritatis Silentis CIC

Veritatis Silentis CIC serves as:

- The institutional axis of the humanitarian mission  
- A legal bridge between IMARCH LTD and the PLATAN+ architecture  
- A public scene for the implementation of models, declarations, and digital forms of action

---

## 3. Architecture of Linkage

- **IMARCH LTD UK** — a registered company acting as the source and founder  
- **Veritatis Silentis CIC** — a mission-driven form formalizing public benefit  
- **PLATAN+** — a humanitarian architecture implemented through the CIC

This linkage is not corporate (i.e. not parent/subsidiary), but mission-based and institutional.
