## 1. Company Name  
Veritatis Silentis CIC *(“Truth is Silent”)*

---

## 2. Legal Form  
A Community Interest Company registered under the Companies Act 2006, operating as a company limited by guarantee.

---

## 3. Company Purpose  
The purpose of the company is to act in the interest of the public by implementing the humanitarian architecture **PLATAN+** through public, educational, and institutional forms.

---

## 4. Poetic Declaration (Preamble)

> Truth is silent, but it acts.  
> We do not adapt PLATAN+ — we translate it into law.  
> We do not create a company — we present a title.

---

## 5. Asset Lock  
- The company shall not distribute profits to its members  
- All income shall be used to advance the mission  
- In case of dissolution, assets shall be transferred to another CIC or charitable organisation

---

## 6. Membership and Guarantee  
- Each member undertakes to contribute up to £1 in the event of dissolution  
- Members shall not receive any private financial benefit

---

## 7. Directors  
- Minimum of one director  
- A director may be an individual or a representative of IMARCH LTD  
- Directors must act in alignment with the public-benefit mission

---

## 8. Linkage with IMARCH LTD  
- IMARCH LTD UK acts as founder and affiliated structure  
- The linkage is not corporate, but mission-based  
- Veritatis Silentis CIC serves as the public axis of the PLATAN+ architecture

---

## 9. Meetings and Decisions  
- Decisions are made by simple majority  
- All actions must align with the CIC’s mission

---

## 10. Amendments  
- Amendments are permitted only if the mission and asset lock are preserved  
- Any changes must be approved by the CIC Regulator
