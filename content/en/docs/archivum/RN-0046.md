description="Official Know-How Act of IMARCH LTD recording the production methodology of the visual styles of Series I as an object of legal protection under the trade secret regime. The document describes not individual artifacts, but the process of their creation, edition and passportization. The methodology consists of four sequential stages: (1) from idea to master file — determination of the style through the summary system passport RN-0044-PS-0000, work with base references, creation of the master file and fixation of SHA-256; (2) from master file to edition — formation of a limited edition of 10 copies with inheritance of the master file's SHA-256 and fixation of the copy number (Edition Index) in the public registry; (3) from edition to passport — formation of a multi-passport of the style (RN-0044-PS-00NN) with two contours: public demo WebP versions (6 artifacts) and private master files (6 artifacts), two sets of markers and two sets of SHA-256, with publication in the VERDICTUM registry; (4) role of base references — passportized paintings of Series I as an integral part of the method, without which it is impossible to verify the compliance of a new artifact with the canon of the style, to reproduce the specificity of the style, to train an executor to work in the style, or to link a new artifact to the history of the series. The Act establishes the legal protection regime, the procedure for applying the method when creating new artifacts in an existing style and when expanding the system (creating Series II, III, etc.), as well as the connection to the VERDICTUM registry and other acts of the ecosystem (RN-0032, RN-0044, RN-0047). The annexes include the list of 12 multi-passports of Series I and the consolidated table of markers (12 styles × 6 artifacts = 72 artifacts). Protection extends to the production methodology as an integrated institutional system, independent of any specific technological implementation."
