####  CONSOLIDATED SYSTEM PASSPORT

### Series I

<a id="section-1"></a>
#### 1. System identification

**Code:** RN-0044-PS-0000
**Series:** I
**Name:** The IMARCH system of visual standards
**Type:** institutional system document
**Base structure:** the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix

**Composition of the system:**

12 visual styles of Series I (I-1 … I-12), each set out in a separate multi-passport of the format RN-0044-PS-00NN.

**The open facet of the system:**

The numerical index of the classical Chinese meridian system ('Zi Wu Liu Zhu', 子午流注) — the circadian cycle of 12 meridians.

**The closed facets of the system:**

The remaining five facets of the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix are treated in the concept of 'Universal Formology' and constitute know-how accessible under NDA.

---

<a id="section-2"></a>
#### 2. Conceptual core

The core principle of the system:

> **The 12 visual styles of Series I form not a list of directions, but a closed structural system, in which each style occupies a fixed position within the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix.**

The system is built on three levels:

**The first level — the numerical index.**
The 12 styles are mapped to the 12 classical Chinese meridians in the circadian sequence 'Zi Wu Liu Zhu'. This gives the system a **clear sequence**, rather than an arbitrary set.

**The second level — the structural models.**
Three equally weighted models (the colour wheel, the Five Phases, the clock face) describe **one and the same cycle** from different angles. They do not contradict one another; they complement one another.

**The third level — the cubic matrix.**
The Chinese matrix of meridians is **one facet** of the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix. The remaining five facets are treated in the concept of 'Universal Formology' (NDA). The cube as a whole is not a square, but a **six-faceted structure**.

**The principal criterion of truth for the system:**
Not the antiquity of the form, but the **result of the practical application** of the proposition within the methodical onboarding of the practical application of knowledge.

---

<a id="section-3"></a>
#### 3. The three reference models

#### 3.1. The colour wheel

The 12 styles are arranged in a circle:

- **Adjacent styles** — complementary in aesthetic, with a smooth transition.
- **Styles six apart** — opposite, contrasting in aesthetic vector.
- **Styles three apart** — neighbouring sectors, moderate contrast.
- **Styles four apart** — a third-order axis, functional opposition.

The colour wheel describes the **aesthetic compatibility** of the styles: which styles may be combined in a single project without visual conflict.

#### 4. The IRSHOM Cubic Matrix

The **IRSHOM Cubic Matrix** is a conceptual structure in which the 12 styles of Series I are considered as **one of six facets**.

**The open facet** — the Chinese matrix of 12 meridians (the circadian cycle 'Zi Wu Liu Zhu'). It is disclosed in the present document.

**The five closed facets** — treated in the concept of 'Universal Formology', they constitute know-how accessible under NDA.

**The cube as a whole** — the structural basis of the system, irreducible to any one of its facets.

**A practical consequence:** the system does not claim the absolute completeness of a single facet. The Chinese matrix is beautiful and self-sufficient, but structurally it is a **flat matrix of the magic square**, whereas the system operates at the level of a **cubic structure**. The remaining five facets complement the open one, allowing the system **not to be confined within a truncated Chinese variant**, but to preserve its completeness.

#### 9. Rules for extending the system

The system of 12 styles **is not closed forever**. The rules for extension are:

#### 9.1. Extension within the open facet

If an **additional level** is identified within the Chinese matrix of 12 meridians (for example, 12 meridians × 2 = 24), the system may be extended to **24 styles** without disrupting its structure. Each new style will occupy a **fixed position** within the extended facet.

#### 9.2. Extension through new facets

The disclosure of a **new facet** of the IRSHOM Cubic Matrix (through Universal Formology) yields a **new set of styles** — not replacing, but **complementing** the existing one. Such a set is presented as a **separate series** (for example, Series II, Series III) with its own consolidated passport.

#### 9.3. The condition of extension

Any extension of the system is permitted **only on the following conditions**:

- the new style has a **fixed position** within the cubic matrix;
- the new style **does not contradict** the existing structural regularities;
- the new style **corresponds** to the criteria of section 8;
- the new style is **entered** into the VERDICTUM registry with a full multi-passport.

#### 9.4. Prohibition of arbitrary extension

**The arbitrary addition of styles without structural justification is prohibited.** The system is not a collection of aesthetics, but a **closed structural matrix**. Extension is permitted only under rules 9.1–9.3.

### 10. The methodological position on tradition

The system of 12 visual styles is built **with an awareness of the base** and **in accordance with the regularity** of the circadian cycle of the classical Chinese meridians.

**The position of the system:**

1. **The system is not a formal logic derived from an arbitrary number.** It is created with an awareness of the base.
2. **The system is not a literal transposition of the Chinese tradition.** Authenticity and endemicity belong rather to the domain of anthropology than to that of mathematics and medicine, which are the system's priorities.
3. **The system is not a local-history museum but a full-cycle production process** with its own list of practical tasks.
4. **The principal criterion of truth** is not the antiquity of the form, but the **result of the practical application** of the proposition within the methodical onboarding of the practical application of knowledge.
5. **Secondary analysis** (colour coding, structural-elemental decomposition, a finite number of elements, the fixed specificity of their systemic organisation) may be carried out as a **separate analytical work** — but this is already a matter of **operational nuance for the technical tuning of attention parameters** during conscious visual scanning of an image.

**A practical consequence:** the system operates not at the level of a single facet, but at the level of the **cubic matrix as a whole**, which allows it to preserve its completeness and not lose itself in a truncated variant, however beautiful the Chinese model may be.
