## I. Preamble

This Memorandum establishes the institutional linkage between:

- **IMARCH LTD UK** (hereinafter — LTD), registered in the jurisdiction of the United Kingdom  
- **Veritatis Silentis OU** (hereinafter — OU), registered in the jurisdiction of Estonia  

The document defines mission objectives, interaction protocols, participation titles, and implementation mechanisms within the PLATAN+ architectural cluster, operating as an instrumental node of the discipline **AISCXEN-8888**.

---

## II. Foundation

The PLATAN+ Memorandum is based on the following documents and acts:

- Charter of IMARCH LTD UK (Edition 1.0)  
- Charter of Veritatis Silentis OU (Edition 1.0)  
- Constitution of the Empire of Truth and Silence (Edition 1.0)  
- Succession Act under CMU Resolution No. 1095  
- Archival materials of the PLATAN program (1993–2025)

---

## III. Mission Objectives

- Support and development of the discipline AISCXEN-8888  
- Formalization of architectural thinking and cognitive-semiotic protocols  
- Emission of participation titles, missions, and scenes  
- Curation of humanitarian, educational, and cultural initiatives  
- Creation of normative linkage between subjects acting within PLATAN+

---

## IV. Structure of the PLATAN+ Cluster

PLATAN+ includes the following institutes:

- Institute of Cognitive Architecture  
- Institute of Legal Scenarios  
- Institute of Architectonic Language  
- Institute of Formological Diplomacy  
- Institute of Practical Arts  
- Institute of Universal Treatises  
- Institute of Educational Scenes  

Each institute operates autonomously within a unified formological linkage.

---

## V. Interaction Protocol

All actions follow a closed-cycle structure:  
**initiation → implementation → verification → distribution → archiving**

Interaction between LTD and OU is carried out through:

- mirrored charters  
- internal acts  
- linkage of appendices  
- digital titles and sigils  

Verification protocols are formalized as internal acts and public publications.

---

## VI. Editorial Policy

- This Edition 2.0 enters into force upon registration of both parties' charters  
- All clarifications and additions are permitted only in the direction of expansion, not reduction or cancellation  
- Full validity is confirmed by the **Act of Validation Completeness**, signed by both parties
