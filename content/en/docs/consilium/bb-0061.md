This is the first digital artifact of series **I-11** — imperial neoclassicism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0011-11.1`.

## On the concept

A man in a blue suit works at a laptop in a dark study. A brass lamp, an
inkwell with a quill and leather-bound volumes form a material setting in
which the modern tool and the imperial tradition exist as one institutional
form. The painterly texture of the background and the deep blue tones sustain
the monumental character of the image.

*Majesty that has become work.*
