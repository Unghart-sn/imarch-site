This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-5** — Verdictum style.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0005-5.6`.

## On the concept

At the center — an open book with golden engraving and a hovering quill;
above the book — a golden "document" emblem. On the left — a marble
inkwell-seal, on the right — a scroll on a stand. Along the edges — marble
columns, around — orbital golden lines. The composition presents law as an
eternal text fixed in material and form.

*Law that has become a text.*

## Technical specifications

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** white marble, gold, cream, warm light
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

## Context of creation

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-5, dedicated
to law as a material institutional system possessing form, weight and
cryptographic credibility. Series limit — 10 copies. Each owner receives a
separate passport as an annex to the base multi-passport of the series.
