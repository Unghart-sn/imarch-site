This is the third digital artifact of series **I-6** — the official ceremonial
memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0006-6.3`.

## On the concept

A bronze relief portrait of a man occupies the left part of the composition;
on the right — engraved inscriptions "IN MEMORIAM" and "MAGNUS DOLOREM ET
DESIDERIUM". In the corners — laurel branches in an ornamental frame. The
composition is built according to the canon of a memorial plaque: the name,
the image and the formula of memory are joined in a single institutional act.

*Memory that has become a formula.*
