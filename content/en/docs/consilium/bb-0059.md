This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-10** — the business portrait.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0010-10.5`.

## On the concept

Five women in business suits are gathered around a table with a model of a
skyscraper. Blueprints, a laptop, a glass of water — the instruments of design
work; behind them — a design drawing in a frame. The composition continues the
canon of the group business portrait, translating it into the sphere of
creation and architecture.

*Project that has become assembly.*
