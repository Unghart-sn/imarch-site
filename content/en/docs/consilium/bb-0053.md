This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-9** — the secret cosmo
laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0009-9.5`.

## On the concept

A round portal reveals two spheres: on the left — a blue starry abyss, on the
right — a red-fiery one. At the center — a pharaoh with a staff, whose figure
joins both mediums. Before the portal stands an old man with a cane. The
composition is built on the principle of axial symmetry: mystery and control,
abyss and fire, antiquity and system — in a single circle.

*The abyss that has become a portal.*
