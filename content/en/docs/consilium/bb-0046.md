This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-8** — the Classical Revival.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0008-8.4`.

## On the concept

A man in a white coat with a smartphone sits at a modern table; behind him —
a large painting of an alchemist-monk at books and flasks. The composition is
built on the contrast between two layers: the laboratory present and the
painterly past, joined by the idea of research.

*Research that has become continuity.*
