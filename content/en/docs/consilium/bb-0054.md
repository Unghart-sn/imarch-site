This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-9** — the secret cosmo
laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0009-9.6`.

## On the concept

An operator in a cap and uniform with crossed arms stands before an enormous
hologram of a pharaoh inscribed in a blue-magenta neon interface. Data
panels, digital schemes and icons form the setting of a secret laboratory.
The composition is built on the contrast between the living observer and the
digital object: control and observation as functions of the system.

*Control that has become observation.*

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** neon blue, magenta, dark metal, gold
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry
