This is the second digital artifact of series **I-8** — the Classical Revival.

## On the concept

A girl with a guitar hovers on a cloud surrounded by putti playing musical
instruments. The composition continues the Renaissance tradition of celestial
concerts: a sacred space where music becomes a form of institutional harmony.
Soft light and earthy tones sustain the painterly canon of the Renaissance.

*Harmony that has become sky.*
