This is the third digital artifact of series **I-4** — academic romanticism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0004-4.3`.

## On the concept

The hands of a researcher in a dark sleeve with a white cuff work with a
manuscript: a magnifying glass, a quill and spectacles — instruments of
academic analysis. A bronze figure of Athena with a shield and a spear against
the background of a candle and a stack of books introduces into the
composition the layer of institutional patronage of knowledge. The candlelight
brings out the text, emphasizing the concentration of the moment.

*Wisdom that has become a method.*
