## On the concept

A cybernetic pharaoh in a golden helmet-mask with luminous blue eyes is placed
against a starry field pierced by blue schematic lines. The ancient sacred
image and the digital network are joined into a single form: the mask becomes
a terminal, the gaze — a communication channel. The composition is frontal and
symmetrical, giving the object the character of an icon of the system.

*The sacred that has become a scheme.*
