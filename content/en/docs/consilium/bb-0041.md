This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-7** — magazine beauty
glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0007-7.5`.

## On the concept

A woman's profile in golden filigree hovers above a flask "L'OR ÉTERNEL";
around — pearl spheres, roses and silk draperies. The composition joins the
portrait tradition of Art Deco and a premium object, creating the image of a
scent as a celestial substance. Warm radiance and soft light transitions
sustain the magazine aesthetic.

*Scent that has become sky.*
