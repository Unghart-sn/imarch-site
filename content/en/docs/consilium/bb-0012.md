This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-2** — the antique bonus
boutique (researcher's cabinet / elite heritage). Registered in the IMARCH LTD
registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0002-2.6`.

## On the concept

A girl in a velvet dress with gold embroidery holds a luminous heart above an
open book. Around her — armillaries, candles, brass instruments, folios and
maps forming the cabinet setting of the researcher. The composition is built
on the contrast between the warm glow of the heart and the dark depth of the
cabinet.

*Knowledge that has become light.*

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** burgundy, gold, brass, dark wood, amber
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-2, dedicated

research and the modern sovereign contour form a single repository of values.
