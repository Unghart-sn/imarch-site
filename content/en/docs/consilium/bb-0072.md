This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-12** — digital minimalism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0012-12.6`.

## On the concept

A face is presented as an anatomical scheme with drawn muscles; hands in
gloves work with instruments at the face. In the background — circular scales,
organ schemes and molecular structures. The composition is built on the
principle of a scientific illustration: the body becomes an object of precise
research and institutional knowledge.

*Body that has become a map.*

## Technical specifications

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** cold blue, sterile white, graphite
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

## Context of creation

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-12, dedicated
to precision as a visual form of sovereign institutional control over matter
and data. Series limit — 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate passport
as an annex to the base multi-passport of the series.
