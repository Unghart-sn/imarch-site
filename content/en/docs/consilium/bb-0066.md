This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-11** — imperial
neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0011-11.6`.

## On the concept

An elderly woman in a judge's robe sits at a table with an open book. A green
lamp, a portrait of a predecessor on the wall, bookcases, a glass of water
and spectacles create a setting of institutional authority. The composition
is built on the canon of the official portrait: central figure, restraint,
material setting.

*Law that has become dignity.*
