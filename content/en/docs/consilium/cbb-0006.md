#### Why Use a Digital Vault? { .fade-in-up }

In professional practice, valuable materials are often lost in disorder rather than through a lack of storage capacity. Documents become scattered across correspondence, local folders and different services, versions become confused, and important artefacts remain without a coherent storage system.

The Digital Vault provides a dedicated, managed environment for materials requiring organised storage and predictable access.

{{< ts-panel >}}
It is suitable for:

* **Timestamp Certificate and Artefact Passport holders.** For keeping registered materials, certificates, cryptographic identifiers, digital copies and supporting documentation together in one place.
* **Authors, researchers and professionals.** For storing essential manuscripts, diagrams, specifications, contracts, technical documentation and final versions without mixing them with drafts and everyday files.
* **Projects and participants within the IMARCH / Chronicle OS ecosystem.** For structured storage of working materials, protected packages, revisions, documentary records and associated objects.
* **Users who value organisation and control.** When the volume of material is modest but the value of each file is high, organised storage takes priority over unlimited space for media content.
{{< /ts-panel >}}

#### Storage Capacity and Expansion { .fade-in-up }

{{< ts-panel >}}
<div class="ts-panel-title">Base Storage · 100 MB</div>
An initial storage allowance for a compact collection of documents, certificates, specifications, digital copies, archives and working materials.

The base capacity allows you to begin using the Vault without purchasing additional storage in advance.
{{< /ts-panel >}}

{{< ts-panel >}}
<div class="ts-panel-title">Additional Storage · +100 MB for £5</div>
As your collection grows, storage can be expanded in successive increments of 100 MB.

Each additional increment costs £5 by subscription.

You add only the capacity you require, scaling your storage gradually in line with your current needs.
{{< /ts-panel >}}

{{< ts-panel >}}
<div class="ts-panel-title">Upon Non-Renewal</div>
If the subscription for additional storage is not renewed, the Digital Vault switches to Read and Download Mode.

Stored files remain available for viewing and downloading. You can retrieve your materials without having to renew the subscription immediately.

In this mode, no new files can be uploaded, and modifications to stored content are restricted in accordance with the applicable access conditions.
{{< /ts-panel >}}

#### Frequently Asked Questions { .fade-in-up }

{{< details "Is the Vault suitable for storing large videos and media libraries?" >}}
No. The Digital Vault is designed for compact storage of significant materials, including documents, certificates, digital copies, archives, technical specifications and protected packages.

It is not positioned as a mass media storage service or an unlimited drive for large video archives.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "What happens when I reach my storage limit?" >}}
Uploads exceeding your current storage allowance are suspended until an additional storage increment is activated.

You may increase your capacity by 100 MB for £5 by subscription.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "Will my files be deleted if I do not renew my additional storage subscription?" >}}
No. Upon non-renewal, the Vault switches to Read and Download Mode. Stored materials remain available for viewing and retrieval.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "Can I store Timestamp Certificates in the Vault?" >}}
Yes. The Vault is intended, among other purposes, for storing certificates, registration records, digital copies of registered materials and supporting documents.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "Can I expand my storage gradually?" >}}
Yes. Additional capacity is activated in increments of 100 MB. You may increase your available storage as required.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "Is the Digital Vault a backup of my computer?" >}}
No. It is a dedicated storage environment for digital materials. It does not replace a comprehensive backup, archival or data recovery system.
{{< /details >}}
