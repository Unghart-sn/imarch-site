This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-11** — imperial
neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0011-11.4`.

## On the concept

A woman with red hair works at a laptop in a bright impressionist setting.
The dense color brushstroke and the golden frame turn the scene into a
painterly canvas, where creation acts as a form of sovereign dignity. The
composition is built on the contrast between the calm figure and the active
painterly background.

*Creation that has become color.*
