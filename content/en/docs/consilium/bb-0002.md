This is the second digital artifact of series **I-1** — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier `RN-0044-PS-0001-1.2`.

## On the concept

A profile portrait of a cybernetic pharaoh — a bearer of code, not of a crown. Gold and biomechanics converge in a single figure: facial plate, hinged joints, integrated optical nodes. The staff with a lapis core is not a regalia but an interface. The background hieroglyphs are written as glowing code — knowledge that has become a protocol.

The contrast between cold blue glow and warm gold sets the principal emotional axis of the artifact: **eternal craft translated into a working system**.
