This is the first digital artifact of series **I-7** — magazine beauty glamour.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0007-7.1`.

## On the concept

A woman's profile is executed in dense golden filigree: the lines of the
coiffure, the face and the neck intertwine into a single ornamental stream.
Closed eyes and a dark background emphasize the serenity of the image, while
the inscriptions "AURORA" and "DARK LUXE" introduce it into a premium
magazine context. The composition is built on the contrast between a living
golden glow and deep shadow.

*Beauty that has become ornament.*
