This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-9** — the secret cosmo
laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0009-9.4`.

## On the concept

An operator in a cap and tie is turned toward a hologram with the profile of
a pharaoh; a cybernetic hand touches the image. The uraeus-cobra, the blue
eyes and the golden collar are joined with the hieroglyphs and numerals of
the background. The composition is built on the gesture of touch: the contact
between the human and the system appears as an act of access.

*Access that has become a ritual.*
