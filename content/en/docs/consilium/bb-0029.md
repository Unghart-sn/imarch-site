This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-5** — Verdictum style.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0005-5.5`.

## On the concept

A golden tree with a crystal-gold crown is crowned with a globe; at its base —
a marble shield with scales. On either side — a scroll with a seal and a
marble triumphal arch. Orbital lines connect the layers of the composition.
The tree acts as a metaphor of institutional continuity rooted in law.

*Law that has become a root.*
