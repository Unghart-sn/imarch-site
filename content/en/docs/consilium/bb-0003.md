This is the third digital artifact of series **I-1** — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier `RN-0044-PS-0001-1.3`.

## On the concept

A symmetrical scene of a collegium: the central pharaoh with the halo-disk of Aten, and four cybernetic satellites on either side. One of them passes fire — a gesture that reads simultaneously as ritual and as technology transfer.

Here I-1 works not through a single figure but through **the architecture of the scene**: hierarchy, modularity, repetition. The composition is built as a structural scheme, not as a portrait. This is why the artifact is the key to understanding the whole series: civilisation as a system of modules, each holding its own function.
