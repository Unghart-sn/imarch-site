This is the first digital artifact of series **I-5** — Verdictum style.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0005-5.1`.

## On the concept

An isometric composition is built around a marble handshake — blue and white
stone with golden veins — symbolizing a contract as a material act. Above it —
a golden globe, around it — orbital golden lines connecting an academic cap
with a diploma, marble "INS" plaques, a "CERTIFICATION" certificate, a "CCN"
medal and a model of an antique temple. Law is presented as a system
possessing form, weight and cryptographic credibility.

*Trust that has become matter.*
