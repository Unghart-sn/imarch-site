This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-6** — the official
ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under
identifier `RN-0044-PS-0006-6.4`.

## On the concept

A bronze portrait of a bearded man in spectacles is inscribed in a strict
rectangular frame with floral corners. A plaque "JOHN SMITH" in the
bottom right corner designates the name. Warm bronze light and a soft shadow
create the effect of the physical presence of the material rather than of the
image.

*A face that has become material.*

## Technical specifications

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** bronze, patina, dark brown, warm gold
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

## Context of creation

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-6, dedicated
to institutional memory, official honour and the material fixation of dignity.
Series limit — 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate passport as an annex
to the base multi-passport of the series.
