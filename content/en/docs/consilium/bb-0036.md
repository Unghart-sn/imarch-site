This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-6** — the official
ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under
identifier `RN-0044-PS-0006-6.6`.

## On the concept

A portrait of a man in imperial attire with a fibula is placed against the
background of a Byzantine mosaic with a laurel halo. Craquelure, losses and
traces of time emphasize the material antiquity of the image. The composition
is built according to the canon of the memorial portrait: the face, the symbol
and the setting are joined in a single institutional tradition.

*Memory that has become a mosaic.*
