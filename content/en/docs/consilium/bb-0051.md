This is the third digital artifact of series **I-9** — the secret cosmo
laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0009-9.3`.

## On the concept

In a laboratory, two researchers in white coats examine a pharaoh enclosed in
a glass capsule with a cold blue glow. On the left — a digital body scheme,
on the right — hieroglyphs on a holographic panel. The composition is built
on the principle of laboratory hierarchy: the object at the center, the
operators on the sides, the interfaces as witnesses.

*Knowledge that has become an object.*
