This is the first digital artifact of series **I-12** — digital minimalism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0012-12.1`.

## On the concept

A biomechanical head with closed eyes is placed at the center of the
composition; surgical hands in gloves work with instruments at its neck.
Behind — digital panels "NEURAL PATHWAY MONITOR" and "CYBERNETIC INTEGRATION",
a robotic manipulator and a precision value of 0.04 mm. The composition is
built on the contrast between sterile whiteness and cold blue light, turning
the operation into an act of institutional control.

*Precision that has become an operation.*

- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
