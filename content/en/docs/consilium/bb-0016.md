This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-3** — veteran post-war
realism (memory / hope / continuity). Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry
under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0003-3.4`.

## On the concept

A veteran in a helmet (a silhouette-shadow) and a veteran in civilian clothes
holding his head form a double portrait. A wife and a daughter with a teddy
bear complete the family group. Symbols of home and infinity remind of
continuity, while warm light brings out the faces, preserving the dignity of
the figures.

*Memory that has become both burden and support.*

## Technical specifications

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** sepia, ochre, dark green, cold blue
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

## Context of creation

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-3, dedicated
to the memory of destruction and the will to restoration as a single act of
testimony. Series limit — 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate passport
as an annex to the base multi-passport of the series.
