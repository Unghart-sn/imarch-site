#### Why timestamp fixation is needed { .fade-in-up }

In professional and creative activity it is often necessary to confirm that a specific piece of material already existed in a particular form before publication, transfer to third parties, the start of negotiations, subsequent revision or another significant event.

A timestamp certificate creates a verifiable record linked to the digital object and the moment of its registration.

{{< ts-panel >}}
It can be used to fix:

* manuscripts, texts, concepts and research materials;
* visual works, photographs, design and musical compositions;
* software code, technical diagrams, architectural solutions and specifications;
* calculations, analytical reports, project documents and interim results;
* versions of proposals, working materials and documents relating to a specific project stage.
{{< /ts-panel >}}

The purpose of the procedure is to create a verifiable time reference point that can later be used when comparing versions, confirming chronology and presenting documentary materials.

#### Verification and documentary applicability { .fade-in-up }

A timestamp certificate is intended to confirm registration data and to verify that the digital object matches the fixed state.

{{< ts-panel >}}
It can be used as additional documentary material when:

* comparing different versions of a digital object;
* confirming the sequence of development or creation of material;
* documenting interim results;
* transferring materials to partners, clients, experts or research organisations;
* preparing an evidence base within a specific legal or professional situation.
{{< /ts-panel >}}

#### Frequently Asked Questions { .fade-in-up }

{{< details "What does a Timestamp Certificate establish?" >}}
It provides a verifiable record associated with a digital object and a recorded timestamp. It may help establish that the corresponding digital data existed in a particular form no later than the recorded time, subject to the technical properties and validity of the timestamping procedure.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "Does the certificate prove authorship or ownership?" >}}
No. A timestamp certificate does not independently establish authorship, ownership, originality, intellectual property rights or the legal validity of a document. It may provide supporting evidence in conjunction with other records and evidence.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "Does IMARCH store my original file?" >}}
The original file is processed to generate its cryptographic hash. It is not retained in the infrastructure after the registration procedure has been completed.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "Can I register confidential material?" >}}
The standard registration procedure does not retain the original file after completion. Users handling highly confidential materials may also consider the Hash-Only Registration option, where available, which allows the registration of a pre-generated cryptographic hash without uploading the original file.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "Can the certificate be used in legal proceedings?" >}}
It may be presented as supporting evidence, subject to the applicable law, the nature of the dispute and the assessment of the relevant authority. The certificate is not a substitute for legal advice, expert examination or other evidence.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "Can I give a certificate to someone else?" >}}
Yes. A Gift Certificate is issued with a unique activation code that may be transferred to another person. The recipient activates the code through the website and uses the registration entitlement for their own digital object.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details "How many registrations are included in the Professional Pack?" >}}
The Professional Pack includes five independent registration entitlements for £90. Each registration may be used for a separate digital object or version.
{{< /details >}}
