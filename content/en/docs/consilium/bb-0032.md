This is the second digital artifact of series **I-6** — the official
ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under
identifier `RN-0044-PS-0006-6.2`.

## On the concept

A bronze relief bust of a moustached man is placed on a patinated plaque;
above it — the inscription "IN MEMORIAM", below it — a plaque with the name
and dates "ARTHUR V. HEMLOCK · 1878–1952". Warm directed light brings out the
face, while the deep shadow of the background gives the composition a
memorial restraint.

*Dignity that has become bronze.*
