This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-12** — digital minimalism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0012-12.4`.

## On the concept

A white android with closed eyes; a robotic manipulator directs a thin laser
beam at a point near the eye. Around — other manipulators, panels with DNA
and molecular structures. The composition is built on axial symmetry and on
the contrast between the white body and the blue glow, emphasizing
high-technology precision.

*Control that has become a beam.*

## Technical specifications

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** sterile white, cold blue, chrome metal
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

## Context of creation

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-12, dedicated
to precision as a visual form of sovereign institutional control over matter
and data. Series limit — 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate passport
as an annex to the base multi-passport of the series.
