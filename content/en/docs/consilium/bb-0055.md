This is the first digital artifact of series **I-10** — the business portrait.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0010-10.1`.

## On the concept

A man in a tweed suit adjusts a cufflink on his cuff. In the mirror behind him
— the reflection of a young workman with blueprints, which introduces into the
composition the layer of professional memory. The warm light of lamps and the
wooden panels of the study emphasize the material texture. The portrait is
built on the canon of the business image, where the personal gesture and the
institutional context are joined in a single action.

*Experience that has become form.*
