This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-10** — the business portrait.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0010-10.6`.

## On the concept

A grey-haired man in a dark suit sits at a table. Behind him — a dense wall of
certificates in frames; a window with twilight, a notebook and a pen on the
table. The composition is built on the canon of the official portrait: the
face, the documents and the institutional setting form a single image of
competence.

*Competence that has become testimony.*
