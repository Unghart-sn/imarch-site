This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-7** — magazine beauty
glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0007-7.6`.

## On the concept

A woman's profile is executed in the technique of repoussé gold against the
background of Art Deco ray panels. Laurel branches, crystal pendants and
stylized flowers frame the figure, while the terracotta-gold palette enhances
the relief materiality. The composition is built according to the canon of
Art Deco: central axis, symmetry, light flux.

*Beauty that has become relief.*
