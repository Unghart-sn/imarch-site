This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-1** — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier `RN-0044-PS-0001-1.4`.

## On the concept

A scene of registration: the pharaoh on the throne, Thoth with the ibis head keeping a record on a tablet, the goddess with the feather of Maat standing nearby as a witness. This is a direct visual parallel to the theme of provenance — **the act of fixation, the act of recording, the act of attestation**.

The three figures divide among themselves three functions: authority (the throne), registration (the tablet), truth (the feather). The composition reads as an institutional protocol translated into a mythological plot. For series I-1, this artifact is the semantic core: it shows how the ancient act of recording becomes an architecture of trust.
