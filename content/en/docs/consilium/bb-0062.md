This is the second digital artifact of series **I-11** — imperial
neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0011-11.2`.

## On the concept

A woman in a blue suit and a white shirt with a dark tie works at a laptop.
A brass lamp, an inkwell and volumes complete the composition, while the
painterly wall with a broad brushstroke gives it depth. The image is built on
the canon of the imperial portrait: central axis, restraint, professional
dignity.

*Dignity that has become form.*
