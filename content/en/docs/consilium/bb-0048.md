This is the sixth digital artifact of series **I-8** — the Classical Revival.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0008-8.6`.

## On the concept

A girl in a hoodie and jeans with a tablet sits on a marble parapet of a
gallery. Behind her — a fresco with a muse reading a scroll. Bookcases, arches
and soft light create a setting in which digital reading and the classical
tradition join as two layers of a single act of cognition.

*Reading that has become continuity.*
