This is the fourth digital artifact of series **I-7** — magazine beauty
glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0007-7.4`.

## On the concept

Two golden hands with diamond incrustation carefully hold a crystal lotus.
Satin draperies and Art Deco ray panels form the background setting, while
warm light emphasizes the premium materiality. The composition is built on
symmetry and the gesture of careful holding, turning care into an
institutional act.

*Care that has become form.*
