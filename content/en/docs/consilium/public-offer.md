#### 1. DEFINITIONS AND TERMS

**1.1. Platform** — the VIS REALITATIS™ ecosystem and related services, managed by IMARCH LTD (London, United Kingdom).

**1.2. Donor** — a natural or legal person making a voluntary earmarked donation (Infrastructure Grant) to support and develop the non-commercial infrastructure of the Platform.

**1.3. Donation (Infrastructure Grant)** — a voluntary, gratuitous, and irrevocable monetary contribution made by the Donor through the Platform's payment services (including WhyDonate). The Donation is not a payment for goods, works, or services, is not subject to value-added tax (VAT), and does not create unconditional reciprocal obligations on the part of the Platform.

**1.4. Loyalty Program (Donor Gratitude Scheme)** — a non-commercial initiative of the Platform under which Donors may, as a token of gratitude for their support, be provided with optional bonuses (Perks). The Program operates in the "Bonus Boutique" (Pre-Seed Perk Store) format.

**1.5. Bonus (Perk)** — an optional provision to the Donor of access to Platform assets in the development stage (Pre-Seed). Bonuses may include:
* priority access to cryptographic priority fixation services (SHA-256);
* submission of a manuscript or project to the Double-Blind peer review circuit;
* accrual of Series 8888 loyalty tokens;
* other forms of early access to prototypes and services as determined by the Platform.

**1.6. Early-Bird Status** — status granted to a Donor who makes a donation at the Pre-Seed stage. This status confers priority in the distribution of bonuses and may be taken into account in future loyalty token offerings.

**1.7. Pre-Seed Asset** — any product, service, module, or technological solution placed in the Bonus Boutique that is under active development or prototyping. A Pre-Seed Asset is not a finalized commercial product; its characteristics, functionality, and launch timelines may change.

#### 2. SUBJECT MATTER AND LEGAL NATURE OF THE DONATION

**2.1. Nature of the Relationship.** This Offer exclusively governs voluntary charitable participation relations. The Donor acknowledges and agrees that:
* they are making a voluntary earmarked contribution to the development of the Platform's non-commercial infrastructure;
* their payment is not a purchase of goods, a pre-order, an investment, or payment for services;
* the Platform does not assume unconditional legal obligations of counter-performance.

**2.2. Irrevocability.** The Donation is final and non-refundable, except in cases of technical error (double debit, incorrect amount) confirmed by the payment service. In such cases, the Donor may contact the Platform's support service by email.

**2.3. Tax Status.** The Donation is not subject to value-added tax (VAT). The Platform does not act as the Donor's tax agent and does not provide advice regarding the tax consequences of the donation in the Donor's jurisdiction.

<hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: #c0b09a; margin: 2rem 0;" />

#### 3. PROCEDURE FOR GRANTING BONUSES

**3.1. Discretionary Nature.** The composition, scope, timing, and availability of bonuses are determined by the Platform at its sole discretion and are not subject to negotiation with the Donor. The Platform reserves the right to:
* change the list of available bonuses at any time;
* refuse to provide a specific bonus without explanation;
* replace one bonus with another of equivalent value at the Platform's discretion.

**3.2. Relationship Between Donation and Bonus.** Receiving a bonus is not a right of the Donor and does not constitute a reciprocal obligation of the Platform. Making a donation does not guarantee receipt of any specific bonus. The Donor acknowledges that the funds paid constitute a voluntary donation, and receiving a bonus is a gesture of gratitude, not the performance of a commercial contract.

**3.3. "As Is" Principle.** All bonuses and Pre-Seed Assets are provided on an "as is" basis. The Platform makes no representations regarding their suitability for the Donor's specific purposes, their commercial potential, finalization timelines, or eventual launch.

**3.4. Pre-Seed Stage Specifics.** The Donor acknowledges that all assets placed in the Bonus Boutique are at the Pre-Seed stage and objectively cannot have fixed consumer properties, complete functionality, or guaranteed release dates. The characteristics of an asset may be materially changed at any stage of development.

<hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: #c0b09a; margin: 2rem 0;" />

#### 4. EARLY-BIRD STATUS AND LOYALTY TOKENS

**4.1. Granting of Status.** Early-Bird Status is automatically granted to the Donor upon making a donation through the Bonus Boutique at the Pre-Seed stage.

**4.2. Consideration of Status.** Early-Bird Status may be taken into account by the Platform when conducting future offerings of Series 8888 loyalty tokens or other ecosystem assets. The terms, volume, and proportions of such consideration will be defined in separate Platform documents at the time of the relevant offering.

**4.3. Non-Exclusivity.** Early-Bird Status does not grant the Donor exclusive rights to the Platform's assets, does not constitute an equity interest, security, or investment instrument.

<hr style="border: none; height: 1px; background: #c0b09a; margin: 2rem 0;" />

**5.1. Disclaimer of Warranties.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Platform disclaims all express and implied warranties with respect to the bonuses, Pre-Seed Assets, and the results of their use.

**5.2. Disclaimer of Damages.** The Platform shall not be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential damages of the Donor arising from:
* changes to the composition or characteristics of a Pre-Seed Asset;

**5.3. Independence from Outcome.** The Donor acknowledges that the value of their donation is not contingent upon the fact, quality, or timing of receiving a bonus.

#### 6. APPLICABLE LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**6.1. Applicable Law.** This Offer and all relations arising in connection therewith shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales.

**6.2. Pre-Trial Procedure.** All disputes and disagreements shall be resolved through negotiations. A claim shall be submitted in writing to the Platform's email address. The response period for a claim is 30 (thirty) calendar days.

**6.3. Judicial Jurisdiction.** Failing amicable settlement through pre-trial procedure, the dispute shall be submitted to the courts of London, United Kingdom.
