This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-1** — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier `RN-0044-PS-0001-1.5`.

## On the concept

A frontal portrait of a winged pharaoh, hands folded on the chest — a gesture of preservation, not of power. The wings are symmetrical, executed as an engineering construction: the feathers read as blades, the metal as a frame. Around the head are orbital circles and service marks. On the sides are gears and technical nodes inscribed in the architectural field.

This is the most "hermetic" artifact of the series: it speaks not of action but of **the state of preservation**. Hence the bronze palette, the absence of bright accents, the restrained light. The image works as a seal, not as a scene.
