This is the second digital artifact of series **I-10** — the business portrait.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0010-10.2`.

## On the concept

A man in a brocade jacket adjusts the tie of a boy standing before him. Behind
him — a portrait of a predecessor in a gilded frame; the soft light of a lamp
brings out the figures. The composition is built as an act of transmission:
care, form and tradition form a single gesture of continuity.

*Continuity that has become a gesture.*
