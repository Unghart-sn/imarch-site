This is the first digital artifact of series **I-6** — the official ceremonial
memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0006-6.1`.

## On the concept

A fine hatched engraving presents a male portrait in a three-piece suit,
inscribed in a round medallion. A dense floral ornament executed in
banknote technique frames the figure and gives the image the character of an
official testimony. The composition is built on strict symmetry and frontal
placement — the canon of the ceremonial portrait translated into the language
of engraving.

*Memory that has become an engraving.*

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** sepia, warm silver, ochre, ivory
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-6, dedicated

Series limit — 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate passport as an annex
to the base multi-passport of the series.
