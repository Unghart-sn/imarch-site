This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-11** — imperial neoclassicism.

## On the concept

A man in a grey suit and spectacles works at a laptop. Behind him — a
geometric Art Deco relief with cubes, pyramids and golden elements,
emphasizing the rational basis of the image. The composition is built on the
canon of the imperial portrait: figure, symbol and setting are joined in a
single system.

*Order that has become form.*
