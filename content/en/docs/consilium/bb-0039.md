This is the third digital artifact of series **I-7** — magazine beauty
glamour. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0007-7.3`.

## On the concept

A woman's profile in a pearl-pink range with flowers in the coiffure and
closed eyes is inscribed in a soft glow. Light curls, luminous bubbles and
semi-transparent details form the impression of serenity. The composition
continues the tradition of the magazine portrait translated into the language
of Art Deco.

*Beauty that has become calm.*
