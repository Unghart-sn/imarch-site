This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-6** — the official
ceremonial memorial style. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under
identifier `RN-0044-PS-0006-6.5`.

## On the concept

An engraved female portrait in a high collar is inscribed in a round medallion.
Around it — a complex banknote mesh and floral rosettes executed in the
technique of fine hatched engraving. The composition continues the tradition
of the official portrait on banknotes and charters, giving the image dignity
and timeless clarity.

*Dignity that has become an engraving.*

## Technical specifications

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** sepia, ivory, ochre, warm silver
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

## Context of creation

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-6, dedicated
to institutional memory, official honour and the material fixation of dignity.
Series limit — 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate passport as an annex
to the base multi-passport of the series.
