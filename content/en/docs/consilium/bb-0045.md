## On the concept

A young man in spectacles works at a laptop in an old library. Behind him,
like living memory, stands a grey-bearded ancient sage with a scroll. Candles,
manuscripts, a bust and arches form a setting in which the modern tool and
the classical tradition exist as a single institutional form.

*Tradition that has become a tool.*
