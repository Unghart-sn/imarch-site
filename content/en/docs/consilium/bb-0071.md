This is the fifth digital artifact of series **I-12** — digital minimalism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0012-12.5`.

## On the concept

A head-sculpture of fine lines is held by two human hands. Around — a digital
network, luminous elements and neural impulses. The composition is built on
the gesture of careful holding: technology and humanity are joined in a
single touch, turning precision into a form of care.

*Technology that has become touch.*
