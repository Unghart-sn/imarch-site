This is the second digital artifact of series **I-12** — digital minimalism.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0012-12.2`.

## On the concept

A female operator in protective spectacles and a mask treats the head of an
android with a portable scanner. On the left — holographic face profiles, on
the right — a shelf with laboratory instruments. The composition is built on
the principle of laboratory hierarchy: the operator, the object and the
interfaces form a single system of care and control.

*Care that has become scanning.*
