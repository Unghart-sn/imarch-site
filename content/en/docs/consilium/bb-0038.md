## On the concept

A golden filigree hand holds a faceted flask "L'OR ÉTERNEL" with golden
liquid. The dark background, particles of golden dust and a diagonal
composition give the scene the character of a premium gesture. The material
of the hand and the flask form a single material system in which care is
presented as an institutional form of dignity.

*Care that has become a gesture.*
