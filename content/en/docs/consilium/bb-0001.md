This is the first digital artifact of series **I-1** — the foundational complex of the IMARCH visual standard. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under the identifier `RN-0044-PS-0001-1.1`.

## On the concept

The artwork is built on the contrast between the sacred architecture of Ancient Egypt and the cybernetic layer. Lapis lazuli, gold, biomechanics — three materials of one civilisational system in which the past has become the architectural foundation of the future.

The central figure is a mother holding a child — an image of continuity, protection and transmission of knowledge across generations. The halo behind her reads simultaneously as a solar disk and as a data node. The hieroglyphic field around her is written as working code — knowledge turned into protocol.

The work was created by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-1, dedicated to the architecture of trust and the preservation of institutional memory. The series limit is 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate passport as an annex to the base multi-passport of the series.
