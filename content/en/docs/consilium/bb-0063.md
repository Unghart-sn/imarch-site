This is the third digital artifact of series **I-11** — imperial
neoclassicism. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0011-11.3`.

## On the concept

A woman in an academic gown and a cap with a tassel holds a tablet with a
digital scheme. A quill in an inkwell, volumes and bookcases form a setting
in which the academic tradition and the modern tool are joined in a single
gesture. The warm light from the window brings out the figure, emphasizing
the official character of the portrait.

*Canon that has become a gown.*

## Technical specifications

- **Vault source resolution:** 4K (master file)
- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** dark green velvet, gold, ochre, warm wood
- **Crypto-fixation:** SHA-256 in the VERDICTUM registry

## Context of creation

The work was executed by IMARCH Studio (Purpurov) within series I-11, dedicated
to imperial majesty as an institutional form and to sovereign dignity as the
basis of rule. Series limit — 10 copies. Each owner receives a separate
passport as an annex to the base multi-passport of the series.
