This is the third digital artifact of series **I-5** — Verdictum style.

## On the concept

A marble Corinthian column with a golden capital is crowned with a globe; on
its body — a golden seal with the scales of justice. On the left — a scroll
with a golden seal, on the right — a marble shield with scales and a laurel
wreath. Orbital golden lines connect the elements into a single system in
which law appears as an axial institutional principle.

*Law that has become an axis.*
