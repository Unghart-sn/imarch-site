This is the third digital artifact of series **I-10** — the business portrait.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0010-10.3`.

## On the concept

A man in a blue suit in a study with bookcases and a globe. Behind him — a
painting with a laurel wreath and an armillary, referring to the academic
tradition. The composition is built on the canon of the official portrait:
central figure, symmetrical setting, institutional symbols.

*Tradition that has become status.*
