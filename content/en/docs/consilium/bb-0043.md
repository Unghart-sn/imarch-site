This is the first digital artifact of series **I-8** — the Classical Revival.
Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier `RN-0044-PS-0008-8.1`.

## On the concept

A woman in a strict business suit with a tablet and a book in her hands stands
in an antique arch. On either side — putti: one holds a "LEX" tablet, the
other — a shield with scales. The fresco background and the city in the
distance join the Renaissance architectural setting with the institutional
contour. The composition is built according to the canon of the niche: the
central figure, the framing, the symbol.

*Canon that has become practice.*
