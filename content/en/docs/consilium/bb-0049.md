This is the first digital artifact of series **I-9** — the secret cosmo
laboratory. Registered in the IMARCH LTD registry under identifier
`RN-0044-PS-0009-9.1`.

## On the concept

A young operator in a cap works at a desk with a holographic screen on which
the profile of a pharaoh emerges in blue-magenta glow. Interface icons at the
bottom of the screen point to the functional character of the image: the
ancient sacred object appears as a working unit of a secret system. The
composition is built on the contrast of the dark background and the neon
light.

*Mystery that has become an interface.*

- **Format:** WebP (preview) / PNG (master)
- **Palette:** neon cyan, magenta, deep dark background
