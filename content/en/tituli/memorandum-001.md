## Memorandum of Civic Consent

I, Unghart, in sound mind and clear memory, recognize the provisions of the Constitution of Imarch as the foundation of the civic community I join voluntarily, consciously, and with a sense of responsibility.

I commit, to the best of my strength, capacity, and intellect, to:

- actively participate in the life of the civic community  
- demonstrate the legitimacy of my civic rights through honorable conduct  
- strive for the diligent fulfillment of my civic duties  
- serve as an example of personal embodiment of Imarch’s philosophy  
- be ruthless toward any form of profanation, substitution, or encroachment upon the sovereignty of Imarchia

This memorandum is a titular scene of consent, accompanied by links to the Constitution, biometric data, and the archive of titles.
