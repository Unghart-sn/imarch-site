## Contents

This section presents an archive of documents registering scenic citizenship, valid at the moment of confirmed alliance between the individual and the IMARCH institution. These relations are formalized based on the Constitution of IMARCH and supplemented by personal initiatives of the citizen, recognized by the Constitutional Court of Jury Members.

The section includes:

- Formalized Certificates of Citizenship
- Memoranda of cooperation and constructive engagement
- Archive of personal titles, honors, memoranda, and contractual documents

Hashed PDF versions are provided upon official request from an organization specifying the purpose of inquiry.
