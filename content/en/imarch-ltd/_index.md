### GENERAL INFORMATION

IMARCH LTD UK is a research and educational company that transforms bureaucracy and infrastructure into a living chronicle. 

<details>
  <summary>About the Company🔻</summary>

>Imarch Ltd is a registered company in the United Kingdom, specializing in trust architecture, legal transparency, and institutional packaging of donor solutions.
We design infrastructure that dots all the i's: from documents to visual systems, from contracts to collective rituals.

>**Registration number:** 16791765
**Legal form:** Private Limited Company (LTD)
**Mission:** to transform technical and legal complexity into clear, reproducible solutions for communities and donors.
