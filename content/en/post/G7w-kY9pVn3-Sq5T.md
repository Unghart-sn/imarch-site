### Admodum Reverende Domine Igore Valeriī fīli!

Spero hanc nuntiationem te in bona forma convenire. Ad te scribo ut te de evolutione gravi certiorem faciam atque gradus sequentes in nostra cooperatione proponam.

Praeterita hebdomade habui conventum cum Nicolao Gavrilī fīlio Ščerbań de programma restitutionis post-conflictualis et socializationis veteranorum in regione Charcoviensi. Ille studium expressit et rogavit ut propositiones concretas redderem. Tibi gratias ago quod tempus antea invenisti ut haec quaestiones perpenderes — noster praevius analysis fundamentum grave factus est.

Ut scis, nostra communis elaboratio — vestis universalis omni tempestati apta «Arca-M-42» — elementum technologicum criticum huius programmatis complexi est. Sine tali solutione, labor systematicus ad adaptationem veteranorum in condicionibus agrestibus et semi-agrestibus imperfectus erit.

Attamen, condicio principalis exsecutionis non solum «ferrum» est, sed etiam «programma».
Basiem programmatis specialistici elaboravi, quae in systema logisticum et administrativum unicum coniungit:

Recipientem ultimum (veteranum, servatorem, voluntarium).

Ordinatorem/distributorem.

Opifices (sartores, suppeditores panni et ornamentorum).

Innovatio systematis in sequentibus consistit:

Substitutio dynamica programmata tegumentorum per modellum subscriptionis — hoc est directio separata technologiarum cyberneticarum resource-saving, quae longitudinalem vitae cycli producti praestat.

Platforma programmatica colloquia juridice valida, coordinationes et proceduras logisticas (circiter 70%) minuit, eas in medium online cum auditu claro transferens.

Systema in universum plures utilitates habet: velocitas explicationis, invulnerabilitas practica catenae suppletionis, vilitas usus et simplicitas.

Meae propositiones concretae tibi, Igore Vladimiri fīli:

Quaestionem institutionalizationis cooperationis considerare. Possibilitates quaero ut opus de veste eiusque ambitu programmatico in instituto, quod repraesentas, formalizetur. Agi potest de creatione laboratorii specialisticae vel gregis operativi de technologiis resource-saving et systematibus cyber-physicis.

Invitatio in structuram internationalem. Sicut et Nicolao Gavrilī fīlio, te quoque invito ut possibilitatem ingrediendi Praesidium Consilii Directorum (Advisory Board) Imarch LTD UK consideres. Tua peritia ut doctor technicarum scientiarum pondus additium et profunditatem technicam nostro repraesentationi internationali dabit, praesertim in contextu integrationis solutionum hardware et software.

Materials recentia inspicere. Ad meliorem comprehensiones amplitudinis, praesentationem-systematis ("landing") et articulum informativum disertum adiungo. Mihi grave est eos invenire quibus hoc nunc interesse et utile esse possit.

Cur hoc nunc criticum est?
Meus prospectus, quem paratus sum arguere: gradus chaos in mundo et in Ucraina praesertim punctum attigit ubi modi traditionales organizationis altam possibilitatem habent suffocandi. Nostra elaboratio — haec est conatus alternativum exemplar logistico-technologicum, nimis flexibile et stabile, proponere, quod in condicionibus huius chaos operari potest.

Gradus sequentes, quos propono:

Colloquium breve online ad praesentationem systematis ("landing") et discussionem potentialium viarum cooperationis cum instituto.

Discussio tuae potentialis participationis in Advisory Board Imarch LTD UK.

Coordinatio nostrorum actionum sequentium cum Nicolao Gavrilī fīlio de programma generali restitutionis.

Domine Igore Valeriī fīli, confido nostra elaborata contributum practicum in sovereignty technologicum et restitutionem Ucrainae fieri posse. Sententiam tuam exspecto.

Cum observantia,

Oleksander Purpurov
Fundator et Director,
Imarch LTD UK

P.S. Simultaneam epistulam cum Nicolao Gavrilī fīlio de ambitu generali cooperationis ago. Te certiorem faciam, si tibi id interesset.

### CONCRETE PROPOSAL FOR A STRATEGIC ALLIANCE: REHABILITATION PROGRAM, UNIVERSAL SUIT, SOFTWARE PLATFORM

Authentic Text in Latin

• Official English Translation • For the purposes of validation under international soft law norms

To the Most Esteemed Mr. Ihor Valeriyovych,

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to inform you of a significant development and to propose the next steps in our collaboration.

Last week, I had a meeting with Mykola Havrylovych Shcherban to discuss a program for post-conflict rehabilitation and socialization of veterans in the Kharkiv region. He expressed interest and asked for specific proposals. I am grateful to you for taking the time earlier to work through these issues – our preliminary analysis has become an important foundation.

As you know, our joint development – the universal all-weather suit "Ark-M-42" – is a critical technological component of this comprehensive program. Without such a solution, systematic work on the adaptation of veterans in field and semi-field conditions would be incomplete.

However, the key condition for implementation is not only "hardware" but also "software."
I have developed the foundation of specialized software that unites into a single logistical and management system:

The end recipient (veteran, rescuer, volunteer).

The organizer/distributor.

Manufacturers (tailors, suppliers of fabrics and fittings).

The innovation of the system is as follows:

Planned dynamic replacement of shells via a subscription model – this is a separate direction of resource-saving cyber-technologies, ensuring a long product life cycle.

The software platform reduces legally valid negotiations, approvals, and logistical procedures (by approximately 70%), transferring them to an online environment with clear audit trails.

The system as a whole has a number of advantages: deployment speed, practical invulnerability of the supply chain, low operating costs, and simplicity.

My concrete proposals to you, Ihor Volodymyrovych:

Consider the issue of institutionalizing cooperation. I am seeking opportunities to formalize work on the suit and its software ecosystem based at the institute you represent. This could involve creating a specialized laboratory or working group on resource-saving technologies and cyber-physical systems.

Invitation to an international structure. As with Mykola Havrylovych, I also invite you to consider the possibility of joining the Presidium of the Board of Directors (Advisory Board) of Imarch LTD UK. Your expertise as a Doctor of Technical Sciences will add additional weight and technical depth to our international representation, especially in the context of integrating hardware and software solutions.

Review current materials. For a better understanding of the scale, I am attaching a system presentation-landing page and a detailed informational article. It is important for me to find those for whom this could be interesting and useful right now.

Why is this critical now?
My view, which I am prepared to argue: the level of chaos in the world and in Ukraine in particular has reached a point where traditional methods of organization have a high chance of failing. Our development is an attempt to propose an alternative, extremely flexible and resilient logistic-technological model that can operate under these conditions of chaos.

Next steps that I propose:

A brief online meeting to present the landing page and discuss potential avenues for cooperation with the institute.

Discussion of your potential participation in the Imarch LTD UK Advisory Board.

Coordination of our next steps with Mykola Havrylovych regarding the overall rehabilitation program.

Ihor Valeriyovych, I am confident that our developments can become a practical contribution to the technological sovereignty and recovery of Ukraine. I await your feedback.

With respect,

Oleksander Purpurov
Founder and Director,
Imarch LTD UK

P.S. I am simultaneously corresponding with Mykola Havrylovych regarding the general framework of cooperation. I will keep you informed if it is of interest to you.

**Date:** 14 December 2025
