#### PURPOSE OF THE METHOD { .fade-in-up }

Modern digital systems have learned to store data, but they are practically incapable of retaining a history of actions as a meaningful sequence of acts.

The absence of such a sequence and the impossibility of organising it correctly lead to situational responses. Management is reduced to a narrow set of reactive, administrative protocols, which inevitably lower the overall effectiveness of the system to a critical minimum.

There is only one way out of this vicious circle: to create the cognitive and technical conditions for institutionalising the human factor in all its manifestations.

STRUCTURE OF THE METHOD { .fade-in-up }
The CCS | Chronical Complex Site method (Method of Oleksandr Purpurov) is presented as a unique architecture for the digital presence of a subject entity.

It combines the philosophy of the chronicle, a multi‑layered system of sites, a consortium façade, and protocols of institutional memory.
