The personal cabinet in our ecosystem is not a formal “account for the sake of a tick box” — it is a single working key to all closed materials, cryptographic recording tools and publication circuits.

Below is a short route that will explain in three minutes why an account is needed, how to enter it without technical friction, and which step to take first.

&nbsp;

#### 1. Why an account is needed: three basic reasons { .fade-in-up }

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)); gap: 1.5rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">🔑 Single access without scattered links and messengers</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">One profile opens access to closed documents (including the Investment Memorandum), presentation materials and working tools without sending files through third-party chats.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">🔐 Personal cryptographic recording</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">Any documents, uploaded artefacts and applications receive an unambiguous link to the subject, a timestamp and a digital fingerprint (SHA-256).</div>
  </div>
  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">📈 Scaling from reader to node operator</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">An account begins as a personal access cabinet, but where needed it expands into the working environment of an independent expert, a journal editorial office or an autonomous institutional node (a university, a professional association, an NGO).</div>
  </div>
</div>

#### 2. How to sign in in 30 seconds (6-digit code) { .fade-in-up }

We have abandoned “magic links”, which often break when opened in smartphone in-app browsers or are blocked by corporate mail filters. Sign-in is built as simply and reliably as possible:

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(180px, 1fr)); gap: 1rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  <div class="institutional-step-card">
    <div style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #b18b56; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">1</div>
    <div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 0.9rem; margin-bottom: 0.25rem;">Your email</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.75rem; color: #6a5a48;">On the sign-in page enter your <strong>email address</strong> and press the button to receive a code.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="institutional-step-card">
    <div style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #b18b56; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">2</div>
    <div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 0.9rem; margin-bottom: 0.25rem;">Six digits</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.75rem; color: #6a5a48;">Open the email or check the notification on screen: the <strong>6-digit code</strong> is shown right in the subject line and the first line of the email (the code is valid for 10 minutes).</div>
  </div>
  <div class="institutional-step-card">
    <div style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; color: #b18b56; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">3</div>
    <div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 0.9rem; margin-bottom: 0.25rem;">Entering the code</div>
    <div style="font-size: 0.75rem; color: #6a5a48;">Enter these 6 digits in the window on the same browser tab — you will immediately be inside the cabinet, without losing the page you started from.</div>
  </div>
</div>

> **Advice for organisations:** if you represent a university, a foundation or a company, register with your organisation's official domain address — this will simplify further verification of status.

#### 3. What is inside: four modules of the cabinet { .fade-in-up }

After signing in, four interconnected working circuits open up before you:

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)); gap: 1.5rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">

  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">👤 Module 1. Profile and participant status</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">Your basic card in the system (private individual, investor/partner, independent expert or organisation representative). A correctly filled profile saves you from re-entering data in applications and automatically opens the relevant sections.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">💼 Module 2. Investment and partnership circuit</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">
      A closed area for reviewing the project's key materials:
      <ul>
        <li>the full text of the <strong>Investment Memorandum</strong>;</li>
        <li>a video presentation of the architecture and operating principles of the system;</li>
        <li>a direct channel for requesting terms and feedback within the protected perimeter.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">🗄️ Module 3. Digital Safe</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">
      A tool for recording authorship, priority and the immutability of your materials:
      <ul>
        <li>you upload a document, manuscript, media file or technical package;</li>
        <li>the system computes its checksum (SHA-256), records the time and generates a verifiable authenticity certificate;</li>
        <li>any document can subsequently be checked for integrity and presented as proof of priority.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">📚 Module 4. Publication circuit (Journals and Nodes)</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">
      Access to the public academic, expert or media space through two clearly separated modes:
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Manual (registry) journal</strong> — direct publication of preprints, institutional reports, declarations and authored multimedia artefacts (including specialised series such as <em>Vis Realitatis</em>) with recording of authorship and time.</li>
        <li><strong>Expert journal</strong> — publication of analytical and academic works that have passed peer review and verification.</li>
      </ul>
      Materials published in the circuit receive their own card with an abstract, tags and a direct link in the ecosystem's common stream.
    </div>
  </div>

</div>

To avoid spending time studying every button at once, choose the scenario that matches your current task:

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)); gap: 1.5rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">

  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">📊 Route A — For investors and strategic partners</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">
      <ol>
        <li>Sign in to your account with the code from the email.</li>
        <li>Open the <strong>Investment Memorandum</strong> section and watch the <strong>video presentation</strong> of the architecture.</li>
        <li>If needed, leave a direct request for detailed terms right from the cabinet.</li>
      </ol>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">✍️ Route B — For authors, researchers and experts</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">
      <ol>
        <li>Fill in your basic participant profile.</li>
        <li>Upload a test document to the <strong>Digital Safe</strong> to see how the digital fingerprint and the recording certificate are generated.</li>
        <li>Prepare a publication in the <strong>Manual</strong> or the <strong>Expert</strong> journal.</li>
      </ol>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="institutional-card">
    <div class="institutional-card-title">🏛️ Route C — For universities, associations or NGOs</div>
    <div class="institutional-card-text">
      <ol>
        <li>Register an account with the organisation's official corporate email.</li>
        <li>Complete the basic verification of the organisation's status.</li>
        <li>Submit a request to deploy your own <strong>institutional node</strong> (on a dedicated subdomain or on your own infrastructure) with a feed of abstracts into the common stream.</li>
      </ol>
    </div>
