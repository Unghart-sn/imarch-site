#### FUNDAMENTAL PROVISIONS:

**Unity of the Corpus:** It is hereby confirmed that the IMARCH system operates on the basis of a single legislative corpus consisting of Volume I (Constitution of Meanings) and Volume II (Codex Structurae).

**Sovereignty and Ownership:** The sole lawful holder of the meanings and intellectual property of the System is IMARCH LTD (United Kingdom). All local nodes and participants recognize the supremacy of this Consolidated Act.

**Recognition by the Council:** Members of the Board of Directors assume their rights and obligations solely through full recognition and acceptance of the provisions of this Constitution.
