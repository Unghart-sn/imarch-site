#### Full Text of the Document

## ACT OF RECOGNITION AND HONORARY DISTINCTION

**For the Technology Pioneers Whose Contributions Have Shaped the Chronicle OS Ecosystem**

#### Preamble

IMARCH LTD officially records its respect and gratitude to international technology companies, research organisations, and developer communities whose products, platforms, and engineering achievements have made the formation of the Chronicle OS ecosystem possible.

Chronicle OS was not created in a vacuum. Its development was made possible by decades of collective scientific, engineering, and technological progress achieved by thousands of researchers, software architects, designers, and innovators around the world. This Act acknowledges that every new system stands on a foundation built by those who came before.

&nbsp;

#### Institutional Recognition and Conferral of Heraldic Status

As a mark of profound professional respect, IMARCH LTD hereby announces the symbolic entry of the following organisations into the Official IMARCH LTD Awards Registry with the collective conferral of the **Order of the Purple Mind, VIII Degree (Institutional)**:

#### Financial-Mission Operational Protocol

In strict accordance with the internal "Protocol for Dynamic Mission-Based Allocation of the Operational Fund," IMARCH LTD establishes that the conferred status of the Order of the Purple Mind (VIII Degree) carries a calculated institutional mission weight.

1. **Operational Fund Allocation:** Proportionally, within 30% of the operational expense pool allocated for ecosystem support, the calculated mission assets are assigned to the corresponding technology profiles in the internal registry.

2. **Infrastructure Upgrade and Asset Allocation:** Should profit scales exceed the linear capacity of services, the corresponding surplus of the operational pool is dynamically directed toward ecosystem infrastructure upgrades, procurement of computing capacity, or strategic buyback of project stakes to maximise collective capitalisation.

&nbsp;

#### Institutional Statement

This document does not create partnership, sponsorship, or affiliation relations between IMARCH LTD and the aforementioned organisations. It is solely a form of professional recognition of their contribution to the advancement of technologies that have directly or indirectly facilitated the creation of Chronicle OS and the PLATAN+ institutional initiative.

&nbsp;

#### Declaration

IMARCH LTD proceeds from the understanding that technological progress is the result of humanity's collective labour. Every generation inherits the legacy of its predecessors and bears the responsibility for its further development. Chronicle OS is regarded precisely as such a contribution to the future.

**We honour those upon whose shoulders we stand.**
