#### REGISTRATION PROCEDURE { .fade-in-up }

<details>
  <summary>Verification principles 🔻</summary>
  
🔹 Registry format  
  The Register of Official Documents of the Project is maintained as a versioned document.  

  🔹 Verification procedure  
  When a new document (or package of documents) is added, a new version of the Register is created, which is fixed by its own hash of the base registry document.  

  🔹 Navigation  
  The hash of the current version of the Register is published on the official company website in a special section, accessible from the facade pages of all sub-project office sites (including VIS REALITATIS™, the pilot module, CCS and others), as well as from the project's main page.  

  🔹 Transparency  
  Thus, the Register exists in real time: its integrity and the procedure for making changes are controlled through hashes, and any participant or partner can verify the current composition of documents by comparing the published hash with the contents of the page.

</details>
