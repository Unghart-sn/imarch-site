#### 1. INTRODUCTION

IMARCH Githronical | Chronicle OS is a social operating system designed to create sustainable communities and organizations of a new type. The project integrates technological, humanitarian and cultural tools, allowing informal initiatives to transform into functioning institutions.

IMARCH is not a charity or a one-time service. It is an infrastructure solution focused on long-term stability, transparency and reproducibility of social processes.

&nbsp;

#### 2. CONTEXT AND PROBLEM

Most social initiatives emerge spontaneously but face the same challenges: lack of institutional form, burnout of participants, lack of trust from partners and donors, inability to scale, loss of accumulated experience.

IMARCH offers a systemic approach to institutionalisation without losing the human dimension.

&nbsp;

#### 3. MISSION AND ARCHITECTURE

IMARCH creates a modular environment where any action can be recorded as an **Act**, a Chronicle is built from acts, and an **Actor** is defined not by a static profile but by the sum of their acts.

Key elements: **Mask** (public facade), **Scene** (context), **Protocol** (rules of interaction). All processes are recorded in an open GitHub-based chronicle, ensuring transparency and verifiability.

&nbsp;

#### 6. REGISTRY OF OFFICIAL DOCUMENTS

All institutional documents that form the legal and scientific foundation of IMARCH are collected in a single Register of Official Documents. Each document included in the Register (act, decree, memorandum, know-how, budget, charter, etc.) has its own SHA-256 hash and is archived separately.

The Register itself is maintained as a versioned document. When a new document (or package of documents) is added, a new version of the Register is created, which is fixed by its own hash of the base registry document. The hash of the current version of the Register is published on the official company website in a special section, accessible from the facade pages of all sub-project office sites (including VIS REALITATIS™, the pilot module, CCS and others), as well as from the project's main page.

Thus, the Register exists in real time: its integrity and the procedure for making changes are controlled through hashes, and any participant or partner can verify the current composition of documents by comparing the published hash with the contents of the page.
