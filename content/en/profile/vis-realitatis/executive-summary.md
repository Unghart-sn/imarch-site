The **Vis Realitatis** project creates a sustainable system of psycho‑emotional and physical support for veterans and their families, based on the proprietary **SunLife-Massage®** method and a digital infrastructure of open reporting.

We bring together modern somatic practices, social technologies, digital transparency and training to restore people’s sense of control, reduce stress levels and form a living mutual‑aid community.

{{< morebutton target="https://imarch.sbs/en/sunlife-massage/" text="ABOUT THE METHOD" >}}

---

## ⚠️ The scale of the problem

- Veterans and their families face chronic stress, PTSD, isolation and a lack of accessible support tools.
- Traditional forms of help are often unavailable, too expensive, or do not address day‑to‑day challenges.
- Veterans’ families bear an emotional as well as an economic burden, and are frequently left alone to cope with stress.

The problem requires a **scalable, accessible and evidence‑based solution** built on training, community and transparency.

---

## 💡 Our solution

We are building a **three‑tier support system**:

1. **Experimental work** – direct assistance to veterans, data collection, protocol refinement.
2. **Couple training** – preparing family or collegial pairs to practise “for home, for family”.
3. **Community + gamification** – a mutual‑aid network that operates without continuous financial input.

The connective core of the project is **bespoke software** that provides:

- a unified participant profile;
- a referral system;
- session and activity tracking;
- gamification;
- Git‑based reporting;
- transparent data for donors.

---

## 🧩 Why our model works

- Every dollar creates a **double effect**: immediate help + a long‑term self‑sustaining network.
- Each trained pair delivers **dozens of free sessions**, creating a multiplier effect.
- The software guarantees full transparency, so donors can see **where every minute and every dollar goes**.

This is not a service project.  
It is a **care infrastructure** project.

---

## 🎯 First‑stage objectives (6–12 months)

- Launch the renovation centre.
- Conduct pilot sessions for veterans.
- Train **50 couples**.
- Prepare the first group of facilitators.
- Launch the MVP of the software core.
- Establish a mutual‑aid network and Git‑based reporting.

---

## 📈 Potential scale

The system grows horizontally:  
each new couple creates a local “resilience node” – a family, a household, a mini‑community.

In the future, the model can be adapted for:

- different regions;
- non‑profit organisations;
- schools and workplaces;
- international rehabilitation projects;
- franchise of private studios and mobile clinics.

---

## 🤝 Why the project deserves support

- We offer an **evidence‑based humanitarian tool**.
- We build **scalable infrastructure**, not a one‑off service.
- We ensure **full transparency and digital reporting**.
- We create a **long‑term mutual‑aid network**.

Your contribution becomes **sustainable social capital** that continues to work even after funding ends.
