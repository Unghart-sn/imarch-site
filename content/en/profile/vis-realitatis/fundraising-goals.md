## 🎯 Why We Raise Funds

The **Vis Realitatis** project is building a sustainable support system for veterans and their families.  
To achieve this, we need resources directed at specific, verifiable tasks.

We fundamentally do not raise funds for “abstract aid.”  
Every contribution works within a clear logic and produces measurable impact.

---

## 🧩 Main Areas of Funding

#### 1. 🏛️ Launch and operation of the Renovation Centre

Funds are used for:
- organising a space for practices;
- conducting pilot programmes with veterans;
- providing basic infrastructure for training and meetings.

The Centre is the assembly point of the whole system.

---

#### 2. 💻 Development of the project’s digital core

A digital platform is essential for:
- tracking participants and practices;
- transparent donor reporting;
- surveys and data collection;
- supporting gamification and referral logic.

Without software, the project cannot be scaled or monitored.

---

#### 3. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Training of couples and specialists

Financing enables:
- training of married and partner pairs;
- preparation of facilitators and method instructors;
- launch of barter mutual‑aid practices.

One trained pair generates dozens of free sessions for others.

---

#### 4. 🔬 Scientometrics and effectiveness analysis

All project participants are involved in an ongoing research process:
- collection of anonymised data;
- analysis of condition dynamics;
- publication of aggregated results.

This improves the quality of the methodology and trust in the project.

---

## 📊 How We Understand Effectiveness

For us, a result is not a report “on funds spent”, but:
- number of trained pairs;
- number of sessions conducted;
- community sustainability without additional injections;
- growth in trust and participant engagement.

---

## 🤝 What This Means for the Donor

By supporting the project, the donor:
- understands **exactly where the funds go**;
- sees the logic of resource use;
- participates in creating a long‑term system, not one‑off aid.

Every contribution is an investment in a care infrastructure that continues to work.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

The fundraising goals of the **Vis Realitatis** project are:
- transparent;
- structured;
- focused on sustainable impact.

We are building a system, not covering a temporary need.
