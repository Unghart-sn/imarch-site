## Why We Show “What Happens Next” in Advance

For us it is important that the donor understands:  
their contribution is not the end, but the beginning of a process.

The Vis Realitatis project develops according to a stage‑by‑stage model, where each subsequent step builds on the results of the previous one.

---

## Step 1. Completion of Stage 1 (12 weeks)

At the end of the current stage the following will be ready:
- approved methodology
- MVP of the organisational software
- prototypes (suit and mobile unit)
- a pilot event
- a public report for donors

This is the verification point of the project.

---

## Step 2. Analysis and Recording of Results

After the stage is completed, the team:
- analyses the achieved KPIs
- collects feedback from participants
- adjusts the methodology and tools
- publishes the final report

Only then is a decision taken about the next stage.

---

## Step 3. Transition to Scaling

If the goals of Stage 1 are met, the project moves on to:
- expanding training programmes
- involving new specialists
- developing the digital platform
- launching additional regional pilots

Each new stage has its own budget and objectives.

---

## Step 4. Long‑Term Perspective

In the long‑term logic, Vis Realitatis:
- builds a sustainable mutual‑aid network
- creates a standard for replicating the practice
- develops a digital ecosystem of trust
- opens up opportunities for international partnerships

---

## What This Means for the Donor

- Your contribution does not “dissolve” – it is recorded in the results
- Each stage has a beginning, a completion and a report
- The project does not expand until the previous step has proven its viability

---

## The Principle We Stand On

We do not promise everything at once.  
We promise to **move forward honestly, step by step, and transparently**.

That is how a sustainable project is built.
