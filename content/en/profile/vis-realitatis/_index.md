#### RENOVATION AND SOCIAL ADAPTATION CENTRE PROJECT { .fade-in-up }

VIS REALITATIS™ ("Energy of Reality", Latin) is a practical programme of human recovery and self-regulation based on the principle of holistic constructive impact on psycho-emotional and physical well‑being through the SUNLIFE-MASSAGE® method.

The project integrates several applied directions:

• self-massage and self-regulation protocols, paired and collective massage practice according to the SUNLIFE-MASSAGE® method
• physical resilience practices  
• nature‑ and tourism‑based recovery formats  
• development of specialised equipment  
• research and educational work  

> The project is implemented as one of the practical modules of the institutional system **IMARCH GITCHRONICAL | CHRONICAL OS 8888™**, where actions are recorded, experience becomes knowledge, and initiative transforms into a sustainable structure.

### GENERAL INFORMATION { .fade-in-up }

The VIS REALITATIS™ programme is built on many years of experience in working with methods of human self-regulation, applied research in the field of body practices and the development of original wellness protocols.

The program's foundation is built upon a comprehensive training and practice system that integrates methods of self-massage and massage therapy, physical resilience, psycho-emotional regulation, applied renovation, and social adaptation.

At the current stage, VIS REALITATIS™ is evolving as an integrated applied programme of human support and recovery, aimed at increasing resilience, inner balance and personal effectiveness under conditions of high stress and pressure.

The project combines practical methodology, research work and institutional packaging of experience within the IMARCH Githronical | Chronicle OS infrastructure.

VIS REALITATIS™ is an applied human recovery and self‑regulation programme that combines practical methodology, research work and institutional infrastructure.

### CONTENT OF THE METHOD { .fade-in-up }

The VIS REALITATIS™ Method (Method of Oleksandr Purpurov) is presented as a unique architecture of institutional reality, combining the philosophy of subjective relations, a methodological base of protocolisation, scenario maps of interaction, a legal framework and visual‑rhythmic artefacts.

It includes:

- 🌀 **Philosophy**: rethinking digital space as an environment for the formation of subjective units.
- 📜 **Methodological base**: clarity protocols for individual, pair and collective relations.
- 🧩 **Scenario maps**: structuring interactions and turning actions into history.
- ⚖️ **Legal framework**: GDPR/DPA compliance and institutional protection of subjective units.
- 🎨 **Visual artefacts**: scroll tables, title blocks and rhythmic formats for onboarding.
- 🚀 **Growth strategy**: accumulation of institutional memory and building trust through the chronicle.
