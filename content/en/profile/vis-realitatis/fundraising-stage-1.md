## 🚀 IMARCH PILOT  
#### 📜 Vis Realitatis Protocol

A **12‑week online pilot launch programme** integrating:  
- 🌿 self‑regulation practice  
- 📝 digital chronicle of participants’ actions  
- 🔬 expert analysis  

to test a new model of institutional organisation.  

---

## 🎯 What We Are Launching

The pilot brings together four operational modules into a single practical system:  

1. 🌿 **SunLife‑Massage Harmonia** — self‑regulation and self‑massage protocol  
2. 📝 **CCS Chronicle Profile** — digital chronicle of actions and participation  
3. 🔬 **Research Track** — preparation of scientific observation and analysis  
4. 🧭 **Formological Consulting** — diagnostics of the environment and development of organisational protocols  

---

## 🧭 Why This Matters

Modern systems often fail because:  
- actions are not recorded,  
- experience is not preserved,  
- institutions lose memory.  

The IMARCH pilot tests a simple sequence:  
➡️ practice → 📝 chronicle → 📊 knowledge → 🏛️ institution  

---

## 📦 Pilot Format

- 👥 **30 participants**  
- ⏳ **12 weeks of online practice**  
- 📝 digital recording of results  
- 🔬 expert observation  
- 📊 analytical report

## 🏛️ What Is IMARCH

**IMARCH** connects practical experience, a digital chronicle of actions and expert knowledge to create new forms of institutional organisation.  

The system begins with real practice, records participants’ actions through the **Chronicle Communication System (CCS)**, analyses the obtained data with the participation of experts, and formulates organisational solutions through **formological consulting**.  

Thus a sequential architecture emerges:  
➡️ experience → 📑 recording → 📊 analysis → 🧩 method → 🏛️ institution

## ⏳ Implementation Timeline (12 weeks)

- **Weeks 1–2:** negotiations, expert payments, formulation of research terms of reference  
- **Weeks 3–6:** research, methodology development, first training materials  
- **Weeks 5–10:** software MVP development (2–3 sprints), work with prototype suppliers  
- **Weeks 11–12:** field pilot, data collection, photo/video, final report  

---

## 🧠 Org‑software MVP: Minimum Feature Set

Focus: **not to spread too thin, but to make the core robust**.  

1. User profile  
2. Cost calculation module  
3. Referral system  
4. Blog module (reports and publications)  
5. Simple report export (PDF / HTML)  

---

## 📊 Stage 1 KPIs

- approved methodology (document, yes/no)  
- at least **5 training artefacts**  
- working software MVP (profile + calculation + blog)  
- concept of specialised clothing and estimate for mobile unit  
- at least **1 pilot event conducted**  
- report prepared with photos, video and financial summary  

---

## ⚠️ Risks and Mitigation Measures

- **Risk:** MVP overload → **Measure:** strict scope (3–5 features)  
- **Risk:** unreliable suppliers → **Measure:** at least 2–3 offers, trial samples  
- **Risk:** ethical dramatisation → **Measure:** pilot before public stories, participant consent

## 📊 How the Funds Are Used

We believe in absolute transparency. Here is how the funds raised are allocated:

#### 🛠️ 70% — Platform Development
- **New features development:** gamification, referral system, integrations
- **Technical infrastructure:** hosting, domains, CDN, security
- **User support:** documentation, training materials
- **Internationalisation:** translations into 7 languages, localisation

#### 🎯 20% — Marketing and Growth
- **Attracting new communities:** NGOs, startups, local initiatives
- **Content marketing:** articles, case studies, research on institutionalisation
- **Partnerships:** collaboration with other platforms and services
- **Events:** online meetings, webinars, hackathons

#### 💼 10% — Administrative Expenses
- **Legal support:** UK LTD maintenance, compliance
- **Accounting:** reports, taxes, audit
- **Operational expenses:** team tools, communications

---

## 📈 Our Financial Transparency Principles

1. **Quarterly reports:** We publish detailed expense reports in the blog
2. **Development priority:** Minimal administrative costs, maximum on the product
3. **Community decides:** Major expenses (over €1,000) are discussed with active participants
4. **Reserve fund:** 10% of all receipts go to a stability fund for 6 months
5. Verification of the intended use of funds in the public space is carried out by the Supervisory Board of the NGO "Children of War"

## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### To whom exactly am I sending my money?

> Our company works exclusively with the WhyDonate platform as the payment operator. No other options are foreseen at this stage of our project's development.

#### 📊 Where can I see the reports?

> Financial reports are prepared quarterly and are available in our blog for project participants.  
The report archive is stored in a closed section; donors access it via GitHub.

#### 💰 What if the project doesn’t take off?

> We are already ensuring sustainability through the OECXAB service (website creation). These funds go towards platform development. Even if growth is slow, the platform will continue to operate.

#### 🌍 What if I’m from another country?

> Support works from any country. WhyDonate accepts most currencies and cards.

#### ⚖️ Are there any legal guarantees?

> As a UK LTD, we provide all necessary documents for institutional partners. For micro‑support via WhyDonate, your contributions are treated as donations with possible symbolic perks, not as investment contracts.
