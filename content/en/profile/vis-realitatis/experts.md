#### Mykola Gavrylovych Shcherban  
<a id="scherban"></a>

**Degree:** Doctor of Medical Sciences, Professor  
**Field:** Medicine; ecology; effects of multicomponent mixtures  
**Affiliation:** Central Research Laboratory of Kharkiv Medical Academy; Kharkiv State Medical University  
**Summary:** Experienced researcher in toxicology and environmental medicine. Supervises research methodology on multicomponent mixtures, responsible for scientific expertise of clinical and laboratory data of the project.  
**Role in the project:** Scientific verification of methods, peer review of research protocols, methodological support.

---

#### Oleksiy Oleksiyovych Kalmykov  
<a id="kalmykov"></a>

**Degree / title:** Doctor of Medical Sciences, Associate Professor; Therapist of the highest qualification category  
**Field:** Clinical medicine; organisation of medical services; management of medical projects  
**Affiliation / projects:** Founder and head of the Network of Medical Clusters (SMC); Research Institute of Evidence‑Based Medicine (RI EB) and its divisions: "Medinfoservice", "Medical Scientific and Methodological Centre", project "My Doctor"; tourism projects "Turinfoservice", "Centre of Tourist Services".  
**Summary:** Expert in organising primary and specialised medical care, implementation of clinical pathways and service models.  
**Role in the project:** Design of clinical network, quality standards, operational model.

---

#### Igor Valeriyovych Grytsuk  
<a id="grytsuk"></a>

**Degree / title:** Doctor of Technical Sciences, Professor  
**Field:** Operation of marine power plants; technical sciences; automotive engineering and equipment operation  
**Affiliation:** Kherson State Maritime Academy (KSMA); cooperation with KhNAHU and other technical universities  
**Summary:** Technical expert on engineering systems, equipment logistics and operational reliability.  
**Role in the project:** Technical assessment of equipment, recommendations on operation and infrastructure safety.

---

#### Yuliya Mykhaylivna Snurnikova  
<a id="snurnikova"></a>

**Degree / title:** Candidate of Philological Sciences, Associate Professor  
**Specialisation:** Journalism, television journalism, online media, speech techniques  
**Affiliation:** V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University; Ukrainian Institute of Arts and Sciences (part‑time)  
**Summary:** Expert in communications, content strategy and media education.  
**Role in the project:** Development of information strategy, content plan, training in public communication and media workshops.

---

#### Anastasiya Ihorivna Bezrodna  
<a id="bezrodnaya"></a>

**Degree:** Candidate of Biological Sciences, specialisation – genetics (03.00.15)  
**Affiliation:** Kharkiv National Medical University (KNMU)  
**Summary:** Researcher in genetics and biomedicine; deals with biomarkers and molecular studies.  
**Role in the project:** Development of biological research protocols, interpretation of results, participation in scientific publications.

---

#### Volodymyr Grygorovych Kravets  
<a id="kravets"></a>

**Status / role:** Community activist; head of branch No. 27, Shevchenkivskyi district, Kharkiv (VO "Children of War")  
**Summary:** Representative of local communities and target groups; provides liaison between the project and veterans and their families.  
**Role in the project:** Engagement of target groups, organisation of local events, collection of feedback from communities.

---

#### Oleksandr Volodymyrovych Purpurov  
<a id="purpurov"></a>

**Status:** Author of the SunLife‑Massage® method  
**Summary:** Developer of the practical massage methodology and training programmes; responsible for the clinical part of the method and instructor training.  
**Role in the project:** Adaptation of the method to the needs of veterans, development of training materials, quality control of practice.
