The Vis Realitatis project builds a sustainable humanitarian infrastructure around the **SunLife-Massage®** method, integrating rehabilitation, training, family support and digital transparency.

The goals are structured in three tiers – short‑term, medium‑term and long‑term – in line with international project management standards.

---

## 🟦 Short‑Term Goals (0–12 months)

#### 1. Launch of the Vis Realitatis pilot centre
- conduct 150 experimental sessions for veterans;  
- collect scientific data, build initial scientometrics;  
- refine protocols based on pilot outcomes.

#### 2. Training the first family pairs
- prepare **50 massage pairs** at the “For the Family” level;  
- form the first barter‑based mutual‑aid nodes;  
- create methodological materials and safety guidelines.

#### 3. Training “Pro”‑level facilitators
- train **10 facilitators** capable of leading groups and pairs;  
- involve facilitators in research work.

#### 4. MVP launch of the digital infrastructure
- profile (mask), referral system, GitChronicle;  
- session tracking, first badges, trust system;  
- integration with landing page and donor reporting.

---

## 🟩 Medium‑Term Goals (12–24 months)

#### 1. Expansion to 3 regions
- launch regional Vis Realitatis nodes;  
- train **150 new pairs**;  
- grow the facilitator network to 30 people.

### 2. SunLife-Massage® Online School
- video course + examinations + supervision;  
- certification for all levels: “For Yourself”, “For the Family”, “Pro”;  
- automate the learning process through the platform.

#### 3. Expanding the research unit
- publications in peer‑reviewed journals;  
- international validation of protocols;  
- prepare documents for certification in the EU/US.

#### 4. Community and barter economy
- launch the global community;  
- develop the reputation system;  
- 10,000 recorded barter sessions.

---

## 🟥 Long‑Term Goals (3–5 years)

#### 1. International Vis Realitatis network
- centres in Europe and the USA;  
- unified specialist training standard;  
- international partnerships and research consortia.

#### 2. SunLife-Massage® personal studio franchise
- ready‑to‑use franchise package;  
- mobile treatment units;  
- regional studios operating under a single protocol and tracking system.

#### 3. Economic sustainability
- self‑financing through training, an aggregator, and franchise fees;  
- minimal dependence on grants;  
- software offered as a SaaS platform for other NGOs.

#### 4. Global social impact
- evidence‑based reduction of stress levels in families;  
- strengthening family bonds;  
- fostering a new culture of care “for home, for family”.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

The goal system ensures:

- predictable growth,  
- a high level of transparency,  
- a sustainable veteran support model,  
- and the potential for international scaling.

The goals are formulated according to the SMART standard and allow donors to see a clear project development trajectory.
