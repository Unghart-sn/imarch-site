## What exactly will the raised funds be used for?

The funds from the current campaign are allocated **exclusively to Stage 1** of the project:
- development and approval of the methodology
- creation of an MVP of the organisational software
- payment for initial expert consultations
- prototypes (suit and mobile unit)
- conducting the pilot test

---

## Why is the fundraising split into stages?

We deliberately moved away from the “collect everything at once” model.  
Staging allows us to:
- reduce risks
- give donors a clear picture of progress
- make decisions based on actual results

---

## How will I know that the project is actually being implemented?

At the end of the stage we publish:
- a financial summary
- a description of the work completed
- photos and video from the pilot
- a brief analytical report

The reporting is publicly available.

---

## What happens after Stage 1 is completed?

After Stage 1, the project moves to the next phase:
- scaling the practice
- expanding training
- developing the digital platform
- preparing international partnerships

The decision on the next stage is made **based on the achieved KPIs**.

---

## Can I support the project in ways other than money?

Yes.  
The project is open to:
- expert involvement,
- partnerships,
- information support,
- organisational assistance.

---

## How is this project different from one‑off charity?

Vis Realitatis creates a **sustainable system**, not one‑off aid.  

A single investment builds a mechanism that continues to work and help families, communities and specialists long afterwards.

---

## Where can I go with additional questions?

You can contact the project team via the feedback form or through the contacts provided on the website.

We are open to dialogue.
