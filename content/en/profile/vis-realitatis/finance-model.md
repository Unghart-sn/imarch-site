The Vis Realitatis financial model is built as a **sustainability mechanism**, not as a one‑off fundraising scheme.  
Every pound invested works multiple times, creating lasting social impact.

---

## 📌 Base Rates and Formats

#### 🔬 Experimental work — **$50 / hour**
- subsidised sessions for veterans;
- collection of scientometric data;
- method adaptation;
- direct assistance to the target group.

#### 👫 Couple training — **$70 / hour**
- individual training of pairs in the SunLife-Massage® method;
- formation of sustainable home practices;
- creation of local mutual‑aid nodes.

#### ♻️ Barter practice — **no monetary transactions**
- trained pairs help others;
- dozens of free sessions without additional funding;
- growth of social sustainability without budget increase.

---

## 🔄 The Principle of Fund Transformation

Each donor contribution goes through three stages:

1. **Payment for professional work**  
   → help for veterans and training.

2. **Transfer of skill**  
   → pairs become autonomous sources of support.

3. **Self‑reproduction of effect**  
   → dozens of free sessions with no new costs.

> The donor finances **not a service**, but a **system** that continues to operate independently.

---

## 📊 Why This Model Is Sustainable

- costs decrease as the network grows;
- training replaces continuous subsidies;
- barter reduces the budget burden;
- digital accounting prevents misuse of funds.

---

## 🔍 Transparency and Control

Financial and operational processes are recorded through a digital tracking system:

- session and training records;
- stage‑by‑stage reporting;
- transparent allocation of resources;
- generation of donor reports.

This allows donors to **see the real impact of their investments**, not just final declarations.

---

## 🌱 Long‑Term Sustainability

As the project develops, additional support sources are launched:

- online courses and certification;
- service aggregator;
- personal studio franchise;
- platform use by other NGOs.

This reduces dependence on grants and creates a self‑sustaining ecosystem.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

The Vis Realitatis financial model is:
- transparent;
- scalable;
- focused on long‑term impact;
- minimises recurring costs;
- turns aid into a sustainable care infrastructure.

Donor funds here are **not spent**, but **put to work**.
