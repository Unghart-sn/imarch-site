#### 1. INTRODUCTION

IMARCH Githronical | Chronicle OS is a social operating system designed to create sustainable communities and organisations of a new type. The project integrates technological, humanitarian and cultural tools, allowing informal initiatives to transform into functioning institutions.

IMARCH is not a charity or a one‑time service. It is an infrastructure solution focused on long‑term stability, transparency and reproducibility of social processes.

#### 2. CONTEXT AND PROBLEM

Most social initiatives emerge spontaneously but face the same challenges: lack of institutional form, burnout of participants, lack of trust from partners and donors, inability to scale, loss of accumulated experience.

IMARCH offers a systemic approach to institutionalisation without losing the human dimension.

#### 6. REGISTRY OF OFFICIAL DOCUMENTS

All institutional documents that form the legal and scientific foundation of IMARCH are collected in a single Registry of Official Documents. Each document included in the Registry (act, decree, memorandum, know‑how, budget, charter, etc.) has its own SHA‑256 hash and is archived separately.

The Registry itself is maintained as a versioned document. When a new document (or a package of documents) is added, a new version of the Registry is created, and this version is fixed by its hash of the base registry document. The hash of the current version of the Registry is published on the company’s official website in a dedicated section, which is accessible from the facade pages of all sub‑project office sites (including VIS REALITATIS™, the pilot module, CCS, and others), as well as from the main project facade.

Thus, the Registry exists in real time: its integrity and the order of changes are controlled through hashes, and any participant or partner can verify the current composition of the documents by comparing the published hash with the content of the page.
