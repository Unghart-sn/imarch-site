The Vis Realitatis project is not focused on one‑off aid, but on **creating sustainable social changes** that persist after donor funding ends.

---

{{< youtube-enhanced id="eftfYRg0DJ0" caption="Vis Realitatis: Social Impact" >}}

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Impact on the Family

Each trained pair gains:
- a practical tool for mutual support;
- reduction of chronic stress levels;
- strengthening of emotional bonds;
- safe somatic care rituals.

The home ceases to be a zone of tension and becomes a space of recovery.

---

## 🔄 Multiplier Effect

One trained pair:
- provides dozens of free sessions for others;
- passes on skills within the community;
- reduces the burden on external support systems.

Thus, help **scales without budget growth**, creating a ripple effect.

---

## 🌐 Mutual Aid Network

The project builds a horizontal network “for home and family”, where:
- everyone can be a recipient of help;
- everyone can become a source of help;
- participation is based on trust and transparency.

The network functions as a living social organism, not as a centralised structure.

---

## 📊 Transparency and Trust

All key actions are recorded in an open reporting system:
- training;
- sessions;
- participation in barter practice;
- social activity.

Transparency builds trust – both within the community and from donors.

---

## 🌱 Long‑Term Changes

The project's social impact is reflected in:
- reduction of psycho‑emotional tension;
- strengthening of family ties;
- involvement of veterans in active social life;
- creation of a new culture of care.

## ✅ Conclusion

The social impact of Vis Realitatis is not an abstract influence, but **concrete changes** that can be observed, measured, and scaled.

The project creates a sustainable mutual aid ecosystem that works **for the family, the community, and society as a whole**.
