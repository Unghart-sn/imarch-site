#### Protocol 8888 activated { .fade-in-up }
Social assistance has reached a dead end: opacity, dependency, institutional amnesia, and locking people into the role of supplicants have become the norm.

**Vis Realitatis** is IMARCH’s response to this failure. We are launching a renovation centre where help turns into a reproducible protocol: public recording, verifiable stages, and an architecture of trust instead of chaos.

Your step is to enter the **first circle of Protocol 8888**. Donor, expert, partner, or pilot participant: every action is recorded, gains institutional weight, and becomes part of the system – not a disappearing gesture of charity.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="custom-cards">
  <div class="custom-card">
    <div class="custom-emoji">🐙</div>
    <div class="custom-title">Why GitHub?</div>
    <div class="custom-desc">Because we don’t need a pretty “trust dashboard” – we need an environment that leaves traces. GitHub records publication history, file changes, dates, comments, and archive structure. It is not a “proof of goodwill”, but a technical environment for verifying the sequence of actions.</div>
    <div class="custom-tag">recording environment</div>
  </div>
  <div class="custom-card">
    <div class="custom-emoji">#️⃣</div>
    <div class="custom-title">Does a hash guarantee truth?</div>
    <div class="custom-desc">A hash by itself does not replace conscience or audit. It solves a different problem: it fixes a specific record in an immutable form. If the record is altered, the hash no longer matches. We do not ask you to trust words – we give a technical marker of data integrity.</div>
    <div class="custom-tag">integrity control</div>
  </div>
  <div class="custom-card">
    <div class="custom-emoji">🏛️</div>
    <div class="custom-title">How is this different from an ordinary NGO?</div>
    <div class="custom-desc">Ordinary NGOs usually raise funds under a general cause and publish reports after the fact. Here, your contribution is immediately embedded into the project architecture: recorded as an event, linked to a stage, artefacts, and outcomes. You are not supporting a foundation as a form, but a protocol as a working system.</div>
    <div class="custom-tag">model difference</div>
  </div>
  <div class="custom-card">
    <div class="custom-emoji">🧾</div>
    <div class="custom-title">Where exactly does the money go?</div>
    <div class="custom-desc">Into the first launch circle: physical centre, Sunlife-Massage® pilot, expert and methodological assembly, recording and reporting system. There is no “raise first, figure out later”. Every budget item exists within a concrete stage.</div>
    <div class="custom-tag">stage budget</div>
  </div>
  <div class="custom-card">
    <div class="custom-emoji">🧪</div>
    <div class="custom-title">What if it is just beautiful packaging?</div>
    <div class="custom-desc">Then the project will collapse at the very first verification cycle. That is exactly why we publish not only theses, but also the traces of work: stages, documents, roles, case studies, reports, and external verification points. Packaging cannot withstand a long‑term chronicle; a working system can.</div>
    <div class="custom-tag">stress test</div>
  </div>
  <div class="custom-card">
    <div class="custom-emoji">🌍</div>
    <div class="custom-title">What will be the outcome beyond “help here and now”?</div>
    <div class="custom-desc">The outcome is not only people supported, but also a formalised reproduction protocol – so that the centre, the method, and the reporting system can be deployed in another country, city, or community without relying on the accidental heroism of individuals.</div>
    <div class="custom-tag">scalable outcome</div>
  </div>
</div>
