## 🚀 Project Development Stages
#### Roadmap from Pilot to a Scalable Humanitarian System

Our project evolves not randomly but along a clear evolutionary line: from the first pilot sessions to an international support network. Each stage builds on the previous one and strengthens the next.

---

## 🔬 1. Experimental Work

The starting stage is a **research phase** where we test and adapt the method to the real-life conditions of veterans and their families.

#### What the pilot includes:
- Sessions with veterans of different categories  
- Collection of physical and emotional responses  
- Fine‑tuning the methodology to address real needs: PTSD, anxiety, sleep disorders, family overload  

#### Extended areas:
- **Scientometrics:** performance metrics, data collection, research notes, preparation of articles  
- **Marketing analytics:** research into target audiences (veterans, families, facilitators, volunteers)  
- **Game‑based practices and animation:** soft ways to attract attention and lower barriers  
- **Participant selection:** identifying people who can become facilitators and community leaders  
- **All‑weather suit:** enables practices:
  - year‑round  
  - in field conditions  
  - as part of the kit for mobile specialists  

#### Outcome:
We obtain an evidence base validated by practice and data.

---

## 👫 2. Couple Training

The second stage creates **micro‑units of family resilience** – pairs who can support each other at home.

#### Stage content:
- Training in basic SunLife-Massage techniques  
- Creation of sustainable “for home, for family” nodes  
- Minimising the need for regular external services  

#### Outcome:
The family gains not a one‑time help but a permanent recovery mechanism.

---

# 🎓 3. Educational Product and Online Course

After the pilot and the first trained couples, we build a full‑fledged mass‑learning system.

#### Course structure:
- **Level 1 — “For Yourself”**  
  Basic stabilisation, work with anxiety and bodily tension  

- **Level 2 — “For the Family”**  
  Working as a pair, spousal support, strengthening relationships  

- **Level 3 — “Pro”**  
  In‑depth training of method specialists  

#### Certification:
Integrated into the gamified IMARCH system:
- levels  
- badges  
- exams  
- reputation  

#### Outcome:
The method becomes accessible to a wide range of people, with guaranteed quality.

---

## 🌐 4. Community and Gamification

The project structure forms a **living community** where everyone can both receive and give support.

#### Components:
- internal social network  
- ratings and levels  
- referral circles  
- automated practice tracking  

Every session conducted means:
- help for another person  
- growth of one’s own competence  
- contribution to reputation  

#### Outcome:
A self‑sustaining social system emerges.

---

## 📱 5. Massage Aggregator

The aggregator is the technological core that turns the humanitarian block into a sustainable economic model.

#### Functions:
- search and booking of specialists  
- commercial infrastructure  
- field testing of the business model  

#### Supporting elements:
- **specialist training only through NGOs / licensed studios**  
- **mobile massage cabin** – turns any space into a therapeutic environment  

#### Outcome:
The project gains its own financial sustainability, reducing dependence on donors.

---

## ✅ Final Conclusion

The project development stages form an integral, sequential system:

- **Experimental work** → evidence base  
- **Couple training** → local sustainability  
- **Educational product** → mass reach  
- **Community** → social fabric  
- **Aggregator** → economic model  

This is not just a plan.  
It is **an evolution from helping one person to an international support system** that works anywhere in the world.
