The **Vis Realitatis** project is open to participation by people with different capacities, resources and motivations.  

We have deliberately structured the system so that **every contribution, financial or non‑financial, is meaningful and recorded**.

Below are the main participation formats.

---

## ❤️ Micro‑donations

**For whom:**  
For those who want to support the project with any amount they are comfortable with.

**How it works:**  
- you make a donation through the crowdfunding platform;
- funds are allocated to a specific project stage (e.g. Stage 1);
- a report is published at the end of the stage.

**Why this matters:**  
Even small contributions, when gathered collectively, make it possible to launch real processes.

---

## 🎯 Targeted donations

**For whom:**  
For donors who need to know **exactly where their funds go**.

**How it works:**  
- support for a specific budget item (methodology, software, prototypes);
- transparent recording in reports;
- the contribution is linked to the results of that stage.

**Outcome:**  
The donor sees a direct connection between their contribution and the achieved impact.

---

## 🤝 Partnership

**For whom:**  
For organisations and initiatives ready to support the project with resources, infrastructure or expertise.

**Forms of partnership:**  
- provision of venues and premises;
- logistical and organisational support;
- technological partnership;
- information support.

**Important:**  
Partnerships are recorded as part of the ecosystem and are reflected in the project’s public materials.

---

## 🎓 Expert involvement

**For whom:**  
For specialists willing to contribute their professional expertise to the development of methodology, research and standards.

**Forms of involvement:**  
- consultations and peer review;
- participation in research and methodological units;
- supervision and evaluation of results.

**Principle:**  
Expert involvement is not one‑off help, but a contribution to the sustainability and quality of the project.

---

## 🌱 Volunteer involvement

**For whom:**  
For people ready to help with their time, skills and engagement.

**Examples:**  
- assistance in organising events;
- communication and participant support;
- participation in pilot activities.

**What matters:**  
Volunteer contributions are recognised and recorded as part of the overall work.

---

## 🔎 Transparency of participation

Regardless of the format chosen:
- every contribution is recorded;
- stage results are published;
- the logic of resource allocation is open.

We are building a project where **participation is not an anonymous gesture, but a conscious contribution to a shared system**.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

You can participate:
- as a donor,
- as a partner,
- as an expert,
- as a volunteer.

Every format is important.  
Every contribution matters.  
Every participation becomes part of a common architecture of trust.
