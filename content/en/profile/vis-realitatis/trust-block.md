## Trust Is Not a Slogan, It Is an Architecture

We do not consider trust a matter of belief or “verbal” reputation.  
For us, trust is the result of a properly constructed system.

That is why Vis Realitatis is designed from the outset as a **transparent and verifiable structure**.

---

## Principle 1. Stages Instead of Promises

We do not raise funds “for everything at once”.

Each project stage has:
- a clear goal,
- a limited timeframe,
- a transparent budget,
- concrete KPIs,
- a mandatory report upon completion.

A new stage is not launched until the previous one is finished and documented.

---

## Principle 2. Transparent Financial Logic

We show in advance:
- exactly what the funds will be used for,
- which expenditure items are foreseen,
- which payments are fixed (including experts),
- what results must be achieved.

For us, a donor’s money is a responsibility, not a resource “on trust”.

---

## Principle 3. Documenting Every Step

All key project actions:
- methodological decisions,
- pilot events,
- developments,
- reports,

are recorded and formatted as verifiable artefacts.

This prevents “dilution of results” and makes it possible to return to each stage.

---

## Principle 4. Ethics of Working with Beneficiaries

We consciously:
- avoid dramatising stories,
- do not use pain as marketing,
- work only with voluntary participation and consent.

First – real help and a pilot.  
Public stories only afterwards and with the participants’ permission.

---

## Principle 5. Limiting Scaling

The project **does not grow faster than it can be held to quality**.

If the system cannot bear the load:
- we do not expand the scope,
- we do not increase fundraising,
- we do not add new regions.

Sustainability is more important to us than speed.

---

## What This Means for the Donor

- You understand where and why your contribution is directed.
- You can see the results of a stage, not promises.
- You are not financing an “idea”; you are supporting a verifiable process.

---

## Our Core Rule

**First structure.  
Then trust.  
And only after that, scaling.**

That is how we are building Vis Realitatis.
