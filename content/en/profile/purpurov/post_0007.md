#### 🎙️ The Press of Social Proof { .fade-in-up }

**If the press shows interest, it means there is something special in what you do – because a journalist swims in a daily stream of content and won’t risk their reputation for trivial gain.**

Today anyone can run a news feed, but a professional environment is different: it applies quality selection criteria and demands a certain standard of presentation. That is what distinguishes a mere flow of information from journalism of a professional level.

Therefore, publication in the press is not so much advertising as it is social proof of the significance of your work. It confirms that your efforts go beyond personal interest and become part of the public conversation.

---

>Media is not advertising. It is proof for society.
