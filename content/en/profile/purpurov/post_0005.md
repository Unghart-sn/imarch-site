#### 🎙️ Without Creativity, There Is No Progress { .fade-in-up }

**We do not exist in isolation, but in connection with society. And when society moves forward, it begins to value not just activity, but the quality of creative contribution. It is the ability to create something new that allows us to break out of the closed circle of repetition.**

Creativity does not necessarily mean artistic expression. Although, where would we be without it...

More often, it is a practical necessity. In my case, I never sought to stand out or demonstrate originality. 

It was simply that every time I took on a task, I discovered that much in that area was either underdeveloped or not brought to a sustainable result. What already existed did not satisfy me.

That is how original solutions and developments emerged. Not for the sake of demonstration, but because the task could not be solved otherwise. I had to take on things that, for various reasons, no one else would. Why it turned out this way will become clear in time. For now, there are only the facts of completed work and created forms.

The Registry of Authorial Acts records not ambitions, but the necessity of action in situations where forward movement was required.
