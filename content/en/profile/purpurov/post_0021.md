#### The Moment of Launch

The previous series of texts described the problem, the architecture and the pilot model.

Now the practical stage begins.

We are launching the first operational circuit of Chronicle OS through Vis Realitatis.

This is not a declaration of intent.

It is the launch of infrastructure.

---

#### The Pilot

The pilot is necessary to test:

• the robustness of the protocols
• the correctness of act recording
• the transparency of reporting
• the viability of the role model

The pilot takes place in a real environment.
No simulations.

Every contribution activates the system.

---

#### Target: 8000 GBP

The starting goal of the first circle is 8000 GBP.

These funds are allocated to:

• launching the operational module
• covering the first support cases
• technical maintenance of the protocol
• establishing a public register

The amount is limited.

The goal is concrete.

---

#### The First Circle of Participants

The first circle is recorded.

Each participant:

• is registered in the system
• receives confirmation of their act of participation
• enters the public register of the first circuit
• becomes part of the institutional memory of the launch

This is not a list of donors.

This is a list of the founders of the first operational level.

#### Principle of Participation

Participation is voluntary.

No emotional pressure.
No urgency for effect.

The system is being launched.

Those who consider the architecture necessary may enter the first circle.

Those who prefer to observe will be able to assess the results of the pilot.

---

#### What Happens Next

After the goal is reached:

• a first‑cycle report is produced
• the structure of acts is published
• roles are recorded
• the possibility of connecting subsequent nodes opens

The pilot moves into operational mode.

---

#### Conclusion

Chronicle OS is not built on promises.

It is built on actions.

The first circle is the point of fixation.

If you are ready to participate,
you enter the architecture from the very beginning.

Calmly.
Consciously.
Structurally.
