### GENERAL INFORMATION { .fade-in-up }

Oleksandr Purpurov is the founder and Managing Director of IMARCH LTD UK, a research and education company that turns bureaucracy and infrastructure into a living chronicle.

<details>
  <summary>About the company🔻</summary>

>IMARCH LTD is a registered company in the United Kingdom, focused on trust architecture, legal clarity, and institutional packaging of donor driven solutions.
We design infrastructure that puts everything in its place, from documents to visual systems, from contracts to collective rituals.

>**Company number:** 16791765  
**Legal form:** Private Limited Company (LTD)  
**Mission:** to turn technical and legal complexity into clear, reproducible solutions for communities and donors.
