One can argue endlessly about who shapes whom. Does a person choose a profession, or does the profession transform the person? In practice, it is always a reciprocal process.

Through work we test our principles; through labour we learn to see and soberly assess reality.

This is not about personal qualities or character. It is about what emerges in the process of activity. About approaches, mistakes, searches, and solutions found. I am sharing not biography for its own sake, but what has proven viable in practice.

A professional story continues. It unfolds in projects, in new challenges, in attempts to make the complex slightly more sustainable. And it is in this living movement that the ongoing conversation takes shape.
