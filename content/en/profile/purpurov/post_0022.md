Not because they don't want to.

The result: improvisation under pressure, missed opportunities, avoidable mistakes.

**Negotiation Architecture** is a complete instrumental package built specifically for your negotiation — not a template, not a course, not coaching.

### How It Works

**Stage 1 — Intake** *(Day 1)*
You complete a structured onboarding brief. We identify context, counterparty, stakes, objectives.

**Stage 2 — Architecture** *(Days 2–4)*
Counterparty analysis, objection map, response script, meeting scenario. Private repo goes live.

**Stage 3 — Live Support** *(Day of negotiation)*
Pre-meeting alignment call (30 min). Repo updated with final notes. Post-meeting debrief (45 min).

**Stage 4 — Next Round** *(within 5 days)*
Full outcome analysis. Updated package for second meeting. Repo archived.
