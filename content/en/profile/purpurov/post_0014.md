#### 🎙️ Alexander Purpurov · IMARCH · Universal Formology { .fade-in-up }

**I grew up in a family where working with systems was simply part of life.**

My mother specialised in methodology within national extracurricular education and environmental programmes. My father headed the chemical laboratory of a major city’s water treatment facilities.

Pedagogy, ecology, applied science. These were not topics of conversation. They were modes of thinking.

It was during those years that a question began to take shape within me, though I would only articulate it clearly decades later:

How are living systems structured?

A person.  
An organisation.  
An idea.  
A society.

---

My professional path may appear diverse: formal training in the arts, research work, design projects, body-oriented practice, the registration of proprietary methodologies, consulting, tourism initiatives, educational programmes.

In 2011, one of my projects reached first position in Google for the phrase “Meaningful Travel”.

From the outside, this may look like a scattered biography.

From within, it was continuity.

I have been exploring the same principle throughout.  
Simply through different forms.

---

Working closely with the human body teaches something essential: it is not technique that matters most, but structure.

A disturbance in one area reflects imbalance in the whole.  
Recovery begins not with the symptom, but with form.

I observed the same principle in organisations.

Strong ideas rarely collapse due to lack of funding.  
More often, they dissolve because they lack a structure capable of sustaining intention over time.

No transparent agreements.  
No mechanism for succession.  
No shared institutional memory.

And each time, the initiative begins again from zero.

This is not coincidence.

It is institutional vacuum.

---

I cannot reshape the entire environment.

But I can build durable infrastructure within it.

This is how IMARCH GitChronicle OS | Chronicle OS emerged.

Not as a document management system.  
But as an operational environment for institutional memory and subjecthood.

The project begins with Vis Realitatis — practical support for veterans.  
From there, the model is designed to scale into professional and mission-driven initiatives.

This is not about building a tool.

It is about establishing a framework capable of outliving its creators.

---

Why now.

Recently, I lost a colleague, a senior academic with whom I had been discussing participation in a veterans’ rehabilitation programme.  
Another professional I once worked with now has a street named in his honour in my home city.

People leave.

Ideas endure only when they are properly anchored.

If a principle is not institutionalised, it disappears with its bearer.

IMARCH GitChronicle OS | Chronicle OS is an attempt to prevent that disappearance.

---

If the question of systemic durability resonates with you, follow the upcoming publications.

I write about how living systems are structured, why they collapse, and how they can be designed to endure.

Practice.  
Architecture.  
Without unnecessary noise.
