# 🏛️ Formological Consulting

## 📐 Basic Module of Institutional Design
A practical methodology that turns an idea, initiative or project into a sustainable institutional form.  

Most projects are built around the founder’s personality.  
But sustainable systems are built around **roles, protocols and acts of recording**.  

Formological Consulting creates a minimal architecture that allows an initiative to outlast the personal circumstances of its participants and continue to develop.

## ❌ Why Good Projects Disappear
Most initiatives die not because they are bad, but because of a lack of structure.  

Most often the reason is one of three:  
- 👥 no distributed roles  
- 🔄 no interaction protocols  
- 📄 no recording of decisions and contributions  

When everything rests on one person, the project disappears with that person’s resources.  
This condition is called **institutional vacuum**.

---

## 🌌 What Is Institutional Vacuum
Institutional vacuum arises when there is an idea and energy, but no structure.  

People act, decisions are made, work gets done – but nothing is recorded and nothing turns into a sustainable form.  
As a result, each new iteration starts almost from scratch.

---

## 🛠️ What Formological Consulting Offers
The method of creating a minimal institutional architecture includes four elements:  
- 🔍 diagnosis of institutional vacuum  
- 👥 formation of a role structure  
- 📑 creation of interaction protocols  
- 📄 recording of acts and decisions  

This is the minimum set that allows an initiative to become a system.

---

## 🌍 Where the Method Is Applied
- 💼 entrepreneurial projects  
- 👩‍🔬 expert communities  
- ❤️ non‑profit initiatives  
- 🎓 educational programmes  
- 👪 family systems  

The task is the same: to create a sustainable form of interaction.

---

## 🚀 What We Are Creating Now
A basic **four‑week module** of formological consulting is being formed.  

It will allow us to:  
- test the methodology  
- collect practical cases  
- create methodological materials  
- prepare the foundation for scaling  

---

## 🧩 Programme Architecture
The programme consists of **4 modules**, each following the formula:  
🧠 Awareness → 📐 Structure → 👐 Practice → 📄 Recording

---

### 📍 Module 1 — Diagnosis of Institutional Vacuum
**Goal:** to understand why the project remains unsustainable.  
**Theory:**  
- the concept of institutional vacuum  
- the difference between an initiative and a structure  
- three basic project failures  

**Practice:**  
- what am I building  
- where is structure absent  
- where does everything rest on me  

**Result:** a clear understanding of the problem.

---

### 📍 Module 2 — Role and Responsibility
**Goal:** to separate the person from the function.  
**Theory:**  
- what a role is  
- why role is more important than personality  
- why projects die with their founder  

**Practice:** create a role map of the project:  
- who makes decisions  
- who is responsible  
- who substitutes  

**Result:** a responsibility scheme emerges.

---

### 📍 Module 3 — Protocol of Trust
**Goal:** to create minimal formalisation.  
**Theory:**  
- what an act is  
- why verbal agreements are unstable  
- what an interaction protocol is  

**Practice:** a one‑page protocol:  
- how we make decisions  
- how we record agreements  
- how we document contributions  

**Result:** the first institutional element.

---

### 📍 Module 4 — Act of Recording
**Goal:** to turn the result into a document.  
**Theory:**  
- what institutional memory is  
- why the unrecorded disappears  

**Practice:** an official project document (memorandum, regulation, act of recording).  

**Result:** the first recorded institution.

---

## 🕒 Format
- 4 weeks  
- 1 meeting per week  
- ⏱️ 60–90 minutes  

**Meeting structure:**  
- 20 minutes theory  
- 40 minutes discussion  
- 30 minutes questions  

💬 A shared chat operates between meetings.

This is the minimal institutional foundation.

# 🗺️ IMARCH Development Roadmap

## 📆 Three‑Year Horizon
IMARCH is being built as an institutional system that develops through practical pilots and the gradual formation of infrastructure.  
The project’s development proceeds in several stages.

---

## 🔹 Stage 1 — Forming the System’s Foundation
**Period:** first year  

The main task is to create the basic elements of the architecture.  

📌 Pilot programmes are launched:  
- 🧘 online facial self‑massage course  
- 🏛️ basic module of formological consulting  
- 💾 Chronicle Communication System pilot  

**These programmes allow us to:**  
- test the methodology  
- collect first cases  
- prepare educational materials  
- form a community of participants  

**Key result:** emergence of working protocols and the first institutional acts.

---

## 🔹 Stage 2 — Scaling Practices
**Period:** second year  

After the pilots are completed, the programmes are expanded.  

📌 We create:  
- full‑fledged educational programmes  
- a series of publications and analytical materials  
- an Ambassador programme  
- an expert community of the project  

The method of formological consulting is applied in different contexts:  
- 💼 entrepreneurial projects  
- 🎓 educational initiatives  
- ❤️ non‑profit organisations  

**Key result:** emergence of a network of projects using the method.

---

## 🔹 Stage 3 — Forming an Institutional Environment
**Period:** third year  

The Chronicle Communication System becomes the full‑fledged digital infrastructure of the project.  

📌 The system allows:  
- recording of actions and decisions  
- formation of institutional memory  
- creation of a transparent interaction environment  

IMARCH turns into a platform for the institutional development of various initiatives.  

**Key result:** a sustainable network of projects and communities.

# 📜 IMARCH Manifesto

The modern world is full of initiatives.  
People create projects, launch communities, unite around ideas.  

But most of these initiatives remain fragile:  
- they depend on the energy of individuals  
- on circumstances  
- on random coincidences  

⚠️ When the founder’s energy runs out, the project disappears.  
This happens again and again.

---

## ❌ The Problem Is Not the People
People know how to create ideas.  
People know how to inspire.  

But ideas and inspiration alone do not create sustainable systems.  
Sustainability appears when actions, roles and decisions take shape.  

This shape is called an **institution**.

---

## 🏛️ What Is an Institution
An institution is not an organisation or a position.  
An institution is a form that outlasts people.  

It arises when:  
- actions are recorded  
- roles are distributed  
- decisions become part of a shared memory  

---

## 🌐 Why IMARCH Exists
IMARCH creates an environment in which initiatives can become institutions.  

To do this, the project connects three elements:  
- 🧘 personal practice  
- 🏛️ organisational form  
- 💾 institutional memory  

Practice creates experience.  
Form creates structure.  
Memory creates sustainability.

---

## 🔹 Small Actions Create Large Systems
IMARCH is not built through declarations.  
It is built through small acts:  
- every recorded contribution  
- every decision taken  
- every formalised protocol  

Over time, these acts accumulate into a system.

---

## 🌟 Founding Cohort
Every institution begins with a small circle of people.  
These people do not just observe – they participate in creating the form.  

The **IMARCH Founding Cohort** is the first circle of participants who help shape the foundation of the system.  
Their contribution is recorded in the project chronicle.

---

## 🔮 The Future Is Built Through Form
Ideas can inspire.  
But only form makes ideas sustainable.  

IMARCH exists so that ideas can become forms.
