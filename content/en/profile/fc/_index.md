#### THE METHOD OF FORMOLOGICAL CONSULTING

Formological Consulting (Method of Oleksandr Purpurov) is a standardised system for analysing and designing institutional environments, based on the principles of form, reproducibility, and the recording of acts.

The method is applied to diagnose structural obstacles, develop interaction protocols, and create sustainable organisational models.

The practical implementation of the method is carried out within the IMARCH architecture and is supported by the digital environment of the Chronicle Communication System.

>The method is regarded as one of the core implementation tools of the Vis Realitatis sub‑project within IMARCH Githronical | Chronicle OS

We are launching the assembly of the basic methodological module IMARCH Pilot Program | Vis Realitatis Protocol within the **pilot module of the Vis Realitatis project** – a space for the first co‑founders, where reality takes shape.

### GENERAL INFORMATION { .fade-in-up }

The method was developed based on extensive research activity, scientific studies, and the Author's total practical experience in the field of Universal Formology of over 30 years.

Today, it is a comprehensive system for correcting the state of relationships, significantly enhancing personal, couple, and collective effectiveness.

&nbsp; 

<details>
  <summary>Method overview🔻</summary>

🔹 **Project essence**  

The method of Formological Consulting is an original technology of institutional design that shifts organisational development from the realm of abstract consultations into the sphere of structured protocols.  

The method is based on the principles of role distribution, formalisation of interaction, and the recording of acts, enabling initiatives and projects to transition from personal energy to a sustainable form.

🔹 **Uniqueness**  

A method, not a consultation: a structured technology for institutional analysis and design.  

Protocol architecture: diagnosis of institutional vacuum, creation of a role map, development of interaction protocols, recording of acts.  

Institutional framework: the method is developed under the auspices of IMARCH LTD UK as part of the Chronicle OS architecture.

🔹 **Target audience**  

Entrepreneurs: structuring projects and distributing responsibility.  

Expert communities: organising sustainable collaborative work.  
Non‑profit initiatives: creating transparent interaction mechanisms.  

Institutions: building a protocol environment for management and decision‑making.

🔹 **Practical tasks**  

Overcoming institutional vacuum in projects.  
Creating a minimal sustainable interaction structure.  
Recording roles, decisions, and participant contributions.  
Building institutional memory for projects.

🔹 **Potential**  

Entrepreneurial sector: structuring startups and teams.  
Educational sector: programmes on institutional thinking and management.  

Non‑profit sector: transparent models of collective responsibility.  
International initiatives: a protocol environment for interaction.

🔹 **Legal framework**  

The method of Formological Consulting is developed within the institutional architecture of IMARCH LTD UK.  
All methodological materials and protocols are published within the Chronicle OS framework, ensuring transparency, the recording of acts, and institutional continuity.

⚖️ **Conclusion:**  

The method of Formological Consulting is a technology of institutional design that connects analytical rigour, practical applicability, and the corporate infrastructure of IMARCH.
