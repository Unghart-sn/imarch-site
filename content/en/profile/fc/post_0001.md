## Why Good Projects Disappear
Most initiatives start with an idea and energy.  
Over time they face problems:  
- everything rests on one person  
- no distributed roles  
- no interaction protocols  
- decisions are not recorded  

⚠️ As a result, even strong projects fall apart.  
This condition is called **institutional vacuum**.

---

## What IMARCH Offers
IMARCH creates a system that moves initiatives from personal energy to a sustainable structure.  

The system consists of three elements:  
- 🧘 Personal practice  
- 🏛️ Organisational form  
- 💾 Institutional memory  

---

- 🧘 **Practice** – self‑massage and state regulation
