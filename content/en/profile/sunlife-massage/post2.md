# 🌿 Online Facial Self‑Massage Course  
### 🕊️ Harmonia

A practical course for restoring personal resources and psycho‑emotional balance.  

This is neither a cosmetic procedure nor a motivational training.  
It is a regular practice of working with the soft tissues of the head and face, aimed at restoring normal blood and lymph circulation and regulating the emotional state.

## 🎯 Why This Is Needed

The modern load on the psyche and body has become constant:  
- 💻 long hours at the computer  
- ⚡ chronic tension  
- 🌙 lack of sleep  
- 😰 stressful situations  

All of this affects the state of the nervous system, the face, and overall well‑being.  
Facial self‑massage gently helps restore balance.  

The practice helps:  
- 🌿 relieve tension in the soft tissues of the head  
- 💧 improve blood and lymph circulation  
- 🧘 reduce psycho‑emotional tension  
- 🔎 restore clarity of perception and concentration  

The aesthetic rejuvenation effect is a secondary outcome of the therapy, but it is often the first noticeable sign of change.  

---

## 👥 Who This Course Is For

The course is designed for three levels of practice:  
- 👤 **For yourself** – to learn how to restore your resources and manage your state  
- 🏡 **For the family** – to help loved ones after stress and strain, based on the acquired knowledge and skills  
- 🎓 **For professionals** – to master the basic protocol of the *Sunlife‑Massage* method  

---

## 🧩 What the Practice Involves

Facial self‑massage is a regular training procedure.  
Effectiveness depends on dosage and regularity of performance.  

The full cycle consists of **6 sequential courses**, each working on different areas of the soft tissues of the head and face.  
One course includes **12 practice sessions**.  

📌 Recommended workload:  
- about **3 hours of practice per week**  
- **2–3 sessions per week**  

The minimum acceptable practice is about **30 minutes per week**.  
Even with minimal dosage, a person feels changes, but maximum results are achieved with regular full practice.  

---

## 🧍 Body Position and Technique

The practice can be performed in different positions:  
- 🧍 standing (full form, including facial muscles and the body’s coordination system)  
- 🪑 sitting  
- 🛏️ lying down  
- ⏱️ short sessions during work breaks  

Even five minutes of practice can significantly reduce the level of emotional tension.  

---

## 👐 Preparing for Practice

Before starting a session, it is recommended to:  
- 🚿 wash your hands  
- 🧴 use a moisturiser suitable for your skin type  
- 🤲 do a short hand warm‑up  

Hand preparation includes exercises to improve blood circulation and palm sensitivity – this is an important stage, as the quality of work depends on tactile perception.  

---

## 🔄 Sequence of Work

The practice is built on the principle of moving from the general to the specific.  
Main areas of influence:  
- neck and collar zone  
- scalp and ears  
- forehead  
- facial mask area  
- area under the chin  

Each session combines body movements, breathing, and hand work.  
The key is understanding the principle of impact and dosage, not a mechanical sequence.  

---

## 💻 Course Format

The course is conducted online; only a smartphone is needed.  
Each session is accompanied by a practical assignment.  

The participant performs the practical exercises independently while following the instructor’s demonstration and records the result:  
- 📷 photo before the session  
- 📷 photo after the session  
- 📝 a brief comment on how they feel  

This helps to see real changes and adjust the practice.  

---

## 👩‍🏫 Participant Support

The practice takes place in small groups.  

The instructor reviews reports daily and answers questions.  
If necessary, additional explanatory videos are recorded.  

This format allows everyone to progress at their own pace and receive support.  

---

## 🔬 Research Module

The course is part of the **Vis Realitatis** pilot programme.  
An observation base for scientific analysis is being built:  
- recording of state dynamics  
- analysis of subjective sensations  
- preparation of materials for publications in scientometric literature  

Thus, the practice becomes not only training but also a research process in evidence‑based medicine, under the guidance of Dr. Alexey Kalmykov, MD, with the participation of Dr. Anastasia Bezrodnaya, PhD in Biology.
