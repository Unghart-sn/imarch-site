### GENERAL INFORMATION { .fade-in-up }

The method was developed based on a unique system of massage therapist training, scientific research and the Author's total practical experience of 50+ years.

Today, it is a comprehensive system for correcting a person’s condition, significantly enhancing their personal effectiveness, moral and physical form.

<details>
  <summary>Method overview🔻</summary>

🔹 **Project essence**

Sunlife‑Massage® is an original method that shifts massage from the service sphere into the sphere of technology. The method is based on a unique research base, unique theoretical, physical, technical and methodological training of the specialist.

🔹 **Uniqueness**

A method, not a service: a structured technology that can be taught and certified.

Complete protocol: covers all stages – diagnosis, technique, completeness of working through the body’s systems, integration of the result.
Protection of IMARCH LTD UK: trademark and corporate framework guaranteeing legitimacy.

🔹 **Target audience**

Specialists: training, certification, standardisation of practice.
Clients: a holistic path to restoring and harmonising the body.
Institutions: a branded product ready for international positioning.

🔹**Potential**

Medical market: integration into rehabilitation and prevention.
Educational market: courses, programmes, specialist certification.

International health and wellness market: positioning as an original system.

🔹 **Legal framework**

Sunlife‑Massage® is a registered trademark.
All materials are published under the umbrella of IMARCH LTD, ensuring corporate protection and trust.

⚖️ Conclusion:
The complete massage protocol method Sunlife‑Massage® is a branded health technology that connects academic rigour, practical effectiveness and corporate protection.
