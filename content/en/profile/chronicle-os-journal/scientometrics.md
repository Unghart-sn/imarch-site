The IMARCH Githronical | Chronicle OS project is a **closed‑cycle research and production enterprise** where ideology, technology, verification and production operate as a single system.

The four blocks — School of Nine Arts (methodology), specialized software (measurement), Chronicle OS Journal (scientometric verification) and Q‑HUB‑STUDIO (real sector) — form a closed loop.

Each element amplifies the others, and the journal serves not as a dead archive but as a **knowledge pipeline** without which the cycle cannot be completed.

Below are the key components of the ecosystem:

{{< strategic-core >}}

&nbsp;

### 📖 Chronicle OS Journal as a structural link of the closed cycle

Within the four‑block structure of IMARCH Githronical, the journal performs the following functions:

- **Scientometric verification**: each article undergoes double‑blind peer review, receives a DOI and a SHA‑256 hash, and is recorded in the registry.
- **Knowledge pipeline**: data standardisation and digital fixation allow a scientific case to be prepared in 30 minutes instead of months.
- **Capitalisation of scientific output**: publications become the basis for grants, licensing and university rankings.
- **Integration with the production unit**: developments from Q‑HUB‑STUDIO (workwear, massage suits, interfaces) undergo scientific verification in the journal and receive DOI as elements of a living knowledge base.

**Result:** the journal is not a storage of articles, but an **active node** without which the «ideology → measurement → verification → production» cycle cannot be closed.
