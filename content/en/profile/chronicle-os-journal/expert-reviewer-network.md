## 🏛️ Institutional Manifesto: Peer Review as Capital { .fade-in-up }

The academic world faces a peer review crisis: experts are expected to provide "free labour" for years to benefit giant publishing corporations, spending weeks proofreading incorrectly formatted materials. **IMARCH Chronicle OS breaks this paradigm.**

According to **Institutional Protocol RN-0031**, we view expert peer review as a measurable professional contribution requiring direct monetisation, institutional recognition, and legal protection. We guarantee that every hour of an expert's work is transformed into personal capital — academic, financial, or status-based.

### How does the expert's time economy work?
1. **Primary screening on the platform side:** Manuscripts undergo automatic plagiarism control, structure verification, and SHA-256 cryptographic hash calculation.
2. **Purified "Blind Vector":** The reviewer receives a structured text without author details (Double-Blind Peer Review).
3. **15-Minute Standard:** Thanks to complete technical pre-processing of the manuscript, the expert's net time for completing the standardised form is **up to 15 minutes**.

## ⚙️ Operational Circuits and Interaction Standards { .fade-in-up }

### 1. Barter Circuit (Academic Exchange)
For actively publishing scientists and heads of scientific schools. For every **6 conducted reviews**, the platform automatically generates a personal digital **Publication Voucher**. The voucher covers 100% of the APC (Article Processing Charge) for your own article, the work of your postgraduate student, or doctoral candidate. The publication includes DOI assignment, registration in the British registry, and cryptographic priority protection.

### 2. Tokenised Circuit (Investment Capital)
For specialists focused on long-term capitalisation. The accrual of **25 Series 8888 tokens** per review is recorded in the Chronicle OS registry. The IMARCH LTD ecosystem redistributes **30% of net commercial profit** (from consulting, commercial APC, and know-how licensing) among token holders. Your tokens are protected against inflation and provide cascading coefficients when voting on grants.

### 3. Commercial Circuit (Fiat Payments)
Direct financial compensation under a consulting services agreement with IMARCH LTD (UK).
* **Fixed rate:** **£25** per fully completed expert form.
* **Speed and comfort:** All articles are submitted in standardised markdown/PDF format with end-to-end navigation. You don't waste time looking for typos — you evaluate methodology and scientific novelty.
* **Payments:** Made monthly via PayPal, Wise, Payoneer, international IBAN, or cryptocurrency equivalent (USDT).
