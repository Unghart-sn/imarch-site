### GENERAL INFORMATION ABOUT THE PLATFORM { .fade-in-up }

Methodological framework: The scientific doctrine of the journal is based on the paradigm of Synthesis of Complex Systems and fully aligns with the global international One Health concept. Biomedical safety, digital and technical infrastructure, and behavioural economics are treated as a single, interconnected operational environment.

## Review Regulations and Stages

### 1. Preprint Registration and Rights Fixation
- Upon submission, the manuscript is assigned a unique system identifier.  
- The source file undergoes cryptographic sealing with the generation of a **SHA‑256 hash**.  
- Only a **Pre‑publication Card** (title, abstract, keywords and hash lock) is published on the official website.  
- The full text of the article is stored in the IMARCH LTD institutional repository to protect the author's intellectual property.  

### 2. Initial Screening and Reviewer Assignment
- The Editor‑in‑Chief checks the material for compliance with the journal's scientific profiles:  
  - Universal Formology  
  - Complex Systems Management  
  - Life Safety  
- If the result is positive, the anonymised text is sent to two independent experts from the editorial board or external reviewers.  

### 3. Review Timelines and Verdicts
- The standard review period is **up to 6 weeks**.  
- Possible decisions:  
  - **Accepted:** the article is moved to the publication status, the final layout is prepared, the text and the author's name are disclosed.  
  - **Minor/Major Revision:** the material is returned to the author for correction of technical or conceptual inaccuracies.  
  - **Rejected:** the article is rejected. The card on the website receives the status *Rejected*, and the full text remains forever closed in the rejection archive without public access.  

### 4. Finality of the Decision
- The decision of the Editorial Board is final.  
- It is not subject to appeal by authors.

#### Regulations and Stages of Expert Evaluation:

1. **Preprint Registration and Rights Fixation:**  
   Upon submission of a manuscript to the editorial office, it is assigned a unique system ID, and the source file is immediately cryptographically sealed with the generation of a SHA-256 hash. Only a **Pre-publication Card** (title, abstract, keywords and hash lock) is published on the official website. The full text of the article is closed in the IMARCH LTD institutional repository to protect the author's intellectual property from plagiarism during the evaluation stage.

2. **Initial Screening and Assignment of «Bears»:**  
   The Editor-in-Chief conducts an initial assessment of the material for compliance with the journal's scientific profiles (Universal Formology, Complex Systems Management). If the material passes the initial sieve, the anonymised text is sent to two independent experts of the Editorial Board (or external reviewers).

3. **Review Timelines and Verdicts:**  
   The standard review period is **up to 6 weeks**. Based on the evaluation results, the reviewers issue one of the following verdicts:  
   * **Accepted:** the article is moved to the publication status, the final layout is prepared, the «guts» of the text and the author's name are disclosed.  
   * **Minor/Major Revision:** the material is returned to the author for correction of technical or conceptual inaccuracies.  
   * **Rejected:** the article fails the review. The card on the website receives the status *Rejected*, and the text itself remains forever closed in the rejection archive («bag») without demonstrating the content to the public, which preserves the reputation of the authors and the purity of the platform.

The decision of the Editorial Board is final and not subject to appeal by applicants.

### ▼ Publication Ethics and APC Policy

{{< company-details-card >}}

The international journal **Chronicle OS** is guided by the principles of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and establishes strict transparency standards for all participants in the publishing process.

#### 1. Publication Ethics and Author Obligations
By submitting material to the Chronicle OS journal, authors guarantee that:
  
* The presented research is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration by other publications.  
* The material contains no elements of plagiarism, data falsification or unlawful borrowing.  
* All co-authors have made a significant contribution to the work and agree to its submission for pre‑verification.

The editorial board reserves the right to use automated scripts and expert evaluation to detect any forms of content manipulation.

#### 2. Article Processing Charge (APC) Policy
The Chronicle OS journal operates as an Open Access model after passing peer review, has no external funding and does not host commercial advertising.

To maintain the digital infrastructure of the platform, a fixed editorial fee (APC) of **£150 (one hundred fifty pounds sterling)** per submitted manuscript is established.

**Crucial regulatory provisions of the APC:**
  
1. The fee is collected by the platform operator — **IMARCH LTD (UK)** — solely to cover the costs of technical support: cryptographic sealing, generation of SHA-256 hashes, payment of independent reviewers («bear sieve») and support of depository servers.
  
2. **Payment of the APC is not a purchase of publication.** The fee is made at the pre‑verification stage to launch the evaluation mechanism.  
3. If, as a result of double‑blind peer review, the article is rejected (verdict *Rejected*), **the APC amount is not refunded**, because the infrastructural work of analysing and reviewing the material has been performed in full. In this case, the material is packaged into a closed archive without the right of public display.

Attempts to put pressure on the Editorial Board, to offer unlawful benefits or to violate academic ethics lead to immediate cancellation of the manuscript at any stage without a refund of any fees paid.

**Privacy Policy of IMARCH LTD**
  
We do not collect personal data of visitors and do not use contact information for mailings.

No information is shared with third parties.
