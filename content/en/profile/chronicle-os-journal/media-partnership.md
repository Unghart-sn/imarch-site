## PRESS RELEASE { .fade-in-up }

IMARCH LTD presents Chronicle OS: a monograph, a research ecosystem, and a new infrastructure for scholarly interaction.

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**London, United Kingdom — 12 June 2026**

**IMARCH LTD** announces the publication of the interdisciplinary monograph *"The Architecture of Sovereign Science and Multi‑Agent Orchestration of Digital Ecosystems"*.

The monograph has been deposited in the international open science repository Zenodo and assigned the DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20611942](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20611942).

The work comprises 11 interconnected chapters addressing scholarly communication, digital verification, research capital management, compliance, peer review, and multi‑agent orchestration of intelligent systems. Each chapter maintains its own version control system and is accompanied by cryptographic registration of materials using the SHA-256 algorithm.

The monograph establishes the methodological, economic, and architectural foundation of the **Chronicle OS** research ecosystem, designed to enhance the transparency of scholarly communications, protect research priority, and coordinate expert interaction in the rapidly evolving landscape of digital technologies and artificial intelligence.

#### Key Project Areas { .fade-in-up }

- **International academic collaboration** — agreements and memoranda with UMSS (Ukraine) and other academic organisations.
- **Development of Universal Formology methodology** — enhancing structural coherence of scientific knowledge and reducing subjectivity in expert assessment.
- **Scholarly publication infrastructure** — IMARCH CitHronical publishing environment with cryptographic priority fixation.
- **Digital research hubs** — Vis Realitatis and CCS projects for data visualisation and collective security.
- **Practical interdisciplinary initiatives** — projects at the intersection of science, technology, education, sports, and civic initiatives.

#### Commentary { .fade-in-up }

> *"We view Chronicle OS not as a standalone software product but as a long‑term research infrastructure that integrates scholarly activity, digital verification, intellectual property, and modern knowledge coordination tools. The publication of the monograph marks a significant milestone in the institutional development of the project and lays the groundwork for international academic cooperation, new research programmes, and future grant initiatives."*
>
> **Alexander Purpurov**, Director, IMARCH LTD
