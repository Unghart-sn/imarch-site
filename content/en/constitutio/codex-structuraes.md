> ## 📐 On disciplinary density

In the process of formulating the *Scientometrics* section, we encountered the need to **assess the disciplinary density** of the corpus of Universal Formology.

Based on internal mapping, the architecture of Formology including AISKHEN-8888, Codex Formologiae, GANKHARMANT, PLATAN+, Custodia, Sunlife-Massage® is already comparable in volume and disciplinary coupling to **1.2–1.5 Kant**.
