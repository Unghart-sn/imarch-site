### Admodum Reverende Domine Nicolae Gavrilī fīli!

In primis, gratias ago denuo pro colloquio aperto atque sententia confirmata. Ut recte notavisti, clavis exsequendi in re positum est. Hic ego, ut fundator et director Imarch LTD UK, sermones nostros in tres propositiones concretas ordino, quae se invicem complete supplent atque fundamentum veri foederis strategici constituunt.

Contextus: Imarch LTD UK est structura internationalis quae formologiam applicatam provehit — methodologiam ad systemata complexa analyzanda, designanda et praedicenda. Nostrum fundamentum: monographia auctoris (2007), XVII annorum instrumentorum evolutio, societas in Britanniarum Regno registrata, et «Constitutio» programmatica solide descripta.

### PROPOSITIO I. CREATIO PLATFORMAE SCIENTIFICAE-APPLICATAE (LABORATORII/CENTRI) IN INSTITUTO FUNDATI
Scopus: Cooperatio in formam institutionalem redigenda ad actionem scientificam, stipendiariam et peritialem legitimam.

Formae concretae interactionis (sine onere in bilancium instituti):

Laboratorium investigationis scientificae sub patrocinio instituti. Nos (Imarch) partes methodologicas et technologicas agimus, instrumentarium investigationis praebentes (exempli gratia, programma Pythia+ ad exempla construenda). Institutum nobis praebet legitimitatem, accessum ad retia academica et facultatem co-directionis doctorandorum.

«Cathedra fundamentalis» vel «Area experimentalis». Forma ad modulos et methodologias educationis probandas in argumentis: analysis firmitatis systematum, accessus formologicus in scientiis socialibus, elementa prognosticorum.

Consortium ad stipendia. Praeparatio communis petitionum ad Fondos Unionis Europaeae (ut Horizon Europe, stipendia ad scientiam civilem) sub thematibus restitutionis post-conflictualis, transformationis digitalis, technologiarum humanitariarum.

Nostrum collatum: Methodologia, administratio proiectus, infrastructura informatica, contactus internationales.
Petitio: Determinatio formae organizationalis-iuridicae instituto maxime acceptae, atque initium operae ad Statuta/Regulationem talis laboratorii componenda.

### PROPOSITIO II. PROIECTUS COMMUNIS: «COMPLEXUS PROGRAMMATUM ADAPTATUS PRO CENTRO RESTITUTIONIS VETERANORUM IN REGIONE CHARCOVIENSI»
Scopus: Non conceptum abstractum, sed productum paratum et ad petitionem concretam aptatum creare.
Gressus concreti:

Ex parte tua — auxilium in obtinenda et specificanda commissione technica (petitione) ab ordinatoribus proiectus restitutionis in Regione Charcoviensi. Quid exacte requiritur? Adaptatio psychologica? Reintegratio socialis? Disciplina novarum artium?

Ex parte nostra — super hac petitione, intra XIV dies, parabimus chartam itinerariam adaptatam, in gradus divisam:

Mandata brevis temporis (0-6 menses): methodologiae diagnosticae, technicae psychologicae urgentes.

Mandata medii temporis (6-18 menses): programmata disciplinae, exemplaria dynamicae gregialis.

Mandata longi temporis (18+ menses): exemplaria integrationis socio-oeconomicae, observatio effectuum diuturnorum.

Exitus: Proiectus communis cum distributione munerum ad clientes et donatores stipendiorum praesentandus.

### PROPOSITIO III. RATIFICATIO SOCIETATIS NICOLAI GREGORII FILII IN STRUCTURA IMARCH LTD UK
Scopus: Foedus nostrum in gradu internationali firmare atque auctoritatem tuam peritialem in actionibus globalibus societatis adhibere.
Propositio concreta:
Invitemus te ad ingrediendum Praesidium Consilii Directorum (Advisory Board) Imarch LTD UK. Hoc est munus honorificum et strategicum, quod comprehendit:

Consultationem de directionibus clavibus evolutionis.

Confirmationem legitimitatis societatis in circulis academicis et negotialibus.

Participationem in delectu et aestimatione proiectorum internationalium.
Ratifcatio — res technica est: omnia documenta corporativa necessaria paramus (decretum nominationis, descriptio potestatum). Memorandum intellectus mutui commune fundamentum optimum huius gradus erit.

SUMMARIUM ET PROXIMI GRADUS
Omnes tres propositiones partes unius strategiae sunt: creare foedus legitimum et operativum inter peritiam tuam academicam et platformam nostram methodologicam internationalem ad quaestiones concretas Ucrainae solvendas.

Ad procedendum propono:

Breve Memorandum intellectus mutui subscribere (fundamentum omnium).

Colloquium operativum (in rete) habere, ut:

Formam potissimam ex Propositione I determinemus.

Particularia petitionis veteranorum (Propositio II) specificemus.

Particularia participationis in Consilio Directorum (Propositio III) tractemus.

Ex parte mea, paratus sum omnia documenta necessaria ad disputandum praeparare.

Domine Nicolae Gavrilī fīli, confido cooperationem tam multifariam novas opportunitates aperire tam pro scientia applicata quam pro proiectis socialis momenti. Sententiam tuam exspecto et ad opus concretissimum paratus sum.

### CONCRETE PROPOSAL FOR A STRATEGIC ALLIANCE: LABORATORY, VETERANS PROJECT, FORMALIZATION OF PARTICIPATION

Authentic Text in Latin 

• Official English Translation • For the purposes of validation under international soft law norms

To the Most Esteemed Mr. Mykola Gavrylovych,

First and foremost, I thank you once again for the open conversation and expressed support. As you rightly noted, the key to implementation lies in specificity. Herein, I, as Founder and Director of Imarch LTD UK, structure our discussions into three concrete proposals that logically complement one another and form the basis for a comprehensive strategic alliance.

Context: Imarch LTD UK is an international structure developing applied formology — a methodology for the analysis, design, and forecasting of complex systems. Our foundation consists of: an authored monograph (2007), seventeen years of tool development, a registered UK company, and a detailed programmatic "Constitution."

PROPOSAL I. CREATION OF A SCIENTIFIC-APPLIED PLATFORM (LABORATORY/CENTER) BASED AT THE INSTITUTE
Goal: To institutionalize cooperation for legitimate scientific, grant-based, and expert activity.

Concrete forms of interaction (without being added to the institute's balance sheet):

A Scientific Research Laboratory under the institute's patronage. We (Imarch) shall act as the methodological and technological partner, providing research instrumentation (e.g., Pythia+ software for modeling). The institute shall provide legitimacy, access to academic networks, and the possibility of co-supervising postgraduate students.

A "Base Department" or "Experimental Platform." A format for piloting educational modules and methodologies on topics such as: system resilience analysis, the formological approach in social sciences, fundamentals of prognostics.

A Consortium for Grants. Joint preparation of applications to European Union funds (e.g., Horizon Europe, grants for citizen science) under the themes of post-conflict recovery, digital transformation, and humanitarian technologies.

Our Contribution: Methodology, project management, IT infrastructure, international contacts.
Request: Determination of the organizational-legal form most acceptable to the institute and initiation of work on the Charter/Regulations for such a laboratory.

PROPOSAL II. JOINT PROJECT: "AN ADAPTED COMPLEX OF PROGRAMS FOR A VETERANS REHABILITATION CENTER IN THE KHARKIV REGION"
Goal: To create not an abstract concept, but a ready-made product adapted to a specific request.
Concrete Steps:

From your side — assistance in obtaining and specifying the technical assignment (request) from the organizers of the rehabilitation project in the Kharkiv region. What exactly is required? Psychological adaptation? Social reintegration? Training in new skills?

From our side — based on this request, within fourteen (14) days, we shall prepare an adapted roadmap, divided into stages:

Short-term tasks (0-6 months): diagnostic methodologies, urgent psycho-techniques.

Medium-term tasks (6-18 months): training programs, models of group dynamics.

Long-term tasks (18+ months): models of socio-economic integration, monitoring of long-term effects.

Outcome: A joint project with a division of roles for presentation to clients and grantors.

PROPOSAL III. FORMALIZATION OF MYKOLA GAVRYLOVYCH'S PARTICIPATION IN THE IMARCH LTD UK STRUCTURE
Goal: To solidify our alliance at the international level and to utilize your expert authority in the company's global activities.
Concrete Proposal:
We invite you to join the Presidium of the Board of Directors (Advisory Board) of Imarch LTD UK. This is an honorary and strategic role, entailing:

Consultation on key development directions.

Strengthening the legitimacy of the company in academic and business circles.

Participation in the selection and evaluation of international projects.
Formalization is a technical matter: we shall prepare all necessary corporate documents (appointment decree, description of powers). Our shared Memorandum of Understanding will serve as the ideal foundation for this step.

SUMMARY AND NEXT STEPS
All three proposals are parts of a single strategy: to create a legitimate, operational alliance between your academic expertise and our international methodological platform to solve Ukraine's concrete challenges.

To proceed, I propose:

Signing a brief Memorandum of Understanding (the foundation for all).

Holding a working meeting (online), in order to:

Determine the priority form from Proposal I.

Specify the details of the veterans' request (Proposal II).

Discuss the details of participation on the Board of Directors (Proposal III).

On my part, I am ready to prepare drafts of all necessary documents for discussion.

Most Esteemed Mr. Mykola Gavrylovych, I am confident that such multifaceted cooperation will open new opportunities both for applied science and for socially significant projects. I await your opinion and am ready for the most concrete work.
