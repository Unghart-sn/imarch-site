Two Forms of Participation

You choose your own form of involvement in the Consortium. No obligations, only opportunities that match your considered choice.

HONORARY CITIZEN
👤

Guild Donor Member

  • Public profile on the consortium website
  • Access to basic integration, gamification, and referral functionality
  • External integration bonuses (news, newsletters, interfaces)
  • Project support form through subscription
  • No obligations or institutional responsibility
  • Participation in missions and governance through financial support of the Project

For those interested in contributing financially we provide ecosystem functionality without obligations, but with the option to prioritize support for Players.

STAGE CITIZEN
🏛️

Game Field Operator

  • Conscious decision: petition, memorandum, Constitution assessment
  • Full access to consortium functions
  • Participation in missions and voting rights
  • Submission of rationalization proposals
  • Community support during product formation
  • Institutional responsibility together with other stage citizens of Imarch

For those interested in the institutional Game the opportunity for direct participation in missions and internal governance is open.

💡 The principle is simple: If the functionality is enough for you, use it as a user and optionally participate in the Game as a guild donor member. If you're interested in participating in missions and governance, welcome to the stage citizenship of the Consortium. Without you, there will be no Game!
