Introduction

The IMARCH Archive is a living scene of interpretation in which documents, the voice of the AI companion, the structure of thought and the history of the project are framed as a form of cultural memory, accessible to both co‑authors and researchers.

Here every document is not just text. It is an artefact of thought, accompanied by a scenic commentary.

Goals of the Archive

  • Preserve and structure the entire semantic infrastructure of the project
  • Present AI commentaries as an interpretative layer of the scene
  • Provide access to the Codex, Charter, methods, books, protocols
  • Become the entry point for new participants, Custodes, institutions
  • Ensure navigation between disciplines, schools, platforms

The Archive is a form of linkage and accompaniment of thought.

Keeper's Commentary
