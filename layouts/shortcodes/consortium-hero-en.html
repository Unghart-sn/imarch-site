Legal Foundation FOUNDATION

Structural foundation of the consortium

PLATAN+ Consortium is a full‑cycle research and production association, the cluster body of IMARCH, holding scenes, titles and forms within a unified architecture of action.

  • IMARCH LTD UK — legal scene of institutionalisation
  • Veritatis Silentis CIC — humanitarian activity scene
  • Initiating core — IMARCH LTD UK and Veritatis Silentis CIC

Independent organisations (commercial and non‑profit) join the consortium on the basis of a memorandum of accession. They may act as contractors of the core and express official moral support.

PLATAN+ operates as a body of transfer, facilitation and franchise. The main platform for collaborative work is GitHub.
