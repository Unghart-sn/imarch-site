Ecosystem and Software — Key to Project Sustainability

Vis Realitatis effectiveness is ensured by its own digital infrastructure.
It integrates accounting, training, practice, and reporting into a single transparent system.

  • Accounting — resource, session, and result control.
  • Training — online school for couples, certification.
  • Practice — schedules, protocols, communication.
  • Reporting — automatic reports for donors.

The software enables scaling assistance, reducing costs, and guaranteeing donors control and trust.
