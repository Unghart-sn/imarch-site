Project Goals and Objectives

We are building a sustainable model of assistance for veterans and their families.
Our goals are divided into stages so that donors can understand how the project develops:

  • 0–12 months — pilot launch, training of first couples, start of digital infrastructure.
  • 12–24 months — scaling to regions, online school, and expansion of scientific base.
  • 3–5 years — international Vis Realitatis network, SunLife-Massage® franchise, and full sustainability.

Our goal system is built according to the international SMART standard: clear, transparent, and measurable.
