GitHub Structure

👥

Ecosystem Roles

  • Core Team: manages the legal and humanitarian scene
  • Associates: contribute via pull‑requests and projects
  • Mission‑Creators: develop body‑oriented practices, art therapy, human technologies
  • Content‑Factory: provides packaging, SEO, visualisation
🎯

Semantic emphasis

GitHub is only a tool. The semantic centre of the consortium is the methodology of humanitarian missions, where the main resource is the human factor. Participants are organisations, experts and ordinary people connected with human technologies, body‑oriented practice and art therapy as developers, testers and consumers.
