Mission of the Game

Creating an alternative space for non‑standard bearers of ideas: creative individuals, philosophers, missionaries.

A space where they can realize their impulses through title scenes, using poetic, classification, and institutional templates to participate in a constructive game of recognition.

To provide an internal foundation for the formation of ideas. To formalize actions within constructive patterns, not spontaneous informality.

🚀 Getting started

He who controls information controls the world. But not in the geopolitical sense. Above all, it is about controlling your own “inner” world.

Such control frees you from the grip of destructive emotions that force you into rigid patterns of automatic reactions, depriving you of freedom of choice.

So start simple: familiarize yourself with the information necessary to gain conscious control over your psycho‑emotional state.

Learn how to use the self‑organization tool we offer. Study the instructions and act. But not mindlessly. As you know, mindless people are taken for a ride.

We have entirely different tasks.
