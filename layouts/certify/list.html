What this proves

  • The exact file (by SHA-256) existed no later than the timestamp date.
  • The file has not been altered since — any change breaks the hash.
  • The certificate was issued by IMARCH and timestamped by an RFC 3161 TSA.
  • The hash is anchored to a Bitcoin block (upgrade after confirmation).

What this does not prove

  • Authorship or ownership of the content.
  • That the content is true, lawful, or complete.
  • The identity of the person who submitted it.
  • Legal validity in any particular jurisdiction.
