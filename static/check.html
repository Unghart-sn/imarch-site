IMARCH LTD — Certificate Verification

Cryptographically provable proof of priority

This page verifies the existence of a document at a given point in time. An RFC 3161 timestamp is final once issued. Bitcoin anchoring via OpenTimestamps is shown as pending until a Bitcoin block confirms it; only then is the Bitcoin attestation final. Verification does not make the document itself indestructible — only the fact of its existence at that time is cryptographically provable.
